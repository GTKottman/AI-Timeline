# Midjourney launches V1, its first AI video model

- **Date:** 2025-06-18
- **Who:** Midjourney
- **Type:** product

Midjourney released V1, its first video model, which turns one image into four five-second clips that can be extended, with low and high motion modes. It was offered to subscribers through Discord and the web. A 2025-06-19 update added a Save for Social Media export. It mattered as Midjourney's formal entry into AI video.
