# OpenAI announces reinforcement fine-tuning alpha for o1 models

- **Date:** 2024-12-06
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** software

On Day 2 of OpenAI's "12 Days" event, OpenAI previewed reinforcement fine-tuning (RFT) for its o1 models. Developers supply tasks and graders that score answers against references, and the model is trained to reason toward correct answers rather than imitate examples. It launched as an alpha research program for universities, researchers and businesses, with wider release expected in 2025. Day 2 is taken as December 6, the second day after the December 5 start, and the sources do not give the exact date.
