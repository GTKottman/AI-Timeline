# Sight Diagnostics Raises $27.8M Series C for AI Blood Testing

- **Date:** 2019-02-14
- **Who:** Sight Diagnostics (Israel); lead investor Longliv Ventures (CK Hutchison); investors also included OurCrowd
- **Type:** funding

Israeli medical device startup Sight Diagnostics closed a $27.8 million Series C led by Longliv Ventures, a healthcare fund owned by CK Hutchison. The company uses AI on point-of-care blood tests, and the money was earmarked for global expansion of its OLO system, US regulatory work and a wider test menu. Longliv's own release put its share at $10 million, and sources differ on the round's lead details. The round showed investor interest in AI for medical diagnostics.
