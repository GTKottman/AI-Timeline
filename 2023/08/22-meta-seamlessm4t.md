# Meta SeamlessM4T: one model for speech and text translation

- **Date:** 2023-08-22
- **Who:** Meta AI
- **Type:** model

SeamlessM4T is a single multilingual model for speech recognition, speech-to-text, speech-to-speech, text-to-text and text-to-speech translation, covering about 100 languages for recognition and text translation, with fewer languages for speech output. Meta said one system reduces errors and delays compared with chained models. It was released under a CC BY-NC 4.0 research license, so it is non-commercial, and Meta released the SeamlessAlign dataset with it.
