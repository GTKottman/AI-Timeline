# Commerce Closes Loophole Letting Advanced AI Chips Reach Chinese Firms' Overseas Units

- **Date:** 2026-05-31
- **Who:** US Commerce Department (BIS); Nvidia (Rubin, Blackwell); AMD (MI350-series); Chinese-headquartered firms with overseas subsidiaries
- **Type:** policy

Weekend guidance from the Commerce Department's Bureau of Industry and Security, dated Sunday, May 31, 2026, says export licences are required for advanced AI chips sold to entities headquartered in China, even when they operate outside China. The gap had been open since May 2025, when Commerce said it would not enforce the Biden-era AI Diffusion rule, and Malaysia was cited as a main location of affected subsidiaries. BIS described the guidance as clarifying licence requirements in place since 2023, and senators asked Commerce Secretary Lutnick to testify.
