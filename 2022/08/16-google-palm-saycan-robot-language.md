# Google PaLM-SayCan: language models grounding helper robots

- **Date:** 2022-08-16
- **Who:** Google Research, with Alphabet's Everyday Robots team
- **Type:** research

Google Research announced PaLM-SayCan, which pairs the PaLM language model with a physical robot. The language model turns a casual request, such as cleaning up a spilled drink, into a sequence of steps, and the robot's learned skill values filter those steps to what it can actually do. In the reported tests the robot chose correct steps in 84% of cases and completed the tasks 74% of the time. It mattered as an early large example of language models controlling embodied robots.
