# Meta brings Meta AI to Meta Quest 3 as a mixed-reality assistant

- **Date:** 2024-07-23
- **Who:** Meta
- **Type:** product

Meta introduced Meta AI with Vision on Meta Quest 3, an experimental version in the US and Canada that lets users ask about their real-world surroundings in Passthrough mode. It does not recognise virtual objects at this time. It extended the Llama-based assistant from phones and glasses into headsets.
