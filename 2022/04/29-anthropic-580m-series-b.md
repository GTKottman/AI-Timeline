# Anthropic raises $580M Series B

- **Date:** 2022-04-29
- **Who:** Anthropic
- **Type:** funding

Anthropic, the AI safety company founded in 2021, raised a $580 million Series B in April 2022, reportedly to fund large experimental infrastructure and safety research on compute-heavy models. Secondary trackers name FTX as lead, but that lead was not confirmed from a primary announcement, so it is left out here. The round made Anthropic one of the best-funded safety-focused labs.
