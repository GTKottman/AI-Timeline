# Alibaba releases Qwen3 open-weight model family

- **Date:** 2025-04-29
- **Who:** Alibaba (Qwen team)
- **Type:** model

Alibaba released Qwen3, a family of open-weight models with six dense models from 0.6B to 32B parameters, released under Apache 2.0. It also included two mixture-of-experts models, Qwen3-235B-A22B and Qwen3-30B-A3B.
