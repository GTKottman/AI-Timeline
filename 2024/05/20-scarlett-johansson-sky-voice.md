# Scarlett Johansson objects to OpenAI Sky voice; OpenAI pauses it

- **Date:** 2024-05-20
- **Who:** Scarlett Johansson, OpenAI, Sam Altman
- **Type:** other

Scarlett Johansson issued a statement saying OpenAI used a ChatGPT voice that sounded like her after she had declined an offer to voice the system. OpenAI said the voice belonged to another actress, then paused the Sky voice in its products and said it did not intend it to resemble Johansson. The dispute became a public test of consent and voice likeness in AI products and fed the push for voice-rights laws.
