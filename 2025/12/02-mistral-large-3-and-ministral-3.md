# Mistral releases Mistral Large 3 and Ministral 3

- **Date:** 2025-12-02
- **Who:** Mistral AI
- **Type:** model

Mistral released Mistral Large 3, a sparse mixture-of-experts model with 675B total and 41B active parameters, and the Ministral 3 family in 3B, 8B and 14B sizes. All models were released under the Apache 2.0 license.
