# OpenAI unveils Voice Engine but withholds broad release

- **Date:** 2024-03-29
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** model

OpenAI announced Voice Engine, a voice-cloning model that produces speech resembling a speaker from a single 15-second audio sample and text. OpenAI said it would share it only with a small set of early testers, not release it widely, citing misuse risk in an election year. Testers agreed not to impersonate people without consent and to disclose AI-generated voices. It was an early example of a lab holding back a capable generative model over impersonation risk.
