# Mistral AI releases Mistral 7B under Apache 2.0

- **Date:** 2023-09-27
- **Who:** Mistral AI
- **Type:** model

Mistral AI released Mistral 7B, a 7.3-billion-parameter model that the company said beat Llama 2 13B on every benchmark it tested. Its Apache 2.0 license made it one of the most popular small open models, and it launched Mistral as a major open-weight lab.
