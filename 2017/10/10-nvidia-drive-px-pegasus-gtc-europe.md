# NVIDIA announces DRIVE PX Pegasus Level 5 autonomous platform

- **Date:** 2017-10-10
- **Who:** NVIDIA
- **Type:** hardware

At GTC Europe in Munich, NVIDIA announced DRIVE PX Pegasus, an autonomous driving computer rated at 320 TOPS and aimed at fully self-driving Level 5 vehicles. NVIDIA said it delivers more than 10 times the performance of DRIVE PX 2. It extended NVIDIA's push from data center AI into automotive AI computing.
