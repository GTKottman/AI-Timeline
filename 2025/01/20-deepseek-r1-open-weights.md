# DeepSeek releases open-weight DeepSeek-R1 reasoning model

- **Date:** 2025-01-20
- **Who:** DeepSeek
- **Type:** model

DeepSeek released DeepSeek-R1 as open weights, a reasoning model that DeepSeek said was trained for far less than comparable Western models. It matched or came close to OpenAI's o1 on math and coding benchmarks while being freely downloadable. It set off a wide reassessment of how cheaply strong reasoning models could be built.
