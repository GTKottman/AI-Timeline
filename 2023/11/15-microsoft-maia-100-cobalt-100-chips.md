# Microsoft unveils Maia 100 AI accelerator and Cobalt 100 CPU

- **Date:** 2023-11-15
- **Who:** Microsoft
- **Type:** hardware

Microsoft unveiled Azure Maia 100, a 5nm AI accelerator for training and running AI workloads, and Cobalt 100, a 128-core Arm-based server CPU, at Ignite. Microsoft said Cobalt offers up to 40% better performance than current Azure Arm chips. The chips were due to roll out in 2024. It signaled that a major cloud provider was building its own AI silicon to reduce reliance on GPUs.
