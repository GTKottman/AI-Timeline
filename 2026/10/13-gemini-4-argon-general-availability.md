# Google makes Gemini 4 Argon generally available

- **Date:** 2026-10-13
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** model

Google made Gemini 4 Argon generally available, after release to the Fairwind Program on 30 September. It raised the output limit to 1,000,000 tokens and improved coding and business tasks.
