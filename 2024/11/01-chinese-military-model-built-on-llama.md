# Chinese researchers build a military AI model on Meta's Llama

- **Date:** 2024-11-01
- **Who:** Chinese researchers (PLA-linked); Meta
- **Type:** policy

Reuters reported that Chinese researchers had built a military-oriented AI model on Meta's open Llama weights, which Meta said was unauthorised. The case showed how open-weight releases can be repurposed by state actors and fed the debate over open-model export and misuse controls.
