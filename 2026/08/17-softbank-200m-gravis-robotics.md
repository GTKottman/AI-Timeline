# SoftBank invests $200 million in Gravis Robotics

- **Date:** 2026-08-17
- **Who:** SoftBank, Gravis Robotics (Zurich)
- **Type:** funding

Gravis Robotics announced on August 17, 2026 that SoftBank is investing $200 million in its Series A, which the company calls the largest Series A in construction robotics. Gravis makes retrofit autonomy kits (Gravis Rack, with the Copilot software layer) that turn existing excavators and other heavy machines into autonomous or semi-autonomous machines, rather than building machines itself. The company claims up to 30% jobsite productivity gains from its own project data, which is unverified.
