# European Commission names High-Level Expert Group on AI

- **Date:** 2018-06-14
- **Who:** European Commission; 52 experts from universities and companies
- **Type:** policy

The European Commission appointed its High-Level Expert Group on Artificial Intelligence, 52 experts drawn from universities and companies, and launched the European AI Alliance. The group was tasked with recommendations on AI policy, ethical, legal and socio-economic questions, and drafting AI ethics guidelines. Its first meeting was on June 27, 2018 in Brussels. It mattered as the start of the EU's official AI ethics guidance work.
