# Measuring Attribution in Natural Language Generation Models (AIS framework)

- **Date:** 2021-12-23
- **Who:** Hannah Rashkin and coauthors, Google Research
- **Type:** paper

Google Research posted an arXiv paper proposing Attributable to Identified Sources (AIS), a framework for checking whether text a generation model produces about the external world is supported by a provided source. The authors describe a two-stage human annotation pipeline and test it on conversational QA, summarization and table-to-text datasets, and they release their evaluation guidelines. It mattered for AI because it gave the field a concrete way to measure factual grounding in generated text.
