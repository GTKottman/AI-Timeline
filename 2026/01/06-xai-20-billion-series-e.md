# xAI Closes $20 Billion Series E

- **Date:** 2026-01-06
- **Who:** xAI; investors Valor Equity Partners, StepStone Group, Fidelity, Qatar Investment Authority, MGX, Baron Capital Group; strategic investors Nvidia and Cisco Investments
- **Type:** funding

xAI announced a $20 billion Series E, above its $15 billion target, with Nvidia and Cisco as strategic backers alongside financial investors. Trade coverage put the valuation at about $230 billion, but xAI did not confirm that figure and individual investor amounts were not broken out. The round came months before xAI's later corporate changes, so the valuation and structure should be checked against primary filings before reuse.
