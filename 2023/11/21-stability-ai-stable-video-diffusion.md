# Stability AI releases Stable Video Diffusion research preview

- **Date:** 2023-11-21
- **Who:** Stability AI
- **Type:** model

Stability AI released Stable Video Diffusion, its first foundation model for generative video, as a research preview. Two image-to-video models were released: SVD, trained to generate 14 frames, and SVD-XT, fine-tuned for 25 frames, both built on the Stable Diffusion image model. The code went on GitHub and the weights on Hugging Face under a research-only license, and Stability said it was not intended for real-world or commercial use at that stage.
