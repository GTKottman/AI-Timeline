# OpenAI Launches ChatGPT Health

- **Date:** 2026-01-07
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** product

OpenAI launched ChatGPT Health, a separate health section inside ChatGPT where users can connect wellness apps such as Apple Health and MyFitnessPal, and in the US, medical records through the b.well network. OpenAI says health chats are kept separate from regular chats and are not used to train its foundation models. Medical-records connections are limited to US users, and the Apple Health connection requires an iPhone.
