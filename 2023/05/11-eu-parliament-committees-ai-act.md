# EU Parliament committees approve AI Act position

- **Date:** 2023-05-11
- **Who:** European Parliament (IMCO and LIBE committees; rapporteurs Brando Benifei and Dragos Tudorache)
- **Type:** policy

The Parliament's Internal Market and Civil Liberties committees adopted their negotiating position on the EU AI Act. The amendments widened the definition of AI, added rules for foundation models and generative AI, and expanded the list of banned uses, though a proposed ban on remote biometric identification in public spaces was contested. The plenary vote was set for June. It mattered because it added generative AI rules to the bill before the Council and Commission negotiations.
