# DeepMind Opens Alberta Research Centre in Edmonton

- **Date:** 2017-07-05
- **Who:** DeepMind; University of Alberta; Alberta Machine Intelligence Institute (AMII); Richard Sutton, Michael Bowling, Patrick Pilarski
- **Type:** research

DeepMind announced a research centre in Edmonton, its first major site outside the UK, starting with about 10 staff. The lab was led by three University of Alberta computing science professors, Richard Sutton, Michael Bowling and Patrick Pilarski, who stayed with AMII while leading it. It mattered because it tied one of the most prominent AI labs to a major academic reinforcement learning group, and Canada's government publicly welcomed it.
