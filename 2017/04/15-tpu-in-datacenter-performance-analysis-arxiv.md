# "In-Datacenter Performance Analysis of a Tensor Processing Unit" posted to arXiv

- **Date:** 2017-04-15
- **Who:** Google (Jouppi et al.)
- **Type:** paper

Google posted a paper analyzing the performance of its first-generation TPU in its own datacenters, against contemporary CPUs and GPUs on production neural network inference workloads. It showed the TPU delivering much higher performance per watt than the alternatives. The paper was presented at ISCA 2017 in June, and it set the template for the public case for custom AI accelerators.
