# Mistral launches Le Chat mobile apps and rebrand

- **Date:** 2025-02-06
- **Who:** Mistral AI
- **Type:** product

Mistral released Le Chat on iOS and Android with a revamped interface, a new logo and a new Android app. One account says the app reached 1 million downloads within two weeks and topped France's free iOS chart. The figures come from secondary coverage.
