# DINO shows self-supervised Vision Transformers learn segmentation-like features

- **Date:** 2021-04-29
- **Who:** Mathilde Caron et al. (Facebook AI Research, Inria, Sorbonne University)
- **Type:** paper

The paper "Emerging Properties in Self-Supervised Vision Transformers" (arXiv 2104.14294, first submitted 29 April 2021) introduced DINO, a label-free self-distillation method. Self-supervised ViT features contain explicit object segmentation information, which supervised ViTs and convnets lacked, and they work well for k-NN classification. DINO reached 80.1% top-1 linear accuracy on ImageNet with a ViT-Base using small patches. The paper was published at ICCV 2021.
