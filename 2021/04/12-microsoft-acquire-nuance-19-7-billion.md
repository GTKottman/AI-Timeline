# Microsoft agrees to acquire Nuance for $19.7 billion

- **Date:** 2021-04-12
- **Who:** Microsoft, Nuance Communications
- **Type:** acquisition

Microsoft announced on April 12, 2021 an all-cash deal to buy speech and conversational AI firm Nuance at $56 per share, valued at $19.7 billion including net debt. Nuance's technology underpins voice recognition in products such as Apple's Siri, and its clinical ambient AI was a core part of the deal. It was Microsoft's largest acquisition since LinkedIn.
