# Simile raises $100M Series A for human-behavior simulation

- **Date:** 2026-02-12
- **Who:** Simile (AI simulation of human behavior); Index Ventures (lead), with Bain Capital Ventures, A* and Hanabi Capital
- **Type:** funding

Simile announced a $100 million Series A to build AI models that predict human behavior using digital twins of real people. Index Ventures announced the round on February 12; Latham & Watkins dated its own item to February 19. Angel investors reportedly included Andrej Karpathy and Fei-Fei Li. It is one of several February rounds for models that simulate people rather than generate content.
