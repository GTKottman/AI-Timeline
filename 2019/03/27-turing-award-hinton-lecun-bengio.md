# ACM names Hinton, LeCun and Bengio 2018 Turing Award laureates

- **Date:** 2019-03-27
- **Who:** ACM; Geoffrey Hinton (Google / Vector Institute), Yann LeCun (NYU / Facebook), Yoshua Bengio (Montreal / Mila)
- **Type:** people

ACM named Geoffrey Hinton, Yann LeCun and Yoshua Bengio the recipients of the 2018 A.M. Turing Award for conceptual and engineering breakthroughs that made deep neural networks a critical part of computing. The $1 million prize is often called the "Nobel Prize of computing." It was a major recognition of deep learning as a field.
