# PyTorch Developer Day 2020 held virtually

- **Date:** 2020-11-12
- **Who:** PyTorch (Facebook AI)
- **Type:** other

PyTorch held a virtual Developer Day on November 12, 2020 for researchers and developers. It had talks on new software releases, academic and industry uses of PyTorch and ML development trends, plus a poster session and networking. It was the framework's first developer conference event and a sign of its growing research and industry adoption.
