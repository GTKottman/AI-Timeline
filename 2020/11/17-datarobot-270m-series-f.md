# DataRobot raises $270M pre-IPO round led by Altimeter Capital

- **Date:** 2020-11-17
- **Who:** DataRobot; led by Altimeter Capital, with T. Rowe Price, BlackRock, Tiger Global, Silver Lake Waterman, B Capital, NEA, Sapphire Ventures and others
- **Type:** funding

DataRobot announced $270 million in pre-IPO funding led by Altimeter Capital on November 17, 2020, valuing the automated machine learning platform company at over $2.7 billion, more than double its Series E valuation. The platform helps enterprises build and deploy AI and ML models automatically. Later, Snowflake, HPE and Salesforce Ventures added $50 million, taking the Series F to $320 million.
