# Google releases Gemini 1.0 Ultra and launches Gemini Advanced

- **Date:** 2024-02-08
- **Who:** Google; Google DeepMind
- **Type:** model

Gemini 1.0 Ultra, Google's largest Gemini model, became available to users through Gemini Advanced, a paid tier at $19.99 a month in the Google One AI Premium plan. Bard was renamed Gemini with a new mobile app. The paid tier was aimed at matching ChatGPT Plus.
