# NVIDIA to supply 260,000 GPUs to South Korea

- **Date:** 2025-10-31
- **Who:** NVIDIA; South Korea's Ministry of Science and ICT; Samsung, SK Group, Hyundai Motor Group, Naver Cloud
- **Type:** hardware

At the APEC summit in Gyeongju, NVIDIA said it would supply more than 260,000 of its GPUs to South Korea. The deal was arranged with the Ministry of Science and ICT and includes AI factories built with Samsung, SK Group and Hyundai Motor Group at about 50,000 GPUs each and Naver Cloud at about 60,000. The rest goes to public-sector projects such as Seoul's National AI Computing Center and to cloud and IT providers. Financial terms were not disclosed; local reports put the plan at up to $10 billion over several years.
