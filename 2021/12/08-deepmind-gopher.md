# DeepMind unveils Gopher 280B language model

- **Date:** 2021-12-08
- **Who:** DeepMind
- **Type:** model

DeepMind introduced Gopher, a 280-billion-parameter language model, and studied how scale changed its performance across tasks. The same write-up covered ethical risks of large models and a retrieval-based approach. It was one of the largest dense models of its time.
