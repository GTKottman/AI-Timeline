# Google makes Gemini 2.0 Flash generally available and previews 2.0 Flash-Lite

- **Date:** 2025-02-05
- **Who:** Google / Google DeepMind
- **Type:** model

Google moved Gemini 2.0 Flash to general availability with higher rate limits and simplified pricing. It also released Gemini 2.0 Pro as an experimental model, which one report says has a 2 million token context window, and put Gemini 2.0 Flash-Lite into public preview as its most cost-efficient model. Google's developer blog gives February 5, 2025 as the date.
