# Meta releases BlenderBot 3 conversational AI

- **Date:** 2022-08-05
- **Who:** Meta AI
- **Type:** model

Meta AI released BlenderBot 3, a chatbot built on the 175-billion-parameter OPT model. Code, datasets and smaller models were public; the 175B model was restricted to approved researchers on request. Unlike BlenderBot 2 it can search the internet to discuss almost any topic, and it learns from live conversations and user feedback, filtering suspicious feedback. Meta said openly that it was "certainly not at a human level," and coverage soon reported wrong and inconsistent answers.
