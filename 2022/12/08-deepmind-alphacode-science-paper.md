# DeepMind AlphaCode published in Science

- **Date:** 2022-12-08
- **Who:** DeepMind (Yujia Li and co-authors)
- **Type:** research

DeepMind published "Competition-level Code Generation with AlphaCode" in Science, with the paper on the issue cover, after the February 2022 blog announcement. On Codeforces contests with more than 5,000 participants, AlphaCode placed around the median competitor, ranking in the top 54.3% on average in simulated evaluations. The peer-reviewed version credited three factors: a clean competitive-programming dataset, efficient transformer sampling, and filtering of large sample sets by program behavior.
