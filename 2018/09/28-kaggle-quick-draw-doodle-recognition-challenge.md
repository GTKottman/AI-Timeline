# Google Research launches the Kaggle Quick, Draw! Doodle Recognition Challenge

- **Date:** 2018-09-28
- **Who:** Google Research
- **Type:** competition

Google Research launched a Kaggle challenge to build doodle classifiers for the Quick, Draw! dataset. Its drawings come from the game and can be incomplete or mislabeled, so entrants had to learn from noisy labels. They were then tested on a manually labeled set drawn from a different distribution. The contest targeted a practical problem: learning from messy, crowd-sourced data.
