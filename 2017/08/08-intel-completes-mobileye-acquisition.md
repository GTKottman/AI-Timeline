# Intel Completes Tender Offer for Mobileye

- **Date:** 2017-08-08
- **Who:** Intel; Mobileye (Amnon Shashua)
- **Type:** acquisition

Intel and Mobileye announced that Intel had completed its tender offer for Mobileye's shares, giving Intel about 84% of the company in a deal valued at about $15.3 billion, announced in March 2017. Mobileye stayed headquartered in Jerusalem under co-founder Amnon Shashua, and the combined unit was to lead Intel's autonomous driving work. It mattered because a leading vision-based driver-assistance chip maker came under a major chipmaker's control for autonomous driving.
