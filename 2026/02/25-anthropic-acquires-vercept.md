# Anthropic acquires Vercept to advance Claude computer use

- **Date:** 2026-02-25
- **Who:** Anthropic; Vercept (co-founders Kiana Ehsani, Luca Weihs, Ross Girshick join Anthropic)
- **Type:** acquisition

Anthropic announced it acquired Vercept, a startup working on AI perception and software interaction, to strengthen Claude's ability to operate computers. Its co-founders joined Anthropic and Vercept's external product was to shut down in the coming weeks. Anthropic cited a rise in Sonnet's OSWorld computer-use score from under 15% in late 2024 to 72.5% (Sonnet 4.6). Terms were not disclosed. It was Anthropic's second recent team deal after Bun.
