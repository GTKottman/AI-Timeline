# Amazon completes $4 billion total investment in Anthropic

- **Date:** 2024-03-27
- **Who:** Amazon, Anthropic, AWS
- **Type:** funding

Amazon completed its $4 billion commitment to Anthropic by investing the remaining $2.75 billion, after an initial $1.25 billion in September 2023. Amazon holds a minority stake with no board seat, and AWS became Anthropic's primary cloud provider, with future models to use AWS Trainium and Inferentia chips. Amazon called it its largest venture investment to date, and it tied Anthropic's training compute to AWS hardware.
