# EU Artificial Intelligence Act enters into force

- **Date:** 2024-08-01
- **Who:** European Commission; European Parliament; Council of the EU
- **Type:** policy

The EU AI Act became law and its obligations start phasing in over the following three years. It is the first comprehensive binding AI regulation in a major economy, with bans on certain practices and extra duties for high-risk and general-purpose models.
