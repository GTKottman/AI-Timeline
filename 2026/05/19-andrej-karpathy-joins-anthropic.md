# Andrej Karpathy joins Anthropic's pre-training team

- **Date:** 2026-05-19
- **Who:** Andrej Karpathy, Anthropic
- **Type:** people

Andrej Karpathy, an OpenAI co-founder and former Tesla AI lead, joined Anthropic's pre-training team under Nick Joseph, as reported by TechCrunch on May 19, 2026. An Anthropic spokesperson said he will help start a team that uses Claude to accelerate pre-training research. Karpathy announced the move himself on X, and Axios called it a major talent win for Anthropic.
