# xAI releases Grok 4.1 and Grok 4.1 Fast

- **Date:** 2025-11-17
- **Who:** xAI
- **Type:** model

xAI released Grok 4.1, a reasoning and vision model focused on conversational quality and everyday helpfulness, followed by Grok 4.1 Fast with a 2M-token context window on Nov 19. The Fast variant aimed at cheap, long-context agent use.
