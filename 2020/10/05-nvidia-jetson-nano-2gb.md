# NVIDIA launches Jetson Nano 2GB developer kit at $59

- **Date:** 2020-10-05
- **Who:** NVIDIA
- **Type:** hardware

At NVIDIA's fall GTC 2020 (October 5), NVIDIA announced the Jetson Nano 2GB developer kit at $59. It is a 2GB-memory version of the Jetson Nano edge AI module, aimed at students, educators and robotics hobbyists.
