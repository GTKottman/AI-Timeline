# Google releases Gemini 3.1 Pro

- **Date:** 2026-02-19
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** model

Google DeepMind released Gemini 3.1 Pro, based on Gemini 3 Pro. It was the first .1 point release in the Gemini line, and Google launched it in preview.
