# Scale AI raises $325 million Series E at $7.3 billion valuation

- **Date:** 2021-04-13
- **Who:** Scale AI; co-led by Dragoneer, Greenoaks Capital and Tiger Global
- **Type:** funding

Scale AI, a data-labeling and training-data platform for machine learning, announced a $325 million Series E at a $7.3 billion valuation on April 13, 2021. Wellington Management and Durable Capital joined as new investors. CEO Alexandr Wang said sales had doubled over the prior year and the company was on track for $100 million in revenue over twelve months. High-quality labeled data was a core bottleneck for supervised AI, and Scale's funding reflected demand for it.
