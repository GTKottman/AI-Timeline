# MLPerf Training v0.6 results published

- **Date:** 2019-07-10
- **Who:** MLPerf (Google, NVIDIA, Intel submitters; Google Cloud and NVIDIA led at-scale wins)
- **Type:** competition

MLPerf released its second round of training benchmark results, v0.6, timing six models (including image classification, object detection, translation, reinforcement learning and Go) to a quality target. Sixty-three entries came from five submitters, up from three in the first round. Google Cloud and NVIDIA each took three at-scale wins, showing that the benchmark had become a common yardstick for AI training hardware and software.
