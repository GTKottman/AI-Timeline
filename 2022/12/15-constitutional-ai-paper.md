# Anthropic publishes Constitutional AI (RLAIF) paper

- **Date:** 2022-12-15
- **Who:** Anthropic (Yuntao Bai, Jared Kaplan et al.)
- **Type:** paper

Anthropic's arXiv paper 'Constitutional AI: Harmlessness from AI Feedback' trained a harmless assistant with almost no human harm labels. The model critiqued and revised its own answers against a short list of written principles, then used AI preference feedback for RL. The method became a core safety-training technique for later assistants.
