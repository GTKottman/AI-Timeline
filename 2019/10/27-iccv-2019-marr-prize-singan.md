# ICCV 2019 best paper (Marr Prize) goes to SinGAN

- **Date:** 2019-10-27
- **Who:** Tamar Rott Shaham, Tali Dekel, Tomer Michaeli (Technion and Google Research); ICCV 2019 organizers
- **Type:** competition

The Marr Prize at ICCV 2019 in Seoul went to "SinGAN: Learning a Generative Model from a Single Natural Image," a generative model trained on one photo that can produce varied, realistic images at several scales. The best student paper award went to "PLMP: Point-Line Minimal Problems in Complete Multi-View Visibility." The conference ran October 27 to November 2, 2019; the exact day of the award presentation is not confirmed, so the date given is the conference start.
