# DeepMind presents WaveRNN at ICML 2018

- **Date:** 2018-07-09
- **Who:** DeepMind
- **Type:** paper

DeepMind's ICML 2018 preview post, published July 9 ahead of the Stockholm conference, featured "Efficient Neural Audio Synthesis". It introduced WaveRNN, a compact single-layer recurrent network with a dual softmax output layer. The paper claimed it matched the quality of the much larger WaveNet, so speech could be generated cheaply enough for practical devices. It mattered because it made high-quality neural speech synthesis far less costly to run.
