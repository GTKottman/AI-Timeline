# DeepSeek quietly releases DeepSeek-V3-0324 under MIT licence

- **Date:** 2025-03-24
- **Who:** DeepSeek
- **Type:** model

DeepSeek posted an updated DeepSeek-V3 checkpoint, V3-0324, to Hugging Face without a formal announcement, and its README was initially empty. Coverage says it improved Chinese writing and search, and front-end web coding, and that it ships under the MIT license. Sources disagree on the total parameter count, reported as 671 or 685 billion.
