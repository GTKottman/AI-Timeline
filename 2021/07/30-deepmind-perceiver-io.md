# DeepMind releases Perceiver IO general architecture

- **Date:** 2021-07-30
- **Who:** DeepMind (Andrew Jaegle, Daniel Zoran, Oriol Vinyals and others)
- **Type:** research

DeepMind's Perceiver IO, a general architecture for structured inputs and outputs, was posted to arXiv (2107.14795) on 30 July 2021. The original Perceiver could only produce simple outputs such as classification; Perceiver IO uses a small latent array with a new decoding procedure to scale to large, structured outputs. The paper reports that it outperforms a BERT-style Transformer baseline on GLUE without input tokenization, and that it sets state of the art on Sintel optical flow.
