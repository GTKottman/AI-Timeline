# Google DeepMind announces UK automated research lab and government partnership

- **Date:** 2025-12-10
- **Who:** Google DeepMind; UK government
- **Type:** research

Google DeepMind announced a partnership with the UK government and its first automated research lab in the UK, due to open in 2026. The lab will use robotics to synthesize and test materials at high volume, tightly integrated with Gemini models, aimed at materials science and clean energy including fusion. Sources differ on the initial focus (superconductors in Fortune and R&D World, batteries and semiconductors in another report), and Google had not disclosed financials or staffing.
