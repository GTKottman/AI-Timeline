# Gupshup acquires AskSid

- **Date:** 2022-04-19
- **Who:** Gupshup; AskSid (Bengaluru)
- **Type:** acquisition

Gupshup, a conversational messaging platform, acquired AskSid, a Bengaluru conversational-AI provider for e-commerce and retail, for an undisclosed sum. AskSid's retail AI models and multilingual support were meant to make the whole shopping journey conversational inside Gupshup's products. Gupshup reported that AskSid automated 92 percent of support conversations for its customers; these are company-reported figures. It was another step in the consolidation of chatbot and conversational-AI vendors.
