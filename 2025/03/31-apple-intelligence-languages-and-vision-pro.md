# Apple Intelligence expands to new languages and Vision Pro

- **Date:** 2025-03-31
- **Who:** Apple
- **Type:** product

Apple expanded Apple Intelligence features to new languages and regions and brought Apple Intelligence to Apple Vision Pro with visionOS 2.4 on the same day. It was a rollout expansion, not a new AI product, and coverage at the time noted that some planned Siri features were still delayed. Only Apple's newsroom archive headlines were seen, not the full releases.
