# Baidu puts Kunlun II AI chip into mass production, unveils Baidu Brain 7.0

- **Date:** 2021-08-18
- **Who:** Baidu (Kunlun Chip unit)
- **Type:** hardware

At Baidu World 2021, held virtually, Baidu said its second-generation Kunlun II AI chip had entered mass production, alongside the Baidu Brain 7.0 AI platform. Baidu claimed roughly two to three times the processing power of the first Kunlun, built on a 7nm process with its second-generation XPU architecture, and aimed at cloud, edge and autonomous driving workloads. One report says the chip was manufactured by Samsung.
