# xAI releases Grok 4 and the SuperGrok Heavy tier

- **Date:** 2025-07-09
- **Who:** xAI
- **Type:** model

xAI released Grok 4, which it called its most intelligent model, through its app for SuperGrok and Premium+ subscribers and through its API. xAI reported about 50% on Humanity's Last Exam for Grok 4 Heavy and 15.9% on ARC-AGI-2 for the base Grok 4 model. It also introduced the SuperGrok Heavy tier with access to the heaviest version of the model.
