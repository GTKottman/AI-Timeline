# Google invests $75 million in A24 for DeepMind AI filmmaking research

- **Date:** 2026-06-23
- **Who:** Google, Google DeepMind, A24
- **Type:** other

Google is investing about $75 million in the independent studio A24 as part of a multiyear research partnership with Google DeepMind to build AI production tools for filmmakers. DeepMind researchers will work with A24 filmmakers and A24 Labs on workflows such as storyboarding. The deal is nonexclusive and gives Google no access to A24's content library or data. Some outlets call it Alphabet's first equity stake in a film studio, which could not be confirmed.
