# Cohere raises $270M Series C at over $2.1B valuation

- **Date:** 2023-06-08
- **Who:** Cohere; investors incl. NVIDIA and Oracle
- **Type:** funding

Enterprise language-model company Cohere raised $270 million in a Series C round backed by NVIDIA and Oracle, valuing it at over $2.1 billion. The round funded its push to sell custom LLMs and APIs to businesses rather than consumers. It was one of the largest generative-AI rounds of the period.
