# Google AudioLM: language modeling approach to audio generation

- **Date:** 2022-10-06
- **Who:** Google Research (Zalan Borsos, Neil Zeghidour and co-authors)
- **Type:** research

Google introduced AudioLM, which generates speech and piano music by treating audio as discrete tokens and modeling them with a language-model objective, with no transcripts needed. The authors reported long-term consistency and high fidelity in the output, and the team released a classifier to detect AudioLM-generated audio. The approach carried the language-modeling recipe from text into sound and later fed into AudioPaLM.
