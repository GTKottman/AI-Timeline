# Canva launches first Magic AI tools

- **Date:** 2023-03-28
- **Who:** Canva
- **Type:** product

Canva debuted its "Magic" AI-powered tools, which automate design tasks such as logo creation and video editing, with features named Magic Replace and Magic Eraser. The release also added brand-management tools including a Brand Hub. It was an early example of a mainstream design product bringing generative AI into everyday editing, before the full Magic Studio suite arrived in October 2023.
