# Facebook AI releases Hateful Memes dataset and challenge

- **Date:** 2020-05-12
- **Who:** Facebook AI (Meta AI) research team; hosted by DrivenData; NeurIPS 2020 competition track
- **Type:** dataset

Facebook AI released the Hateful Memes dataset of more than 10,000 new multimodal examples, with images licensed from Getty Images to avoid copyright problems, along with a challenge and a $100,000 prize pool. The task is to detect hate speech where the meaning comes from combining image and text, which single-modality classifiers miss. The challenge drew more than 3,300 entrants.
