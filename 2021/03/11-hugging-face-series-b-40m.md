# Hugging Face raises $40 million Series B

- **Date:** 2021-03-11
- **Who:** Hugging Face; lead investor Addition, with Lux Capital, A.Capital and Betaworks
- **Type:** funding

Hugging Face closed a $40 million Series B on March 11, 2021 to grow its open-source natural language processing library and model hub. The funding targeted building an open community around transformer models, which later became the main distribution point for open-weight models. Coverage said the company planned to expand its open-source community for language model development.
