# Amazon to invest up to $25 billion more in Anthropic

- **Date:** 2026-04-20
- **Who:** Amazon, Anthropic
- **Type:** funding

Amazon will invest $5 billion now and up to $20 billion more tied to commercial milestones. In return Anthropic plans to spend over $100 billion on AWS over ten years and secure up to 5 GW of capacity, with about 1 GW of Trainium2 and Trainium3 capacity expected online by year-end. It ties one frontier lab's compute and funding to a single cloud, a template that other labs are now copying.
