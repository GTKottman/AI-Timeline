# NVIDIA unveils Clara platform for medical-instrument AI

- **Date:** 2018-09-12
- **Who:** NVIDIA
- **Type:** product

NVIDIA unveiled Clara at GTC Japan in Tokyo, a platform for building AI into medical imaging and instruments. It paired the Clara AGX hardware, built on the Xavier AI module and Turing GPUs, with a software development kit for processing data from CT, MRI and ultrasound systems. The SDK went to early-access partners first, with broader release planned for the following year. It mattered because it pushed GPU-based AI from research into clinical device pipelines.
