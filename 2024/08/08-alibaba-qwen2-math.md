# Alibaba Qwen team releases Qwen2-Math models

- **Date:** 2024-08-08
- **Who:** Alibaba Qwen team
- **Type:** model

The Qwen team released Qwen2-Math, a math-specialised family built on Qwen2 in 1.5B, 7B and 72B sizes, each as base and instruct variants. Alibaba claimed the 72B instruct model beat GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Gemini 1.5 Pro and Llama-3.1-405B on math benchmarks, a company-reported result. The launch was English-only, with bilingual models planned, and Qwen2.5-Math followed later that year.
