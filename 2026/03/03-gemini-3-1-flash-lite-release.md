# Google releases Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite

- **Date:** 2026-03-03
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** model

Google released Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite, a low-latency model for high-volume jobs such as translation and text classification. It extended the Gemini 3.1 line to cheap, fast tiers for developers.
