# UK publishes "A pro-innovation approach to AI regulation" white paper

- **Date:** 2023-03-29
- **Who:** UK Department for Science, Innovation and Technology
- **Type:** policy

The UK government published a white paper that chose not to create a new AI-specific law and instead asked existing sector regulators to apply five cross-cutting principles: safety, transparency, fairness, accountability and contestability. The government set goals of growth, public trust and UK leadership in AI. It mattered as the reference model for a sector-based, non-statutory approach that contrasted with the EU's AI Act.
