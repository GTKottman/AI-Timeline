# BEGAN: Boundary Equilibrium Generative Adversarial Networks

- **Date:** 2017-03-31
- **Who:** David Berthelot, Thomas Schumm, Luke Metz
- **Type:** paper

arXiv paper submitted March 31, 2017 that proposes an autoencoder-based discriminator trained with a loss derived from the Wasserstein distance. A balance term keeps the generator and discriminator in equilibrium, and the paper gives a convergence measure and a knob that trades sample diversity against visual quality. The paper reported CIFAR-10 image generation results.
