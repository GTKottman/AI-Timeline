# Meta creates Superintelligence Labs under Alexandr Wang

- **Date:** 2025-06-30
- **Who:** Meta; Mark Zuckerberg; Alexandr Wang; Nat Friedman
- **Type:** people

In an internal memo, Mark Zuckerberg created Meta Superintelligence Labs and placed Meta's AI teams under it. This included the large language model and product teams and the FAIR research group, along with a new lab for next-generation models. Alexandr Wang, who had joined from Scale AI, became chief AI officer, and Nat Friedman was set to lead AI products and applied research. The memo said Meta would spend hundreds of billions of dollars on AI in the coming years. Sources say the unit was later split into smaller divisions, but the date of that split was not found.
