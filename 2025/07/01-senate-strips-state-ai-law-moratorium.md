# Senate votes 99-1 to strip AI law moratorium from budget bill

- **Date:** 2025-07-01
- **Who:** US Senate; Sens. Marsha Blackburn, Maria Cantwell; Sen. Thom Tillis
- **Type:** policy

In the early hours of 2025-07-01 the Senate voted 99-1 to remove a ten-year ban on state AI regulation from the Republican budget reconciliation bill. The amendment was offered by Sens. Marsha Blackburn and Maria Cantwell, and Sen. Thom Tillis cast the only no vote. Sources say 17 Republican governors and 40 state attorneys general opposed the ban. It mattered because it kept US states free to write their own AI laws.
