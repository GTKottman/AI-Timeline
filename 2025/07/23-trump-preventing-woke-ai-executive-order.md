# Trump signs executive order on Preventing Woke AI in the Federal Government

- **Date:** 2025-07-23
- **Who:** President Donald Trump; OMB; US federal agencies
- **Type:** policy

Executive Order 14319 requires federal agencies to buy only large language models that meet two "Unbiased AI Principles": truth-seeking and ideological neutrality. Vendors must disclose system prompts, specifications and evaluations but not model weights, and OMB must issue guidance within 120 days. It mattered because it tied federal AI purchasing to content standards for model outputs.
