# Mobileye unveils EyeQ Ultra autonomous driving chip at CES 2022

- **Date:** 2022-01-04
- **Who:** Mobileye (Intel)
- **Type:** hardware

At CES 2022 on January 4, Mobileye unveiled the EyeQ Ultra, which it described as its most advanced system-on-chip for autonomous driving, rated at 176 TOPS and aimed at Level 4 vehicles. Mobileye said first silicon was expected at the end of 2023 and automotive-grade production in 2025. It is dedicated inference hardware for self-driving perception and planning.
