# Munich court rules Suno infringed GEMA music copyrights

- **Date:** 2026-07-31
- **Who:** Munich Regional Court I (42nd Civil Chamber), GEMA, Suno
- **Type:** policy

On July 31, 2026 the Munich Regional Court I largely ruled for the music rights society GEMA against AI music generator Suno. The court found that training on GEMA works in the US, and storing and reproducing them in Europe, infringed German and US copyright law, and it rejected Suno's argument that its outputs were not recognisably similar to the originals. Suno must stop the reproduction and training and disclose infringement-related revenue, with damages still to be set. One secondary source reports fines of up to 250,000 euros per future breach; this is not confirmed elsewhere. The judgment is not yet enforceable. It is GEMA's second win against an AI company, after a November 2025 ruling against OpenAI.
