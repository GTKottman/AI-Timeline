# EU Council and Parliament reach provisional deal to simplify AI rules

- **Date:** 2026-05-07
- **Who:** Council of the EU; European Parliament
- **Type:** policy

The Council and Parliament reached a provisional agreement to simplify and streamline the EU AI Act, following the Commission's Digital Omnibus proposal from November. It confirmed a softer timetable for high-risk rules.
