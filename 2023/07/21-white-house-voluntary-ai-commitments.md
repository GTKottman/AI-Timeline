# White House secures voluntary AI safety commitments from seven companies

- **Date:** 2023-07-21
- **Who:** White House; Amazon, Anthropic, Google, Inflection, Meta, Microsoft, OpenAI
- **Type:** policy

The White House announced voluntary commitments from seven AI companies on safety, security and trust. The companies agreed to pre-release testing, partly by independent experts, cybersecurity and insider-threat safeguards, third-party vulnerability reporting, watermarking or similar mechanisms for AI-generated content, and public reports on model capabilities and limits. Legal commentary noted the commitments were not enforceable, but they signaled issues the administration would likely take up in future rules.
