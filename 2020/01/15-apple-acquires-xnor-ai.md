# Apple acquires Xnor.ai for on-device AI

- **Date:** 2020-01-15
- **Who:** Apple; Xnor.ai (Seattle, spun out of Allen Institute for AI)
- **Type:** acquisition

Apple bought Xnor.ai, a Seattle company making efficient machine learning and image recognition for low-power devices, for a reported price of about $200 million. Xnor's approach relied on quantized neural networks that run on the device rather than in the cloud. Apple confirmed only that it buys smaller technology companies from time to time. Coverage tied the deal to Apple's on-device AI and privacy strategy.
