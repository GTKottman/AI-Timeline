# Gmail launches Smart Compose, neural sentence completion while you type

- **Date:** 2018-05-16
- **Who:** Google (Gmail team)
- **Type:** product

Google introduced Smart Compose, a Gmail feature that suggests sentence completions as people type, building on the earlier Smart Reply. The model combines a bag-of-words encoding of the subject and previous message with a recurrent language model. TPUs brought its response time down to tens of milliseconds, so suggestions could appear while typing. It put a neural language model into everyday email writing.
