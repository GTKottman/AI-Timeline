# Google releases Gemini 3.8 Flash and a cyber variant

- **Date:** 2026-09-02
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** model

Google released Gemini 3.8 Flash, built on Gemini 3.7 Flash. Google also released Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber, a cybersecurity-focused variant, which is available only through its restricted Fairwind Program to vetted governments, critical infrastructure operators and core technology platforms.
