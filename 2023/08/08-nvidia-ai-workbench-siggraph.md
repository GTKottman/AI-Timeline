# NVIDIA AI Workbench and Hugging Face training service at SIGGRAPH

- **Date:** 2023-08-08
- **Who:** NVIDIA; Hugging Face
- **Type:** software

At SIGGRAPH 2023, NVIDIA announced AI Workbench, a toolkit for building, moving and collaborating on AI projects across PCs, workstations, data centers, cloud and DGX Cloud. It also launched a Hugging Face training service powered by NVIDIA DGX Cloud and version 4.0 of NVIDIA AI Enterprise. The moves aimed to make custom generative-AI model training easier for enterprises.
