# OMB memo M-24-10 sets federal rules for agency AI use

- **Date:** 2024-03-28
- **Who:** White House Office of Management and Budget
- **Type:** policy

OMB issued Memorandum M-24-10, which implements the October 2023 Biden AI executive order across federal agencies. It requires each agency to name a Chief AI Officer within 60 days, publish an AI use-case inventory, and run impact assessments before using "safety-impacting" or "rights-impacting" AI, with noncompliant uses to be ended by December 1, 2024. It is the main federal rulebook for government use of AI.
