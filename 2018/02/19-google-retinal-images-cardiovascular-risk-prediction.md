# Google predicts cardiovascular risk factors from retinal photos

- **Date:** 2018-02-19
- **Who:** Google Research (with medical collaborators)
- **Type:** research

Google Research described a deep learning model trained on retinal images from 284,335 patients. It predicted cardiovascular risk factors such as smoking status and blood pressure, and estimated heart attack and stroke risk with moderate accuracy. Attention heatmaps showed which parts of each eye image drove each prediction, such as blood vessels for blood pressure. It showed that a routine eye photo could carry medical signal the eye doctor does not read.
