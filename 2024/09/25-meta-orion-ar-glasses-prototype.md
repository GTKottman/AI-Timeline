# Meta unveils Orion AR glasses prototype at Meta Connect

- **Date:** 2024-09-25
- **Who:** Meta
- **Type:** hardware

At Meta Connect on September 25, 2024, Meta showed Orion, a working prototype of holographic AR glasses, previously codenamed Project Nazare. The glasses project images onto the lenses and take input from voice, eye tracking, hand tracking and an EMG wristband. Meta said Orion is not for sale and has no release date. The reveal showed the on-device AI and sensing stack Meta wants for future wearables.
