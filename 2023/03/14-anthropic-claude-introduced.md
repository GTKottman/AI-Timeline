# Anthropic introduces Claude, a conversational assistant

- **Date:** 2023-03-14
- **Who:** Anthropic
- **Type:** model

Anthropic launched Claude, a conversational assistant offered through a chat interface and API. It was positioned as a rival to ChatGPT, and access was limited at first.
