# India AI Impact Summit held in New Delhi

- **Date:** 2026-02-19
- **Who:** Government of India (IndiaAI Mission)
- **Type:** other

India hosted the AI Impact Summit at Bharat Mandapam in New Delhi, held February 18 to 20, 2026 per press reports. Prime Minister Modi was scheduled to open the summit and address the leaders' plenary on February 19. Pre-event coverage named global tech leaders as expected attendees, but attendance was not confirmed in the sources checked.
