# Message Passing Neural Networks predict molecular properties

- **Date:** 2017-04-04
- **Who:** Justin Gilmer, Samuel S. Schoenholz, Patrick F. Riley, Oriol Vinyals, George E. Dahl (arXiv paper); Google announced the work on its research blog on April 7, 2017 with Google Brain, Google Accelerated Science, DeepMind and the University of Basel
- **Type:** research

arXiv paper (first version April 4, 2017) that unifies several graph neural networks under one framework, Message Passing Neural Networks (MPNNs). The authors tested MPNN variants on the QM9 benchmark of molecules with computed properties and reported state-of-the-art results on it. Google's April 7 blog post described 11 properties accurate enough to help chemists while running up to 300,000 times faster than DFT calculation, though the authors cautioned that generalization to more diverse molecules remained open.
