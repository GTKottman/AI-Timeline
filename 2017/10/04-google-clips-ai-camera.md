# Google Clips hands-free AI camera unveiled with Pixel 2

- **Date:** 2017-10-04
- **Who:** Google
- **Type:** hardware

Google unveiled Clips at its October 4, 2017 hardware event, a small hands-free camera that uses on-device machine learning to decide when to shoot. It stores its photos and videos locally rather than uploading them, and reports listed a price of $249. Clips was one of the first consumer devices to put a learned "decide what is worth capturing" model into a standalone camera.
