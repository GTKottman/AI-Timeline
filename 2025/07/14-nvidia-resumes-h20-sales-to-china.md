# NVIDIA says it will resume H20 chip sales to China

- **Date:** 2025-07-14
- **Who:** NVIDIA (Jensen Huang); US government
- **Type:** policy

In a blog post, NVIDIA said it would resume sales of its H20 AI chip to China after the April export licence requirement had blocked them. The move followed a US decision to lift the restriction. Markets moved the next day, and NVIDIA's shares rose to a record above $4 trillion in market value on July 15. Reports say the White House linked the change to NVIDIA's pledge to invest in US AI servers. Analysts noted that US-China uncertainty remained. Sources give July 14 or 15 as the date.
