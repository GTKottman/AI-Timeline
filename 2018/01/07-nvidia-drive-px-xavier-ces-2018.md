# NVIDIA presents Xavier SoC as DRIVE PX Xavier at CES 2018

- **Date:** 2018-01-07
- **Who:** NVIDIA
- **Type:** hardware

NVIDIA presented its Xavier system-on-chip as DRIVE PX Xavier, an AI computer for self-driving cars, at its pre-CES event in Las Vegas. NVIDIA described Xavier as an 8-core CPU and 512-core Volta GPU chip that delivers 30 trillion operations per second at 30 watts. It also announced new driverless vehicle partners, including Volkswagen, Uber and Baidu.
