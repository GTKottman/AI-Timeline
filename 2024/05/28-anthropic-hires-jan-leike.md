# Anthropic hires former OpenAI alignment lead Jan Leike

- **Date:** 2024-05-28
- **Who:** Anthropic; Jan Leike
- **Type:** people

Jan Leike, who left OpenAI earlier in May 2024 over concerns that safety had lost priority, announced he was joining Anthropic to continue alignment research. His new work covers scalable oversight, weak-to-strong generalization and automated alignment research. It was one of the most closely watched researcher moves of the year.
