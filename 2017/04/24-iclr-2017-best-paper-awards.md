# ICLR 2017 best paper awards: rethinking generalization and private-data transfer

- **Date:** 2017-04-24
- **Who:** Chiyuan Zhang, Samy Bengio, Moritz Hardt, Benjamin Recht, Oriol Vinyals (Google / Berkeley / MIT); Nicolas Papernot, Martín Abadi, Úlfar Erlingsson, Ian Goodfellow, Kunal Talwar (Google / Penn State)
- **Type:** competition

ICLR 2017 in Toulon gave two best paper awards. "Understanding Deep Learning Requires Rethinking Generalization" showed that large networks can memorize random labels, which challenged standard generalization theory. "Semi-Supervised Knowledge Transfer for Deep Learning from Private Training Data" introduced the PATE approach for training on sensitive data with a privacy guarantee. The awards highlighted both a core theory question and a practical privacy method.
