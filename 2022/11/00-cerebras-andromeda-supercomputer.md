# Cerebras unveils Andromeda AI supercomputer

- **Date:** 2022-11-00
- **Who:** Cerebras Systems
- **Type:** hardware

Cerebras unveiled Andromeda, an AI supercomputer built from 16 CS-2 systems with wafer-scale chips, which it said delivers more than one exaflop of AI compute in a Santa Clara data center. The company said it was available for commercial and academic work. It was an example of wafer-scale hardware being sold as a turnkey training cluster rather than a single accelerator. The exact day in November was not confirmed.
