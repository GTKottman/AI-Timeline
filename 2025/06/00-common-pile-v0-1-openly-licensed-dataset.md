# EleutherAI releases Common Pile v0.1, an 8TB openly licensed text dataset

- **Date:** 2025-06-00
- **Who:** EleutherAI, University of Toronto, Hugging Face, Allen Institute for AI
- **Type:** dataset

EleutherAI and partners released The Common Pile v0.1, an 8TB pretraining corpus built from openly licensed and public domain text across about 30 sources. They also released two 7B models trained on it, Comma v0.1-1T and Comma v0.1-2T. The authors report results competitive with models trained on unlicensed text at similar budgets; those results are self-reported. The exact day in June 2025 is not confirmed.
