# AlphaGo beats Ke Jie 3-0 at Future of Go Summit

- **Date:** 2017-05-27
- **Who:** DeepMind (Google), Ke Jie
- **Type:** competition

AlphaGo Master won all three games against world number one Go player Ke Jie in Wuzhen, China, on May 23, 25 and 27, 2017. The series was a public milestone showing superhuman play in Go, a game long seen as a hard challenge for AI. After the summit DeepMind announced that AlphaGo would retire from competitive play.
