# Mistral AI Raises €3 Billion Series D Led by Samsung

- **Date:** 2026-09-08
- **Who:** Mistral AI; led by Samsung Electronics, with EQT's Scaleup Europe Fund and PSG Equity as co-leads; other investors include Advent, BlackRock funds and the Grand Duchy of Luxembourg
- **Type:** funding

Mistral announced a €3 billion Series D on September 8, 2026 at a post-money valuation above €21 billion. Coverage calls it the largest equity round ever completed by a European tech company. The money is for data centres and compute, and the CEO said owned compute should roughly double in five years. The round follows a €1.7 billion Series C led by ASML in September 2025. Dollar figures vary by outlet, and Samsung's stake size was not confirmed by the companies.
