# Somerville, Massachusetts bans government use of facial recognition

- **Date:** 2019-06-27
- **Who:** Somerville City Council (sponsor: Councilor Ben Ewen-Campen; Mayor Joseph Curtatone supportive)
- **Type:** policy

Somerville's city council voted to ban government use of facial recognition, covering police investigations and municipal surveillance programs. It was the first East Coast city to pass such a ban and the second US city after San Francisco. Data gathered through the technology is treated as unlawfully obtained and cannot be used in trials. The ban applies to public-space use only. Vote date is 27 June per the Gazette and ACLU of Massachusetts; WBUR reported it on 28 June.
