# Baichuan releases open-source Baichuan 2 model family

- **Date:** 2023-09-06
- **Who:** Baichuan AI
- **Type:** model

Baichuan AI released the Baichuan 2 family of open models in Beijing, including 7B and 13B base models and chat models, with a 4-bit quantized version. The company said the models beat Llama 2 on MMLU and CMMLU, a company claim not independently checked. The official model card says the models can be used free in commercial applications only after requesting a commercial license by email. The release added a bilingual Chinese-English open-weight option alongside Llama 2.
