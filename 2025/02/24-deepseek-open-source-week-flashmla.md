# DeepSeek starts Open Source Week with FlashMLA

- **Date:** 2025-02-24
- **Who:** DeepSeek
- **Type:** software

DeepSeek began a week of releasing one open-source infrastructure repository each day. Day 1 was FlashMLA, a decoding kernel for multi-head latent attention on NVIDIA Hopper GPUs, for variable-length sequences. Day 2 was DeepEP, a communication library for mixture-of-experts training and inference. The week ran until February 28, 2025. The performance figures are DeepSeek's own.
