# NVIDIA launches Jetson Nano 2GB developer kit at $59

- **Date:** 2020-10-05
- **Who:** NVIDIA
- **Type:** hardware

At NVIDIA's GTC fall 2020 keynote, NVIDIA announced the Jetson Nano 2GB, a cheaper variant of its Jetson Nano edge AI developer module priced at $59. It lowered the entry cost of running GPU-accelerated neural networks on small embedded boards for hobbyists, students and robotics prototyping. Reviewers reported existing Nano workloads ran unchanged on the 2GB version.
