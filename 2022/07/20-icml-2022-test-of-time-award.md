# ICML 2022 Test of Time award

- **Date:** 2022-07-00
- **Who:** ICML 2022 (Battista Biggio, Blaine Nelson, Pavel Laskov)
- **Type:** competition

At ICML 2022 in Baltimore (17-23 July), the conference gave its Test of Time award to Biggio, Nelson and Laskov for their 2012 paper "Poisoning Attacks against Support Vector Machines." The award recognized early work on adversarial attacks on machine learning models, a field that became central to AI safety and security research. The exact day of the award announcement was not found.
