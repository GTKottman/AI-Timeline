# IBM and City of Seoul AI virtual assistant for citizens

- **Date:** 2020-08-20
- **Who:** IBM; City of Seoul, South Korea
- **Type:** product

IBM and the City of Seoul launched an AI virtual assistant for citizens, built on IBM Watson. It supported the city's "I Care You" survey of how COVID-19 changed residents' daily lives, and used Watson Discovery to categorize and analyze the answers. IBM said the project went from development to launch in about three weeks; that timing is IBM's own account.
