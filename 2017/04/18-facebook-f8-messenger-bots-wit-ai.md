# Facebook F8 2017: Messenger bot platform and Wit.ai automatic answers

- **Date:** 2017-04-18
- **Who:** Facebook (Messenger team); Wit.ai (Facebook-owned)
- **Type:** product

At F8 2017, Facebook's Messenger update added a Discover tab for finding bots, Chat Extensions for group conversations with businesses, and Wit.ai-powered automatic answers so Pages could reply to frequently asked questions such as opening hours and directions. VentureBeat reported more than 100,000 bots on Messenger, up from 33,000 the previous September.
