# US requires licence for Nvidia H20 exports to China, Nvidia takes $5.5B charge

- **Date:** 2025-04-09
- **Who:** US Commerce Department; NVIDIA
- **Type:** policy

The US government told NVIDIA that it would need a licence to export its H20 accelerator to China, Hong Kong and Macau, and to other chips with similar bandwidth. NVIDIA said it expected about $5.5 billion in charges tied to H20 inventory and purchase commitments. On April 14, officials said the licence requirement would stay in place for an indefinite period, citing the risk of diversion to a Chinese supercomputer. Reuters later reported in August 2025 that the US had reversed the ban and started issuing licences.
