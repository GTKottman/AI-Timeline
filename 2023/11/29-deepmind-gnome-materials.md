# DeepMind's GNoME reports 2.2 million new stable crystals

- **Date:** 2023-11-29
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** research

DeepMind's GNoME deep learning system predicted the stability of new inorganic crystals, reporting 2.2 million newly discovered crystals, about 380,000 of them predicted to be stable. It showed AI doing large-scale scientific discovery in materials for chips, batteries and solar cells.
