# Reflection AI raises $2 billion Series B at $8 billion valuation

- **Date:** 2025-10-09
- **Who:** Reflection AI (founded by Misha Laskin and Ioannis Antonoglou, ex-DeepMind), NVIDIA
- **Type:** funding

Reflection AI, a startup building open-source frontier models and autonomous coding agents, raised a $2 billion Series B at an $8 billion valuation. NVIDIA is named as lead by most reports, with others including DST, Lightspeed, B Capital and Eric Schmidt among the investors. The round was framed around open-source frontier models and autonomous coding agents.
