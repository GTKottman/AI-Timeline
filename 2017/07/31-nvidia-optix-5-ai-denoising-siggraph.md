# NVIDIA Announces OptiX 5.0 With AI Denoising at SIGGRAPH 2017

- **Date:** 2017-07-31
- **Who:** NVIDIA
- **Type:** software

At SIGGRAPH 2017 in Los Angeles, NVIDIA announced the OptiX 5.0 SDK, a GPU ray-tracing framework that adds AI-based denoising and accelerated motion blur, with availability expected in November. NVIDIA also presented AI research and SDK tools aimed at content creators. It mattered because a neural network was used as a practical denoiser inside a production rendering pipeline.
