# Pluribus beats professional poker players in six-player no-limit hold'em

- **Date:** 2019-07-11
- **Who:** Carnegie Mellon University and Facebook AI (Noam Brown, Tuomas Sandholm)
- **Type:** research

Pluribus defeated professional players in six-player no-limit Texas Hold'em, including Darren Elias and Chris Ferguson, in a paper published in Science. It ran on a single server using under 128GB of memory, unlike earlier game-solving AIs that needed supercomputers. The researchers said the approach could apply to real-world problems with hidden information. Facebook chose not to release the bot.
