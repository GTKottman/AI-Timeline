# OpenAI ships GPT-5.4 mini and nano

- **Date:** 2026-03-17
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** model

OpenAI released GPT-5.4 mini and nano, smaller versions of GPT-5.4. Mini is available through the API, Codex and ChatGPT, and nano is API-only. The Decoder reported that the new models cost up to four times as much as the GPT-5 mini and nano models.
