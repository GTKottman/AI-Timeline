# NVIDIA DRIVE Constellation autonomous vehicle simulator

- **Date:** 2018-03-27
- **Who:** NVIDIA
- **Type:** product

Announced in the GTC 2018 keynote, DRIVE Constellation is a two-server system for testing self-driving cars in simulation. One server runs DRIVE Sim to generate simulated camera, lidar and radar data; the other runs the DRIVE Pegasus driving stack, whose driving commands feed back into the simulator in a loop running 30 times a second. NVIDIA pitched it as a way to cover billions of simulated miles, including rare corner cases, and Huang referenced a pedestrian death involving a self-driving test car the week before.
