# OpenAI introduces GPTs, customizable ChatGPT versions

- **Date:** 2023-11-06
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** product

OpenAI introduced GPTs at its first DevDay, customized ChatGPT versions with tailored instructions, knowledge files and tools that users could build and share without coding. It also announced a planned GPT Store for sharing and selling them, an early step toward agent-like AI apps.
