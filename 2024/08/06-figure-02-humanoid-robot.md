# Figure introduces Figure 02 humanoid robot

- **Date:** 2024-08-06
- **Who:** Figure AI
- **Type:** hardware

Figure 02 was unveiled with four-fingered hands with an opposable thumb, a 25 kg (55 lb) lift capacity, six cameras and an onboard vision-language model for visual reasoning. It was presented as a step toward general-purpose humanoid labour in factories.
