# Stanford releases 2026 AI Index Report

- **Date:** 2026-04-13
- **Who:** Stanford Institute for Human-Centered AI (HAI)
- **Type:** research

The 2026 AI Index is Stanford HAI's annual report covering research, technical performance, responsible AI, economy, policy and public opinion. It reports that Terminal-Bench success rose from 20% in 2025 to 77.3% in 2026.
