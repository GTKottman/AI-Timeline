# NVIDIA CES 2022 keynote: Omniverse Avatar and DRIVE partners

- **Date:** 2022-01-04
- **Who:** NVIDIA
- **Type:** product

At its CES 2022 keynote NVIDIA highlighted AI across its product lines, including Omniverse, which it made free for individuals, and Omniverse Avatar for conversational digital characters. It also showed DRIVE Hyperion automotive work, with suppliers, EV makers and trucking companies announcing plans for intelligent vehicles on NVIDIA DRIVE. The keynote framed AI as the common thread across the year's hardware and software.
