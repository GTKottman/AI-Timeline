# xAI limits Grok image generation to paid users after sexual deepfakes

- **Date:** 2026-01-09
- **Who:** xAI
- **Type:** product

X restricted Grok's image generation and editing features to paying subscribers after its tool was used to make non-consensual sexualised images. The standalone Grok app still allowed image generation without a subscription.
