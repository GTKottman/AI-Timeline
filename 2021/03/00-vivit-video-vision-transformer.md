# Google publishes ViViT, a video vision transformer

- **Date:** 2021-03-00
- **Who:** Anurag Arnab, Mostafa Dehghani, Georg Heigold, Chen Sun, Mario Lucic, Cordelia Schmid (Google Research)
- **Type:** paper

ViViT (arXiv 2103.15691) adapted the Vision Transformer to video classification, extracting spatio-temporal tokens and encoding them with transformer layers. It proposed factorised spatial and temporal variants to handle long token sequences, and it showed that pretrained image models help on small video datasets. The authors reported state-of-the-art results on Kinetics 400 and 600, Epic-Kitchens, Something-Something v2 and Moments in Time. The paper appeared at ICCV 2021. The exact arXiv day was not confirmed.
