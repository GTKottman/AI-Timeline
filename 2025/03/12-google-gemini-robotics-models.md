# Google DeepMind announces Gemini Robotics models for physical robots

- **Date:** 2025-03-12
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** model

Google DeepMind announced two robotics models built on Gemini 2.0. Gemini Robotics is a vision-language-action model that outputs physical actions to control robots directly. Gemini Robotics-ER focuses on embodied reasoning and is meant to connect to roboticists' own low-level controllers.
