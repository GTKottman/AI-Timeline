# OpenAI finalizes $110B funding round at $730B pre-money valuation

- **Date:** 2026-02-27
- **Who:** OpenAI; Amazon ($50B, $15B upfront), Nvidia ($30B), SoftBank ($30B)
- **Type:** funding

OpenAI finalized a $110 billion funding round at a $730 billion pre-money valuation, anchored by Amazon, Nvidia and SoftBank. OpenAI said the money goes to infrastructure, global distribution and new products. Amazon's $50 billion was to be paid in stages, with $35 billion conditional. The round was later reported as closing at $122 billion (see the 2026-03-31 entry).
