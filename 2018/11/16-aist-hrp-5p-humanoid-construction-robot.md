# AIST releases HRP-5P, a humanoid built for heavy labor

- **Date:** 2018-11-16
- **Who:** Japan's National Institute of Advanced Industrial Science and Technology (AIST), with Kawada
- **Type:** hardware

AIST announced the HRP-5P, a 182 cm, 101 kg humanoid prototype aimed at heavy labor in construction and hazardous sites. It has 37 degrees of freedom, the most in the HRP series. The robot showed autonomous drywall handling, locating, lifting and carrying large panels with both arms. The HRP-5P had already been shown at the World Robot Expo in Tokyo in October 2018.
