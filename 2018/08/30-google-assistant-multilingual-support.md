# Google Assistant gains multilingual mode for two of six languages

- **Date:** 2018-08-30
- **Who:** Google
- **Type:** product

Google launched multilingual support for the Google Assistant. Users who pick two of six supported languages (English, Spanish, French, German, Italian and Japanese) can speak either one without changing settings. The Assistant identifies the spoken language, runs two monolingual speech recognizers at the same time, and ranks their results. Google said it was working on handling more than two languages at once, with no timeline.
