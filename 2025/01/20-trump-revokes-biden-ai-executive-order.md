# Trump revokes Biden's 2023 AI executive order

- **Date:** 2025-01-20
- **Who:** President Donald Trump, White House
- **Type:** policy

Executive Order 14148, signed January 20, 2025, revoked dozens of Biden-era executive actions, including EO 14110 of October 30, 2023, the 2023 AI executive order. On January 23, 2025, Trump signed EO 14179, "Removing Barriers to American Leadership in Artificial Intelligence", which revoked further Biden-era AI policies and ordered an AI Action Plan. The January 20 revocation came one day before the Stargate announcement, which is recorded separately in this timeline.
