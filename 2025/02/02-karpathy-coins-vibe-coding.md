# Andrej Karpathy coins the term "vibe coding"

- **Date:** 2025-02-02
- **Who:** Andrej Karpathy
- **Type:** people

Former OpenAI and Tesla AI researcher Andrej Karpathy posted about "vibe coding," a style of programming where the user describes what they want to an AI model, accepts the generated code, and steers by feel rather than reading every line. The term caught on quickly and became a shorthand for AI-assisted software building.
