# Meta publishes Chameleon mixed-modal early-fusion model paper

- **Date:** 2024-05-17
- **Who:** Meta FAIR
- **Type:** paper

Meta published "Chameleon: Mixed-Modal Early-Fusion Foundation Models," describing models that tokenize images and text into one sequence and process them in a single transformer, trained on about 10 trillion tokens of interleaved data. The authors reported state-of-the-art image captioning and results that matched or exceeded Gemini Pro and GPT-4V in human judgments on mixed-modal long-form generation. The arXiv date is May 17 per coverage; the paper is the research version, and Meta's later public release was narrower.
