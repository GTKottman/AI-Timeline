# Taiwan's Artificial Intelligence Basic Act takes effect

- **Date:** 2026-01-14
- **Who:** Taiwan Legislative Yuan and government
- **Type:** policy

Taiwan's Artificial Intelligence Basic Act, which includes data-protection rules, entered into force on January 14, 2026, after the Legislative Yuan adopted it in late December 2025. It is one of the first national AI framework laws in Asia to take effect, setting out basic principles and obligations for AI development and use.
