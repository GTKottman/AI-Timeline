# Google's Soft Actor-Critic learns robot skills from real-world experience

- **Date:** 2019-01-18
- **Who:** Google AI (Tuomas Haarnoja, Sergey Levine) with UC Berkeley
- **Type:** research

Google AI explained Soft Actor-Critic (SAC), a deep reinforcement learning method, as a practical way to train robots directly from real-world experience. The post said SAC solves real-world robot tasks in only a handful of hours and works across a variety of environments with a single set of hyperparameters. The approach was first announced in December 2018 on the BAIR blog.
