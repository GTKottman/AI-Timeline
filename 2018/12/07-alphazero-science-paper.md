# DeepMind publishes AlphaZero in Science: chess, shogi and Go from self-play

- **Date:** 2018-12-07
- **Who:** DeepMind (David Silver et al.)
- **Type:** paper

DeepMind published the peer-reviewed Science paper "A general reinforcement learning algorithm that masters chess, shogi, and Go through self-play" (Science 362, 1140–1144; published 7 December 2018). Starting only from the rules, AlphaZero beat Stockfish in chess, Elmo in shogi (about 91% of games) and AlphaGo Zero in Go. It showed one algorithm, with no human game data, could reach superhuman play in several games.
