# Ofcom says chatbot content falls under the Online Safety Act

- **Date:** 2024-11-08
- **Who:** Ofcom (UK)
- **Type:** policy

Ofcom, the UK online safety regulator, published an open letter saying that user-to-user services and search services that involve chatbot-generated content are covered by the Online Safety Act 2023. It followed user-made chatbots that imitated real people, including the children Molly Russell and Brianna Ghey. It made the UK one of the first regulators to state that chatbot output can fall under platform duties.
