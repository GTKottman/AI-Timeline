# Huawei moves up Ascend 960DT training chip and unveils Atlas 960 SuperPoD

- **Date:** 2026-09-17
- **Who:** Huawei (David Wang Tao, rotating chairman)
- **Type:** hardware

At Huawei Connect 2026 in Shanghai, Huawei said its Ascend 960DT training chip is now due in Q1 2027, about three quarters earlier than planned. The 960PR inference chip is listed for Q3 2027, with Ascend 970 and 980 for 2028 and 2029. It also unveiled the Atlas 960 SuperPoD (called Atlas 960E by some outlets), which supports up to 4,096 Ascend processors over an upgraded UnifiedBus interconnect. These are vendor roadmap claims, and sources differ on how far the 960DT date moved.
