# Microsoft releases phi-1.5, a 1.3-billion-parameter model

- **Date:** 2023-09-10
- **Who:** Microsoft Research
- **Type:** model

Microsoft released phi-1.5, a 1.3-billion-parameter language model trained on the phi-1 data sources plus new synthetic NLP texts, with generic web-crawl data excluded. It was released as an open research model without instruction tuning or RLHF. Microsoft reported near state-of-the-art results for models under 10 billion parameters on its own evaluation. It showed that small models trained on curated synthetic text could compete with much larger ones.
