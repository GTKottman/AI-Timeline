# DeepSeek releases open-weights DeepSeek-V3

- **Date:** 2024-12-26
- **Who:** DeepSeek
- **Type:** model

DeepSeek released DeepSeek-V3, a 671-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts language model with 37 billion parameters active per token, pre-trained on 14.8 trillion tokens, with weights and a technical report published openly. DeepSeek said it beat other open models such as Qwen2.5-72B and Llama-3.1-405B on several benchmarks and matched GPT-4o and Claude-3.5-Sonnet on others. These are DeepSeek's own reported results. It was text-only at launch.
