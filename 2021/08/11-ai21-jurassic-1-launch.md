# AI21 Labs launches Jurassic-1 and AI21 Studio

- **Date:** 2021-08-11
- **Who:** AI21 Labs
- **Type:** model

AI21 Labs launched Jurassic-1 language models through AI21 Studio, an open-beta API. The largest model, Jumbo, had about 178 billion parameters and a 250,000-token vocabulary with multi-word tokens. It was a commercial rival to GPT-3 for developers.
