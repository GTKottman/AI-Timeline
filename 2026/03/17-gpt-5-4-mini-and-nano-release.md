# OpenAI ships GPT-5.4 mini and nano

- **Date:** 2026-03-17
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** model

OpenAI released GPT-5.4 mini and nano, smaller variants of GPT-5.4. Mini reached free ChatGPT users and nano was API-only, at a price four times that of the GPT-5 equivalents.
