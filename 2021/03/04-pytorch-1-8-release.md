# PyTorch 1.8 released with stable torch.fft and torch.linalg

- **Date:** 2021-03-04
- **Who:** Meta AI (PyTorch team)
- **Type:** software

PyTorch 1.8 shipped on March 4, 2021 with more than 3,000 commits since 1.7. The release made NumPy-style FFT support (torch.fft) and linear algebra APIs (torch.linalg) stable and added autograd support for complex tensors. Distributed training gained pipeline parallelism, RPC profiling, gradient compression communication hooks and better NCCL reliability.
