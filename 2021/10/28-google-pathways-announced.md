# Google announces Pathways AI architecture

- **Date:** 2021-10-28
- **Who:** Google (Jeff Dean)
- **Type:** research

Jeff Dean introduced Pathways, a proposed architecture for a single model that handles thousands of tasks, learns new ones quickly and mixes data types. It set a direction away from one-task-per-model training.
