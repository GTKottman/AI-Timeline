# DeepScale raises $15M Series A

- **Date:** 2018-04-03
- **Who:** DeepScale (Mountain View, CA); investors Point72, next47, Autotech Ventures, Trucks Venture Capital
- **Type:** funding

DeepScale, a startup building efficient deep-learning perception software for ADAS and self-driving vehicles, announced a $15 million Series A led by Point72 and next47, with additional money from existing investor Autotech Ventures and Trucks Venture Capital. Its pitch was perception software for mass-produced vehicles rather than research fleets, which is why small, efficient networks mattered. One later report said Tesla acquired DeepScale, citing CNBC.
