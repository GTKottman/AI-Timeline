# xAI releases Grok Imagine 1.0

- **Date:** 2026-02-02
- **Who:** xAI
- **Type:** model

xAI released Grok Imagine 1.0, its video generation model. Per xAI, it makes clips of up to 10 seconds at 720p and generates audio at the same time as the video.
