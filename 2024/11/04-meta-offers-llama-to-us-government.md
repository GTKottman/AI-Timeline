# Meta offers Llama to US government for national security use

- **Date:** 2024-11-04
- **Who:** Meta AI
- **Type:** policy

Meta made its Llama models available to US government agencies and defence contractors for national security applications, in a blog post by Nick Clegg. It marked a shift from the company's earlier stance on military use of its open models.
