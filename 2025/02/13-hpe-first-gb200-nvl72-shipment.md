# HPE announces shipment of its first NVIDIA Grace Blackwell system

- **Date:** 2025-02-13
- **Who:** HPE; NVIDIA
- **Type:** hardware

HPE announced that it had shipped its first NVIDIA Grace Blackwell system, the GB200 NVL72 rack with 72 Blackwell GPUs in one liquid-cooled rack. HPE's release is the clearest official vendor shipment announcement the sources checked. Press reports earlier that winter said the first racks had reached cloud providers after production problems were resolved, and QCT later announced its own first GB200 NVL72 shipment in May 2025.
