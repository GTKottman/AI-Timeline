# Google launches TensorFlow Quantum

- **Date:** 2020-03-09
- **Who:** Google (X, Google Research, University of Waterloo, NASA Quantum AI Lab, Volkswagen)
- **Type:** software

Google announced TensorFlow Quantum, an open-source library for prototyping quantum machine learning models. It lets developers build hybrid models that combine classical neural networks with quantum circuits, built on the Cirq quantum circuit library. At launch it ran only on simulators, not on real quantum processors.
