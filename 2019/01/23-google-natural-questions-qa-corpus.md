# Google releases Natural Questions, a question answering corpus built from real search queries

- **Date:** 2019-01-23
- **Who:** Google AI Language (Tom Kwiatkowski, Michael Collins and colleagues)
- **Type:** dataset

Google released Natural Questions (NQ), a corpus of about 300,000 real, anonymized Google search questions. Annotators marked long and short answers from Wikipedia pages, and a 5-way annotated subset served as an evaluation set. The authors framed it as a harder test than trivia, because answering NQ questions needs deeper reading of a document. The companion paper appeared in the Transactions of the Association for Computational Linguistics in 2019 and became a standard benchmark for open-domain question answering.
