# Meta releases Segment Anything Model (SAM) and the SA-1B dataset

- **Date:** 2023-04-05
- **Who:** Meta AI
- **Type:** model

Meta released the Segment Anything Model, a promptable image segmentation model, under the Apache 2.0 license. It came with SA-1B, a dataset of over one billion masks that Meta licensed for research use only. SAM became a widely used foundation model for image segmentation.
