# Epoch AI releases FrontierMath, a hard expert-level math benchmark

- **Date:** 2024-11-08
- **Who:** Epoch AI (with mathematicians including IMO gold medalists)
- **Type:** dataset

Epoch AI released FrontierMath, a set of hundreds of original, research-level math problems built to replace saturated benchmarks such as MATH and GSM8K. The problem writers included people with IMO experience who collectively hold 14 IMO gold medals. At launch, models including GPT-4o and Gemini 1.5 Pro solved less than 2 percent of the problems.
