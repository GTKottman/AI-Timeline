# Microsoft unveils seven in-house MAI models at Build 2026

- **Date:** 2026-06-03
- **Who:** Microsoft AI (Mustafa Suleyman), Microsoft
- **Type:** model

At Build 2026 in San Francisco, Microsoft launched a family of seven in-house models led by MAI-Thinking-1, described as its first purpose-built reasoning model, a sparse mixture-of-experts design reported at about one trillion total parameters with 35 billion active. The family also includes MAI-Code-1-Flash for GitHub Copilot and Visual Studio Code, MAI-Image-2.5 and MAI-Transcribe-1.5. It matters as Microsoft's push to build frontier-class models of its own beyond its OpenAI partnership; the performance and cost claims are Microsoft-reported.
