# NVIDIA launches AI LaunchPad

- **Date:** 2021-06-23
- **Who:** NVIDIA (launch partner Equinix)
- **Type:** product

NVIDIA unveiled AI LaunchPad, a program that gives enterprises instant access to NVIDIA-powered AI infrastructure through hybrid-cloud providers. Equinix was the first partner, offering Ampere-based GPU systems and BlueField DPUs on Platform Equinix, with AI Enterprise software and DGX SuperPOD capacity behind it. The same period brought Fleet Command for deploying AI applications at distributed edge sites. The program aimed to move enterprises from prototypes to production without buying their own AI hardware.
