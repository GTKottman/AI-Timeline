# Judging LLM-as-a-Judge with MT-Bench and Chatbot Arena

- **Date:** 2023-06-09
- **Who:** Lianmin Zheng, Wei-Lin Chiang and colleagues (UC Berkeley and collaborators; FastChat / LMSYS code)
- **Type:** research

The paper introduced MT-bench, a multi-turn question benchmark, and Chatbot Arena, a crowdsourced battle platform for chat assistants. It showed that strong LLM judges such as GPT-4 agree with human preferences at about the same rate humans agree with each other (over 80%). It also studied position, verbosity and self-enhancement biases. The MT-bench questions, expert votes and human-preference conversations were released publicly through the FastChat repository.
