# AIST releases HRP-5P, a humanoid built for heavy labor

- **Date:** 2018-09-27
- **Who:** Japan's National Institute of Advanced Industrial Science and Technology (AIST), with Kawada
- **Type:** hardware

AIST announced the HRP-5P, a 182 cm, 101 kg humanoid prototype with 37 degrees of freedom. The release showed autonomous gypsum board installation. A static exhibit was planned at World Robot Expo 2018 in Tokyo, October 17 to 21.
