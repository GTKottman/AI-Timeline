# Meta AudioCraft: open-source MusicGen, AudioGen and EnCodec

- **Date:** 2023-08-03
- **Who:** Meta AI
- **Type:** software

Meta open-sourced AudioCraft, a library bundling MusicGen (text-to-music), AudioGen (text-to-sound effects) and the EnCodec audio codec under the MIT license. Meta released code and weights for researchers and practitioners and noted that MusicGen's training music skewed Western with English metadata. The date is the coverage date seen; the exact release day in early August was not checked.
