# Anthropic releases Claude Haiku 4.5

- **Date:** 2025-10-15
- **Who:** Anthropic
- **Type:** model

Anthropic released Claude Haiku 4.5, its small model, to all users and through the API as claude-haiku-4-5. It costs $1 per million input tokens and $5 per million output tokens. Anthropic said it matched Claude Sonnet 4's coding performance at about a third of the cost and more than twice the speed.
