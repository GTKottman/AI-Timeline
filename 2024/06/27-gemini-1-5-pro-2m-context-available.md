# Google opens 2 million token context for Gemini 1.5 Pro to all developers

- **Date:** 2024-06-27
- **Who:** Google / Google DeepMind
- **Type:** product

Google made the 2-million-token context window of Gemini 1.5 Pro available to all developers, after it had been announced at I/O in May behind a waitlist. Two million tokens is roughly 1.4 million words, two hours of video or 22 hours of audio. The same release added code execution to the Gemini API, brought Gemma 2 to Google AI Studio, and added context caching for Gemini 1.5 Pro and 1.5 Flash.
