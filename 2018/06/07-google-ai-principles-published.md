# Google publishes its AI principles after Project Maven backlash

- **Date:** 2018-06-07
- **Who:** Google / Alphabet (Sundar Pichai)
- **Type:** policy

Google published seven AI principles and a list of applications it would not pursue, including weapons and surveillance that violate norms. The move came after employee protest over Project Maven, a Pentagon program that used AI to analyse drone footage. Google said it would not seek a follow-on Maven contract, while keeping other government and military work. It became an early example of a major AI company setting public ethical limits.
