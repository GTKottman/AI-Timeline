# NeurIPS 2025 Best Paper Awards Announced

- **Date:** 2025-11-26
- **Who:** NeurIPS (Conference on Neural Information Processing Systems)
- **Type:** competition

NeurIPS 2025 announced four Best Paper awards (one from the Datasets and Benchmarks track) and three runner-up papers. One of the Best Papers is "Artificial Hivemind," which introduces the Infinity-Chat dataset of 26,000 open-ended queries and studies mode collapse in language models. Secondary coverage disagrees on some other winner titles, so the official NeurIPS blog is the authority for the full list.
