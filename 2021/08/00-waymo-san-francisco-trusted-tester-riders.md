# Waymo opens San Francisco rider program to trusted testers

- **Date:** 2021-08-00
- **Who:** Waymo (Alphabet)
- **Type:** product

Waymo opened a waitlist for its self-driving ride service in San Francisco in August 2021, initially offering rides to pre-approved "trusted testers." Waymo's December 2021 year-in-review later confirmed it had begun welcoming new riders in the city, with hundreds using Waymo One and tens of thousands on the waitlist. It extended Waymo's driverless ride service beyond its Phoenix base and was a step toward real-world robotaxi deployment. The exact August day was not found in the sources reviewed.
