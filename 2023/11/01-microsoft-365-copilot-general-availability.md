# Microsoft 365 Copilot becomes generally available to enterprises

- **Date:** 2023-11-01
- **Who:** Microsoft
- **Type:** product

Microsoft 365 Copilot became generally available to enterprise customers on November 1, 2023, at $30 per user per month for E3, E5, Business Standard and Business Premium plans. It was sold through Enterprise Agreements with a 300-seat minimum at launch, and government and education buyers were left without a timeline. It turned the AI assistant from a preview into a paid, workplace-wide product.
