# Alibaba Qwen releases Qwen2.5-VL open vision-language models

- **Date:** 2025-01-26
- **Who:** Alibaba Qwen team
- **Type:** model

Qwen2.5-VL is a family of open vision-language models in 3B, 7B and 72B sizes, with base and instruction-tuned variants, covering images, video, documents and computer use on desktop and mobile. It was released on Hugging Face and ModelScope, with the 7B model under Apache 2.0. A 32B variant followed in March 2025.
