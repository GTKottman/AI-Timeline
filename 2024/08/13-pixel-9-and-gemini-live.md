# Google launches Pixel 9 with Gemini Live and on-device Gemini Nano

- **Date:** 2024-08-13
- **Who:** Google
- **Type:** product

At its "Made by Google" event Google launched the Pixel 9 line and Gemini Live, a voice conversation mode in the Gemini app that Google presented as replacing Google Assistant on Android. Gemini Live rolled out first to paid Android subscribers, with iOS to follow, and users could interrupt it and change topics mid-conversation. The Pixel 9 ran Gemini Nano on-device on the Tensor G4 for features such as custom weather reports.
