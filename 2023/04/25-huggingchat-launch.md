# Hugging Face launches HuggingChat

- **Date:** 2023-04-25
- **Who:** Hugging Face (built on the OpenAssistant project, LAION)
- **Type:** product

Hugging Face launched HuggingChat, an open-source chat interface built on the OpenAssistant model, as a free rival to ChatGPT. It was presented as the best open chat model available at the time, though users could not save long-term chat history at launch. It mattered as an open, hosted chat product built on openly trained weights. Hugging Face later shut the service down in favour of a new, more integrated product; the closure date was not confirmed here.
