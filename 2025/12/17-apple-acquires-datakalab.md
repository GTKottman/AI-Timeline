# Apple quietly acquires Paris AI startup Datakalab

- **Date:** 2025-12-17
- **Who:** Apple; Datakalab (Paris-based AI startup)
- **Type:** acquisition

Apple acquired Datakalab, a Paris-based AI startup, in a deal reported on December 17, 2025. Terms were not disclosed. Reports described the deal as quiet, and it adds to Apple's pattern of small AI acquisitions.
