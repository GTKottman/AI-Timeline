# Bloomberg publishes BloombergGPT, a 50B-parameter finance model

- **Date:** 2023-03-30
- **Who:** Bloomberg (nine authors led by Shijie Wu)
- **Type:** model

Bloomberg released a paper on BloombergGPT, a 50-billion-parameter model trained on a mix of 363 billion tokens of Bloomberg's own financial data and about 345 billion tokens of general text. The authors claimed large gains on finance tasks without losing general benchmark performance, and promised to publish training logs. It mattered as a clear example of a company training a domain-specific model from scratch on proprietary data. The performance claims are the authors' own.
