# OpenAI Five wins 99.4% of public games in open online arena

- **Date:** 2019-04-22
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** competition

After beating OG, OpenAI opened OpenAI Five to the public for an online arena, played April 18-21, 2019. A report published April 22, 2019 said the bots had played 42,729 cooperative and competitive matches and had a 99.4% victory rate. Sources disagree on the exact win count: GamesBeat reports 4,075 wins, while Engadget, quoting OpenAI, reports 7,215 competitive wins. The result showed the system could beat a large field of human players, not just one professional team.
