# Meta announces AI Research SuperCluster (RSC)

- **Date:** 2022-01-24
- **Who:** Meta AI
- **Type:** hardware

Meta announced the AI Research SuperCluster, an NVIDIA A100-based system that was already training large NLP and computer vision models, with a full build planned for mid-2022. Meta said it was aiming for training models with trillions of parameters, and it reported about 20x faster computer-vision workflows and 3x faster large-scale NLP training than its earlier system. Reported GPU counts differ by source, so the exact size is not settled here.
