# Trump signs Executive Order 14409 on voluntary early government access to frontier AI models

- **Date:** 2026-06-02
- **Who:** President Donald Trump, US federal government (Treasury, NSA, CISA, Commerce)
- **Type:** policy

Executive Order 14409, "Promoting Advanced Artificial Intelligence Innovation and Security", creates a voluntary program in which developers can give the government access to covered frontier models up to 30 days before wider release to trusted partners. The government would identify covered models through a classified benchmarking of advanced cyber capabilities, and agencies have 60 days to design the framework. The order explicitly creates no mandatory licensing or preclearance, and it is a scaled-back version of a May draft that asked for 90 days.
