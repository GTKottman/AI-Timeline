# Stanford HAI publishes the 2025 AI Index report

- **Date:** 2025-04-07
- **Who:** Stanford Institute for Human-Centered AI (HAI)
- **Type:** research

Stanford HAI published the 2025 AI Index, its annual data report on AI, on April 7, 2025. It found record business investment and use of AI, reports of productivity gains, and a U.S. lead in top models that China is closing. Its takeaways also say inference cost for GPT-3.5-level performance fell more than 280-fold from November 2022 to October 2024, and hardware costs fell 30% a year.
