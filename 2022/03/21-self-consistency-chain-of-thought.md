# Self-Consistency Improves Chain-of-Thought Reasoning

- **Date:** 2022-03-21
- **Who:** Google Research
- **Type:** paper

Self-consistency samples several varied reasoning paths from a language model and keeps the answer they agree on most. It raised chain-of-thought accuracy on arithmetic and commonsense benchmarks without any extra training.
