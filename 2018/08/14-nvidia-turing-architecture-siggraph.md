# Nvidia unveils Turing GPU architecture with RT and Tensor cores

- **Date:** 2018-08-14
- **Who:** Nvidia (Jensen Huang)
- **Type:** hardware

At SIGGRAPH 2018 in Vancouver, Nvidia unveiled the Turing architecture in a keynote by Jensen Huang, with dedicated RT cores for real-time ray tracing and Tensor cores for deep-learning inference. Initial products were the Quadro RTX 8000, 6000 and 5000, with estimated street prices of about $10,000, $6,300 and $2,300. Turing put dedicated AI inference hardware into mainstream graphics GPUs.
