# Alibaba releases open-source Wan2.2 video models

- **Date:** 2025-07-28
- **Who:** Alibaba (Wan team)
- **Type:** model

Alibaba released Wan2.2, an open-source video generation suite under Apache 2.0 with a mixture-of-experts design that uses one expert for early denoising and one for later refinement. The lineup included text-to-video and image-to-video 14B models and a hybrid 5B model that handles both tasks. It mattered as a strong openly licensed video model that consumer GPUs could run.

Some sources date the announcement to 2025-07-29.
