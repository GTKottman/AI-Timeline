# "Slaughterbots" video released by Future of Life Institute

- **Date:** 2017-11-12
- **Who:** Future of Life Institute
- **Type:** policy

The Future of Life Institute released Slaughterbots, a short film about cheap autonomous microdrone swarms that use AI and facial recognition to target people. It was made to push for arms-control rules on lethal autonomous weapons. It became one of the best-known pieces of advocacy in the autonomous weapons debate.
