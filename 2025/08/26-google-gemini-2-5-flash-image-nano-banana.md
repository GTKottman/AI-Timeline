# Google releases Gemini 2.5 Flash Image, nicknamed Nano Banana

- **Date:** 2025-08-26
- **Who:** Google (Gemini team)
- **Type:** model

Google released Gemini 2.5 Flash Image, nicknamed "nano-banana", for image generation and editing. It is known for character consistency across images, fusing multiple images, and precise natural-language edits. It was available through the Gemini API, Google AI Studio and the Gemini app. It first appeared anonymously on LMArena. Sources differ on the launch day, 26 or 27 August.
