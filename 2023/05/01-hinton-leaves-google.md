# Geoffrey Hinton leaves Google to speak about AI risks

- **Date:** 2023-05-01
- **Who:** Geoffrey Hinton; Google
- **Type:** people

Geoffrey Hinton, one of the deep learning pioneers, left Google so he could speak openly about the dangers of AI, which the New York Times reported on May 1. He said Google had "acted very responsibly" and that he left so he could speak without weighing the effect on the company. He warned of bad actors using AI for disinformation and said it was not inconceivable that AI could end up wiping out humanity. His departure gave the AI risk debate a prominent insider voice.
