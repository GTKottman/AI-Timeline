# Google announces LaMDA dialogue model

- **Date:** 2021-05-18
- **Who:** Google
- **Type:** model

Google announced LaMDA (Language Model for Dialogue Applications) at its I/O developer conference. It is a large language model tuned for open-ended conversation, aiming for responses that stay on topic and are sensible and specific.
