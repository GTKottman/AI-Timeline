# Facebook opens AI research lab in Montreal

- **Date:** 2017-09-15
- **Who:** Facebook AI Research (FAIR), Joelle Pineau, McGill University, MILA, CIFAR, Université de Montréal
- **Type:** people

Facebook opened a Facebook AI Research lab in Montreal, led by McGill professor Joelle Pineau, starting with about 10 researchers and aiming to grow. Facebook pledged $7 million in support of Montreal's AI research community with partners CIFAR, MILA, McGill and Université de Montréal. It was FAIR's second AI research lab outside the US, after Paris.
