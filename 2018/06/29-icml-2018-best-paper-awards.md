# ICML 2018 best paper awards announced

- **Date:** 2018-06-29
- **Who:** International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML) 2018 organisers
- **Type:** competition

The ICML 2018 best paper awards went to "Obfuscated Gradients Give a False Sense of Security" (Athalye, Carlini, Wagner), a critique showing that many adversarial-defence papers relied on gradient masking, and to "Delayed Impact of Fair Machine Learning" (Liu, Dean and colleagues at Berkeley). The conference itself ran July 10 to 15 in Stockholm. The awards pushed the research community to test robustness claims more rigorously.
