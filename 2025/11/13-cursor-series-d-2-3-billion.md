# Cursor raises $2.3 billion at $29.3 billion valuation

- **Date:** 2025-11-13
- **Who:** Anysphere (Cursor); investors Accel, Coatue, Thrive, a16z, NVIDIA, Google
- **Type:** funding

AI coding editor Cursor closed a $2.3 billion Series D at a $29.3 billion post-money valuation, with new backers including NVIDIA and Google. It said the money would go to frontier model training, including its Composer coding model, and to enterprise sales. The round showed investor appetite for AI coding tools as a top revenue category.
