# Meta SeamlessM4T: one model for speech and text translation

- **Date:** 2023-08-22
- **Who:** Meta AI
- **Type:** model

SeamlessM4T is a single multilingual model for speech recognition, speech-to-text, speech-to-speech, text-to-text and text-to-speech translation, covering up to about 100 languages depending on the task. Meta said one system reduces errors and delays compared with chained models. It was released under a research license for non-commercial use, and Meta planned to integrate it into its apps.
