# Hugging Face releases DistilBERT

- **Date:** 2019-10-02
- **Who:** Hugging Face (Victor Sanh et al.)
- **Type:** model

DistilBERT is a smaller, faster distilled version of BERT that keeps most of its accuracy and is much lighter to run. It was released with a NeurIPS workshop paper, and it became one of the most widely used compact models. Hugging Face's Transformers library, then renamed from pytorch-transformers, added it alongside TensorFlow 2.0 support.
