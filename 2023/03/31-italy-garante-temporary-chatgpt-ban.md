# Italy's privacy regulator temporarily bans ChatGPT

- **Date:** 2023-03-31
- **Who:** Italy's Garante per la protezione dei dati personali; OpenAI
- **Type:** policy

Italy's data protection authority temporarily limited OpenAI's processing of Italian users' data and blocked ChatGPT in Italy. It cited a March 20 data breach, the lack of a legal basis for mass collection of personal data used in training, missing notice to users, and no age verification. OpenAI disabled the service for Italian users. The Garante reinstated the service on April 28 after OpenAI added an opt-out form and expanded privacy disclosures.
