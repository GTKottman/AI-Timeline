# Microsoft Research releases Orca 2 small reasoning models

- **Date:** 2023-11-00
- **Who:** Microsoft Research
- **Type:** model

Microsoft Research released Orca 2 in 7B and 13B sizes, fine-tuned from Meta's Llama 2 on synthetic data that teaches small models to pick different reasoning strategies per task, such as step-by-step reasoning, extract-then-answer or direct answering. Microsoft said the models matched or beat models five to ten times larger on complex zero-shot reasoning tests. The weights were released for research under a Microsoft Research license. The exact day was not confirmed from a primary source.
