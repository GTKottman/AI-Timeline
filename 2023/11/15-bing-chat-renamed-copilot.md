# Microsoft renames Bing Chat to Copilot

- **Date:** 2023-11-15
- **Who:** Microsoft
- **Type:** product

At Microsoft Ignite, Microsoft renamed Bing Chat, its GPT-4 powered chatbot launched in February, to Copilot in Bing, and rebranded the enterprise version as Copilot as well. The move unified consumer and commercial assistants under the Copilot name, which Microsoft had been applying across its products since September. The rebrand made Copilot the single brand for Microsoft's AI assistant across Windows, Microsoft 365 and Bing.
