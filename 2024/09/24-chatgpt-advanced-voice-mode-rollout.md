# OpenAI rolls out Advanced Voice Mode to paying ChatGPT users

- **Date:** 2024-09-24
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** product

OpenAI began rolling out Advanced Voice Mode to paying ChatGPT Plus and Team users, with Enterprise and Edu users to follow the next week. The release added five new voices (Arbor, Maple, Sol, Spruce and Vale), improved accents, Custom Instructions and Memory, and a redesigned on-screen interface. The feature had been demoed in May 2024 and first went to a limited group of Plus users in July, so the September rollout made real-time, speech-to-speech conversation available to a mass audience.
