# Google DeepMind releases Gemini Robotics 1.5 and Gemini Robotics-ER 1.5

- **Date:** 2025-09-25
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** model

Google DeepMind released Gemini Robotics 1.5, a vision-language-action model that turns visual input and instructions into motor commands, and Gemini Robotics-ER 1.5, an embodied reasoning model that plans tasks and can use tools such as web search. DeepMind said a skill learned on the ALOHA 2 robot could move to Franka and Apptronik's Apollo humanoid without retraining. ER 1.5 became available in preview through the Gemini API and Google AI Studio, while Gemini Robotics 1.5 was limited to select partners.
