# Microsoft releases Phi-3.5 Mini, MoE and Vision models

- **Date:** 2024-08-21
- **Who:** Microsoft
- **Type:** model

Microsoft released three Phi-3.5 models: Phi-3.5-Mini-Instruct (3.8B, 128K context), Phi-3.5-MoE-Instruct (a mixture-of-experts model with 6.6B active parameters) and Phi-3.5-Vision-Instruct (4.2B, text and image input). The Mini model was trained on 3.4 trillion tokens, and Microsoft released the models under the MIT license. Microsoft's own benchmarks claimed the models beat Gemini 1.5 Flash and Llama 3.1 on some tasks, a company-reported result.
