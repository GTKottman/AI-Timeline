# NeurIPS 2018 best paper awards

- **Date:** 2018-12-03
- **Who:** NeurIPS 2018 organisers
- **Type:** competition

The NeurIPS 2018 conference ran December 3 to 8 in Montreal, and the organisers announced its best paper awards at the opening session. The four winners were "Non-delusional Q-learning and Value-iteration", "Optimal Algorithms for Non-Smooth Distributed Optimization in Networks", "Nearly Tight Sample Complexity Bounds for Learning Mixtures of Gaussians via Sample Compression Schemes", and "Neural Ordinary Differential Equations". The Neural ODE paper became one of the most cited ideas of the year for continuous-depth models.
