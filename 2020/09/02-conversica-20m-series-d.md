# Conversica raises $20 million Series D for AI sales and marketing assistants

- **Date:** 2020-09-02
- **Who:** Conversica; lead investor Hollyport Capital; also Providence Strategic Growth, Toba Capital, Savano Capital Partners
- **Type:** funding

Conversica announced a $20 million growth equity Series D on September 2, 2020, led by Hollyport Capital. The company sells AI virtual assistants that hold automated email conversations with sales and marketing leads. The round brought its institutional capital to about $89 million and was earmarked for sales, marketing, partner ecosystem and product work.
