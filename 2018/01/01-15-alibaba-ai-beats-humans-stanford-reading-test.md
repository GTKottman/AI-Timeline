# Alibaba AI outscores humans on Stanford reading comprehension test

- **Date:** 2018-01-15
- **Who:** Alibaba
- **Type:** research

Alibaba reported an AI system that scored higher than human readers on the Stanford Question Answering Dataset (SQuAD), a benchmark that tests whether a model can answer questions from a passage. It was one of the first claims of superhuman reading comprehension, and it was followed by similar results from Microsoft and others.
