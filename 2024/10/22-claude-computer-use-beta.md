# Anthropic launches computer use in public beta with upgraded Claude 3.5 models

- **Date:** 2024-10-22
- **Who:** Anthropic
- **Type:** product

Anthropic released an upgraded Claude 3.5 Sonnet and announced a new Claude 3.5 Haiku due later that month. It offered "computer use" in public beta, letting Claude read screens and operate a desktop by moving the cursor and typing. It was among the first general models to act on a computer through a screenshot-and-control loop.
