# Stability AI releases Stable Diffusion 3.5

- **Date:** 2024-10-22
- **Who:** Stability AI
- **Type:** model

Stable Diffusion 3.5 launched on October 22 as Stable Diffusion 3.5 Large, with 8.1 billion parameters, and a distilled Large Turbo. Medium, with 2.5 billion parameters, was added on October 29. The open licence allowed use by many small businesses and researchers, keeping open image generation active after Stability's leadership change.
