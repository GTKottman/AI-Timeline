# Anthropic gives $20M to Public First Action

- **Date:** 2026-02-12
- **Who:** Anthropic; Public First Action (501(c)(4) advocacy group)
- **Type:** policy

Anthropic announced a $20 million donation to Public First Action, a group backing congressional candidates who favor AI safety rules and planning to support 30 to 50 bipartisan candidates. Reports differ on what the money can fund; Anthropic later said it cannot be used for election ads. The gift put a frontier lab's money behind a pro-regulation political push ahead of the 2026 elections.
