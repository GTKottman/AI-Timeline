# Distill launches as an interactive journal for machine learning

- **Date:** 2017-03-20
- **Who:** Chris Olah and Shan Carter (founding editors, Google Brain), with OpenAI, DeepMind and YC Research
- **Type:** other

On March 20, 2017 Google's research blog announced Distill, an open science journal and ecosystem for clear, interactive explanations of machine learning. It launched with OpenAI, DeepMind and YC Research as partners. Launch articles covered attention in neural networks, t-SNE visualization and neural networks generating handwriting.
