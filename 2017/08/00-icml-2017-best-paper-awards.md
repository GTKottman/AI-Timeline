# ICML 2017 best paper and test-of-time awards in Sydney

- **Date:** 2017-08-00
- **Who:** Pang Wei Koh and Percy Liang (Stanford, best paper); Sylvain Gelly and David Silver (test of time)
- **Type:** competition

The ICML 2017 awards went to Koh and Liang for "Understanding Black-box Predictions via Influence Functions," which shows how to trace a model's prediction back to its training data. Gelly and Silver received the test-of-time award for combining online and offline knowledge in UCT, the search method behind later game-playing systems. The conference ran 6-11 August 2017 in Sydney; the exact day the award was announced was not found in the sources.
