# ByteDance launches Seed 2.1 Pro and Seed 2.1 Turbo models

- **Date:** 2026-06-23
- **Who:** ByteDance Seed (Volcano Engine)
- **Type:** model

ByteDance announced Seed 2.1 Pro and Seed 2.1 Turbo at its Volcano Engine FORCE conference on June 23, 2026, with commercial pricing live the next day. Pro is the top model and powers the Doubao 2.1 Pro assistant; Turbo is a faster, cheaper option for high-volume enterprise use. ByteDance claims Pro beats Claude Opus 4.6 on several benchmarks at about 80% lower cost. These are vendor claims that have not been independently checked.
