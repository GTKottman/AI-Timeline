# Waymo raises $2.25 billion in first outside funding round

- **Date:** 2020-03-02
- **Who:** Waymo (Alphabet); investors Silver Lake, CPP Investments, Mubadala, Magna, Andreessen Horowitz, AutoNation
- **Type:** funding

Waymo, Alphabet's self-driving unit, announced its first outside investment, an initial close of $2.25 billion. It was the first time the autonomous-vehicle unit took outside money rather than being funded only by its parent. The round gave the company capital for its autonomous driving software and robotaxi work at a time when the self-driving sector was expensive to scale.
