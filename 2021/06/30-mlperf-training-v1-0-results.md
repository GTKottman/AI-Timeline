# MLCommons releases MLPerf Training v1.0 results

- **Date:** 2021-06-30
- **Who:** MLCommons (MLPerf); Nvidia, Google, Intel, Graphcore and other submitters
- **Type:** benchmark

MLCommons published MLPerf Training v1.0 on June 30, 2021. The round drew submissions from 13 organizations and more than 650 peer-reviewed results. It added speech-to-text and 3D medical imaging benchmarks to the suite, which times how long models take to reach a quality target. Nvidia led, Google's TPU v4 was previewed, and Intel and Graphcore expanded their participation. Best results improved by up to 2.1x over the prior round.
