# Amazon Alexa launches in Ireland

- **Date:** 2018-01-25
- **Who:** Amazon
- **Type:** product

Amazon announced that Alexa and Echo devices were available in Ireland. Customers could buy the Echo, Echo Plus and Echo Dot through Amazon.co.uk and pay in euro, and local skills came from Irish partners such as RTE News. It extended Alexa to another English-speaking market in Europe.
