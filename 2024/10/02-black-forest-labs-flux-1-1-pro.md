# Black Forest Labs releases FLUX 1.1 [pro] and its API

- **Date:** 2024-10-02
- **Who:** Black Forest Labs
- **Type:** model

FLUX 1.1 [pro] became the flagship image model of Black Forest Labs. It generates images six times faster than its predecessor while improving quality, and it was offered through the beta BFL API. Its release marked the company's move from open research releases toward a commercial API business.
