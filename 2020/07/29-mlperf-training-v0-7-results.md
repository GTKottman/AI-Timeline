# MLPerf Training v0.7 Results

- **Date:** 2020-07-29
- **Who:** MLCommons / MLPerf; Google; NVIDIA; Intel; Huawei; Inspur and others
- **Type:** competition

MLPerf released Training v0.7 results covering eight benchmarks (image classification, recommendation, translation, Go and others), with 138 results from nine submitting organizations. NVIDIA reported records across all eight tests in the commercially available category, driven by its A100 GPUs. Google reported record times on six of eight benchmarks using TPU v4 chips in a research (not yet available) category. Intel previewed 3rd-generation Xeon (Cooper Lake) results and Huawei Ascend 910 systems appeared in entries.
