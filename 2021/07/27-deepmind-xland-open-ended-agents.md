# DeepMind XLand: agents trained through open-ended play

- **Date:** 2021-07-27
- **Who:** DeepMind
- **Type:** research

DeepMind introduced XLand, a procedurally generated 3D multiplayer world with billions of tasks. Agents trained on it continually as the tasks adjusted to their progress, and they then solved unseen tasks such as hide and seek and capture the flag without any human demonstration data. It showed a route to broadly capable agents through generated environments.
