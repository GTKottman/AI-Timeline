# AAAI-19 Outstanding Paper: combining tree-search methods in reinforcement learning

- **Date:** 2019-01-29
- **Who:** Yonathan Efroni, Gal Dalal, Bruno Scherrer, Shie Mannor (Technion, INRIA)
- **Type:** competition

At AAAI-19 in Honolulu, the Outstanding Paper award went to "How to Combine Tree-Search Methods in Reinforcement Learning," which studies how to combine tree-search planning with value-based RL. Its proposed fix backs up returns from the optimal tree path to the root's descendants instead of only at the leaves. The awards were presented on 2019-01-29, during the conference that ran from 2019-01-27 to 2019-02-01.
