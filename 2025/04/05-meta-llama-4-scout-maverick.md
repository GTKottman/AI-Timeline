# Meta releases Llama 4 Scout and Maverick, its first natively multimodal open models

- **Date:** 2025-04-05
- **Who:** Meta AI
- **Type:** model

Meta released Llama 4 Scout and Llama 4 Maverick as open-weight models, described by Meta as the first open-weight natively multimodal models. Scout offered a 10 million token context window, the largest of the release. Meta also previewed Llama 4 Behemoth, which was still in training.
