# OpenAI Superalignment Fast Grants

- **Date:** 2023-12-14
- **Who:** OpenAI, with Eric Schmidt as partner
- **Type:** funding

OpenAI opened $10 million in Superalignment Fast Grants for technical research on aligning and securing superhuman AI. Awards ranged from $100K to $2M for academic labs, nonprofits and individual researchers, plus one-year $150K graduate fellowships, with applications due 18 February 2024. The grants were aimed at outside alignment research while OpenAI's own Superalignment team was in its early stage.
