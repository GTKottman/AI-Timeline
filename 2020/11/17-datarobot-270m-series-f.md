# DataRobot raises $270M pre-IPO round led by Altimeter Capital

- **Date:** 2020-11-17
- **Who:** DataRobot; led by Altimeter Capital
- **Type:** funding

DataRobot announced $270 million in pre-IPO funding led by Altimeter Capital on November 17, 2020, valuing the automated machine learning platform company at about $2.7 billion, more than double its Series E valuation. Coverage said it was likely the company's last venture round before going public.
