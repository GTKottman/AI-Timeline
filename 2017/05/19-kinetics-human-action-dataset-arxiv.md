# The Kinetics Human Action Video Dataset

- **Date:** 2017-05-19
- **Who:** Will Kay, João Carreira, Andrew Zisserman and others (Google DeepMind / Google)
- **Type:** dataset

arXiv paper introducing the Kinetics human action video dataset: 400 human action classes with at least 400 clips per class, each about 10 seconds long and taken from a separate YouTube video. Classes include human-object actions such as playing instruments and human-human actions such as shaking hands. It became one of the main benchmarks for video action recognition.
