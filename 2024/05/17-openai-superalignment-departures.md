# Ilya Sutskever and Jan Leike leave OpenAI; Superalignment team dissolved

- **Date:** 2024-05-17
- **Who:** OpenAI; Ilya Sutskever; Jan Leike
- **Type:** people

OpenAI co-founder and chief scientist Ilya Sutskever resigned, followed by alignment lead Jan Leike, who wrote that safety culture had "taken a backseat to shiny products." OpenAI dissolved its Superalignment team and spread its members across other groups. The departures put AI safety governance at a lab under public scrutiny.
