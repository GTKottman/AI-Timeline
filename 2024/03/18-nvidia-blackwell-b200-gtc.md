# NVIDIA unveils Blackwell architecture and B200 GPU at GTC 2024

- **Date:** 2024-03-18
- **Who:** NVIDIA
- **Type:** hardware

At GTC 2024 in San Jose, NVIDIA unveiled the Blackwell architecture, the B200 GPU, and the GB200 superchip with NVL72 rack configurations. The two-die B200 was made on TSMC 4NP and carried 192 GB of HBM3e. NVIDIA claimed large gains over Hopper, including up to 30x LLM inference for GB200 systems, though those figures are vendor claims. It set the hardware roadmap for the next wave of frontier model training and inference.
