# Tesla Model X on Autopilot crashes into highway barrier and kills driver

- **Date:** 2018-03-23
- **Who:** Tesla; US National Transportation Safety Board (NTSB) investigation
- **Type:** other

A Tesla Model X with Autopilot engaged hit a damaged crash attenuator on Highway 101 in Mountain View, California, and the driver, Walter Huang, died. The NTSB's preliminary report said the car sped up to about 70.8 mph in the seconds before impact and had Autopilot engaged for about 19 minutes before the crash, with hands-on warnings given. The crash drove later debate over driver-assist limits and driver monitoring. The NTSB's final board findings in 2020 blamed over-reliance on driver-assist technology and weak federal oversight.
