# EU Artificial Intelligence Act enters into force

- **Date:** 2024-08-01
- **Who:** European Commission; European Parliament; Council of the EU
- **Type:** policy

The EU AI Act took effect on August 1, 2024, and its rules phase in over the following years. Rules for general-purpose AI models apply 12 months later, and the Commission planned a related Code of Practice by April 2025. The Act's bans on certain practices and its duties for high-risk and general-purpose models come in on this schedule.
