# Google and Hebrew University publish Dreamix video editing

- **Date:** 2023-02-02
- **Who:** Google Research and The Hebrew University of Jerusalem
- **Type:** paper

The paper "Dreamix: Video Diffusion Models are General Video Editors" was submitted to arXiv on 2 February 2023. Dreamix edits a video from a text prompt by running a video diffusion model on a low-resolution version of the source clip and adding high-resolution detail that matches the prompt. A short fine-tune on the source video keeps color, pose, object size and camera motion close to the original. It also animates still images and generates subject-driven video, which made text-driven video editing a credible research direction.
