# Uber self-driving test vehicle kills pedestrian in Tempe, Arizona

- **Date:** 2018-03-18
- **Who:** Uber (Advanced Technologies Group)
- **Type:** other

An Uber autonomous test vehicle in self-driving mode, with a human backup driver at the wheel, struck and killed pedestrian Elaine Herzberg in Tempe, Arizona, on the night of March 18. Coverage called it likely the first pedestrian death involving a self-driving vehicle. Uber suspended its autonomous testing in Pittsburgh, Tempe, San Francisco and Toronto, and the NTSB opened an investigation. The case became a major reference point for safety rules and oversight of AI-driven vehicles.
