# Microsoft renames Bing Chat to Copilot

- **Date:** 2023-11-15
- **Who:** Microsoft
- **Type:** product

At Microsoft Ignite, Microsoft renamed Bing Chat, its GPT-4 powered chatbot launched in February, to Copilot in Bing, and renamed the enterprise version Bing Chat Enterprise to Copilot as well. Microsoft said the change was meant to give consumer and commercial customers one unified Copilot experience. The rebrand made Copilot the single name for Microsoft's AI assistant in Bing.
