# Biden signs first National Security Memorandum on AI

- **Date:** 2024-10-24
- **Who:** White House (President Joe Biden)
- **Type:** policy

President Biden signed a National Security Memorandum on AI, the first national-security directive focused on the technology. It set three objectives: keeping US AI leadership safe and trustworthy, letting national-security agencies use AI, and shaping international AI governance. It made the US AI Safety Institute the primary US government point of contact with private AI developers, with guidance on testing and managing dual-use foundation model risks due within 180 days. It was issued with a companion framework on AI risk management in national security, and it also has a classified annex.
