# ICML 2019 Best Paper Awards

- **Date:** 2019-06-11
- **Who:** ICML 2019 organizers (Long Beach, California); award papers from Google Brain Zurich, Max Planck Institute, ETH Zurich, University of Cambridge and PROWLER.io
- **Type:** other

ICML 2019 gave its Best Paper Award to "Challenging Common Assumptions in the Unsupervised Learning of Disentangled Representations," a joint paper from Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems, ETH Zurich and Google Brain Zurich. A second Best Paper went to "Rates of Convergence for Sparse Variational Gaussian Process Regression" from Cambridge and PROWLER.io. The ICML Organizing Committee announced the awards on June 11, 2019.
