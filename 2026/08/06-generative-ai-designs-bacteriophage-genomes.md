# Researchers use generative AI to design working bacteriophage genomes

- **Date:** 2026-08-06
- **Who:** Stanford and Arc Institute researchers (published in Science)
- **Type:** research

Researchers published in Science that they used Evo-family genome language models to design complete bacteriophage genomes from the phage ΦX174 template. They synthesized and tested nearly 300 generated phages and found 16 that performed well against E. coli. A cocktail of generated phages overcame resistance in two E. coli strains that had resisted ΦX174-like phages.
