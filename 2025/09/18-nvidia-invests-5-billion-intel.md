# Nvidia invests $5 billion in Intel

- **Date:** 2025-09-18
- **Who:** NVIDIA, Intel
- **Type:** funding

NVIDIA agreed to buy $5 billion of Intel common stock at $23.28 per share, subject to regulatory approval, and the two companies said they would co-develop custom data center products for AI infrastructure and PC products. Intel shares jumped about 30% in premarket trading after the news. The deal was a major signal that NVIDIA was willing to take stakes in rivals' chip businesses to secure its position in AI infrastructure.
