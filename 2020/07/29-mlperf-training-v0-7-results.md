# MLPerf Training v0.7 Results

- **Date:** 2020-07-29
- **Who:** MLCommons / MLPerf; NVIDIA; Google; Intel; Huawei
- **Type:** competition

MLPerf released Training v0.7 results on July 29, 2020. NVIDIA said it submitted results on all eight benchmarks and set records in the commercially available category, using A100 GPUs. Google reported record times on six of eight benchmarks in the preview category with its TPU v4 chips. Intel previewed 3rd-generation Xeon (Cooper Lake) results, and Huawei submitted Ascend 910 systems.
