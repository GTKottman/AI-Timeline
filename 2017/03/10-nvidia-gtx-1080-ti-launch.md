# NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1080 Ti launch

- **Date:** 2017-03-10
- **Who:** NVIDIA
- **Type:** hardware

NVIDIA's GeForce GTX 1080 Ti went on sale on March 10, 2017, at a launch price of $699. It used the Pascal architecture with 11 GB of GDDR5X memory and 3584 CUDA cores. NVIDIA said it was up to 35% faster than the GTX 1080.
