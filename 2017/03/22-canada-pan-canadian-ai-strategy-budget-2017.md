# Canada's 2017 budget funds $125 million Pan-Canadian AI Strategy

- **Date:** 2017-03-22
- **Who:** Government of Canada (Finance Minister Bill Morneau); CIFAR to administer the strategy
- **Type:** policy

Canada's federal budget, tabled March 22, 2017, set aside $125 million for a Pan-Canadian Artificial Intelligence Strategy covering research, talent development and industry partnerships.
