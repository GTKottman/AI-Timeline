# NVIDIA strikes about $20 billion licensing and asset deal with Groq

- **Date:** 2025-12-24
- **Who:** NVIDIA; Groq
- **Type:** acquisition

NVIDIA signed a non-exclusive licensing agreement for Groq's inference technology, and Groq founder Jonathan Ross and other team members joined NVIDIA. Groq said it would remain independent. CNBC reported the deal at about $20 billion, but neither company disclosed a price.
