# Google previews Gemma 3n, a mobile-first open model

- **Date:** 2025-05-20
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** model

Google's developer blog announced a preview of Gemma 3n, the first open model built on a new architecture that Google said would also power the next Gemini Nano. Developers could try it in an early preview before the wider release. The later official launch, covered in July 2025, offered E2B and E4B sizes that need about 2 to 3 GB of memory and accept image, audio, video and text input.
