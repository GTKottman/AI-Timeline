# OpenAI InstructGPT paper: training language models to follow instructions

- **Date:** 2022-03-00
- **Who:** OpenAI (Long Ouyang and co-authors)
- **Type:** paper

OpenAI posted the full InstructGPT paper, which describes fine-tuning GPT-3 with supervised learning, a reward model trained on human preference rankings, and reinforcement learning. The results showed labelers preferred outputs from the 1.3B InstructGPT model over the 175B GPT-3 model. The January 2022 blog post had announced the approach, and this paper gave the method and data in detail. The exact arXiv day was not confirmed.
