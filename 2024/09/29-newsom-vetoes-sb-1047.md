# Governor Newsom vetoes SB 1047

- **Date:** 2024-09-29
- **Who:** Gov. Gavin Newsom; Sen. Scott Wiener
- **Type:** policy

Governor Gavin Newsom vetoed SB 1047, arguing that it targeted model size rather than the risk of how a system is deployed. He announced separate AI initiatives with Stanford HAI and the Carnegie Endowment the same day. The veto left California without a frontier-model safety statute.
