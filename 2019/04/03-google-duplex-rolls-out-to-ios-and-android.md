# Google Duplex booking assistant rolls out to iOS and Android

- **Date:** 2019-04-03
- **Who:** Google
- **Type:** product

Google expanded Duplex, its AI system that makes restaurant reservations by phone, beyond Pixel phones to iOS and Android devices in the US, with Android 5.0 or later required. Calls open with a message saying the call is from Google, and businesses can opt out of receiving the automated calls. The rollout showed conversational voice AI working in live phone calls with real businesses.
