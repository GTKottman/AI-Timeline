# Trump approves Nvidia H200 chip sales to approved Chinese customers

- **Date:** 2025-12-08
- **Who:** Trump administration (White House, Commerce Department); Nvidia
- **Type:** policy

On December 8, 2025 President Trump announced on Truth Social that the US would allow Nvidia H200 sales to approved Chinese customers, with a 25% payment to the US government. Blackwell and the upcoming Rubin chips were excluded. The Commerce Department was still finalizing the terms, and Beijing's response was uncertain. It mattered because it loosened the AI-chip export line toward China at the frontier-GPU tier.
