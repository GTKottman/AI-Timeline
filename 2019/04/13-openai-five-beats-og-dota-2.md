# OpenAI Five beats OG 2-0 in Dota 2

- **Date:** 2019-04-13
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** competition

OpenAI Five won a best-of-three exhibition against Team OG, the reigning The International champions, sweeping 2-0 in San Francisco. Illusions and summoning were disabled, and each side was capped at 17 heroes. The bots had trained for about 10 months and over 45,000 hours of self-play, showing deep reinforcement learning working in a complex team game.
