# Google launches Dataset Search in beta

- **Date:** 2018-09-05
- **Who:** Google
- **Type:** product

Google launched Dataset Search in beta, a search engine for finding public datasets across the web. It relies on dataset publishers marking up their pages with schema.org metadata, which Google's crawler reads to build dataset records. The first release covered environmental and social science data, government data and datasets from news organizations such as ProPublica. For AI work it made training and benchmark datasets far easier to find, and it left beta in January 2020.
