# Stanford releases the 2023 AI Index report

- **Date:** 2023-04-03
- **Who:** Stanford Institute for Human-Centered AI (HAI)
- **Type:** research

Stanford HAI published its annual AI Index, a data-heavy report on the state of AI covering benchmark progress, industry versus academic model output, legislation and public opinion. The 2023 edition tracked AI legislation across 127 countries, up from 25 the year before, and noted that industry now produced most significant models. It mattered as the most-cited yearly reference for measuring AI progress and policy.
