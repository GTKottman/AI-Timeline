# OpenAI and Amazon sign $38 billion AWS compute deal

- **Date:** 2025-11-03
- **Who:** OpenAI, Amazon Web Services (AWS)
- **Type:** funding

OpenAI agreed to a $38 billion compute arrangement with AWS to run its AI workloads on Amazon EC2 UltraServers with NVIDIA GB200 and GB300 GPUs, with AP reporting access to hundreds of thousands of chips. Amazon said capacity would be deployed before the end of 2026, with room to expand into 2027. The deal came less than a week after OpenAI reworked its Microsoft partnership, and it showed OpenAI spreading its compute supply across more than one cloud provider.
