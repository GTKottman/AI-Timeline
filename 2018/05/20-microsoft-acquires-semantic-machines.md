# Microsoft acquires Semantic Machines

- **Date:** 2018-05-20
- **Who:** Microsoft (David Ku, CTO of AI and Research); Semantic Machines
- **Type:** acquisition

Microsoft announced it had acquired Semantic Machines, a conversational AI startup that had raised just under $21 million from investors including General Catalyst and Bain Capital Ventures. Financial terms were not disclosed. Microsoft tied the deal to Cortana and the Azure Bot Service, and said it would set up a conversational AI center of excellence in Berkeley.
