# Qualcomm acquires Arduino

- **Date:** 2025-10-07
- **Who:** Qualcomm, Arduino
- **Type:** acquisition

Qualcomm agreed to acquire Arduino, the open-source hardware and software platform used by millions of developers, to extend its reach into robotics, IoT and edge AI. The price was not disclosed, and the deal is subject to regulatory approval. Qualcomm said the Arduino brand and its open-source approach would stay, and it launched the Arduino UNO Q, built on the Qualcomm Dragonwing platform. Some outlets date the announcement October 8.
