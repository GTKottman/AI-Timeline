# Microsoft Translator switches Chinese and German to next-generation neural translation

- **Date:** 2018-11-14
- **Who:** Microsoft Translator (Microsoft Research)
- **Type:** model

Microsoft Translator put new neural machine translation systems into production for Chinese and German, to and from English. Microsoft's human evaluations reported up to an 11% overall quality gain over its previous production API. The systems adapt research models that Microsoft earlier said reached human parity on Chinese news translation into English. More languages were promised in the following months. It mattered as one of the first large commercial rollouts of research-grade neural translation to real users.
