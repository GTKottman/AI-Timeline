# AlphaZero preprint: superhuman chess and shogi by self-play

- **Date:** 2017-12-05
- **Who:** DeepMind (Google)
- **Type:** paper

DeepMind posted a preprint describing AlphaZero, a single algorithm that learned chess, shogi and Go from self-play with no human game data. It beat Stockfish and Elmo after hours of training. The paper showed that one general learning method could master several games, not just Go.
