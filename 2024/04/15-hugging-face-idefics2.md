# Hugging Face releases Idefics2 vision-language model

- **Date:** 2024-04-15
- **Who:** Hugging Face
- **Type:** model

Hugging Face released Idefics2, an 8-billion-parameter open vision-language model under Apache 2.0, built on Mistral-7B and SigLIP and processing images at native resolution up to 980x980. It handles visual question answering, document extraction and multi-image reasoning, and was trained on a curated mix of about 50 datasets called The Cauldron. Its open license and small size made strong multimodal models usable by community developers.
