# Facebook F8 2017: Messenger bot platform and Wit.ai automatic answers

- **Date:** 2017-04-18
- **Who:** Facebook (Messenger team); Wit.ai (Facebook-owned)
- **Type:** product

At F8 2017, Facebook's Messenger update added a Discover tab for finding bots, Chat Extensions for group conversations with businesses, and Wit.ai-powered automatic answers so Pages could reply to frequently asked questions such as opening hours and directions. Facebook's developer blog cited more than 100,000 monthly active bots.
