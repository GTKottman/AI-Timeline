# AWS launches SageMaker Studio Lab

- **Date:** 2021-12-01
- **Who:** Amazon Web Services (AWS)
- **Type:** product

AWS launched Amazon SageMaker Studio Lab at re:Invent 2021 as a free service for learning and experimenting with machine learning. It provided a JupyterLab environment with CPU and GPU training, 15 GB of persistent storage, and sign-up needing only an email address and no AWS account or credit card. It mattered for AI because it lowered the cost of entry for students and hobbyists to hands-on ML work.
