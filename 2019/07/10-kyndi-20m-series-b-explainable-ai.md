# Kyndi Raises $20M Series B for Explainable AI

- **Date:** 2019-07-10
- **Who:** Kyndi (explainable AI platform); led by Intel Capital, with UL Ventures and PivotNorth Capital
- **Type:** funding

Kyndi, a company building an explainable AI platform that derives insights from documents, closed a $20 million Series B led by Intel Capital. The money was for growing engineering and sales, and Intel Capital's Sunil Sanghavi joined the board. Sources differ on total funding raised to date, but agree on the Series B amount. The round reflected investor interest in AI systems whose answers can be traced to their sources.
