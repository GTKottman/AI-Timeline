# Anthropic launches Research and a Google Workspace integration for Claude

- **Date:** 2025-04-15
- **Who:** Anthropic
- **Type:** product

Anthropic launched Research, a feature for Claude that searches across sources to answer questions, in early beta for Max, Team and Enterprise users in the United States, Brazil and Japan. It also added an integration with Google Workspace, letting Claude search Gmail and Calendar and review Google Docs, in beta for all paid users.
