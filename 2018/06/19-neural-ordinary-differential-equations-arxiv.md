# "Neural Ordinary Differential Equations" posted to arXiv

- **Date:** 2018-06-19
- **Who:** Ricky T. Q. Chen, Yulia Rubanova, Jesse Bettencourt, David Duvenaud (University of Toronto)
- **Type:** paper

The paper replaces a fixed stack of neural network layers with a learned continuous transformation computed by a numerical ODE solver. This gives memory-efficient models that trade accuracy against speed. It later won a best paper award at NeurIPS 2018, and it inspired a line of continuous-depth models.
