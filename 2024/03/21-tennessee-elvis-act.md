# Tennessee ELVIS Act signed into law

- **Date:** 2024-03-21
- **Who:** State of Tennessee (Governor Bill Lee)
- **Type:** policy

Tennessee enacted the ELVIS Act, which extends the state's publicity-rights law to cover a person's voice, including AI-simulated voices, and makes unauthorized AI voice cloning actionable. It took effect July 1, 2024. Tennessee was described as the first state to pass a law of this kind, aimed at musicians and performers whose voices could be cloned without consent.
