# Google releases Conceptual Captions, a 3.3 million image-caption dataset

- **Date:** 2018-09-05
- **Who:** Google AI
- **Type:** dataset

Google AI introduced Conceptual Captions, about 3.3 million image and caption pairs automatically extracted and filtered from billions of web pages. In human evaluations, models trained on it generalized better than models trained on MS-COCO. The release also launched the Conceptual Captions Challenge, giving researchers a large, cleaner alternative to hand-written caption data.
