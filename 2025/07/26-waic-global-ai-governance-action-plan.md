# China publishes Global AI Governance Action Plan at WAIC 2025

- **Date:** 2025-07-26
- **Who:** Government of China; World AI Conference 2025 (Shanghai)
- **Type:** policy

China released the Global AI Governance Action Plan at the World AI Conference and High-Level Meeting on Global AI Governance in Shanghai, a 13-point agenda on open cooperation, equity, safety and sovereignty, including an International AI Science Group and a Global AI Governance Dialogue under the UN. Coverage noted it followed the US AI Action Plan by two days and that implementing bodies were not yet named. It mattered as China's formal proposal for a multilateral AI governance framework.
