# ACLU sues Clearview AI over faceprints in Illinois

- **Date:** 2020-05-28
- **Who:** ACLU, ACLU of Illinois, Clearview AI
- **Type:** policy

The ACLU and ACLU of Illinois, with four other nonprofits, filed suit in Cook County Circuit Court against Clearview AI. The complaint said Clearview collected faceprints of Illinois residents without notice or consent, in breach of the state's Biometric Information Privacy Act. It followed the January 2020 New York Times investigation into Clearview's face database and became one of several legal challenges to facial recognition training and sale.
