# Open Philanthropy grants OpenAI $30 million

- **Date:** 2017-03-31
- **Who:** Open Philanthropy Project; OpenAI; Holden Karnofsky
- **Type:** funding

Open Philanthropy recommended $30 million in general support to OpenAI, paid as $10 million a year over three years. The grant came with a seat for Open Philanthropy's Holden Karnofsky on OpenAI's board, who with one other board member oversaw OpenAI's safety and governance work. The grant page was published in March 2017; the exact day (March 31) comes from a secondary page, not the grant page itself.
