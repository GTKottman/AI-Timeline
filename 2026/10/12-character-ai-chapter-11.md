# Character.AI files for Chapter 11 bankruptcy

- **Date:** 2026-10-12
- **Who:** Character.AI
- **Type:** funding

Character Technologies, the company behind Character.AI, filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection on October 12, 2026, after the lawsuits it settled in January. Disney agreed to pay $20 million for some of the company's intellectual property and to provide transition services, and the company plans a court-supervised sale by the end of the year.
