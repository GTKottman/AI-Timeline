# Crusoe raises $1.375 billion Series E at over $10 billion valuation

- **Date:** 2025-10-23
- **Who:** Crusoe, Mubadala Capital, Valor Equity Partners, NVIDIA, Founders Fund
- **Type:** funding

AI data-center infrastructure company Crusoe announced an oversubscribed $1.375 billion Series E at a valuation above $10 billion, co-led by Mubadala Capital and Valor Equity Partners, with NVIDIA and Founders Fund participating. The money funds the build-out of AI compute capacity, and Crusoe is described by Bloomberg as an OpenAI partner.
