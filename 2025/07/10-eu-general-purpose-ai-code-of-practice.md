# European Commission publishes the General-Purpose AI Code of Practice

- **Date:** 2025-07-10
- **Who:** European Commission (EU AI Office); independent expert drafters
- **Type:** policy

The European Commission published the General-Purpose AI Code of Practice, a voluntary tool for providers of general-purpose AI models under the EU AI Act. It has chapters on transparency, copyright, and safety and security, and maps to Articles 53 and 55 of the AI Act. It arrived late, since drafting had been due in May, and it came just before the obligations for general-purpose AI models took effect on August 2, 2025. Providers who sign can show compliance by following it.
