# ICML 2023 outstanding paper awards

- **Date:** 2023-07-25
- **Who:** ICML 2023 organizers; winners incl. Defazio and Mishchenko; Kirchenbauer et al.; Abbe, Bengio, Lotfi and Rizk; Kozuno et al. (OMRON SINIC X)
- **Type:** competition

ICML 2023, held in Honolulu from July 23 to 29, gave outstanding paper awards to several papers. Among them were "Learning-Rate-Free Learning by D-Adaptation" and "A Watermark for Large Language Models", which described a way to embed statistically detectable signals in LLM output. ICML announced the awards by post on July 25, 2023; AIhub covered them the next day.
