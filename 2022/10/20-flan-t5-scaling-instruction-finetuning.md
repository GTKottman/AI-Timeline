# Scaling Instruction-Finetuned Language Models (Flan-T5)

- **Date:** 2022-10-20
- **Who:** Google Research
- **Type:** paper

The paper studied how instruction fine-tuning scales with the number of tasks, model size and chain-of-thought training data. The resulting Flan-T5 models showed that instruction tuning helps large models generalize to new tasks.
