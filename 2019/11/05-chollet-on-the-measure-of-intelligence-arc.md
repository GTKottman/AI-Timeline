# Chollet Publishes "On the Measure of Intelligence" and the ARC Benchmark

- **Date:** 2019-11-05
- **Who:** François Chollet (Google)
- **Type:** paper

François Chollet posted "On the Measure of Intelligence" to arXiv (1911.01547) on November 5, 2019, with a revised version on November 25. The paper argues that AI progress needs a clear measure of intelligence, and it defines intelligence as skill-acquisition efficiency across diverse tasks. It critiques benchmarking AI on games and single tasks, and it introduces the Abstraction and Reasoning Corpus (ARC), a benchmark built on human-like core-knowledge priors to measure general fluid intelligence. ARC became a widely used test for general reasoning in later years.
