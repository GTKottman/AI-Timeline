# Google Imagen: photorealistic text-to-image diffusion

- **Date:** 2022-05-23
- **Who:** Google Research
- **Type:** model

Google posted the Imagen paper, a text-to-image diffusion model that pairs a large frozen language model text encoder with cascaded diffusion stages. The paper reported that scaling the frozen language encoder improved image-text alignment more than scaling the image model did.
