# AWS unveils Amazon Q assistant and Trainium2 chip at re:Invent

- **Date:** 2023-11-28
- **Who:** Amazon Web Services
- **Type:** product

At re:Invent 2023 in Las Vegas, AWS CEO Adam Selipsky unveiled Amazon Q, a generative AI assistant for work, with initial applications for AWS developers in preview. TechCrunch reported pricing starting at $20 per user per month, and Amazon Q could draw on business data from Microsoft 365, Slack, Salesforce, Dropbox and S3 while respecting access controls. The keynote also announced the Trainium2 AI training chip and Graviton4. The date is the keynote day, Tuesday of re:Invent week, which sources place on November 28 without stating it directly.
