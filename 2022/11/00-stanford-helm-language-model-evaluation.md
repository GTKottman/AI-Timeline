# Stanford HELM: Holistic Evaluation of Language Models

- **Date:** 2022-11-00
- **Who:** Stanford Center for Research on Foundation Models and HAI
- **Type:** research

Stanford CRFM published HELM, a benchmark and toolkit that measures language models with 7 metrics (accuracy, calibration, robustness, fairness, bias, toxicity, efficiency) across 16 core scenarios, plus 26 targeted scenarios. The paper measured each metric on the core scenarios where feasible, which was 87.5% of the time. The exact day of the arXiv posting was not confirmed.
