# ChatGPT search rolls out to all users, free tier included

- **Date:** 2024-12-16
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** product

On Day 8 of the "12 Days" event, OpenAI made ChatGPT search available to all logged-in users where ChatGPT is offered, including free users on the web, desktop and mobile apps. Search had first launched in late October 2024 for paid tiers, so the change was the wider free access rather than a new feature. Coverage differs on whether a logged-in account is required.
