# Google's deep learning system grades prostate cancer more accurately than pathologists

- **Date:** 2018-11-16
- **Who:** Google AI with pathology collaborators
- **Type:** research

Google AI researchers built a deep learning system that assigns Gleason patterns to regions of prostatectomy slides and then summarizes an overall Gleason grade group. In validation it reached 70% overall accuracy, against 61% for average US board-certified general pathologists. It also identified patients at higher risk of recurrence after surgery better than the pathologist grades did.
