# EU Council gives final approval to the AI Act

- **Date:** 2024-05-21
- **Who:** Council of the European Union
- **Type:** policy

The Council of the EU formally gave its final approval, the last step before publication. The Act became the first comprehensive binding AI law in a major economy, with obligations phased in from 2025.
