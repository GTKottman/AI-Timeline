# Allen Institute for AI launches Iconary, a drawing and guessing game with humans

- **Date:** 2019-02-00
- **Who:** Allen Institute for Artificial Intelligence (AI2)
- **Type:** research

AI2's computer vision team launched Iconary in February 2019, a Pictionary-style game in which an AI partner draws and guesses together with a human. It was presented as a test of collaborative, grounded communication between people and AI. The exact day in February was not confirmed, so the file uses day 00.
