# DiscoGAN: discovering cross-domain relations with GANs (arXiv)

- **Date:** 2017-03-15
- **Who:** Taeksoo Kim, Moonsu Cha, Hyunsoo Kim, Jung Kwon Lee, Jiwon Kim (SK Telecom / SKT Brain)
- **Type:** paper

The paper "Learning to Discover Cross-Domain Relations with Generative Adversarial Networks" appeared on arXiv (1703.05192). It introduced DiscoGAN, which learns relations between two image domains from unpaired data, and showed style transfer, such as between shoes and handbags or faces, while keeping pose and identity. It was later published at ICML 2017, and the official code is public on GitHub, which made unpaired image-to-image translation practical.
