# DeepMind Ithaca restores and dates ancient Greek inscriptions

- **Date:** 2022-03-09
- **Who:** DeepMind with Ca' Foscari University of Venice, University of Oxford and the Athens University of Economics and Business
- **Type:** research

DeepMind published Ithaca in Nature, a neural network that restores missing text in damaged ancient Greek inscriptions, estimates where they were found, and dates them. It was trained on 78,608 digitized inscriptions, reached about 62% accuracy on text restoration and 71% on geographic attribution, and raised historians' accuracy from 25% to 72% when they used it. The code was released open source, and the paper appeared on the cover of Nature's 10 March 2022 issue.
