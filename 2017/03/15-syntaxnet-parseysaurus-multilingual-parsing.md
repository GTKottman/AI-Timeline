# SyntaxNet upgrade: ParseySaurus models and multilingual parsing competition

- **Date:** 2017-03-15
- **Who:** Google (David Weiss, Slav Petrov)
- **Type:** software

Google announced a major SyntaxNet upgrade with ParseySaurus, pretrained character-based parsing models that infer the grammatical role of words never seen in training. The post reported error reductions of up to 25% over Parsey's Cousins, especially for morphologically rich languages such as Russian, Turkish and Hungarian. It also launched a multilingual parsing competition at CoNLL, judged on a new 1,000-sentence test set annotated across 45 languages.
