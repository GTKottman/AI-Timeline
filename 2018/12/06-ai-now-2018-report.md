# AI Now Institute publishes its 2018 report with 10 accountability recommendations

- **Date:** 2018-12-06
- **Who:** AI Now Institute (New York University)
- **Type:** policy

The AI Now 2018 report offered ten practical recommendations for building accountability frameworks around powerful AI systems. It covered issues such as bias, surveillance and the use of AI in public institutions. It was one of the main civil-society reference points on AI governance that year.
