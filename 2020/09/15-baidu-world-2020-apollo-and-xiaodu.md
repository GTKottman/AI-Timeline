# Baidu World 2020 shows Apollo autonomous driving, Duxiaoxiao assistant and Baidu Brain 6.0

- **Date:** 2020-09-15
- **Who:** Baidu
- **Type:** product

Baidu held Baidu World 2020 as a virtual conference under the theme "Intelligence of Everything", broadcast with CCTV. The Apollo autonomous driving platform demonstrated full driving functions in a livestream and showed 5G remote driving. Baidu also released the Duxiaoxiao virtual assistant app and upgraded its Baidu Brain open AI platform to version 6.0. It was a visible push of Baidu's AI stack into driving, assistants and enterprise platforms.
