# Stability AI releases Stable Audio 3.0 music and sound models

- **Date:** 2026-05-20
- **Who:** Stability AI
- **Type:** model

Stable Audio 3.0 is a family of four audio models: Small SFX and Small (459M parameters each, up to two minutes), Medium (1.4B) and Large (2.7B). Medium and Large can generate tracks longer than six minutes. Three models are open-weight on Hugging Face, Large is API or enterprise-only, and Stability says all models were trained on licensed data, including AudioSparx and Freesound material. The release is a licensed-data alternative to the large music generators in the market.
