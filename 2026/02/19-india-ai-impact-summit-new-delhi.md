# India AI Impact Summit held in New Delhi

- **Date:** 2026-02-19
- **Who:** Government of India (IndiaAI Mission); attendees included Sundar Pichai, Sam Altman, Demis Hassabis, Dario Amodei and Jensen Huang (listed in pre-event coverage)
- **Type:** other

India hosted the AI Impact Summit in New Delhi, the fourth of the global AI summits, with over 700 sessions and delegations from more than 100 countries. Prime Minister Modi was scheduled to address the main plenary on February 19. One post-event report said India would add 20,000 GPUs on top of 38,000 already provisioned under the IndiaAI Mission. Sources disagree on the exact dates (some say 16–20 February), so 19 February is the plenary day, and the GPU figures come from one report.
