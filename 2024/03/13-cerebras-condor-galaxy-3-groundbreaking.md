# Cerebras breaks ground on Condor Galaxy 3 AI supercomputer

- **Date:** 2024-03-13
- **Who:** Cerebras Systems; G42
- **Type:** hardware

Cerebras and G42 announced the build of Condor Galaxy 3, an 8 exaFLOPs AI supercomputer made of 64 CS-3 systems built on the WSE-3 wafer-scale chip. The cluster sits in Dallas, Texas, and is part of the Condor Galaxy network. It showed wafer-scale hardware being scaled into real data centres.
