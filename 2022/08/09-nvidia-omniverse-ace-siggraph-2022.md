# NVIDIA Omniverse AI tools and Avatar Cloud Engine at SIGGRAPH 2022

- **Date:** 2022-08-09
- **Who:** NVIDIA
- **Type:** software

At SIGGRAPH 2022, NVIDIA announced a large release of Omniverse, its 3D collaboration platform, with new AI-powered tools for building virtual worlds and connectors to tools such as Blender, Unity, Houdini and Siemens Xcelerator. Jensen Huang introduced the Omniverse Avatar Cloud Engine (ACE) for digital humans, with an updated Audio2Face model that generates facial animation from voice audio. NVIDIA also showed two neural graphics SDKs, including Kaolin WISP for neural fields in PyTorch.
