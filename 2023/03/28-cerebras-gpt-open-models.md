# Cerebras releases seven open Cerebras-GPT models

- **Date:** 2023-03-28
- **Who:** Cerebras Systems
- **Type:** model

Cerebras released seven GPT-style language models ranging from 111M to 13B parameters under Apache 2.0, with weights and training recipe included. All seven were trained on Cerebras CS-2 systems, and the company framed the release as a counter to closed-model access. It mattered as a fully open, compute-optimal model family that researchers could reproduce and build on.
