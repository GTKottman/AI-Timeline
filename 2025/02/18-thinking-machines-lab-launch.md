# Mira Murati launches Thinking Machines Lab

- **Date:** 2025-02-18
- **Who:** Thinking Machines Lab; Mira Murati, Barret Zoph, John Schulman
- **Type:** people

Mira Murati, the former OpenAI CTO, launched Thinking Machines Lab with about 30 researchers and engineers, roughly two-thirds of them ex-OpenAI, and drew staff from rivals including OpenAI, Meta and Mistral. Barret Zoph was named technology chief and John Schulman chief scientist. The company said it would build AI systems that reflect human values, integrate research and product teams from the start, and share code, datasets and model specifications for alignment research. The launch post is dated February 18, 2025, and some outlets reported February 19 because of time zones.
