# "Slaughterbots" video released by Future of Life Institute

- **Date:** 2017-11-12
- **Who:** Future of Life Institute
- **Type:** policy

The Future of Life Institute released Slaughterbots, a short film about swarms of cheap autonomous microdrones that use AI and facial recognition to target people. It was made to push for arms-control rules on lethal autonomous weapons, and it was timed for a UN meeting on the Convention on Certain Conventional Weapons in Geneva. It went viral.
