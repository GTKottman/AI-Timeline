# Baidu open-sources the ERNIE 4.5 model family

- **Date:** 2025-06-30
- **Who:** Baidu
- **Type:** model

Baidu began open-sourcing its ERNIE 4.5 family on 2025-06-30 as it had announced, a set of about 10 multimodal large-model variants. It was a reversal for CEO Robin Li, who had favoured closed models. It mattered because it made one of China's largest model families open weights, as a direct challenge to closed US labs.
