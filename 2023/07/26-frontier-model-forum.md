# Anthropic, Google, Microsoft and OpenAI launch Frontier Model Forum

- **Date:** 2023-07-26
- **Who:** Anthropic, Google, Microsoft, OpenAI
- **Type:** other

Four leading AI developers launched the Frontier Model Forum, an industry body to advance AI safety research, identify best practices for developing and deploying frontier models, and share knowledge with policymakers, academics and civil society. Its first step was an advisory board to guide strategy. It came days after the White House voluntary commitments and was the first joint industry safety body of this kind.
