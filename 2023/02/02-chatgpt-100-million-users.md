# ChatGPT passes 100 million users

- **Date:** 2023-02-02
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** product

Reports said ChatGPT reached 100 million monthly users by early February 2023, making it the fastest-growing consumer application on record. The milestone showed mass consumer demand for generative AI and pushed rival companies to respond.
