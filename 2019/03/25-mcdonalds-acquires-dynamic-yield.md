# McDonald's acquires Dynamic Yield for reported $300 million

- **Date:** 2019-03-25
- **Who:** McDonald's, Dynamic Yield (Israeli AI personalization startup)
- **Type:** acquisition

McDonald's announced it would acquire Dynamic Yield, an Israel-based personalization and decision-logic company, in a deal reported at more than $300 million and described as its largest in about two decades. The technology was meant to tailor drive-thru digital menu boards by weather, time of day, traffic and customer choices, with plans extending to kiosks and the mobile app. It showed a large restaurant chain buying AI-driven personalization to change in-store ordering. The price came from press reports, since McDonald's did not disclose terms.
