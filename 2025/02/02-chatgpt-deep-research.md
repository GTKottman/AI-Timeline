# OpenAI launches ChatGPT Deep Research

- **Date:** 2025-02-02
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** product

OpenAI released Deep Research, an agent in ChatGPT that browses the web on its own for many minutes and writes a cited report. It was an early example of the "research agent" product category that later spread to other AI labs.
