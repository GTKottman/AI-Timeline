# Baidu posts Deep Voice 3 fully convolutional text-to-speech paper

- **Date:** 2017-10-20
- **Who:** Baidu Research (Wei Ping, Kainan Peng, Andrew Gibiansky, Sercan Arik, Ajay Kannan, Sharan Narang), with Jonathan Raiman (OpenAI) and John Miller (UC Berkeley)
- **Type:** paper

Deep Voice 3 is a fully convolutional, attention-based neural text-to-speech system that matches leading neural TTS in naturalness while training about ten times faster. It was trained on over 800 hours of audio from more than 2,000 speakers, and the paper reports serving ten million queries per day on a single GPU server. It showed convolutional sequence models could scale TTS to many speakers, a direction that later shaped multi-speaker voice cloning.
