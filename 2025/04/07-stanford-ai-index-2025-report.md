# Stanford HAI publishes the 2025 AI Index report

- **Date:** 2025-04-07
- **Who:** Stanford Institute for Human-Centered AI (HAI)
- **Type:** research

The eighth edition of the AI Index, Stanford HAI's annual data report, was published on April 7, 2025. It found record business investment and use of AI, reports of productivity gains, and a U.S. lead in top models that China is closing. New sections covered the AI hardware landscape and estimates of inference cost. The report is a common reference for policymakers and researchers.
