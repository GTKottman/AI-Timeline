# Huawei Unveils Atlas 900, Its "Fastest AI Training Cluster"

- **Date:** 2019-09-18
- **Who:** Huawei (Huawei Connect 2019, Shanghai); Peng Cheng Laboratory collaboration mentioned
- **Type:** hardware

Huawei unveiled the Atlas 900 AI training cluster at Huawei Connect 2019 and said it trained ResNet-50 in 59.8 seconds, which it described as 10 seconds faster than the previous record. The cluster is built on thousands of Ascend 910 processors, linked with Huawei's HCCS, PCIe 4.0 and 100G RoCE interconnects, and offered through Huawei Cloud at discounted rates for universities and research institutes. The record figure is Huawei's own claim and was not independently verified in the sources. The event also introduced Huawei's first computing strategy.
