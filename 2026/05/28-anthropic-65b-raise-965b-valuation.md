# Anthropic raises $65 billion at $965 billion valuation

- **Date:** 2026-05-28
- **Who:** Anthropic; Altimeter, Dragoneer, Sequoia
- **Type:** funding

Anthropic raised $65 billion from investors including Altimeter Capital, Dragoneer and Sequoia Capital at a reported $965 billion valuation. It was one of the largest private AI rounds on record.
