# Google launches the Speech Commands dataset for keyword spotting

- **Date:** 2017-08-24
- **Who:** Google (TensorFlow team)
- **Type:** dataset

Google released the Speech Commands dataset, a collection of short recordings of spoken words such as "yes", "no" and "stop" for training and benchmarking keyword spotting models. It gave researchers and hobbyists a common, open benchmark for small-footprint voice command recognition, a task used in smart speakers and devices.
