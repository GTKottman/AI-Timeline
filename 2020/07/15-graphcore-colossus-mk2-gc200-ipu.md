# Graphcore launches second-generation IPU (Colossus Mk2 GC200)

- **Date:** 2020-07-15
- **Who:** Graphcore
- **Type:** hardware

Graphcore unveiled the Colossus Mk2 GC200, a 59.4-billion-transistor AI processor with 1,472 cores and 900MB of in-processor memory, along with the IPU-Machine M2000 system holding four chips in a 1U box. The company claimed up to 9.3x faster BERT-Large training than the first generation and said systems could scale to 64,000 IPUs. It was positioned directly against Nvidia's A100, making it one of the few serious non-GPU challengers for AI training.
