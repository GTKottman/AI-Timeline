# ElevenLabs raises $500M Series D at $11B valuation

- **Date:** 2026-02-05
- **Who:** ElevenLabs; Sequoia (lead), with a16z, ICONIQ, Lightspeed and others
- **Type:** funding

ElevenLabs closed a $500 million Series D led by Sequoia at an $11 billion valuation, more than triple its prior mark within a year. The company reported over $330 million in ARR for 2025 and said the money will go to ElevenAgents, its enterprise voice and conversational-agent platform. Total funding reported at about $781 million. The round marks voice AI as one of the most heavily valued application categories.
