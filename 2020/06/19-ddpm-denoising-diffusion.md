# Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models paper posted

- **Date:** 2020-06-19
- **Who:** UC Berkeley (Ho, Jain, Abbeel)
- **Type:** paper

The arXiv paper "Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models" showed that diffusion models, trained to reverse a gradual noising process, can produce high-fidelity images. It reported a state-of-the-art FID of 3.17 on unconditional CIFAR-10, with 256x256 LSUN samples comparable to ProgressiveGAN. The paper started the diffusion-model wave behind later image generators.
