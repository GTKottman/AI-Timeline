# EU Council gives final approval to the AI Act

- **Date:** 2024-05-21
- **Who:** Council of the European Union
- **Type:** policy

The Council of the EU formally gave its final approval to the AI Act, the last step before publication, following the European Parliament's March vote. Obligations were phased in from 2025, with prohibited-AI rules applying first. Sidley described it as the world's first standalone law governing AI use.
