# Anysphere (Cursor) raises $900 million at $9.9 billion valuation

- **Date:** 2025-06-05
- **Who:** Anysphere (Cursor); Thrive Capital (lead), Andreessen Horowitz, Accel, DST Global
- **Type:** funding

Anysphere, maker of the Cursor AI code editor, raised $900 million at a $9.9 billion valuation, led by Thrive Capital. The company said its annualized revenue had passed $500 million and was doubling about every two months. Bloomberg reported the round on June 5, 2025.
