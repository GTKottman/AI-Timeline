# Z.ai releases GLM-5.2 open-weight model with 1M-token context

- **Date:** 2026-06-13
- **Who:** Z.ai (Zhipu AI)
- **Type:** model

Z.ai released GLM-5.2 under an MIT license, reaching GLM Coding Plan subscribers on June 13, 2026, with Hugging Face weights reported to follow around June 16. Reports cite about 753 billion total parameters with roughly 40 billion active and a 1M-token context, up from 200K in GLM-5.1. Early figures from independent evaluators were 81.0 on Terminal-Bench 2.1 and 62.1 on SWE-bench Pro, and Z.ai published no official benchmark suite at launch. It matters as a large open-weight coding model with a very long context. The exact weight-release date and parameter count differ between sources.
