# Google Assistant gains multilingual mode for two of six languages

- **Date:** 2018-08-30
- **Who:** Google
- **Type:** product

Google launched multilingual support for the Google Assistant. Users who pick two of six supported languages can switch between them mid-query without changing settings. The Assistant identifies the spoken language, runs two monolingual speech recognizers in parallel, and ranks their results. Google planned a third language later. It showed a speech system handling code-switching in production.
