# IBM stops offering general-purpose facial recognition

- **Date:** 2020-06-08
- **Who:** IBM (CEO Arvind Krishna)
- **Type:** policy

In a letter to Congress, IBM said it would no longer offer general-purpose facial recognition software and would not develop or research it further. The letter came amid the protests after George Floyd's death and set out policy proposals on racial equality in law enforcement. It was the farthest-reaching of the June 2020 moves by large tech vendors, which included pauses on police sales by Amazon and Microsoft.
