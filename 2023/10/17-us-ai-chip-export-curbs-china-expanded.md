# US expands AI chip export curbs on China

- **Date:** 2023-10-17
- **Who:** US Department of Commerce, Bureau of Industry and Security; Nvidia
- **Type:** policy

The Bureau of Industry and Security issued rules that expanded licensing requirements for advanced AI chips sent to China. The rules changed the performance thresholds so that Nvidia's China-specific chips, the A800 and H800, were covered, and added due diligence and anti-circumvention measures. BIS also added Chinese firms Biren Technology and Moore Threads to the Entity List, so exports to them require a US license. The rules took effect later in November 2023, according to law-firm memos, and tightened the hardware that Chinese AI labs could legally buy.
