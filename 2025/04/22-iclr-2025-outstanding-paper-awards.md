# ICLR 2025 Outstanding Paper Awards

- **Date:** 2025-04-22
- **Who:** ICLR (International Conference on Learning Representations)
- **Type:** competition

The ICLR 2025 committee named three Outstanding Papers from about 3,700 accepted submissions: "Safety Alignment Should be Made More Than Just a Few Tokens Deep", "Learning Dynamics of LLM Finetuning" and "AlphaEdit: Null-Space Constrained Model Editing for Language Models". Honorable mentions went to papers including "Data Shapley in One Training Run" and "SAM 2". The safety-alignment paper argues that refusal behaviour in aligned models sits in only the first few output tokens.
