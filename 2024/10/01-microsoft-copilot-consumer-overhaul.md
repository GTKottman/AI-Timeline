# Microsoft overhauls consumer Copilot with voice and vision

- **Date:** 2024-10-01
- **Who:** Microsoft
- **Type:** product

Microsoft separated its consumer Copilot from Bing and added Copilot Voice and Copilot Vision, plus Think Deeper. Vision and Think Deeper were tested in Copilot Labs by a limited number of Copilot Pro subscribers, while Copilot Voice was not tied to Pro. Azure also announced GPT-4o Realtime audio, the model behind the voice features.
