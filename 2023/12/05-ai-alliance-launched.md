# AI Alliance launched by IBM and Meta

- **Date:** 2023-12-05
- **Who:** IBM, Meta, with more than 50 founding members including AMD, Intel, Hugging Face, Stability AI, CERN, NASA, Oracle, Cerebras and the Linux Foundation
- **Type:** other

IBM and Meta launched the AI Alliance, an international community of developers, companies and research institutions that aims to advance open, safe and responsible AI. The stated focus is open innovation and open science in AI. It mattered because it grouped major chipmakers and open-model developers against the closed, frontier-lab model of development, and Google, Microsoft and Nvidia were not among the founding members.
