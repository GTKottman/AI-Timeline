# Illinois enacts law banning AI therapy (HB 1806)

- **Date:** 2025-08-01
- **Who:** Illinois Governor JB Pritzker; Illinois Department of Financial and Professional Regulation
- **Type:** policy

Illinois enacted the Wellness and Oversight for Psychological Resources Act (HB 1806), which bars using AI for mental-health and therapeutic decision-making and stops licensed professionals from using AI to make therapy decisions or interact with clients therapeutically. The regulator can fine violators up to $10,000. It mattered as one of the first state laws aimed directly at AI therapy.

Sources differ on the signing date: a law-firm alert says 1 August, the state press release dated 4 August says "Friday".
