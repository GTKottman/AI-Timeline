# Anthropic launches the Anthropic Institute think tank

- **Date:** 2026-03-11
- **Who:** Anthropic; Jack Clark
- **Type:** other

Anthropic launched the Anthropic Institute, a policy and research think tank led by co-founder Jack Clark. It was set up as Anthropic's public-facing body for AI impact research during a period of government conflict.
