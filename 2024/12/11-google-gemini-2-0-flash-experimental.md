# Google announces experimental Gemini 2.0 Flash

- **Date:** 2024-12-11
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** model

Google made an experimental Gemini 2.0 Flash available to developers through the Gemini API, AI Studio and Vertex AI, and rolled it out to Gemini users on desktop and mobile web. Google said it was about twice as fast as Gemini 1.5 Pro while performing better, with multimodal input support. Google also said it would add the model to AI Overviews in Search.
