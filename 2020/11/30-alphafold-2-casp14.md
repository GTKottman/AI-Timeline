# DeepMind's AlphaFold 2 solves protein structure grand challenge at CASP14

- **Date:** 2020-11-30
- **Who:** DeepMind
- **Type:** research

AlphaFold 2 scored far above all other entries in the 14th CASP protein structure prediction competition, with accuracy approaching experimental methods. Researchers called it a solution to the decades-old protein folding problem. The result had major consequences for biology and drug discovery.
