# Stability AI releases open StableLM language models

- **Date:** 2023-04-19
- **Who:** Stability AI
- **Type:** model

Stability AI released the alpha StableLM family, with 3B and 7B base models trained on a new dataset that Stability described as up to 1.5 trillion tokens, released under CC BY-SA 4.0. Instruction-tuned research models were tuned on open datasets including Alpaca, Dolly and ShareGPT, under a non-commercial licence. It mattered as Stability's move from image generation into open text models, joining the wave of openly licensed LLMs.
