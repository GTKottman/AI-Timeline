# Anthropic names Accenture's Faculty as first embedded independent evaluator

- **Date:** 2026-09-18
- **Who:** Anthropic; Accenture (Faculty)
- **Type:** other

Anthropic named Faculty, the AI unit Accenture bought earlier in 2026, as its first embedded evaluator. Faculty will evaluate and red-team models, conduct alignment assessments and test model safeguards.
