# AMD Instinct MI300X and MI300 series AI accelerators

- **Date:** 2023-06-13
- **Who:** AMD
- **Type:** hardware

AMD unveiled the Instinct MI300X, a generative-AI accelerator on CDNA 3 with up to 192 GB of HBM3 memory, and the MI300A, an APU for HPC and AI. It also announced the Instinct Platform (eight MI300X per board) and a PyTorch partnership. AMD positioned the chip as a memory-rich alternative to Nvidia's H100 for running and training large language models.
