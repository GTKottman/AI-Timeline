# Black Forest Labs launches with $31M and the FLUX.1 image models

- **Date:** 2024-08-01
- **Who:** Black Forest Labs; Robin Rombach; Andreas Blattmann; Patrick Esser
- **Type:** model

Black Forest Labs, founded by former Stability AI researchers, announced itself with a $31 million seed round and released the FLUX.1 family of text-to-image models: [pro], [dev] and [schnell] variants, with dev and schnell open. The models were widely praised for text and hand rendering and were later integrated into Grok and Mistral's Le Chat.
