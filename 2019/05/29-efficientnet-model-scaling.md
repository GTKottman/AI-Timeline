# Google introduces EfficientNet with compound model scaling

- **Date:** 2019-05-29
- **Who:** Google AI (Mingxing Tan, Quoc V. Le)
- **Type:** model

Google's EfficientNet family scales depth, width and input resolution together with one compound coefficient, starting from a baseline found by neural architecture search. The largest model, EfficientNet-B7, reached 84.4% top-1 ImageNet accuracy while being about 8.4x smaller than the previous best ConvNet at the time. The paper was posted to arXiv on May 28, 2019 and published at ICML 2019.
