# GLUE natural language understanding benchmark

- **Date:** 2018-04-20
- **Who:** Alex Wang, Amanpreet Singh, Julian Michael, Felix Hill, Omer Levy, Samuel R. Bowman (arXiv:1804.07461)
- **Type:** dataset

GLUE is a multi-task benchmark and analysis platform for natural language understanding. It combines nine sentence and sentence-pair NLU tasks, a diagnostic dataset for specific linguistic phenomena, and an online platform for evaluating models on all nine tasks. The multi-task design rewards sharing knowledge across tasks, which helps tasks with very little training data. It became one of the main leaderboards for general-purpose language models.
