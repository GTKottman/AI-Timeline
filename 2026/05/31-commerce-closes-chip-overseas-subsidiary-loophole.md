# Commerce Closes Loophole Letting Advanced AI Chips Reach Chinese Firms' Overseas Units

- **Date:** 2026-05-31
- **Who:** US Commerce Department; Nvidia (Rubin, Blackwell); AMD (MI350-series); Chinese-headquartered firms with overseas subsidiaries
- **Type:** policy

Weekend Commerce guidance said license requirements apply to advanced AI chips sold to any entity headquartered in China, even when that entity is located outside China. The gap had been open since May 2025, when Commerce said it would not enforce the Biden-era AI Diffusion rule, and Malaysia was cited as the main location of the affected subsidiaries. A former State Department official said a related gap remains for foundry due diligence at TSMC and other foundries, and senators called for Commerce Secretary Lutnick to testify.
