# Microsoft launches $40 million AI for Humanitarian Action program

- **Date:** 2018-09-24
- **Who:** Microsoft (Brad Smith, president)
- **Type:** other

Microsoft committed $40 million over five years to AI for Humanitarian Action, a grant and partnership program with NGOs and humanitarian groups. Its four focus areas were disaster response, children's needs, refugees and displaced people, and human rights. It was modeled on Microsoft's AI for Earth and AI for Accessibility programs and was the third part of its AI for Good effort. It mattered as a large corporate funding commitment steering AI toward humanitarian work.
