# California Governor signs SB 53, the first US frontier AI safety law

- **Date:** 2025-09-29
- **Who:** Governor Gavin Newsom; California legislature
- **Type:** policy

California Governor Gavin Newsom signed SB 53, the first US state law aimed at catastrophic risk from frontier AI.
