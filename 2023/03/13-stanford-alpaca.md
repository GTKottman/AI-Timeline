# Stanford releases Alpaca, a cheap instruction-tuned LLaMA 7B model

- **Date:** 2023-03-13
- **Who:** Stanford CRFM
- **Type:** model

Stanford researchers fine-tuned LLaMA 7B on 52K instruction demonstrations generated with text-davinci-003, for under about $600. It behaved similarly to text-davinci-003 in their preliminary tests and showed that small open models could be tuned cheaply to follow instructions.
