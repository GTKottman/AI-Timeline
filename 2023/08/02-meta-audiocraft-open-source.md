# Meta AudioCraft: open-source MusicGen, AudioGen and EnCodec

- **Date:** 2023-08-02
- **Who:** Meta AI
- **Type:** software

Meta released AudioCraft, a framework bundling MusicGen (text-to-music), AudioGen (text-to-sound effects) and the EnCodec audio codec. The code is released under the MIT license, while the pretrained model weights are under CC-BY-NC 4.0 (non-commercial). Meta said MusicGen was trained on licensed music and AudioGen on public sound effects, and it aimed the release at researchers and practitioners to reproduce and build on.
