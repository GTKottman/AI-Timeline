# Alibaba AI outscores humans on Stanford reading comprehension test

- **Date:** 2018-01-15
- **Who:** Alibaba
- **Type:** research

Alibaba reported an AI model that scored 82.44 on the Stanford Question Answering Dataset (SQuAD), slightly above the human score of 82.304. The model answers questions from Wikipedia passages. It was one of the first claims of superhuman reading comprehension, and Microsoft's system scored higher a day later.
