# Anthropic releases Claude Instant 1.2

- **Date:** 2023-08-09
- **Who:** Anthropic
- **Type:** model

Anthropic released Claude Instant 1.2 to businesses through its API. The model carried over strengths from Claude 2 and scored higher on math, coding and reasoning, including 58.7% on the Codex evaluation (up from 52.8%) and 86.7% on GSM8K (up from 80.9%), with less hallucination and more jailbreak resistance. It was offered alongside Claude 2 as Anthropic's faster, cheaper model.
