# EU reaches provisional agreement on the AI Act

- **Date:** 2023-12-08
- **Who:** European Parliament, Council of the EU, European Commission
- **Type:** policy

Negotiators from the European Parliament, the Council and the Commission reached a provisional political deal on the EU Artificial Intelligence Act after a late trilogue session. The text kept the risk-based approach and added rules for general-purpose and foundation models. Banned practices included social scoring, behavioural manipulation, and emotion recognition in workplaces and educational institutions.
