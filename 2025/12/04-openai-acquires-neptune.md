# OpenAI agrees to acquire Neptune

- **Date:** 2025-12-04
- **Who:** OpenAI; Neptune (Neptune Labs, experiment tracking and training-monitoring tools)
- **Type:** acquisition

OpenAI announced an agreement to acquire Neptune, a startup whose tools let researchers track experiments and monitor model training. The Information reported the all-stock deal at less than $400 million; Neptune's own announcement says financial terms were undisclosed. Neptune is withdrawing its hosted service, with the hosted app and API set to turn off in early March 2026 (sources differ by a day). The acquisition brings model-training observability in-house for OpenAI's research teams.
