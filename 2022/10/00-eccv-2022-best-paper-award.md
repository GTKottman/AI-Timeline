# ECCV 2022 best paper award

- **Date:** 2022-10-00
- **Who:** European Conference on Computer Vision (ECCV) 2022 program committee; winning team from the University of Wisconsin-Madison (X. Zhen, Z. Meng, R. Chakraborty, V. Singh)
- **Type:** other

The ECCV 2022 Best Paper Award went to "On the Versatile Uses of Partial Distance Correlation in Deep Learning." The conference ran October 23 to 27, 2022 in Tel Aviv, and the award was presented in a special session there. The university reported the winning paper was picked from more than 5,000 submissions. Two best paper honorable mentions were also named: "Pose-NDF" and "A Level Set Theory for Neural Implicit Evolution under Explicit Flows."
