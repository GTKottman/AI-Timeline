# Pentagon adopts five AI ethics principles

- **Date:** 2020-02-24
- **Who:** U.S. Department of Defense (DoD), Defense Innovation Board, Defense Secretary Mark Esper, CIO Dana Deasy
- **Type:** policy

The DoD formally adopted five principles for the ethical development and use of AI, including a "responsibility" principle that keeps humans accountable and a "governable" principle that requires the ability to disengage deployed systems. The principles were drawn up over about 15 months by the Defense Innovation Board and cover combat and non-combat uses. Critics said they were non-binding and fell short of stronger restrictions, which made them an early example of how military AI ethics would be debated.
