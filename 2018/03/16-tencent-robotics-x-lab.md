# Tencent launches Robotics X lab

- **Date:** 2018-03-16
- **Who:** Tencent (Tencent AI Lab; Tencent VP Yao Xing)
- **Type:** other

Tencent set up Robotics X, a robotics lab in Shenzhen tied to its AI Lab, around the second annual Tencent AI Lab Forum (Shenzhen, March 15-16, about 400 participants). Yao Xing said the lab would explore the link between virtual and real worlds. The same forum brought a deal with Nature Research to co-organize international AI conferences, set up a medical research team and publish AI-themed special issues. It showed a major Chinese platform company expanding from software AI research into physical robots.
