# ILSVRC 2017 final results announced, last ImageNet challenge

- **Date:** 2017-07-17
- **Who:** ImageNet team (Stanford / UNC / Princeton and others); winners include SENet authors and IC&USYD
- **Type:** competition

The official ILSVRC 2017 results were released on 17 July 2017, and the challenge workshop on 26 July closed out the ImageNet Challenge series. Secondary sources credit the Squeeze-and-Excitation Networks (SENet) model with the classification win, at about 2.25% top-5 error, and IC&USYD with the video detection win (mAP 0.817 on the provided data). It mattered because it marked the end of the benchmark that had driven deep learning vision progress since 2012.
