# Meta releases Segment Anything Model 2 and the SA-V dataset

- **Date:** 2024-07-29
- **Who:** Meta AI
- **Type:** model

Meta released Segment Anything Model 2 (SAM 2), which extends the original SAM from still images to video: it segments any object in an image or video and tracks it consistently across frames in real time. It was released under the Apache 2.0 license, and Meta shipped the SA-V dataset (CC BY 4.0) with about 51,000 videos and more than 600,000 masklets alongside it. Meta reported that its annotation tool with SAM 2 in the loop was about 8.4 times faster than per-frame SAM, a figure taken from Meta's own claims.
