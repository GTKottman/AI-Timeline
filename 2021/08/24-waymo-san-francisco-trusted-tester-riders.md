# Waymo opens San Francisco rider program to trusted testers

- **Date:** 2021-08-24
- **Who:** Waymo (Alphabet)
- **Type:** product

Waymo launched its Waymo One trusted tester program in San Francisco with a blog post on August 24, 2021, starting with a small group of pre-approved riders. Rides were free and a safety operator stayed on board "for now". Riders had to give feedback and sign a non-disclosure agreement. The launch took Waymo's driverless ride service beyond its Phoenix base.
