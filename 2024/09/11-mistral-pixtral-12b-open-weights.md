# Mistral releases Pixtral 12B, an open-weight vision-language model

- **Date:** 2024-09-11
- **Who:** Mistral AI
- **Type:** model

Mistral released Pixtral 12B, a 12-billion-parameter model that takes text and images together, built on its Nemo 12B language model with a vision encoder of roughly 400 million parameters. The weights first went out by a torrent magnet link posted on X on September 11, under the Apache 2.0 license. Mistral's own announcement page is dated September 17, 2024.
