# Meta cuts about 600 roles in Superintelligence Labs

- **Date:** 2025-10-22
- **Who:** Meta, Meta Superintelligence Labs, Alexandr Wang
- **Type:** people

Meta cut about 600 AI roles in its Superintelligence Labs, affecting FAIR and the AI infrastructure and product teams but not the TBD Lab. Chief AI Officer Alexandr Wang's internal memo argued that a smaller team would mean fewer conversations needed to make a decision. Affected staff were encouraged to apply for other roles at Meta, and most were expected to find them. It came months after Meta's large talent-hiring spree for the lab.
