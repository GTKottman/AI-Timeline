# Cerebras Prices and Lists on Nasdaq in Largest US IPO of 2026

- **Date:** 2026-05-14
- **Who:** Cerebras Systems (Nasdaq ticker CBRS)
- **Type:** funding

Cerebras priced its IPO at $185 per share on May 13, above its marketed range, and began trading on Nasdaq on May 14. The company sold 30 million shares for about $5.55 billion, the largest US IPO of 2026 so far, and reported demand more than 20 times the shares offered. Reports put the opening price at $350 (one outlet says $385), with an intraday high near $386 and a close of $311.07, up about 68%. Valuation figures vary by source, so take market capitalization from SEC filings.
