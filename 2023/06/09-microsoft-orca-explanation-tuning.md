# Microsoft Orca: 13B model learns from GPT-4 explanation traces

- **Date:** 2023-06-09
- **Who:** Microsoft Research
- **Type:** research

Microsoft published Orca, a 13-billion-parameter model trained on GPT-4 step-by-step explanation traces rather than only GPT-4's final answers. The authors called this explanation tuning. Coverage reported that Orca beat Vicuna-13B and approached ChatGPT on some benchmarks, which showed that small models could inherit more of a large model's reasoning. The date is the coverage date seen; the arXiv posting day was not checked.
