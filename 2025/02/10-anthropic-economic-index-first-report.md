# Anthropic publishes the first Anthropic Economic Index report

- **Date:** 2025-02-10
- **Who:** Anthropic
- **Type:** research

Anthropic published its first Anthropic Economic Index, a study of how people use Claude across occupations and tasks. It maps Claude conversations to tasks in the U.S. Department of Labor's O*NET occupational taxonomy and separates automation from augmentation. A companion dataset was released on Hugging Face as "Data release 1". The method became the basis for later Economic Index reports.
