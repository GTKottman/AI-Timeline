# Inflection AI launches with $225M

- **Date:** 2022-05-13
- **Who:** Inflection AI (Reid Hoffman, Mustafa Suleyman, Karen Simonyan)
- **Type:** funding

Inflection AI, a Palo Alto public benefit corporation founded in 2022 by Reid Hoffman, Mustafa Suleyman and Karen Simonyan, disclosed a $225 million equity raise in an SEC filing. Investors were not named at the time. The company was set up to build consumer-facing conversational AI, which it later launched as the Pi chatbot.
