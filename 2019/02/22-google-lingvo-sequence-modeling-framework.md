# Google open-sources Lingvo, a TensorFlow framework for sequence modeling

- **Date:** 2019-02-22
- **Who:** Google Brain (Jonathan Shen and co-authors)
- **Type:** software

Google released Lingvo, an open-source TensorFlow framework for sequence-to-sequence models used in machine translation, speech recognition and speech synthesis. It supports architectures from RNN sequence models to Transformers and was described as already used across more than 20 research papers over two years. The accompanying arXiv paper presented it as a modular, scalable research tool for language and speech work.
