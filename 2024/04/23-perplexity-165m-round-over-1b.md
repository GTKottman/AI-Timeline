# Perplexity raises $165M and is valued above $1 billion

- **Date:** 2024-04-23
- **Who:** Perplexity AI
- **Type:** funding

Perplexity closed a $165 million round that took its valuation above $1 billion, announced alongside its mobile app. It made Perplexity one of the fastest-growing AI startups of 2024 and a clear rival to search incumbents.
