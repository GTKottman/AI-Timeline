# Ziff Davis sues OpenAI over training and attribution

- **Date:** 2025-04-25
- **Who:** Ziff Davis (IGN, CNET, Eurogamer, others); OpenAI
- **Type:** policy

Ziff Davis, owner of IGN, CNET and other publishers, sued OpenAI in Delaware federal court for copyright infringement, DMCA violations, unjust enrichment and trademark dilution. It alleges OpenAI copied its articles and trained on them despite robots.txt opt-outs, stripped copyright management information, and attributed answers to Ziff Davis brands. It seeks damages of at least hundreds of millions of dollars and destruction of models trained on its content; OpenAI said its use is fair use.
