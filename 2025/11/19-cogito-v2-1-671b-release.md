# Deep Cogito releases Cogito v2.1 671B

- **Date:** 2025-11-19
- **Who:** Deep Cogito
- **Type:** model

Deep Cogito released Cogito v2.1, an open reasoning model with 671B parameters and 128K context. It was one of the larger open-weight reasoning releases of late 2025.
