# Google adapts deep convolutional networks to find protein crystals for drug design

- **Date:** 2018-07-12
- **Who:** Google Research, working with the MARCO initiative
- **Type:** research

Google researchers adapted deep convolutional networks to recognize protein crystallization outcomes in lab images. Finding usable crystals still depended on people reviewing images by hand, which could miss crystals. The model reached over 94% accuracy, and crystal structures are needed to design targeted drugs.
