# SoftBank plans 10 GW AI data center in Ohio

- **Date:** 2026-03-20
- **Who:** SoftBank
- **Type:** other

SoftBank is seeking to build an AI computing complex capable of drawing 10 gigawatts at a former US Energy Department uranium enrichment site in Ohio. Its reported power plan is about $33 billion of gas-fired generation to be installed by the end of the decade. It is a plan under pursuit, not a finalized build, but it signals the scale of AI infrastructure now being sited around new power supply.
