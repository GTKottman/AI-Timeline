# Google hires Character.AI co-founder Noam Shazeer in licensing deal

- **Date:** 2024-08-02
- **Who:** Google; Character.AI; Noam Shazeer
- **Type:** acquisition

Google rehired Noam Shazeer, co-author of the Transformer paper and co-founder of Character.AI, and signed a non-exclusive licensing agreement for Character.AI's technology. It was one of several "acqui-hire" style deals in which big tech firms took AI talent and models without buying the company.
