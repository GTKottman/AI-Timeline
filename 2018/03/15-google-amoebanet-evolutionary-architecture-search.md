# Google Brain uses evolution to discover AmoebaNet image classifiers

- **Date:** 2018-03-15
- **Who:** Google Brain
- **Type:** research

Google Brain used evolutionary algorithms to evolve image classifier architectures, starting from simple seed models and then from a cell-based structure designed by experts. The resulting AmoebaNet models reached state-of-the-art accuracy, though the search needed hundreds of GPUs or TPUs running for days. The post reported that evolution beat reinforcement learning in the early stages of the search.
