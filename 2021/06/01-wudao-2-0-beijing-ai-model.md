# Beijing Academy of AI announces Wu Dao 2.0

- **Date:** 2021-06-01
- **Who:** Beijing Academy of Artificial Intelligence (BAAI)
- **Type:** model

BAAI announced Wu Dao 2.0, a multimodal model with about 1.75 trillion parameters, roughly ten times GPT-3, using a mixture-of-experts design. It was presented as China's largest pretrained model at the time, and it was a state-backed answer to Western large language models.
