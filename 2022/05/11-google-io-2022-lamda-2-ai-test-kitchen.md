# Google I/O 2022: LaMDA 2 and AI Test Kitchen

- **Date:** 2022-05-11
- **Who:** Google (Sundar Pichai)
- **Type:** product

At Google I/O on May 11, 2022, Sundar Pichai introduced LaMDA 2 as Google's most advanced conversational AI and opened AI Test Kitchen, a demo app where a small group could try it. Pichai stressed the early nature of the demos, including the possibility of offensive text being generated. Participants could rate responses and give feedback.
