# OpenAI launches Study Mode in ChatGPT

- **Date:** 2025-07-29
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** product

OpenAI launched Study Mode in ChatGPT, which walks students through problems step by step with questions and hints rather than giving direct answers. It was available to logged-in users on Free, Plus, Pro and Team plans, with ChatGPT Edu access due in the following weeks. TechCrunch reported that with the mode enabled, ChatGPT tests understanding and may withhold direct answers until the student engages.
