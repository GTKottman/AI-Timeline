# Google DreamFusion: text-to-3D from a 2D diffusion model

- **Date:** 2022-09-29
- **Who:** Google Research and UC Berkeley
- **Type:** paper

DreamFusion creates 3D models from text by optimizing a NeRF until its renders look plausible to a frozen 2D text-to-image model. It needed no 3D training data, which opened a path to 3D generation from the image models already available.
