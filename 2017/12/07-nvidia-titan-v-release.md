# NVIDIA releases Titan V with Volta GPU

- **Date:** 2017-12-07
- **Who:** NVIDIA
- **Type:** hardware

NVIDIA unveiled the Titan V at the NIPS conference, a desktop card built on the Volta GV100 chip with 5,120 CUDA cores, 640 Tensor Cores and 12 GB of HBM2 memory. It sold for $2,999, and NVIDIA claimed 110 teraflops for AI work. It put Tensor Core mixed-precision training within reach of individual researchers.
