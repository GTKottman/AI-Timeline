# China's Cyberspace Administration releases draft algorithmic recommendation rules

- **Date:** 2021-08-27
- **Who:** Cyberspace Administration of China (CAC)
- **Type:** policy

The CAC published the draft "Internet Information Service Algorithmic Recommendation Management Provisions" for public comment, with comments accepted until September 26. It would require recommendation providers to let users switch off personalized recommendations, disclose how their algorithms work, avoid designs that induce addiction or excessive spending, and submit algorithms that influence public opinion for approval. Analysts called it a first-of-its-kind rulebook aimed specifically at recommender systems. Named affected firms included ByteDance, Tencent and Kuaishou.
