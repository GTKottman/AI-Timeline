# Seoul AI Summit ends with the Seoul Declaration on AI safety

- **Date:** 2024-05-21
- **Who:** Governments of the EU, US, UK, Korea and others
- **Type:** policy

Leaders from the EU, the US, the UK, Japan, Korea, Australia, Canada, France, Germany, Italy, Singapore and others signed the Seoul Declaration for Safe, Innovative and Inclusive AI, following the 2023 Bletchley summit. Ministers adopted a separate ministerial statement on May 22. The signatories agreed to set up an international network of AI Safety Institutes.
