# Anthropic accuses DeepSeek, Moonshot and MiniMax of distillation attacks

- **Date:** 2026-02-23
- **Who:** Anthropic; DeepSeek; Moonshot AI; MiniMax
- **Type:** research

Anthropic said three Chinese AI labs, DeepSeek, Moonshot AI and MiniMax, ran distillation attacks on Claude through about 24,000 fraudulent accounts. The labs produced more than 16 million exchanges with Claude, including over 150,000 from DeepSeek, over 3.4 million from Moonshot and over 13 million from MiniMax.
