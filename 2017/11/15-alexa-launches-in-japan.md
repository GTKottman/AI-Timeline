# Amazon Alexa launches in Japan

- **Date:** 2017-11-15
- **Who:** Amazon
- **Type:** product

Amazon launched Alexa in Japan with Japanese-language support, one of the first major expansions of its voice assistant beyond English. Alexa then expanded to Canada and Belgium in December 2017 and to Ireland and Australia in early 2018.
