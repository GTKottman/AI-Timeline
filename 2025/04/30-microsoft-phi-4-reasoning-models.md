# Microsoft releases Phi-4-reasoning, Phi-4-reasoning-plus and Phi-4-mini-reasoning

- **Date:** 2025-04-30
- **Who:** Microsoft
- **Type:** model

Microsoft released a family of small reasoning models. Phi-4-reasoning and Phi-4-reasoning-plus are 14-billion-parameter dense models built on Phi-4. The plus version is further tuned with reinforcement learning and produces about 50% more tokens. Phi-4-mini-reasoning is the smallest of the three. The Azure AI model card gives April 30, 2025 as the release date and an MIT licence, while Gigazine dated its coverage May 1.
