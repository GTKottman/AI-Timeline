# NVIDIA presents Xavier SoC as DRIVE PX Xavier at CES 2018

- **Date:** 2018-01-08
- **Who:** NVIDIA
- **Type:** hardware

At CES 2018 NVIDIA re-presented its Xavier system-on-chip as DRIVE PX Xavier, an AI computer for self-driving cars. It was designed to handle perception and driving decisions on board. Xavier was presented as a successor to the DRIVE PX 2 platform.
