# Google publishes Meena, a large open-domain chatbot

- **Date:** 2020-01-27
- **Who:** Google Research Brain team (Daniel Adiwardana, Minh-Thang Luong, Quoc V. Le, et al.)
- **Type:** model

The Meena paper, "Towards a Human-like Open-Domain Chatbot," described a 2.6-billion-parameter sequence-to-sequence model trained on public social media conversations. It introduced the Sensibleness and Specificity Average metric for judging chatbot replies. It showed that a larger neural chatbot could hold more coherent conversations than prior open-domain systems.
