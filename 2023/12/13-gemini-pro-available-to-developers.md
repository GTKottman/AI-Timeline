# Google makes Gemini Pro available to developers and Cloud customers

- **Date:** 2023-12-13
- **Who:** Google; Google DeepMind
- **Type:** product

Gemini 1.0 Pro became available to developers and enterprise customers through Google AI Studio and Vertex AI, a week after the Gemini family was announced. It was free to use within rate limits through the Gemini API. It opened Google's new flagship model to outside builders for the first time.
