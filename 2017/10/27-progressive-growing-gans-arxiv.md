# NVIDIA posts Progressive Growing of GANs paper

- **Date:** 2017-10-27
- **Who:** NVIDIA (Tero Karras, Timo Aila, Samuli Laine, Jaakko Lehtinen)
- **Type:** paper

The paper introduced training GANs by starting at low resolution and adding layers progressively to model finer detail. The authors reported faster, more stable training and a record unsupervised CIFAR-10 Inception score of 8.80, and the method produced high-resolution face images far beyond earlier GAN output. It was later published at ICLR 2018 and became a widely used GAN training technique.
