# Artists file class action against Stability AI, Midjourney and DeviantArt

- **Date:** 2023-01-13
- **Who:** Sarah Andersen, Kelly McKernan, Karla Ortiz (plaintiffs)
- **Type:** policy

Three artists filed a federal class action in San Francisco against Stability AI, Midjourney and DeviantArt, arguing image generators trained on copyrighted art make derivative works. It was among the first major US copyright suits over generative image training.
