# Cognition agrees to acquire Windsurf after Google's $2.4 billion licensing deal

- **Date:** 2025-07-14
- **Who:** Cognition (Devin); Windsurf; Google
- **Type:** acquisition

Cognition announced a definitive agreement to buy Windsurf, the AI coding tool, days after Google paid about $2.4 billion to license Windsurf's technology and hire its CEO Varun Mohan and co-founder Douglas Chen, plus research staff. The Google deal was a non-exclusive licence rather than an acquisition, and it followed OpenAI's roughly $3 billion bid that had stalled. The Cognition deal covered Windsurf's IP, product, brand and over 350 enterprise customers, and Cognition said all Windsurf employees would share in it. The purchase price was not disclosed, and sources disagree on whether the deal had closed.
