# Google signs Kairos Power deal for 500 MW of advanced nuclear power

- **Date:** 2024-10-14
- **Who:** Google, Kairos Power
- **Type:** funding

Google and Kairos Power agreed to deploy a US fleet of advanced small modular reactors totaling 500 MW by 2035, with the first plant targeted for 2030. Kairos would build, own and operate the reactors and sell the power, grid services and environmental benefits to Google under power purchase agreements. Google cited rising AI-driven electricity demand and the need for round-the-clock carbon-free power as the reason for the deal. No locations or total cost were given at announcement.
