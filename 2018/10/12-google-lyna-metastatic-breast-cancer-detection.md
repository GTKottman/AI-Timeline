# Google's LYNA deep learning tool detects breast cancer spread in lymph nodes

- **Date:** 2018-10-12
- **Who:** Google AI (pathology research team)
- **Type:** research

Google AI presented LYNA, a deep learning tool that detects breast cancer that has spread to lymph nodes. The post reported that it correctly distinguished slides with metastatic cancer from slides without it 99% of the time. In a simulated diagnostic task with six board-certified pathologists, LYNA helped them detect small metastases more reliably and halved average slide review time (about one minute per slide instead of two). It showed a medical imaging model helping human experts directly.
