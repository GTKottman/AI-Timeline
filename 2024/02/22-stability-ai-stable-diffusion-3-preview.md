# Stability AI previews Stable Diffusion 3

- **Date:** 2024-02-22
- **Who:** Stability AI
- **Type:** model

Stability AI announced an early preview of Stable Diffusion 3, a text-to-image model using a diffusion transformer architecture, aimed at multi-subject prompts, image quality and spelling accuracy. Access was limited to a waitlist for researchers to test performance and safety before public release. It mattered because it marked Stability's move from U-Net diffusion models to a transformer architecture for image generation.
