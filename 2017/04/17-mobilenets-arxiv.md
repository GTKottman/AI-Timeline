# MobileNets: efficient CNNs for mobile vision

- **Date:** 2017-04-17
- **Who:** Andrew G. Howard, Menglong Zhu, Bo Chen, Dmitry Kalenichenko, Weijun Wang, Tobias Weyand, Marco Andreetto, Hartwig Adam (Google)
- **Type:** paper

arXiv paper introducing MobileNets, lightweight convolutional networks built on depthwise separable convolutions. Two global hyperparameters let a builder trade latency against accuracy to fit a resource budget. The paper reported strong ImageNet results against other popular models of the time and showed use in detection, fine-grained classification, face attributes and geo-localization.
