# OpenAI launches GPT-4o mini

- **Date:** 2024-07-18
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** model

OpenAI released GPT-4o mini as its most cost-efficient small model, replacing GPT-3.5 in ChatGPT, where it became the default for free users. The API price was 15 cents per million input tokens and 60 cents per million output tokens, with a 128K context window and 82% reported on MMLU. Text and vision were available at launch, with audio and video planned later. It cut the cost of capable AI sharply for developers building at scale.
