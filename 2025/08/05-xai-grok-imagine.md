# xAI launches Grok Imagine image generator with a "spicy mode"

- **Date:** 2025-08-05
- **Who:** xAI
- **Type:** product

xAI launched Grok Imagine, an image generator in Grok that included a "spicy mode" for sexualized output. The launch drew criticism over content safety and was part of xAI's push to offer generative media in its app.
