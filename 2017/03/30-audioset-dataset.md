# AudioSet: 2 million human-labelled YouTube sound clips released

- **Date:** 2017-03-30
- **Who:** Google Research (Jort F. Gemmeke, Daniel P. W. Ellis and team)
- **Type:** dataset

Google announced AudioSet on March 30, 2017: an ontology of 632 audio event classes and about 2.08 million human-labelled 10-second clips from YouTube. Segments were nominated from YouTube metadata and content search, then checked by human raters. The release included clip URLs, the labels judged present and precomputed audio features.
