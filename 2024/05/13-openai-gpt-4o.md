# OpenAI introduces GPT-4o

- **Date:** 2024-05-13
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** model

GPT-4o is a single model that reasons across audio, vision and text in real time, with much faster voice response than earlier ChatGPT voice modes. It was offered to free and paid users. It pushed multimodal, low-latency assistants into everyday use.
