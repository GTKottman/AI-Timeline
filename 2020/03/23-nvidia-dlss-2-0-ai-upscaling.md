# Nvidia announces DLSS 2.0 AI upscaling

- **Date:** 2020-03-23
- **Who:** Nvidia
- **Type:** software

Nvidia released DLSS 2.0, an update to its neural-network image reconstruction for RTX GPUs. It dropped the per-game training of the first version in favour of one generalized network, added motion vectors and combined the output with temporal anti-aliasing. The new model reportedly ran about twice as fast, which showed that neural networks could be used in real time inside graphics pipelines.
