# Google's QT-Opt reaches 96% grasp success with distributed deep Q-learning

- **Date:** 2018-06-28
- **Who:** Google Brain Robotics
- **Type:** research

Google introduced QT-Opt, a distributed deep Q-learning method for continuous action spaces with large-scale distributed optimization. It was trained offline on grasping data from seven robots and then fine-tuned on a real robot. The system reached a 96% grasp success rate on objects it had not seen, against 78% for the team's earlier supervised approach. It showed deep RL working at robot-fleet scale.
