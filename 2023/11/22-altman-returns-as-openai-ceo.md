# Sam Altman reinstated as OpenAI CEO with new board

- **Date:** 2023-11-22
- **Who:** OpenAI (Sam Altman, Greg Brockman, Bret Taylor, Larry Summers, Adam D'Angelo); Microsoft (Satya Nadella)
- **Type:** people

OpenAI agreed in principle to bring Sam Altman back as CEO, with a new initial board of Bret Taylor as chair, Larry Summers and Adam D'Angelo. Greg Brockman returned as president. Microsoft, OpenAI's largest investor, was given a non-voting board observer seat, and Nadella called the board changes a first step toward stable governance. The reinstatement came about five days after the firing, after pressure from staff who threatened to follow Altman to Microsoft.
