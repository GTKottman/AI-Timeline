# OpenAI to acquire Astral, maker of uv, Ruff and ty

- **Date:** 2026-03-19
- **Who:** OpenAI, Astral
- **Type:** acquisition

OpenAI announced it would acquire Astral, the company behind the open-source Python tools uv, Ruff and ty, and bring the team into its Codex division. The deal was not closed at announcement and needed regulatory approval; terms were undisclosed. It matters because core Python developer tooling is moving under a coding-agent vendor, which raised open-source concerns that OpenAI answered with a pledge to keep supporting Astral's open-source products.
