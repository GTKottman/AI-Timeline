# Nvidia shows Omniverse Avatar and Replicator at GTC Fall 2021

- **Date:** 2021-11-00
- **Who:** NVIDIA; Omniverse, Maxine SDK
- **Type:** product

At NVIDIA's GTC Fall 2021 keynote, the company introduced Omniverse Avatar and Omniverse Replicator, along with Project Tokkio, Maxine and DRIVE Concierge demos. A digital twin of Jensen Huang answered questions in the keynote, powered by the Maxine AI SDK. Omniverse Avatar aimed at interactive AI characters, and Replicator at generating synthetic training data for robotics and AI. The exact keynote day was not confirmed.
