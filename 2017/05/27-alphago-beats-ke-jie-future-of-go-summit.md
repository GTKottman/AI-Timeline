# AlphaGo beats Ke Jie 3-0 at Future of Go Summit

- **Date:** 2017-05-27
- **Who:** DeepMind (Google), Ke Jie
- **Type:** competition

AlphaGo Master won all three games against Ke Jie, then the world No. 1 Go player, at the Future of Go Summit in Wuzhen, China, on May 23, 25 and 27, 2017. The series was a public milestone for superhuman play in Go. After the third game DeepMind announced that AlphaGo would retire from competitive play.
