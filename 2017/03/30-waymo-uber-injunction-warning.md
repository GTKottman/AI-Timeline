# Judge warns Uber it may face injunction in Waymo trade-secret case

- **Date:** 2017-03-30
- **Who:** Waymo (Alphabet), Uber, Judge William Alsup, Anthony Levandowski
- **Type:** other

On March 30, 2017 Judge William Alsup said he might grant Waymo an injunction against Uber if Uber's key executive Anthony Levandowski refused to testify, after Levandowski's lawyers said he would invoke the Fifth Amendment. Waymo alleged that Levandowski downloaded more than 14,000 files before leaving to found Otto, which Uber later bought. The injunction hearing was set for May 3, 2017.
