# Nvidia releases Maxine, Jarvis and TAO AI tools at GTC 2021

- **Date:** 2021-04-12
- **Who:** NVIDIA
- **Type:** software

At its April 2021 GTC keynote Nvidia made several AI developer tools available. Maxine offered SDKs for video conferencing and streaming effects, including noise removal and virtual backgrounds. Jarvis provided a framework for conversational AI services, with speech recognition, translation and text-to-speech; Nvidia cited real-time translation for five languages. TAO was a GUI-driven workflow framework for building enterprise AI. Nvidia's performance claims were its own.
