# Google launches Gemini CLI, an open-source terminal AI agent

- **Date:** 2025-06-25
- **Who:** Google
- **Type:** software

Google released Gemini CLI, an open-source (Apache 2.0) AI agent that runs Gemini in the terminal for coding and other tasks. Signing in with a personal Google account gave free access to Gemini 2.5 Pro, with a preview allowance of 60 requests per minute and 1,000 per day.
