# Meta unveils Orion AR glasses prototype at Meta Connect

- **Date:** 2024-09-25
- **Who:** Meta
- **Type:** hardware

At Meta Connect on September 25, 2024, Meta showed Orion, a working prototype of holographic AR glasses that project images onto the lenses and take input from voice, eye tracking, hand tracking and an EMG wristband. Meta said Orion is a prototype not for sale, to be used internally and by a limited set of employees and outside testers. Meta has given no public release date. The reveal showed the on-device AI and sensing stack Meta wants for future wearables.
