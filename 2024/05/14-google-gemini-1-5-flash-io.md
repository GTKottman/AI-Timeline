# Google announces Gemini 1.5 Flash at I/O 2024

- **Date:** 2024-05-14
- **Who:** Google
- **Type:** model

At Google I/O 2024, Google introduced Gemini 1.5 Flash, a lighter-weight model designed to be fast and efficient to serve at scale. Google called it the fastest Gemini model served in the API. It widened the Gemini range from large to low-latency tiers.
