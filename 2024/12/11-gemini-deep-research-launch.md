# Google launches Deep Research in Gemini Advanced

- **Date:** 2024-12-11
- **Who:** Google
- **Type:** product

Google launched Deep Research in the Gemini app for Gemini Advanced subscribers, starting in English on desktop and mobile web. The agent drafts a multi-step research plan that the user can approve or revise, searches the web repeatedly, and writes a report with sources that can be exported to Google Docs. It ran on the Gemini 1.5 Pro model, and the mobile app and Workspace accounts were slated for early 2025.
