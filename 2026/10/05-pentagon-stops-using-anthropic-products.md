# US Department of Defense says it has stopped using Anthropic products

- **Date:** 2026-10-05
- **Who:** US Department of Defense; Anthropic; Palantir
- **Type:** policy

The Pentagon said it had stopped using Anthropic products, including in Palantir's Maven Smart System. It concluded the dispute that began with Hegseth's February demands.
