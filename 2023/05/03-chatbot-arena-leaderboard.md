# LMSYS launches Chatbot Arena leaderboard

- **Date:** 2023-05-03
- **Who:** LMSYS (UC Berkeley, CMU, UCSD)
- **Type:** competition

LMSYS launched Chatbot Arena, where visitors send one prompt to two anonymous models, vote on the better answer, and the votes feed an Elo-style leaderboard. Its first results, covering April 24 to May 1, 2023 and about 4.7k votes, ranked vicuna-13b first at 1169 Elo and koala-13b second at 1082. It mattered as a human-preference benchmark that became a widely cited reference for chatbot quality.
