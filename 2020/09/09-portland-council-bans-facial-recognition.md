# Portland City Council bans facial recognition by city bureaus and private businesses

- **Date:** 2020-09-09
- **Who:** Portland, Oregon City Council (Commissioner Jo Ann Hardesty, Mayor Ted Wheeler)
- **Type:** policy

On September 9, 2020 the Portland City Council unanimously passed two ordinances. One bars city bureaus from using facial recognition technology, effective immediately. The other bans private businesses from using it, effective January 1, 2021. The ordinances cite documented bias against Black people, women and older people. Coverage described it as the first such private-sector ban by a US city, and it added to the growing city-level restrictions on facial recognition.
