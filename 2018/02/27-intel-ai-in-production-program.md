# Intel launches "AI: In Production" program

- **Date:** 2018-02-27
- **Who:** Intel (launch partner AAEON; early customer CONEX)
- **Type:** product

Intel introduced the "AI: In Production" program to help developers move AI prototypes into commercial devices. It was built around the Movidius Myriad 2 VPU, with AAEON as the first partner. Its aim was to lower the cost and difficulty small companies face when scaling AI prototypes into products.
