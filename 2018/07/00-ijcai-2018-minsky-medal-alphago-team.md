# IJCAI 2018 gives inaugural Marvin Minsky Medal to the AlphaGo team

- **Date:** 2018-07-00
- **Who:** IJCAI; DeepMind AlphaGo team (including David Silver)
- **Type:** other

At the IJCAI-ECAI 2018 opening keynote in Stockholm (conference July 13-19, 2018), IJCAI gave the inaugural Marvin Minsky Medal for outstanding achievements in AI to the DeepMind team behind AlphaGo. The same keynote named seven Distinguished Papers, replacing the old "Best Papers" title. The exact day of the announcement is not confirmed by the sources; a Silicon Republic article from October 2017 reported the medal ahead of the conference. It mattered because a major AI honour went to a game-playing research team.
