# Hassabis proposes an independent frontier AI standards body

- **Date:** 2026-07-14
- **Who:** Demis Hassabis, Google DeepMind
- **Type:** policy

On July 14, 2026 Demis Hassabis published the essay "A Framework for Frontier AI and the Dawning of a New Age", proposing an industry-funded, federally overseen standards body modelled on FINRA. Frontier labs would submit their most advanced models for evaluation on cyber, biological, deception and agentic risks up to 30 days before release, voluntarily at first and mandatory only once the tests prove reliable. Coverage differs on the body's funding and role for national labs. Endorsements reported by one secondary source are unverified.
