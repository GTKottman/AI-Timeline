# Pentagon Defense Innovation Board issues five ethical principles for AI

- **Date:** 2019-10-31
- **Who:** Defense Innovation Board (US Department of Defense advisory board)
- **Type:** policy

After about 15 months of deliberation, the Defense Innovation Board recommended five ethical principles for the Defense Department's use of AI: responsible, equitable, traceable, reliable and governable. It applies to both combat and noncombat systems and puts accountability on the humans who build and deploy them. The board also listed a dozen technical and organizational measures to support the principles. The recommendations were advisory and not automatically adopted by the Pentagon.
