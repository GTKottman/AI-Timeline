# Mistral AI and Nvidia release Mistral NeMo 12B

- **Date:** 2024-07-18
- **Who:** Mistral AI; Nvidia
- **Type:** model

Mistral AI and Nvidia released Mistral NeMo, a 12-billion-parameter model with a 128k context window, under the Apache 2.0 license, with base and instruct checkpoints. It was trained with quantisation awareness so it runs in FP8 with little loss, and it was packaged as an Nvidia NIM microservice for workstation GPUs. Mistral's own benchmarks put it ahead of Gemma 2 9B and Llama 3 8B, a claim that was not independently verified in the sources.
