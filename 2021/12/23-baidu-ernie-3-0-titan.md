# Baidu posts ERNIE 3.0 Titan, a 260-billion-parameter Chinese model

- **Date:** 2021-12-23
- **Who:** Baidu
- **Type:** model

Baidu posted the ERNIE 3.0 Titan paper to arXiv on December 23, 2021. The model has over 260 billion parameters and is described as the largest Chinese dense pretrained model to date, with a knowledge-enhanced pretraining setup and an online distillation framework. The authors reported state-of-the-art results on 68 NLP datasets. Import AI noted that training used both Nvidia V100 GPUs and Huawei Ascend 910 chips.
