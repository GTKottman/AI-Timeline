# Megatron-Turing NLG 530B training paper

- **Date:** 2022-01-28
- **Who:** Microsoft and NVIDIA (Shaden Smith et al.)
- **Type:** paper

Microsoft and NVIDIA submitted a paper to arXiv on 28 January 2022 (arXiv 2201.11990) describing how they trained MT-NLG, a 530-billion-parameter generative language model, using DeepSpeed and Megatron. It covers the 3D parallelism approach and the infrastructure used. The write-up gave other labs a concrete recipe for training models at this scale.
