# Long Range Arena benchmark for efficient transformers

- **Date:** 2020-11-08
- **Who:** Google Research (Tay, Dehghani and collaborators)
- **Type:** dataset

The arXiv paper "Long Range Arena: A Benchmark for Efficient Transformers" introduced a unified benchmark of tasks with 1K to 16K token sequences across text, images and mathematical expressions. It was used to compare ten efficient Transformer variants under one protocol. The benchmark gave the efficient-attention field a common yardstick for long-sequence models.
