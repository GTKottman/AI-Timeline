# Vector Institute opens in Toronto

- **Date:** 2017-03-30
- **Who:** Vector Institute; Geoffrey Hinton (chief scientific advisor); Government of Canada and Ontario; corporate donors
- **Type:** other

The Vector Institute, an independent non-profit AI research institute, opened at the MaRS Discovery District in Toronto on March 30, 2017. Geoffrey Hinton was named chief scientific advisor, and Finance Minister Bill Morneau, Ontario Premier Kathleen Wynne and Toronto Mayor John Tory attended the launch. It was backed by federal and provincial governments and corporate donors.
