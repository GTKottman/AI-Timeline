# OpenAI acquires Jony Ive's io hardware startup for about $6.5 billion

- **Date:** 2025-05-21
- **Who:** OpenAI; io (Jony Ive, Tang Tan, Evans Hankey)
- **Type:** acquisition

OpenAI agreed to buy io, the AI hardware startup co-founded by former Apple designer Jony Ive, in a deal valued at about $6.5 billion. OpenAI already held a 23% stake from an earlier arrangement. Reports say about 55 engineers, designers and researchers would join OpenAI, with Peter Welinder leading the new io division. The deal was expected to close in the summer, and the first device was expected in 2026.
