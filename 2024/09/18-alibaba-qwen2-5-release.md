# Alibaba releases Qwen2.5 model family

- **Date:** 2024-09-18
- **Who:** Alibaba Cloud (Qwen team)
- **Type:** model

Qwen2.5 expanded the Qwen family with many model sizes and specialised variants, trained on a larger dataset than Qwen2. It became one of the most-downloaded open model families of late 2024.
