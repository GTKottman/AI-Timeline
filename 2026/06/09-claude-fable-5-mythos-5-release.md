# Anthropic releases Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5

- **Date:** 2026-06-09
- **Who:** Anthropic
- **Type:** model

Anthropic announced Claude Fable 5, a public Mythos-tier model with safeguards on high-risk areas such as cybersecurity and biology, and Mythos 5, a less restricted model available through a trusted-access programme. It was the first public model in the Mythos tier.
