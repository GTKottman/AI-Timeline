# Google launches Nano Banana Pro (Gemini 3 Pro Image)

- **Date:** 2025-11-20
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** model

Google released Nano Banana Pro, its Gemini 3 Pro-based image model, with 2K and 4K output, multi-image composition and character consistency across several people. It was released as a preview and set a new bar for AI image editing with accurate text rendering.
