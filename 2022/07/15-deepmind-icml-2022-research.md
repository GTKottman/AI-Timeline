# DeepMind presents 30 papers at ICML 2022

- **Date:** 2022-07-15
- **Who:** DeepMind
- **Type:** research

DeepMind published a summary of its work at ICML 2022 in Baltimore, where its teams presented 30 papers, 17 with external collaborators. Its oral presentations focused on reinforcement learning, including improved policy composition and an exploration method that avoids reward bonuses. The post is a snapshot of the lab's research direction going into late 2022.
