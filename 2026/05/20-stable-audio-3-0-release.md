# Stability AI releases Stable Audio 3.0 music and sound models

- **Date:** 2026-05-20
- **Who:** Stability AI
- **Type:** model

Stable Audio 3.0 is a family of four audio models: Small SFX and Small (459M parameters each, up to about two minutes), Medium (1.4B, tracks up to 6:20) and Large (2.7B). Small SFX is aimed at sound effects, and Small at music on consumer hardware. Small SFX, Small and Medium are open-weight on Hugging Face; Large is available only through the Stability AI API, through partner fal.ai, or under enterprise licensing. Stability says all models were trained on licensed data.
