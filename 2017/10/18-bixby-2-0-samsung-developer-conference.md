# Samsung unveils Bixby 2.0 at its developer conference

- **Date:** 2017-10-18
- **Who:** Samsung Electronics
- **Type:** product

Samsung presented Bixby 2.0 at its developer conference in San Francisco, opening the assistant to third-party developers through a Bixby SDK. It was meant to let apps and devices plug into Samsung's assistant, competing with the Google and Amazon developer platforms.
