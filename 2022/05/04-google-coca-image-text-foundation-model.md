# Google CoCa: Contrastive Captioners are Image-Text Foundation Models

- **Date:** 2022-05-04
- **Who:** Google Research (Jiahui Yu, Zirui Wang, Vijay Vasudevan, Legg Yeung, Mojtaba Seyedhosseini, Yonghui Wu)
- **Type:** paper

Google posted CoCa to arXiv on May 4, 2022 (arXiv 2205.01917). It is a single image-text model trained with both a contrastive loss and a captioning loss, so one model serves classification, retrieval, VQA and captioning. The paper reports 86.3% zero-shot top-1 accuracy on ImageNet, and it was later published in Transactions on Machine Learning Research.
