# EU Council and Parliament reach provisional agreement to simplify AI Act

- **Date:** 2026-05-07
- **Who:** Council of the EU; European Parliament
- **Type:** policy

The Council and Parliament reached a provisional agreement on the AI Omnibus, which simplifies the AI Act. High-risk obligations move to 2 December 2027 for Annex III systems and 2 August 2028 for Annex I systems, from the previous date of 2 August 2026. The agreement still needs formal adoption by both bodies.
