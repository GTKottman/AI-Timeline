# Texas Responsible AI Governance Act (TRAIGA) signed

- **Date:** 2025-06-22
- **Who:** Texas Governor Greg Abbott; Texas Legislature
- **Type:** policy

Texas signed HB 149, the Texas Responsible AI Governance Act, which bans developing or deploying AI for listed illegal purposes, sets rules for government AI use, creates a Texas AI Council and a regulatory sandbox, and takes effect 2026-01-01. The final version was narrowed from earlier drafts modelled on the EU AI Act. It mattered as one of the first comprehensive state AI laws, and a scaled-back one.
