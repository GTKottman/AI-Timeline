# ACL 2020 Best Paper: CheckList behavioral testing of NLP models

- **Date:** 2020-07-00
- **Who:** Marco Tulio Ribeiro (Microsoft), Tongshuang Wu, Carlos Guestrin (University of Washington), Sameer Singh (UC Irvine)
- **Type:** competition

ACL 2020 gave the Best Overall Paper award to "Beyond Accuracy: Behavioral Testing of NLP Models with CheckList." The paper proposes a task-agnostic matrix of linguistic capabilities and test types, plus a tool that generates test cases. In a user study, practitioners using CheckList wrote about twice as many tests and found nearly three times as many bugs. The conference was held in July 2020; the exact award day was not confirmed, so the day is 00.
