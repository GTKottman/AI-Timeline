# Stanford AI Index Report 2022

- **Date:** 2022-03-16
- **Who:** Stanford Institute for Human-Centered AI (AI Index steering committee, co-chaired by Jack Clark)
- **Type:** other

Stanford HAI released the fifth annual AI Index on March 16, 2022, a report of about 230 pages. It covers research and development, technical performance, ethics, the economy, education and policy. It became one of the main reference datasets people cite for the state of AI.
