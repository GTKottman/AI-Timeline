# Slamcore raises $16M (EUR 15M) Series A

- **Date:** 2022-05-03
- **Who:** Slamcore; ROBO Global Ventures and Presidio Ventures (reported leads)
- **Type:** funding

Slamcore, a vision-based location and mapping startup for robots, autonomous machines and consumer electronics, raised a Series A of about $16 million (EUR 15 million). Strategic investors included Samsung Ventures, Toyota Ventures and Yamato Holdings. Its visual SLAM software lets low-cost cameras give robots real-time location and mapping, which is a core perception problem for mobile AI. The date follows the funding.tech.eu tracker; Startup Intros lists May 1, 2022, and the lead investors are reported differently across outlets.
