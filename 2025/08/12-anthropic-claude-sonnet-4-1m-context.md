# Anthropic expands Claude Sonnet 4 to 1 million token context

- **Date:** 2025-08-12
- **Who:** Anthropic
- **Type:** model

Anthropic made a 1 million token context window available in public beta for Claude Sonnet 4 on the Anthropic API, a five-fold increase. Prompts above 200K tokens were priced higher, and access began with Tier 4 developers before widening. Amazon Bedrock and Vertex AI support followed by 2025-08-26.
