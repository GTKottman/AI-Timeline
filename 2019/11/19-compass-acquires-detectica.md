# Compass Acquires AI Startup Detectica

- **Date:** 2019-11-19
- **Who:** Compass (real estate platform); Detectica (New York AI startup; founders Foster Provost and Panos Ipeirotis, NYU)
- **Type:** acquisition

Compass, the real estate brokerage platform, acquired Detectica, a New York City AI and machine learning startup, in an acqui-hire of its four-person team. The founders joined Compass with two other employees, and Compass's CTO Joseph Sirosh led the integration. Compass said the team would deepen its AI and ML work on personalized recommendations and similar-home matching. Terms were not disclosed.
