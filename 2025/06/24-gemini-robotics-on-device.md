# Google DeepMind announces Gemini Robotics On-Device

- **Date:** 2025-06-24
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** model

Google DeepMind released Gemini Robotics On-Device, a vision-language-action model built to run locally on bi-arm robots without a data connection. It was offered through an SDK for evaluation in the MuJoCo simulator and for adapting the model with as few as 50 to 100 demonstrations. Access required signing up to a trusted tester programme. The model was first trained on the ALOHA platform and later adapted to others, including Apptronik's Apollo humanoid and the Franka FR3.
