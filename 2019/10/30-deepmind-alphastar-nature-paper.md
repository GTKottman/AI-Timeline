# DeepMind publishes AlphaStar grandmaster results in Nature

- **Date:** 2019-10-30
- **Who:** DeepMind (Oriol Vinyals, Demis Hassabis)
- **Type:** research

DeepMind's Nature paper reported that AlphaStar reached Grandmaster level in StarCraft II on Battle.net in all three races, ranking above 99.8% of active players. The agent used a camera-style view and capped action rates to compete under human-like limits.
