# NIST announces US AI Safety Institute leadership and consortium

- **Date:** 2024-02-08
- **Who:** US Department of Commerce, NIST
- **Type:** policy

NIST announced the key elements of the new US AI Safety Institute (USAISI), including its executive leadership and a consortium of commercial and academic participants. The institute was created in response to the October 2023 executive order on safe and trustworthy AI, and the FY2024 appropriations included $10 million to stand it up. It became the main federal body for testing and evaluating frontier AI models.
