# Google releases Gemini 2.5 Flash Image, nicknamed Nano Banana

- **Date:** 2025-08-26
- **Who:** Google (Gemini team)
- **Type:** model

Google released Gemini 2.5 Flash Image, nicknamed "nano-banana", for image generation and editing. It is known for character consistency across images, fusing multiple images, and precise natural-language edits. It was available through the Gemini API, Google AI Studio and the Gemini app. It first appeared anonymously on LMArena, where it beat well-known models in blind tests. OpenRouter lists August 26, 2025, while other sources give August 27.
