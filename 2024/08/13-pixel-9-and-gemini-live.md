# Google launches Pixel 9 with Gemini Live and on-device Gemini Nano

- **Date:** 2024-08-13
- **Who:** Google
- **Type:** product

At its "Made by Google" event Google launched the Pixel 9 line and Gemini Live, a voice conversation mode in the Gemini app. Gemini Live rolled out first to paid Android subscribers in English with ten voices, and iOS was to follow. The Pixel 9 ran Gemini Nano on-device on the Tensor G4 for features such as custom weather reports. It was one of the first phones to put a conversational voice AI in its default assistant.
