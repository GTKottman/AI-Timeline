# European Parliament adopts its negotiating position on the EU AI Act

- **Date:** 2023-06-14
- **Who:** European Parliament
- **Type:** policy

Parliament voted 499 in favour, 28 against and 93 abstentions to adopt its stance on the AI Act, a risk-based framework that bans uses such as social scoring and predictive policing. It also added transparency duties for general-purpose models like ChatGPT before trilogue talks with member states.
