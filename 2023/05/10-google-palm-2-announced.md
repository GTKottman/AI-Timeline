# Google unveils PaLM 2 at I/O 2023

- **Date:** 2023-05-10
- **Who:** Google
- **Type:** model

At Google I/O, Google unveiled PaLM 2 in four sizes (Gecko, Otter, Bison and Unicorn), with stronger multilingual, reasoning and coding skills. Google said more than 25 products and features were powered by PaLM 2, and Bard was upgraded to it.
