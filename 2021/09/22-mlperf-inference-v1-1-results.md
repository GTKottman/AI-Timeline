# MLCommons releases MLPerf Inference v1.1 results

- **Date:** 2021-09-22
- **Who:** MLCommons (MLPerf); submitters including Nvidia, Intel, Qualcomm, Inspur, Dell, HPE, Alibaba
- **Type:** benchmark

MLCommons released MLPerf Inference v1.1 results on September 22, 2021. Twenty-plus organizations submitted datacenter and edge systems, and MLCommons reported that most systems improved 5 to 30% in five months, with some more than doubling. The suite measures how fast trained models run predictions across closed and open divisions. Vendor claims of wins were made by individual submitters.
