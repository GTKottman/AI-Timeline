# China's algorithmic recommendation rules take effect

- **Date:** 2022-03-01
- **Who:** Cyberspace Administration of China (CAC), with MIIT, Ministry of Public Security and SAMR
- **Type:** policy

China's Internet Information Service Algorithmic Recommendation Management Provisions were issued on December 31, 2021 and took effect on March 1, 2022. They require platforms to publish the rules of their recommendation algorithms and to let users turn off personalized recommendations. It was one of the first binding national rules on AI-driven ranking and recommendation systems.
