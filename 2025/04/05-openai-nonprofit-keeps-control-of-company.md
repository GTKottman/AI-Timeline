# OpenAI nonprofit will keep control of the company

- **Date:** 2025-05-05
- **Who:** OpenAI; OpenAI nonprofit board; Attorneys General of California and Delaware
- **Type:** policy

OpenAI announced that its nonprofit will remain in control of the company, reversing plans to let the for-profit arm operate without nonprofit control. The for-profit arm will become a public benefit corporation. OpenAI credited discussions with civic leaders and the California and Delaware attorneys general offices for the change.
