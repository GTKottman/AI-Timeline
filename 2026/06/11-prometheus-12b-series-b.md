# Prometheus raises $12 billion Series B at $41 billion valuation

- **Date:** 2026-06-11
- **Who:** Prometheus (co-led by Jeff Bezos and Vik Bajaj); investors include JPMorgan, BlackRock, Goldman Sachs, DST Global, Arch Venture Partners
- **Type:** funding

Physical-AI startup Prometheus announced a $12 billion Series B at a valuation of roughly $41 billion. The company is building what Bezos calls an "artificial general engineer" for designing and manufacturing physical products, and says it is not building robots. The money is mainly for compute, and the company, with about 150 employees, has not yet shipped a product. It is one of the largest AI rounds of the period and a sign of investor appetite for AI aimed at engineering and manufacturing.
