# China's State Council releases New Generation AI Development Plan

- **Date:** 2017-07-20
- **Who:** State Council of the People's Republic of China
- **Type:** policy

China's State Council released the New Generation Artificial Intelligence Development Plan, a national strategy with goals up to 2030 for AI research, industry and talent. It set a target for China to become the world's primary AI innovation center by 2030. It is one of the first national AI strategies.
