# OpenAI system reaches gold-level score at IOI 2025

- **Date:** 2025-08-00
- **Who:** OpenAI (Sheryl Hsu)
- **Type:** competition

OpenAI's system took part in the online track of the 2025 International Olympiad in Informatics under the same five-hour limit and 50-submission cap as humans, placing sixth overall and first among AI entrants. It used an ensemble of general-purpose reasoning models, the same core model that earned IMO gold, rather than a custom model. It mattered as a second olympiad gold-level result for an OpenAI system in a single summer, though reaction was sceptical.
