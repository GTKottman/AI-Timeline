# Google Cloud TPU v5p AI accelerator announced

- **Date:** 2023-12-06
- **Who:** Google (Google Cloud, Google DeepMind)
- **Type:** hardware

Google announced the Cloud TPU v5p, its most powerful AI accelerator at the time, alongside the Gemini model and an "AI Hypercomputer" system. Each v5p pod holds 8,960 chips linked by a high-speed interconnect. Google said it delivers about twice the FLOPS and three times the high-bandwidth memory bandwidth of TPU v4, which mattered because it was a direct claim about the cost of training frontier-scale models on Google's own silicon.
