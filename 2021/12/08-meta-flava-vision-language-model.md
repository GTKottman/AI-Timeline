# Meta AI releases FLAVA, a foundational vision-and-language model

- **Date:** 2021-12-08
- **Who:** Facebook AI Research (Singh, Hu, Goswami, Couairon, Galuba, Rohrbach, Kiela)
- **Type:** model

Facebook AI Research posted FLAVA (arXiv, December 2021), a single model meant to serve as a general foundation across vision, language and combined vision-language tasks. It combines contrastive cross-modal training with fusion-based multimodal training, which earlier systems usually used separately. The authors evaluated it on 35 tasks. FLAVA was published at CVPR 2022, and its code is in Hugging Face Transformers. Hugging Face lists the paper's date as 2021-12-08.
