# UK publishes its first National AI Strategy

- **Date:** 2021-09-22
- **Who:** UK Department for Digital, Culture, Media & Sport (DCMS)
- **Type:** policy

The UK government published its first National AI Strategy, a ten-year plan to make Britain a global AI superpower. It rested on three pillars: investing in the long-term AI ecosystem, spreading AI benefits across sectors and regions, and governing AI effectively. A regulatory white paper was promised for 2022.
