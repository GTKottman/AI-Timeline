# OpenAI Five loses to top Dota 2 team at The International

- **Date:** 2018-08-23
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** competition

OpenAI Five lost two games against a team of top human Dota 2 professionals at The International in Vancouver. The bots used hero lineups supplied by a third party rather than drafting themselves. MIT Technology Review reported that humans remained better at Dota 2 for now. The result set the next target for game-playing AI in complex team environments.
