# China's algorithmic recommendation rules take effect

- **Date:** 2022-03-01
- **Who:** Cyberspace Administration of China (CAC), with MIIT, Ministry of Public Security and SAMR
- **Type:** policy

China's Internet Information Service Algorithmic Recommendation Management Provisions took effect on March 1, 2022, after being issued at the end of December 2021. They require platforms to disclose how recommendation algorithms work, let users switch off or delete the tags that drive targeting, and bar algorithms used to spread fake news or manipulate public opinion. It was one of the first binding national rules on AI-driven ranking and recommendation systems, and it set the template for later Chinese AI rules.
