# Meta expands Ray-Ban Meta glasses with Meta AI vision

- **Date:** 2024-04-23
- **Who:** Meta
- **Type:** product

Meta announced new Ray-Ban Meta glasses styles and expanded access to Meta AI with vision, so the assistant could answer questions about what the wearer was looking at. It was an early consumer deployment of a multimodal assistant on wearable hardware.
