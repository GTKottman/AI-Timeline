# Getty Images files US lawsuit against Stability AI in Delaware

- **Date:** 2023-02-03
- **Who:** Getty Images
- **Type:** policy

Getty Images filed a US suit in Delaware federal court on February 3, 2023, accusing Stability AI of copying about 12 million copyrighted photos, with captions and metadata, to train Stable Diffusion for a competing business. It extended Getty's earlier UK action into US courts.
