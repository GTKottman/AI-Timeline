# Qwen2.5-VL-32B open-weight vision model

- **Date:** 2025-03-24
- **Who:** Alibaba Qwen team
- **Type:** model

Alibaba's Qwen team released Qwen2.5-VL-32B-Instruct, a 32-billion-parameter vision-language model tuned with reinforcement learning. Secondary coverage reports it beats the earlier 72B Qwen2.5-VL on MMMU and MathVista and beats similar-size models such as Gemma 3-27B on several multimodal tests. Sources differ by a day (OpenRouter lists March 24, Gigazine's article is dated March 25), and the benchmark figures come from third-party reporting, not the Qwen blog.
