# NeurIPS 2025 Best Paper Awards Announced

- **Date:** 2025-11-26
- **Who:** NeurIPS (Conference on Neural Information Processing Systems)
- **Type:** competition

NeurIPS 2025 announced four Best Paper awards (one from the Datasets and Benchmarks track) and three runner-up papers. Named winners reported by secondary coverage include "Artificial Hivemind," which introduces the Infinity-Chat dataset of 26,000 diverse queries with 31,000 human annotations, and a paper on scaled LLM gating from the Qwen team. Sources agree on the counts but not every winner title, so the official NeurIPS awards page is the authority for the full list.
