# Hugging Face raises $235M Series D at $4.5B valuation

- **Date:** 2023-08-24
- **Who:** Hugging Face; led by Salesforce Ventures with Google, Amazon, NVIDIA, Intel, AMD, Qualcomm, IBM
- **Type:** funding

Hugging Face announced a $235 million Series D at a $4.5 billion valuation, led by Salesforce Ventures with a group of chip, cloud and software investors. The round brought its total capital raised to about $395 million. The valuation was roughly double its May 2022 level. Hugging Face is the main hub for open models and datasets.
