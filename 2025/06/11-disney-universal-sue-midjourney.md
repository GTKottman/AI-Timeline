# Disney and Universal sue Midjourney for copyright infringement

- **Date:** 2025-06-11
- **Who:** Disney, Universal; Midjourney
- **Type:** policy

Disney and Universal sued Midjourney, the image generator, for copyright infringement, in what was described as the first major Hollywood studio copyright action against a generative AI developer. The case tests whether training on copyrighted characters and films is allowed. Warner Bros. Discovery filed a parallel suit later in 2025.
