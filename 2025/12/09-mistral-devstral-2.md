# Mistral releases Devstral 2 and Devstral Small 2 coding models

- **Date:** 2025-12-09
- **Who:** Mistral AI
- **Type:** model

Mistral released Devstral 2, a 123B-parameter model, and Devstral Small 2, a 24B-parameter model, together with the open-source Mistral Vibe CLI, a command-line coding assistant powered by Devstral.
