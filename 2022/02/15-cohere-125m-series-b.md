# Cohere raises $125M Series B

- **Date:** 2022-02-15
- **Who:** Cohere (Aidan Gomez, Ivan Zhang, Nick Frosst); led by Tiger Global with Radical Ventures, Index Ventures and Section 32
- **Type:** funding

Toronto-based Cohere, which sells API access to large language models, announced a $125 million Series B. The money was earmarked for platform development, team growth and a new Palo Alto office, and the company hired Bill MacCartney to run engineering there. The round was one of the larger early bets on companies selling LLMs as a service rather than building end products.
