# Amazon unveils Nova foundation models at re:Invent

- **Date:** 2024-12-03
- **Who:** Amazon / AWS
- **Type:** model

At AWS re:Invent, Amazon launched its first in-house family of foundation models, Nova: Micro (text), Lite and Pro (multimodal), and Premier (the most capable, due later), plus Nova Canvas for images and Nova Reel for video. The models were offered through Amazon Bedrock.
