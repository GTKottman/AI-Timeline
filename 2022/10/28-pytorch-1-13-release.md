# PyTorch 1.13 released

- **Date:** 2022-10-28
- **Who:** PyTorch community (Meta AI)
- **Type:** software

PyTorch 1.13 was released on October 28, 2022. It moved BetterTransformer to stable for faster Transformer inference and made functorch a beta feature, shipped as a separate package. Beta Apple M1 support and CUDA 11.7 support were included, and CUDA 10.2 and 11.3 were deprecated. The release combined over 3,700 commits from 467 contributors since 1.12.1.
