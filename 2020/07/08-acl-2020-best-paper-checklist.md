# ACL 2020 Best Paper: CheckList Behavioral Testing of NLP Models

- **Date:** 2020-07-08
- **Who:** Marco Tulio Ribeiro (Microsoft), Tongshuang Wu, Carlos Guestrin (University of Washington), Sameer Singh (UC Irvine)
- **Type:** competition

The ACL 2020 Best Paper award went to "Beyond Accuracy: Behavioral Testing of NLP Models with CheckList." The paper proposes a task-agnostic test grid of linguistic capabilities and test types, plus a tool that generates test cases. It found serious failures in commercial and state-of-the-art NLP models. In a user study, practitioners using CheckList wrote about twice as many tests and found nearly three times as many bugs. The day is from a search snippet of the ACL announcement; the Allen School post about the award is dated August 13, 2020.
