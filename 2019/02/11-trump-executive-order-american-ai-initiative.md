# Trump signs Executive Order 13859 launching the American AI Initiative

- **Date:** 2019-02-11
- **Who:** White House / US government (President Donald Trump)
- **Type:** policy

Trump signed Executive Order 13859, "Maintaining American Leadership in Artificial Intelligence," which created the American AI Initiative. It set five lines of effort: more federal AI research investment, opening federal computing and data resources, AI technical standards, an AI workforce, and international engagement. The order put coordination under the NSTC Select Committee on Artificial Intelligence, but it included no dollar figures.
