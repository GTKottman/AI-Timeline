# Google adds Imagen 3 image generation to Gemini and launches Custom Gems

- **Date:** 2024-08-28
- **Who:** Google / Google DeepMind
- **Type:** product

Google added Imagen 3 to the Gemini app and launched Custom Gems, user-built chatbot personas. Imagen 3 was at first limited to Gemini Advanced subscribers in the US and English, with broader access promised within days, and Google had paused image generation in Gemini in February after historically inaccurate images. Google said the model blocks photorealistic images of identifiable people, minors and extreme violence. One secondary source dates the Imagen 3 model release to August 16, but that is not confirmed, so this entry uses the Gemini rollout date.
