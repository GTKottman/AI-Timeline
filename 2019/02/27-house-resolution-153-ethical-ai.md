# US House introduces H.Res.153 on ethical AI development

- **Date:** 2019-02-27
- **Who:** Rep. Brenda Lawrence (D-MI) and Rep. Ro Khanna (D-CA)
- **Type:** policy

Lawrence and Khanna introduced H.Res.153, a non-binding House resolution calling for AI to be developed in a "safe, responsible, and democratic" way. It lists ten aims, including stakeholder engagement, transparency and explainability, privacy, accountability for automated decisions, and safety and control. It was endorsed by the Future of Life Institute, BSA, IBM and Facebook, and was referred to committee, where it died; it was still one of the most concrete congressional statements on AI ethics at the time.
