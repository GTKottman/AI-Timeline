# Apple announces M1 chip with 16-core Neural Engine

- **Date:** 2020-11-10
- **Who:** Apple
- **Type:** hardware

Apple announced the M1, its first Apple-silicon chip for Mac, with a 16-core Neural Engine rated at up to 11 trillion operations per second. The chip put dedicated ML acceleration into mainstream consumer laptops and desktops.
