# Samsung Galaxy S24 ships with Google Gemini Nano and Gemini Pro

- **Date:** 2024-01-17
- **Who:** Samsung; Google
- **Type:** product

Samsung's Galaxy S24 lineup, unveiled in January 2024, was the first major phone family to run Google's Gemini models on device and in the cloud. Gemini Nano handled on-device tasks and Gemini Pro powered cloud features. It moved generative AI into mainstream Android hardware at scale.
