# Microsoft hires Inflection AI founders and staff, licenses models

- **Date:** 2024-03-19
- **Who:** Microsoft, Inflection AI, Mustafa Suleyman, Karen Simonyan
- **Type:** acquisition

Microsoft hired Inflection AI co-founders Mustafa Suleyman and Karen Simonyan plus much of the startup's staff, to lead a new consumer AI unit called Microsoft AI. Microsoft took no equity stake, and Bloomberg reported it paid about $650 million for access to Inflection's models and legal protections. The deal was a structure that avoided a formal acquisition, and it raised antitrust and conflict-of-interest questions because Reid Hoffman sat on Microsoft's board.
