# Google Brings BERT to Search

- **Date:** 2019-10-25
- **Who:** Google Search
- **Type:** product

Google announced it was using BERT, its bidirectional transformer language model, to better understand search queries. Google said it affected about one in ten US English searches, with the biggest gains on longer, conversational queries where small words such as prepositions change the meaning. BERT was also applied to featured snippets, and other languages were to follow. It was one of the first large-scale production uses of a deep transformer language model in a mainstream product.
