# DeepMind AlphaProof and AlphaGeometry 2 reach silver-medal level at the IMO

- **Date:** 2024-07-25
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** research

AlphaProof and AlphaGeometry 2 solved four of six problems at the International Mathematical Olympiad, scoring 28 of 42 points, a silver-medal standard. DeepMind described it as the first AI system to reach medal level at the competition. The result showed that formal reasoning combined with learned search could handle olympiad-level proofs.
