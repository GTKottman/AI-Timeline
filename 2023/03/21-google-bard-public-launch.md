# Google opens Bard chatbot to the public

- **Date:** 2023-03-21
- **Who:** Google
- **Type:** product

Google opened its Bard chatbot to users in the US and UK who joined a waitlist. Google described Bard as an experiment built on a lighter build of its LaMDA model and positioned it as a direct response to ChatGPT and Bing Chat. Access was rolled out on a rolling basis.
