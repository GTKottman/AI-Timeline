# DeepMind hires Lila Ibrahim as first COO

- **Date:** 2018-04-11
- **Who:** DeepMind (Demis Hassabis); Lila Ibrahim
- **Type:** people

DeepMind named Lila Ibrahim its first Chief Operating Officer, announced in a blog post by CEO Demis Hassabis. Ibrahim had most recently been COO of Coursera and earlier worked as a microprocessor designer at Intel and as chief of staff at Kleiner Perkins. Hassabis said she would help design, build and manage the company's next phase of growth. It marked DeepMind building out its business and operations leadership alongside its research staff.
