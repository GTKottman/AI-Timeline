# European Parliament adopts three resolutions on AI ethics, civil liability and intellectual property

- **Date:** 2020-10-20
- **Who:** European Parliament (resolutions 2020/2012(INL), 2020/2014(INL), 2020/2015(INI))
- **Type:** policy

On October 20, 2020 the European Parliament adopted three non-binding resolutions asking the European Commission to propose EU legislation. One set out an ethical framework for AI, robotics and related technologies built on values such as safety, transparency and accountability. Another called for a harmonised civil liability regime for AI systems. The third asked for an impact assessment on intellectual property in AI development and held that AI systems should not have legal personality. Together they shaped the EU's legislative push on AI that followed the February 2020 white paper.
