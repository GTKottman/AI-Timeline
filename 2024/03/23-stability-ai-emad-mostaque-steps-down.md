# Emad Mostaque steps down as Stability AI CEO

- **Date:** 2024-03-23
- **Who:** Emad Mostaque; Stability AI
- **Type:** people

Stability AI founder Emad Mostaque resigned as chief executive. The board named COO Shan Shan Wong and CTO Christian Laforte interim co-CEOs. The departure came as the Stable Diffusion maker was short of cash and facing copyright suits, and it marked a change in leadership at one of the best-known open image-model companies.
