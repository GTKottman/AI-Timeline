# DeepMind partners with Cancer Research UK Imperial Centre on mammography ML

- **Date:** 2017-11-24
- **Who:** DeepMind (Google), Cancer Research UK Imperial Centre, Royal Surrey County Hospital
- **Type:** research

DeepMind announced a research project with the Cancer Research UK Imperial Centre and Royal Surrey County Hospital to test whether machine learning can improve breast cancer detection in mammograms. It planned to analyse de-identified historic mammograms from about 7,500 women in the OPTIMAM database.
