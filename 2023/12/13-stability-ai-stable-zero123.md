# Stability AI releases Stable Zero123

- **Date:** 2023-12-13
- **Who:** Stability AI
- **Type:** model

Stability AI released Stable Zero123, a view-conditioned image model that generates novel views of an object from a single input image. It is built on Stable Diffusion 1.5 and trained on a filtered subset of the Objaverse 3D dataset, and a Japanese report says it outperforms Zero123-XL. It was released under a non-commercial research license, and it is a building block for text-to-image-to-3D pipelines. The December 13 date and the training-speed comparison were not confirmed from a primary source.
