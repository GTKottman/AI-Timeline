# SambaNova raises $676 million Series D

- **Date:** 2021-04-13
- **Who:** SambaNova Systems; led by SoftBank Vision Fund 2, with Temasek and GIC
- **Type:** funding

SambaNova Systems, an AI chip and dataflow-systems startup, raised a $676 million Series D on April 13, 2021, led by SoftBank Vision Fund 2 with new investors Temasek and GIC. The company said total funding passed $1 billion and valuation passed $5 billion. The round funded competition with established chip makers in the AI hardware market. Figures come from the company's own announcement.
