# Google unveils Veo text-to-video model

- **Date:** 2024-05-14
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** model

At Google I/O 2024, Google DeepMind announced Veo, a text-to-video model that it said could produce 1080p video over a minute long, with editing from written instructions. Access started as a limited preview through VideoFX, with SynthID watermarking on outputs. Veo was presented as a direct answer to OpenAI's Sora and was tied to Gemini's multimodal training.
