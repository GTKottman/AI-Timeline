# Tesla holds AI Day and unveils Dojo

- **Date:** 2021-08-19
- **Who:** Tesla
- **Type:** hardware

At its first AI Day, Tesla unveiled Dojo, a custom supercomputer for training neural networks on camera data. The D1 chip was a 7 nm design, and the planned cluster would house 3,000 of them. It was an early example of a carmaker building its own AI training hardware.
