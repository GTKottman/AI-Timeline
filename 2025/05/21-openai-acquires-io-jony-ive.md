# OpenAI acquires Jony Ive's io hardware startup for about $6.5 billion

- **Date:** 2025-05-21
- **Who:** OpenAI; io (Jony Ive, Scott Cannon, Evans Hankey, Tang Tan)
- **Type:** acquisition

OpenAI agreed to buy io, the AI hardware startup co-founded by former Apple designer Jony Ive, in an all-stock deal valued at about $6.5 billion. OpenAI already held a 23% stake from an earlier arrangement. Reports say about 50 engineers, designers and researchers would join OpenAI, with Peter Welinder leading the new io division. The deal was expected to close in the summer, and the first device was expected in 2026. Sources describe it as OpenAI's largest acquisition to date.
