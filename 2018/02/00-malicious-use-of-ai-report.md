# "The Malicious Use of Artificial Intelligence" report published

- **Date:** 2018-02-00
- **Who:** 26 co-authors from 14 institutions, including the Future of Humanity Institute (Oxford), Centre for the Study of Existential Risk (Cambridge), OpenAI, Center for a New American Security and Electronic Frontier Foundation
- **Type:** policy

A 26-author report, "The Malicious Use of Artificial Intelligence: Forecasting, Prevention, and Mitigation," mapped how AI could be misused in digital, physical and political security. It described four vignettes and made recommendations for researchers, companies and policymakers, including discussion of whether some AI research should be kept secret. It mattered because it was one of the first cross-institution safety reports to set out concrete misuse scenarios. The exact publication day was not confirmed in the sources, so the day is recorded as 00.
