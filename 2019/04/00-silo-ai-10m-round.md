# Silo.AI raises EUR 10 million

- **Date:** 2019-04-00
- **Who:** Silo.AI (Helsinki)
- **Type:** funding

Helsinki-based Silo.AI, which describes itself as the largest private AI services provider in the Nordics, raised EUR 10 million to grow its team and expand into the European market. Funding came partly from Business Finland's Growth Engine program and partly from angel investors. The round was tracked as April 2019, but the exact day was not confirmed by a company press release in the sources, and one article about it is dated June 2019.
