# US export rule on advanced AI chips to China takes effect

- **Date:** 2022-10-07
- **Who:** US Department of Commerce (BIS)
- **Type:** policy

On October 7, 2022, the Bureau of Industry and Security issued an interim final rule that tightened export controls on advanced computing chips and some semiconductor manufacturing equipment to China. The rule aimed to limit China's ability to buy and make high-end chips used in military applications, and it expanded controls for 28 Chinese entities on the Entity List.
