# ServiceNow to acquire Loom Systems for AIOps

- **Date:** 2020-01-22
- **Who:** ServiceNow; Loom Systems (Israel)
- **Type:** acquisition

ServiceNow agreed to buy Loom Systems, an Israeli AIOps company, to add machine learning to its IT service and operations products. The plan was to apply AI to a knowledge base of past incidents and fixes and to automate remediation. Financial terms were not disclosed. ServiceNow expected the deal to close by the end of Q1 2020. It was an enterprise AI-for-IT-operations acquisition.
