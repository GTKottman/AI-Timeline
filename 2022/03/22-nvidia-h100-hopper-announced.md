# NVIDIA announces Hopper architecture and H100 GPU

- **Date:** 2022-03-22
- **Who:** NVIDIA
- **Type:** hardware

NVIDIA unveiled the Hopper GPU architecture and the H100 data-center GPU at its March 2022 GTC conference. Hopper was built for transformer-scale training and inference.
