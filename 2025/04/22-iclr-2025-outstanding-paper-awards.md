# ICLR 2025 Outstanding Paper Awards

- **Date:** 2025-04-22
- **Who:** ICLR (International Conference on Learning Representations)
- **Type:** competition

The ICLR 2025 committee named three Outstanding Papers: "Safety Alignment Should be Made More Than Just a Few Tokens Deep", "Learning Dynamics of LLM Finetuning" and "AlphaEdit: Null-Space Constrained Model Editing for Language Models". Honorable mentions went to "Data Shapley in One Training Run", "SAM 2" and "Faster Cascades via Speculative Decoding". The safety-alignment paper argues that safety alignment should reach beyond the first few output tokens.
