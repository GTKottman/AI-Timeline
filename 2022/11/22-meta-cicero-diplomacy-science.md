# Meta CICERO plays Diplomacy at human level

- **Date:** 2022-11-22
- **Who:** Meta FAIR Diplomacy team (lead author Andrew Goff)
- **Type:** research

Meta published CICERO in Science, an agent that combines a strategic reinforcement-learning planner with a language model that negotiates with human players. Over 40 online games against 82 humans without them knowing it was a bot, CICERO ranked in the top 10% of players who played more than one game. The work showed that a language model could be tied to planning to act in a game that depends on persuasion and alliances, though the authors noted it still sent messages with errors.
