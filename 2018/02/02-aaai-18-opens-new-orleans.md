# AAAI-18 conference opens in New Orleans

- **Date:** 2018-02-02
- **Who:** Association for the Advancement of Artificial Intelligence (AAAI); program chairs Sheila McIlraith and Kilian Weinberger
- **Type:** other

The AAAI-18 conference ran February 2 to 7, 2018 at the Hilton New Orleans Riverside. Organizers received a record of over 3,800 paper submissions and accepted 933, an acceptance rate just under 25 percent. Synced's coverage of the opening framed the meeting around learning. It mattered as a major venue for AI research that year.
