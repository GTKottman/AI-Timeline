# Apple quietly acquires Paris AI startup Datakalab

- **Date:** 2023-12-17
- **Who:** Apple; Datakalab (Paris-based AI startup)
- **Type:** acquisition

Apple acquired Datakalab, a Paris-based AI and computer-vision startup, in a deal that closed on December 17, 2023, and was first reported in April 2024. Apple did not confirm the deal and the amount was not disclosed. Reports tied it to Apple's on-device AI work, and several Datakalab staff joined Apple.
