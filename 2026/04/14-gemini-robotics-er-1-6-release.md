# Google DeepMind releases Gemini Robotics-ER 1.6

- **Date:** 2026-04-14
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** model

Gemini Robotics-ER 1.6 is an embodied-reasoning model for robots, offered to developers through the Gemini API and Google AI Studio. It improves spatial reasoning, multi-view perception, pointing and counting, detects task completion, and adds instrument reading for gauges and meters. Boston Dynamics integrated it into its Orbit AIVI-Learning platform for customers.
