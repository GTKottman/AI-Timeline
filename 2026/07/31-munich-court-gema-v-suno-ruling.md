# Munich court rules Suno infringed GEMA music copyrights

- **Date:** 2026-07-31
- **Who:** Munich Regional Court I (42nd Civil Chamber, case 42 O 763/25), GEMA, Suno
- **Type:** policy

On July 31, 2026 the Munich Regional Court I ruled in favour of the music rights society GEMA against AI music generator Suno. The court found that training on GEMA works in the US, plus storing and reproducing them in Europe, violated German and US copyright law, and it rejected Suno's text-and-data-mining defence. Suno must stop the reproduction and training, disclose infringement-related revenue, and faces fines up to 250,000 euros per future breach, with damages still to be set. The judgment is not yet enforceable, and Suno says it disagrees and is weighing an appeal. It is GEMA's second win against an AI company, after a November 2025 ruling against OpenAI.
