# Genesis open-source physics engine and simulation platform launches

- **Date:** 2024-12-19
- **Who:** Genesis project (CMU, Stanford, MIT CSAIL, NVIDIA, Tsinghua and other labs)
- **Type:** software

Genesis launched as an open-source universal physics engine for robotics, written in Python, with rigid body, MPM and other solvers. The project documentation claims it runs 10-80x faster than GPU-accelerated simulators such as Isaac Gym and MJX; these are the developers' own claims. The first release included the physics engine and simulation platform, and the team said a generative layer that turns natural-language prompts into simulation data would follow later.
