# GSA strikes OneGov deal giving Claude to all three branches of US government for $1

- **Date:** 2025-08-12
- **Who:** US General Services Administration (GSA); Anthropic
- **Type:** policy

GSA announced a OneGov agreement with Anthropic that lets agencies in all three branches use Claude for Enterprise and Claude for Government for up to a year at a nominal fee, with approval for Congress and the judiciary reported as pending. Claude for Government supports FedRAMP High workloads. It followed GSA's similar $1 ChatGPT Enterprise deal with OpenAI for the executive branch. It mattered as a government-wide purchasing route for frontier models.
