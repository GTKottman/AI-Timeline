# Mistral AI acquires Austrian physics-AI startup Emmi AI

- **Date:** 2026-05-19
- **Who:** Mistral AI, Emmi AI
- **Type:** acquisition

Mistral acquired Emmi AI, a Linz-based startup building physics AI models for industrial simulation in aerospace, automotive, energy and semiconductors. Emmi's team of more than 30 researchers joins Mistral's science and applied-AI divisions, and Linz becomes a Mistral office. Reuters reported the deal on May 19; the price was not disclosed, and one outlet's reported figure of about EUR 300 million is unconfirmed.
