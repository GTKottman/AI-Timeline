# Anthropic runs Super Bowl ads promising an ad-free Claude

- **Date:** 2026-02-08
- **Who:** Anthropic
- **Type:** product

Anthropic ran Super Bowl ads during Super Bowl 60 on February 8, 2026, under the campaign "A Time and a Place." The ads stated that Claude will remain ad-free, in contrast with OpenAI's ChatGPT ads.
