- **Date:** 2024-08-06
- **Who:** Figure AI
- **Type:** hardware

Figure 02 was unveiled with five-fingered hands, a 25 kg lift capacity, six cameras and an onboard vision-language-action model. It was presented as a step toward general-purpose humanoid labour in factories.
