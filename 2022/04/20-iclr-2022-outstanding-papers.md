# ICLR 2022 outstanding paper awards announced

- **Date:** 2022-04-20
- **Who:** ICLR 2022 program chairs (Yan Liu, Chelsea Finn, Yejin Choi, Marc Deisenroth)
- **Type:** competition

The ICLR 2022 program chairs announced seven Outstanding Paper Awards and three Honorable Mentions on April 20, 2022. The winners include "Bootstrapped Meta-Learning" and "Neural Collapse Under MSE Loss: Proximity to and Dynamics on the Central Path".
