# Google releases ALBERT, a lighter BERT

- **Date:** 2019-09-26
- **Who:** Google Research (Zhenzhong Lan, Mingda Chen, Sebastian Goodman, Kevin Gimpel, Piyush Sharma, Radu Soricut)
- **Type:** paper

ALBERT ("A Lite BERT") cut BERT's size by sharing parameters across layers and factorizing the embedding matrix, then trained with a new sentence-order objective. It reached state-of-the-art results on GLUE, RACE and SQuAD with far fewer parameters than BERT-large. It made strong language models cheaper to train and store.
