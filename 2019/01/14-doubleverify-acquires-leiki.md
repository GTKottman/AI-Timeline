# DoubleVerify acquires Helsinki AI firm Leiki

- **Date:** 2019-01-14
- **Who:** DoubleVerify; Leiki Ltd.
- **Type:** acquisition

DoubleVerify, a brand-safety and ad-measurement company, announced it had acquired Leiki, a Helsinki semantic AI company whose engine classifies the content of any text. The deal had closed on 2018-12-27 as an all-cash and all-stock offer, and no price was disclosed. DoubleVerify said it would use the technology to strengthen contextual classification for brand reputation and to build a publisher-support business.
