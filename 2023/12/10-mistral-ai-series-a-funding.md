# Mistral AI raises 385 million euros in its second funding round

- **Date:** 2023-12-10
- **Who:** Mistral AI
- **Type:** funding

Mistral AI announced a 385 million euro round that made it one of France's AI unicorns at about a $2 billion valuation. The round was led by Andreessen Horowitz with Lightspeed participating. The raise came days before the Mixtral 8x7B release and gave the French startup the capital to compete with US frontier labs on open-weight models.
