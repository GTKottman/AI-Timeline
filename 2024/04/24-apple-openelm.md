# Apple releases OpenELM open language models

- **Date:** 2024-04-24
- **Who:** Apple
- **Type:** model

Apple released OpenELM, a family of small open language models (270M to 3B parameters, base and instruction-tuned) meant to run on devices. Apple published weights, training logs, checkpoints and training configurations on the Hugging Face Hub, all trained on public datasets.
