# NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin partner production systems at Computex

- **Date:** 2022-05-24
- **Who:** NVIDIA; more than 30 partners (including Advantech, AAEON, Basler, Connect Tech, Stereolabs)
- **Type:** hardware

At Computex 2022 in Taipei, more than 30 NVIDIA partners announced the first wave of production systems built on the Jetson AGX Orin module, including servers, edge appliances, industrial PCs and carrier boards. NVIDIA said Jetson AGX Orin production modules would ship in July, with Orin NX modules following in September. The launch put Orin-class AI compute into edge robots, cameras and AIoT devices, where local inference was becoming a priority.
