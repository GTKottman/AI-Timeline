# Disney invests $1 billion in OpenAI with Sora character licensing deal

- **Date:** 2025-12-11
- **Who:** Disney; OpenAI
- **Type:** funding

Disney agreed to invest $1 billion in OpenAI and signed a three-year licence letting users generate Sora videos with Disney characters. It was one of the first large studio deals for generative video, and Disney later exited the arrangement in March 2026.
