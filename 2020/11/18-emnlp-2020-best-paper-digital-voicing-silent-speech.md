# EMNLP 2020 best paper: Digital Voicing of Silent Speech

- **Date:** 2020-11-18
- **Who:** David Gaddy and Dan Klein (UC Berkeley); EMNLP 2020
- **Type:** competition

"Digital Voicing of Silent Speech" won a best paper award at EMNLP 2020. It converts silently mouthed words, read from facial EMG sensors, into audible speech. The authors trained on EMG from silent articulation rather than only vocalized speech, by moving audio targets onto silent signals. Word error rate fell from 64% to 4% in one condition, and the authors released a silent and vocalized EMG dataset with code.

Note: the date is the date of the Synced report on the award; the EMNLP 2020 conference ran in mid-November.
