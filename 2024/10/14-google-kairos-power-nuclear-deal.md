# Google signs Kairos Power deal for 500 MW of advanced nuclear power

- **Date:** 2024-10-14
- **Who:** Google, Kairos Power
- **Type:** funding

Google and Kairos Power agreed to deploy a US fleet of advanced small modular reactors totaling 500 MW by 2035, with the first plant targeted for 2030. Kairos would build and operate the reactors and sell the power to Google under purchase agreements, starting with a 50 MW demonstration project in Tennessee. Google said nuclear offers round-the-clock power without carbon emissions, which was the reason given for the deal.
