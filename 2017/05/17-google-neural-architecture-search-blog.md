# Google Brain: using machine learning to explore neural network architecture

- **Date:** 2017-05-17
- **Who:** Quoc V. Le and Barret Zoph (Google Brain)
- **Type:** research

Google Research blog post by Quoc Le and Barret Zoph describing neural architecture search, in which a recurrent controller network proposes network designs and is trained with reinforcement learning to maximize the accuracy of the models it generates. The authors said the search space of architectures is too large to design by hand and hoped the method would let non-experts build networks suited to their needs. It built on their ICLR 2017 paper on neural architecture search with reinforcement learning, and it is the lineage of Google's later AutoML work.
