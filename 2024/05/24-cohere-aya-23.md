# Cohere For AI releases Aya 23 open multilingual models

- **Date:** 2024-05-24
- **Who:** Cohere For AI
- **Type:** model

Cohere For AI released Aya 23 as open-weights models at 8 billion and 35 billion parameters, covering 23 languages. It trades the breadth of the earlier Aya 101 (101 languages) for deeper coverage of fewer languages, combining a Command-family base with the Aya Collection of instruction data. Cohere reported that it outperformed Gemma, Mistral and Mixtral on the covered languages, a claim of its own. It was a major open release focused on non-English performance.
