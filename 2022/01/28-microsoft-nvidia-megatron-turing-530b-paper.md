# Megatron-Turing NLG 530B training paper

- **Date:** 2022-01-28
- **Who:** Microsoft and NVIDIA (Shaden Smith, Mostofa Patwary, Brandon Norick, Bryan Catanzaro and others)
- **Type:** paper

Microsoft and NVIDIA posted a paper describing how they trained MT-NLG, a monolithic 530-billion-parameter generative language model, using DeepSpeed and Megatron. It covers the 3D parallelism approach, the training corpus and data curation, and zero-, one- and few-shot evaluation. The write-up gave other labs a concrete recipe for training models at this scale.
