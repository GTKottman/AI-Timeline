# Google DeepMind publishes GenCast, an AI ensemble weather forecaster

- **Date:** 2024-12-04
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** research

GenCast, a diffusion-based ensemble model, was published in Nature on December 4, 2024. It outputs probabilistic forecasts out to 15 days. DeepMind reported that it beat the ECMWF ENS system on 97.2% of targets across 1,320 combinations of variables and lead times, and on extreme heat and wind forecasts. These are DeepMind's own figures, and the independent evaluation was not found.
