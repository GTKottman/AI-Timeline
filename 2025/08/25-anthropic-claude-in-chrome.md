# Anthropic launches Claude in Chrome browser extension

- **Date:** 2025-08-25
- **Who:** Anthropic
- **Type:** product

Anthropic released Claude in Chrome, an extension that lets Claude view pages, click buttons and fill out forms for the user. It started as a pilot with 1,000 Max plan users from a waitlist and later expanded to Pro, Team and Enterprise. Anthropic reported that prompt injection on web pages was the main risk, with an attack success rate of 23.6% before mitigations and 11.2% in autonomous mode, and it advised avoiding sensitive financial, legal and medical sites.
