# Google employees protest Project Maven in open letter

- **Date:** 2018-04-04
- **Who:** Google employees (about 3,100 signatories, per coverage); Google CEO Sundar Pichai; US Department of Defense Project Maven
- **Type:** policy

An open letter signed by about 3,100 Google employees, including dozens of senior engineers, asked Sundar Pichai to cancel Project Maven, a Pentagon program that adapted TensorFlow to analyze drone imagery. The letter also asked Google to adopt a policy against building warfare technology. The protest put AI military use on the agenda inside a major AI company, and Google later published AI principles in June 2018 that ruled out weapons and surveillance uses.
