# TII releases Falcon 40B open-source model

- **Date:** 2023-05-25
- **Who:** Technology Innovation Institute (Abu Dhabi)
- **Type:** model

TII released Falcon 40B, a 40-billion-parameter model trained on one trillion tokens of RefinedWeb data, with weights distributed as an open-source package. TII described the weights as open source for research and commercial use. It mattered as one of the largest openly released models of the period and a sign that Gulf states were entering the open model race.
