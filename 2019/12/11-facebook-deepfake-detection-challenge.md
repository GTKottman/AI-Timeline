# Facebook launches Deepfake Detection Challenge with 100,000-video dataset

- **Date:** 2019-12-11
- **Who:** Facebook AI (with Microsoft, Partnership on AI, Kaggle, academic partners)
- **Type:** competition

Facebook launched the Deepfake Detection Challenge at NeurIPS 2019 in Vancouver, with a dataset of more than 100,000 videos made with paid actors and labeled for AI manipulation. Kaggle hosted the leaderboard, with a $500,000 first prize and a run into March 2020. Facebook had committed $10 million in September 2019. It was a large, open benchmark push to speed up detection of manipulated media.
