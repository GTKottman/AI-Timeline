# Nvidia ships GeForce RTX 3080 and RTX 30 series on Ampere

- **Date:** 2020-09-17
- **Who:** Nvidia
- **Type:** hardware

Nvidia unveiled the GeForce RTX 3070, RTX 3080 and RTX 3090 around 1 September 2020. The RTX 3080 went on sale on 17 September, the RTX 3090 on 24 September, and the RTX 3070 in October. The cards used the Ampere microarchitecture, the same family as the A100 data-center GPU. They put Ampere tensor cores in consumer hands, which made desktop GPUs a practical option for local deep-learning work.
