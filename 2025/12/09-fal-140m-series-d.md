# Fal raises $140M Series D led by Sequoia

- **Date:** 2025-12-09
- **Who:** Fal (generative media inference platform); Sequoia (lead), Kleiner Perkins, NVentures, Alkeon Capital
- **Type:** funding

Fal announced a $140 million Series D for its real-time generative media infrastructure, led by Sequoia with Kleiner Perkins and Nvidia's NVentures participating. One report put the valuation at $4.5 billion, including secondary sales. The round signals investor appetite for inference platforms that serve image and video models to developers.
