# Kuaishou releases Kling 1.6 video model

- **Date:** 2024-12-19
- **Who:** Kuaishou (Kling AI)
- **Type:** model

Kuaishou released Kling 1.6, an AI video model, in December 2024, with a Standard tier at up to 720p and a Pro tier at up to 1080p. The Pro tier adds first-and-last-frame control, where the user sets the opening and closing frames. The exact launch day and these tier details come from a secondary glossary source, not Kuaishou's own announcement.
