# Apple releases OpenELM open language models

- **Date:** 2024-04-24
- **Who:** Apple
- **Type:** model

Apple released OpenELM, a family of small open language models (270M to 3B parameters, base and instruction-tuned) meant to run on devices. Apple published weights, training logs, checkpoints and training configurations on the Hugging Face Hub, all trained on public datasets, plus code to convert the models for Apple's MLX framework. Its license was not a standard open-source license, which The Register flagged. It was Apple's first public release of its own LLM weights.
