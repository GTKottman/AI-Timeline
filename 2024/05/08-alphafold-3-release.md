# Google DeepMind and Isomorphic Labs introduce AlphaFold 3

- **Date:** 2024-05-08
- **Who:** Google DeepMind; Isomorphic Labs
- **Type:** model

AlphaFold 3 predicts the structure and interactions of biomolecules, including proteins, DNA, RNA and ligands, extending AlphaFold 2 beyond single proteins. It used a diffusion-based approach. The model was a major step for AI in biology and drug discovery.
