# Tyler Perry puts $800M studio expansion on hold over Sora

- **Date:** 2024-02-23
- **Who:** Tyler Perry; OpenAI Sora
- **Type:** other

Filmmaker Tyler Perry paused a planned $800 million studio expansion after seeing OpenAI's Sora video samples, citing concern that generative video could reduce the need for physical sets and crews. It was an early, concrete example of AI video changing production investment decisions in Hollywood.
