# Amazon fall 2019 event: Echo Studio and Alexa multilingual mode

- **Date:** 2019-09-25
- **Who:** Amazon (Alexa, Echo, Ring, Eero)
- **Type:** product

At its September 25, 2019 devices event Amazon introduced a large Echo lineup, including the Echo Studio, a $199 speaker with five drivers and Dolby Atmos support that it called its first high-end smart speaker. Alexa software news in the same event included a multilingual mode and paid celebrity voices. Its voice assistant was pushed further into everyday home hardware. Echo Studio and Alexa feature details come from aggregated coverage rather than the original articles.
