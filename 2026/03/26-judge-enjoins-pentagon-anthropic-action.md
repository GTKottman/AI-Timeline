# Federal judge issues temporary injunction against Pentagon action on Anthropic

- **Date:** 2026-03-26
- **Who:** US federal court; Anthropic; Pentagon
- **Type:** policy

A federal judge issued a temporary injunction against the Pentagon's actions against Anthropic. The ruling paused the supply-chain-risk measures while the case proceeded.
