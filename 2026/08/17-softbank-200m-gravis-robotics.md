# SoftBank invests $200 million in Gravis Robotics

- **Date:** 2026-08-17
- **Who:** SoftBank, Gravis Robotics (Zurich)
- **Type:** funding

Gravis Robotics announced on August 17, 2026 that SoftBank is investing $200 million in its Series A, a round reported at a $1 billion post-money valuation (a press figure not stated in the company's release). Gravis makes retrofit autonomy kits for existing excavators and other heavy machines rather than building machines, and it spun out of ETH Zurich in 2022. The company claims up to 30% jobsite productivity gains, based on its own project data, and calls the round the largest Series A in construction robotics. Those claims are unverified.
