# Deep Voice 2: multi-speaker neural text-to-speech (arXiv)

- **Date:** 2017-05-24
- **Who:** Sercan Ö. Arık, Gregory Diamos, Andrew Gibiansky, John Miller, Kainan Peng, Wei Ping, Jonathan Raiman, Yanqi Zhou (Baidu Silicon Valley AI Lab)
- **Type:** paper

Baidu's "Deep Voice 2" (arXiv 1705.08947) added learned speaker embeddings to neural text-to-speech, so one model could produce hundreds of voices. The authors reported that each speaker needed under half an hour of data while keeping speaker identity close to the original. It was published at NIPS 2017 and showed that neural TTS could scale to many speakers from little data.
