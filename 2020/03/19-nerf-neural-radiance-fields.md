# NeRF: neural radiance fields for view synthesis

- **Date:** 2020-03-19
- **Who:** Mildenhall, Srinivasan, Tancik, Barron, Ramamoorthi, Ng (UC Berkeley and collaborators)
- **Type:** paper

The arXiv paper "NeRF: Representing Scenes as Neural Radiance Fields for View Synthesis" represented a scene as a neural network that maps a 3D position and viewing direction to density and colour. Novel views are rendered by passing camera rays through the network and applying classic volume rendering. The method, fitted from a sparse set of posed photos, started a large line of neural 3D scene reconstruction work.
