# European Commission publishes "Artificial Intelligence for Europe"

- **Date:** 2018-04-25
- **Who:** European Commission
- **Type:** policy

The Commission published COM(2018) 237, its first strategy on AI. It rests on three pillars: public and private investment in AI, preparing for socio-economic change through education and labour-market measures, and an ethical and legal framework based on EU values and the Charter of Fundamental Rights. It was a formal EU response to the European Council's call for a common approach to AI.
