# Montreal Declaration for a Responsible Development of AI is officially launched

- **Date:** 2018-12-04
- **Who:** Universite de Montreal with the Fonds de recherche du Quebec and partners including CIRANO
- **Type:** policy

The Montreal Declaration for a Responsible Development of Artificial Intelligence was officially launched in Montreal. It is a set of ethical principles for AI development, built on more than a year of consultations with citizens, experts, policymakers and industry. A preliminary version had come out of the Forum Responsible AI in November 2017. The declaration was an early national-level attempt to set out responsible AI principles outside the big tech companies.
