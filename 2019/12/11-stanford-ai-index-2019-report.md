# Stanford HAI releases the 2019 AI Index report

- **Date:** 2019-12-11
- **Who:** Stanford Institute for Human-Centered AI (HAI)
- **Type:** research

The AI Index report tracked the state of AI across research output, data sets, and economic and policy trends. It tracked three times as many data sets as the 2018 edition. MIT's Initiative on the Digital Economy reported that the report was released on December 11, 2019. It became a standard reference for measuring AI progress.
