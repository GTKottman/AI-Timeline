# Meta unveils Emu Video and Emu Edit research models

- **Date:** 2023-11-16
- **Who:** Meta AI
- **Type:** model

Meta announced Emu Video, a text-to-video model that first generates an image from the prompt and then a video from the text and image, producing 512x512, four-second clips at 16 frames per second. It also announced Emu Edit, an image-editing model driven by text instructions. Meta said raters preferred Emu Video over Make-A-Video by 96% on quality and 85% on faithfulness. Both were research milestones built on the Emu image model shown at Connect in September.
