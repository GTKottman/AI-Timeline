# AWS makes SageMaker Ground Truth generally available

- **Date:** 2018-11-28
- **Who:** Amazon Web Services
- **Type:** product

At re:Invent 2018, AWS made SageMaker Ground Truth generally available in five regions. The service labels training data by combining human annotators (Mechanical Turk, third-party vendors or the customer's own employees) with a model that learns from their labels and applies labels to much of the rest. AWS said it reduces costs by up to 70% compared with human annotation. It mattered as a managed tool for the data-labeling step that most ML projects spend the most effort on.
