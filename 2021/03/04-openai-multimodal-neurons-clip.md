# OpenAI publishes multimodal neurons in CLIP

- **Date:** 2021-03-04
- **Who:** OpenAI (with Distill)
- **Type:** research

OpenAI and Distill published an analysis of CLIP, showing that individual neurons respond to the same concept whether it appears as a picture, as text, or as a symbol. The best-known example was a "Spider-Man" neuron. The work also exposed "typographic attacks", where text written on an object fools the classifier, which made the robustness limits of vision-language models concrete.
