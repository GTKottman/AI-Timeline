# Tencent Hunyuan-T1 reasoning model

- **Date:** 2025-03-21
- **Who:** Tencent
- **Type:** model

Tencent released Hunyuan-T1, a reasoning model built on its TurboS base with a hybrid Transformer-Mamba mixture-of-experts design, trained with large-scale reinforcement learning. Tencent claims 87.2 on MMLU-Pro and prices it at 1 yuan per million input tokens and 4 yuan per million output tokens, which it pitched against DeepSeek-R1. All figures are Tencent's own. Coverage gives March 21 or 22, 2025 as the launch day.
