# Sam Altman testifies at Senate AI oversight hearing

- **Date:** 2023-05-16
- **Who:** OpenAI (Sam Altman); IBM (Christina Montgomery); Senate Judiciary Subcommittee on Privacy, Technology and the Law (Richard Blumenthal, Josh Hawley)
- **Type:** policy

Sam Altman testified before the Senate Judiciary Subcommittee in a hearing titled "Oversight of AI: Rules for Artificial Intelligence." Altman argued that government intervention would be critical to mitigating the risks of powerful models and voiced support for a national AI regulator. IBM's Christina Montgomery also testified. Al Jazeera framed it as the first in a series of congressional hearings on AI rules.
