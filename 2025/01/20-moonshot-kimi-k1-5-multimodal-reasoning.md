# Moonshot AI launches Kimi k1.5, a multimodal reasoning model

- **Date:** 2025-01-20
- **Who:** Moonshot AI
- **Type:** model

Moonshot AI released Kimi k1.5, a multimodal model that accepts text and images and was trained for long-chain-of-thought reasoning, with a short-chain variant for concise answers. Moonshot's technical report says both variants match or beat OpenAI o1 and DeepSeek-R1 on reasoning benchmarks. The model was offered free through the Kimi assistant. Sources disagree on the launch day (January 19 or 20), and on whether the weights are open or proprietary.
