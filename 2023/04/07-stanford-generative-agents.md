# Stanford and Google publish "Generative Agents" paper

- **Date:** 2023-04-07
- **Who:** Stanford University and Google Research
- **Type:** research

The paper introduced generative agents: language-model characters that store their experiences in natural language, reflect on them over time and retrieve memories to plan behaviour. The authors ran 25 such agents in a Sims-style town sandbox and found they produced believable social behaviour. It mattered as a template for memory, reflection and planning architectures in later agent systems. It was published at UIST 2023.
