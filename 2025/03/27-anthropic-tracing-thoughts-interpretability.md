# Anthropic publishes circuit-tracing research on how Claude thinks

- **Date:** 2025-03-27
- **Who:** Anthropic
- **Type:** research

Anthropic published two interpretability papers, "Circuit Tracing: Revealing Computational Graphs in Language Models" and "On the Biology of a Large Language Model." They use attribution graphs to follow the internal steps of Claude 3.5 Haiku. The findings include a shared concept space across languages, planning several words ahead when writing rhyming poetry, and parallel paths for mental arithmetic that differ from the method the model describes. The work was a step toward inspecting how large models reach their answers.
