# Workday acquires AI company Sana for $1.1 billion

- **Date:** 2025-09-16
- **Who:** Workday, Sana
- **Type:** acquisition

Workday agreed to buy Sana, the AI company behind the Sana Learn and Sana Agents products, for about $1.1 billion. The definitive agreement was announced on September 16, 2025. Workday plans to use Sana's technology for an employee-facing experience that combines knowledge, data, actions and learning. Sana says its products serve over one million users across hundreds of enterprises.
