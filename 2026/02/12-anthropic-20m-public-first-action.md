# Anthropic gives $20M to Public First Action

- **Date:** 2026-02-12
- **Who:** Anthropic; Public First Action (501(c)(4) advocacy group)
- **Type:** policy

Anthropic announced a $20 million donation to Public First Action, a group backing congressional candidates who favor AI safety rules and planning to support 30 to 50 bipartisan candidates. Anthropic said the money is for public education and policy work and cannot be used to influence the election of any candidate. The gift put a frontier lab's money behind a pro-regulation political push ahead of the 2026 elections.
