# Meta AI publishes Toolformer

- **Date:** 2023-02-09
- **Who:** Meta AI Research and Universitat Pompeu Fabra (Timo Schick and colleagues)
- **Type:** paper

The paper "Toolformer: Language Models Can Teach Themselves to Use Tools" was submitted to arXiv on 9 February 2023. The model learns in a self-supervised way when to call external APIs such as a calculator, search engine, translator and calendar, and how to use the results in later text, with only a few demonstrations per tool. A 6.7B-parameter GPT-J model with tool use beat the 175B GPT-3 on selected tasks. The work became an early reference point for tool-using language model agents.
