# Samsung Galaxy S24 ships with Google Gemini Nano and Gemini Pro

- **Date:** 2024-01-17
- **Who:** Samsung; Google
- **Type:** product

Samsung unveiled the Galaxy S24 lineup at its Unpacked event in San Jose on January 17, 2024, with Google's Gemini built in. Gemini Nano ran on-device tasks, and Gemini Pro, run through Google Cloud, powered cloud features such as in Samsung Notes and Voice Recorder. Android Authority reported that Samsung was the first Android maker to use Gemini Pro and Gemini Nano on the same phone.
