# Toyota, DENSO and SoftBank Vision Fund Invest $1B in Uber's Advanced Technologies Group

- **Date:** 2019-04-18
- **Who:** Uber (Advanced Technologies Group), Toyota, DENSO, SoftBank Vision Fund
- **Type:** funding

Uber announced a $1 billion investment in its self-driving unit, Advanced Technologies Group, from Toyota, DENSO and the SoftBank Vision Fund. The joint announcement valued the new ATG entity at $7.25 billion post-money. Toyota also agreed to contribute up to another $300 million over three years. The deal was a major funding event for a self-driving AI program, weeks before Uber's IPO.
