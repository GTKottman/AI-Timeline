# Deep reinforcement learning from human preferences paper

- **Date:** 2017-06-12
- **Who:** Paul Christiano, Jan Leike, Tom Brown, Miljan Martic, Shane Legg, Dario Amodei (OpenAI and DeepMind)
- **Type:** paper

The paper showed that agents can learn complex behaviours, including Atari games and simulated robot locomotion, from comparisons of short trajectory segments judged by non-expert humans, without access to the reward function. Human input was under 1% of the agent's environment interactions. It is an early basis for the reward-modelling ideas later used in RLHF.
