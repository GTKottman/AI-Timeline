# Stability AI releases Stable Diffusion 2.0

- **Date:** 2022-11-24
- **Who:** Stability AI (with LAION and OpenCLIP)
- **Type:** model

Stability AI released Stable Diffusion 2.0 as open software. The release switched to the OpenCLIP text encoder developed with LAION, generated at 512 and 768 pixels, added an upscaler for 2048-pixel output, and trained on an aesthetic and filtered subset of LAION-5B. It also introduced depth-to-image and updated inpainting models, and it removed artist-style prompting, which drew user backlash.
