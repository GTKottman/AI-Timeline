# DeepMind launches Ethics & Society research unit

- **Date:** 2017-10-00
- **Who:** DeepMind (Google)
- **Type:** research

DeepMind announced a research unit called DeepMind Ethics & Society to study the social and ethical impact of AI, with a team dedicated to the question. It was one of the first research groups at a major AI lab to be set up specifically for AI ethics and policy.
