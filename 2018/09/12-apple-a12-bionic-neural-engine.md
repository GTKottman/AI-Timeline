# Apple's A12 Bionic adds an eight-core Neural Engine to the iPhone XS

- **Date:** 2018-09-12
- **Who:** Apple
- **Type:** hardware

Apple announced the A12 Bionic with an eight-core Neural Engine for its iPhone XS and XS Max. Apple said the engine could handle about 5 trillion operations per second, against 600 billion for the A11. A "smart compute system" chooses between CPU, GPU and Neural Engine for each task. Apple also opened the engine to Core ML, making it an on-device acceleration target for third-party machine learning apps.
