# Level AI $20M Series B

- **Date:** 2022-03-01
- **Who:** Level AI; led by Battery Ventures
- **Type:** funding

Level AI, a company that applies machine learning to customer-service conversations, announced a $20 million Series B led by Battery Ventures. It is a small deal, but it shows venture money flowing into applied conversational AI during early 2022.
