# Inflection AI launches with $225M

- **Date:** 2022-05-13
- **Who:** Inflection AI (Reid Hoffman, Mustafa Suleyman, Karen Simonyan)
- **Type:** funding

Inflection AI, a Palo Alto company founded in 2022, disclosed a $225 million equity raise in a Form D filed May 13, 2022 (first sale April 28, 2022). The filing names Suleyman, Hoffman and Simonyan as related persons, and 27 investors. Investors were not named at the time. The company was set up to build consumer conversational AI, which it later launched as the Pi chatbot.
