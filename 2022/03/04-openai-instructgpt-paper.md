# OpenAI InstructGPT paper: training language models to follow instructions

- **Date:** 2022-03-04
- **Who:** OpenAI (Long Ouyang, Jeff Wu and co-authors)
- **Type:** paper

OpenAI posted the InstructGPT paper, "Training language models to follow instructions with human feedback" (arXiv 2203.02155, 4 March 2022). It fine-tunes GPT-3 in three stages: supervised learning on labeler demonstrations, a reward model trained on human rankings of outputs, and reinforcement learning against that reward model. Labelers preferred outputs from the 1.3B InstructGPT model over those of the 175B GPT-3.
