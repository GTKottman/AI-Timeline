# Google DeepMind introduces Gemini, a natively multimodal model family

- **Date:** 2023-12-06
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** model

Google DeepMind introduced Gemini, a family of multimodal models that work across text, image, audio, video and code. It was positioned as Google's most capable model at launch and a direct competitor to GPT-4.
