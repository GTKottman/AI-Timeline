# Amazon Alexa launches in Japan

- **Date:** 2017-11-08
- **Who:** Amazon
- **Type:** product

Amazon announced that Alexa and the Echo, Echo Plus and Echo Dot were available in Japan, with Japanese-language support and over 250 skills from Japanese developers at launch. Access was by invitation at first, and the devices began shipping the following week. It was one of the first major expansions of Alexa beyond English.
