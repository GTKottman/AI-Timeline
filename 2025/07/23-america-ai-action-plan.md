# White House publishes America's AI Action Plan with three executive orders

- **Date:** 2025-07-23
- **Who:** US White House
- **Type:** policy

The White House published America's AI Action Plan and signed three accompanying executive orders. The plan set out federal priorities for AI development, infrastructure and exports. It followed the January revocation of the previous administration's AI safety order.
