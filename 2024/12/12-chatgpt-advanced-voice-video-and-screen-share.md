# ChatGPT Advanced Voice gains live video and screen sharing

- **Date:** 2024-12-12
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** product

OpenAI rolled out live video and screen sharing inside Advanced Voice Mode on Day 6 of its "12 Days" event. It started in the ChatGPT mobile apps for paid tiers, and usage was capped daily per plan. The feature had been demoed in May 2024, so the rollout came about seven months after the demo.
