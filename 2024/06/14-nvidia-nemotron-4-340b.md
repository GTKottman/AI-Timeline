# Nvidia releases Nemotron-4 340B model family

- **Date:** 2024-06-14
- **Who:** Nvidia
- **Type:** model

Nvidia released Nemotron-4 340B, a family of Base, Instruct and Reward models trained on about 9 trillion tokens and shipped under the Nvidia Open Model License with weights on Hugging Face. Nvidia framed it mainly as a way to generate synthetic training data, with the Instruct model producing data and the Reward model filtering it. It was optimized for NeMo and TensorRT-LLM, and it was one of the largest fully open-weight models of 2024.
