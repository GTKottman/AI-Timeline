# Swin Transformer wins ICCV 2021 Marr Prize

- **Date:** 2021-10-00
- **Who:** Ze Liu, Yutong Lin, Yue Cao, Han Hu et al. (Microsoft Research Asia with USTC, Xi'an Jiaotong, Tsinghua)
- **Type:** competition

The Swin Transformer paper, "Hierarchical Vision Transformer Using Shifted Windows," received the ICCV 2021 Marr Prize, the conference's top paper honor. The model limits self-attention to shifted local windows, which gives linear cost in image size and makes transformers practical as general vision backbones for classification, detection and segmentation. It reported 87.3% top-1 on ImageNet-1K and 58.7 box AP on COCO test-dev. The exact award day during the October conference was not confirmed.
