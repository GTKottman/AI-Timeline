# Alibaba unveils Tongyi Qianwen chatbot model

- **Date:** 2023-04-11
- **Who:** Alibaba Cloud
- **Type:** model

Alibaba unveiled Tongyi Qianwen, a large language model trained mainly on Chinese and English text, and said it would embed it in DingTalk, Tmall Genie and its other business products and open it to cloud customers. The launch was a direct answer to ChatGPT and Baidu's Ernie Bot. It mattered as one of the first large-scale Chinese-company chatbot launches after ChatGPT. Reports differ on the exact day: one says Alibaba first announced it on April 7 through WeChat, and the cloud-summit unveiling is dated April 11.
