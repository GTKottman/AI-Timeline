# John Hopfield and Geoffrey Hinton win the Nobel Prize in Physics

- **Date:** 2024-10-08
- **Who:** John Hopfield; Geoffrey Hinton
- **Type:** people

The Royal Swedish Academy awarded the physics prize for foundational discoveries that enable machine learning with artificial neural networks. Hopfield's associative memory network and Hinton's Boltzmann machine work were cited. It was a recognition of the field's roots and a high-profile moment for AI within science.
