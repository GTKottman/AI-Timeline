# Zhipu releases open-source GLM-4.5 and GLM-4.5-Air

- **Date:** 2025-07-28
- **Who:** Zhipu AI (Z.ai)
- **Type:** model

Zhipu released GLM-4.5 as open source, which it called its most powerful model so far. The full model has 355 billion total parameters with 32 billion active, and GLM-4.5-Air has 106 billion total with 12 billion active. Both support hybrid thinking and non-thinking modes with a 128K context window. The Hugging Face model card lists the weights under the MIT license.
