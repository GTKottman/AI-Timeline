# Mistral releases Pixtral 12B, an open-weight vision-language model

- **Date:** 2024-09-11
- **Who:** Mistral AI
- **Type:** model

Mistral released Pixtral 12B, a 12-billion-parameter model that takes text and images together, built on its Nemo 12B language model with a vision encoder of roughly 400 million parameters. The weights went out first by torrent under the Apache 2.0 license, and Mistral said the model would come to its chat and API platforms later. Sources give dates between September 1 and 17; the September 11 date is the one most reports support.
