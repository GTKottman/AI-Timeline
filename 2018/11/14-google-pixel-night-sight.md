# Google launches Night Sight for low-light photos on Pixel phones

- **Date:** 2018-11-14
- **Who:** Google (Pixel camera team)
- **Type:** product

Google introduced Night Sight, a Pixel camera mode that takes sharp, low-noise photos in very dim light without a tripod or flash. It builds on the HDR+ burst pipeline and uses a learning-based white balancer, with exposure and frame count chosen from motion and stability. It worked on the main and selfie cameras of all three Pixel generations, showing learned image processing as a consumer feature.
