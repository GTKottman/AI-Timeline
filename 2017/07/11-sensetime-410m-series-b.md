# SenseTime Closes $410 Million Series B

- **Date:** 2017-07-11
- **Who:** SenseTime; CDH Investments (B1 lead); Sailing Capital (B2 lead)
- **Type:** funding

Chinese computer vision startup SenseTime announced a $410 million Series B round, which it described as a record for a single AI funding round. CDH Investments led the B1 tranche and Sailing Capital led B2. The company said the money would go to AI infrastructure, product expansion and new areas such as autonomous driving. The round made SenseTime one of China's most heavily funded AI companies at the time.
