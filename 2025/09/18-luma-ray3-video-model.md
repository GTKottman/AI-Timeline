# Luma AI releases Ray3 video model

- **Date:** 2025-09-18
- **Who:** Luma AI
- **Type:** model

Luma AI released Ray3, a video-generation model in its Ray family. It added to the wave of text-to-video systems released in late 2025 that competed with Google Veo and OpenAI Sora on realistic motion and length.
