# Google's LYNA deep learning tool detects breast cancer spread in lymph nodes

- **Date:** 2018-10-12
- **Who:** Google AI (pathology research team)
- **Type:** research

Google AI presented LYNA, a deep learning tool that detects breast cancer that has spread to lymph nodes. The post reported studies in which it distinguished slides with cancer from those without in 99% of cases. In a simulated diagnostic task, six pathologists using LYNA detected small metastases more reliably and reviewed slides in about half the time. It showed a medical imaging model helping human experts directly.
