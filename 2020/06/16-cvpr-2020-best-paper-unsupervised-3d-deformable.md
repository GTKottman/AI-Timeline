# CVPR 2020 best paper: unsupervised learning of 3D deformable objects

- **Date:** 2020-06-16
- **Who:** Oxford VGG (S. Wu, C. Rupprecht, A. Vedaldi), CVPR 2020 organisers
- **Type:** competition

The main CVPR 2020 best paper award went to "Unsupervised Learning of Probably Symmetric Deformable 3D Objects from Images in the Wild," which learns 3D shape and pose from ordinary image collections without annotated 3D data. The award was given during the fully virtual conference, which received a record 5,865 valid submissions and accepted 1,467 papers. It marked the year's shift of top vision research to online conference formats.
