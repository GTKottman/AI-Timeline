# Graphcore announces Bow IPU with wafer-on-wafer technology

- **Date:** 2022-03-03
- **Who:** Graphcore (built with TSMC)
- **Type:** hardware

Graphcore announced the Bow IPU, its third-generation AI processor and the first built with 3D wafer-on-wafer stacking, developed with TSMC. Graphcore claimed up to 40% higher performance and 16% better power efficiency on real AI workloads than its predecessor, with the same core count and memory. Bow Pod systems were announced as shipping, adding a non-GPU training option to the market.
