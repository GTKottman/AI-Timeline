# Anysphere (Cursor) raises $900 million at $9.9 billion valuation

- **Date:** 2025-06-00
- **Who:** Anysphere (Cursor); Thrive Capital (lead), Andreessen Horowitz, Accel, DST Global
- **Type:** funding

Anysphere, maker of the Cursor AI code editor, raised $900 million at a $9.9 billion valuation, led by Thrive Capital. The company said its annualized revenue passed $500 million and was doubling about every two months. It mattered as one of the largest AI coding-tool rounds of 2025.

Exact day not confirmed from the sources (Bloomberg and TechCrunch coverage, June 2025).
