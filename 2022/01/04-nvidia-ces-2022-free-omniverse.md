# NVIDIA CES 2022 keynote makes Omniverse free for individual creators

- **Date:** 2022-01-04
- **Who:** NVIDIA
- **Type:** product

At its CES 2022 keynote on January 4, 2022, NVIDIA announced a free version of its Omniverse 3D platform for individual creators with GeForce RTX or RTX GPUs. Omniverse Enterprise stayed the paid tier for businesses. The keynote also added Nucleus Cloud file sharing and Audio2Face updates such as blendshape support and export to MetaHuman Creator. Coverage did not confirm Omniverse Avatar as part of the CES announcements.
