# Ilya Sutskever and Jan Leike leave OpenAI; Superalignment team dissolved

- **Date:** 2024-05-17
- **Who:** OpenAI; Ilya Sutskever; Jan Leike
- **Type:** people

OpenAI co-founder and chief scientist Ilya Sutskever announced his departure on May 14, followed by alignment lead Jan Leike on May 17, who wrote that safety culture and processes had "taken a backseat to shiny products." OpenAI disbanded the Superalignment team and integrated its members across its research efforts. The departures put AI safety governance at a lab under public scrutiny.
