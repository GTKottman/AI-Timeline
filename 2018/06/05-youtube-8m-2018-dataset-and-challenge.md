# Google updates YouTube-8M and launches the second video understanding challenge

- **Date:** 2018-06-05
- **Who:** Google Research
- **Type:** dataset

Google released an updated YouTube-8M dataset with 6.1 million video URLs labeled with 3,862 visual entities, averaging about three labels per video. It also launched the second YouTube-8M video understanding challenge on Kaggle, which capped final model size at 1GB and gave travel awards to the top five teams, with a workshop at ECCV 2018. It gave video researchers a large labeled benchmark.
