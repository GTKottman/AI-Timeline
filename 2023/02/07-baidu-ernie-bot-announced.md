# Baidu announces ERNIE Bot (Wenxin Yiyan)

- **Date:** 2023-02-07
- **Who:** Baidu
- **Type:** product

Baidu announced ERNIE Bot, a ChatGPT-style chatbot built on its ERNIE large language model, and said it would finish internal testing and launch publicly in March 2023. Baidu's Hong Kong shares rose about 15% on the news, which showed how strongly the ChatGPT wave was reaching Chinese tech markets. It was one of the first major Chinese competitors to ChatGPT and a sign that large language model chatbots would be built by the big Chinese platforms as well as US labs.
