# Anthropic raises $3.5 billion Series E at a $61.5 billion valuation

- **Date:** 2025-03-03
- **Who:** Anthropic; Lightspeed Venture Partners (lead), Bessemer, Cisco Investments, D1 Capital, Fidelity, General Catalyst, Jane Street, Menlo Ventures, Salesforce Ventures
- **Type:** funding

Anthropic raised $3.5 billion in a Series E at a $61.5 billion post-money valuation, led by Lightspeed Venture Partners. The money was for AI development and expanding compute capacity. The date is taken from a Monday announcement reported by law firm Orrick on March 3, 2025. One source says Lightspeed put in $1 billion, but the sources checked did not confirm this.
