# OpenAI announces reinforcement fine-tuning alpha for o1 models

- **Date:** 2024-12-06
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** software

On Day 2 of OpenAI's "12 Days" event, OpenAI previewed reinforcement fine-tuning (RFT) for its o1 models. Developers supply tasks and graders that score answers against reference answers, and the model is trained to reason toward correct answers. It launched as an alpha research program for universities, research institutes and enterprises, with a wider release expected in 2025. The exact day of the Day 2 livestream is not given in the sources; the event began on December 5.
