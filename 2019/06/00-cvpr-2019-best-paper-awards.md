# CVPR 2019 Best Paper Awards

- **Date:** 2019-06-00
- **Who:** CVPR 2019 organizers; winners from Carnegie Mellon, University of Toronto, and the StyleGAN authors (Karras, Laine, Aila)
- **Type:** other

CVPR 2019 gave its Best Paper to "A Theory of Fermat Paths for Non-Line-of-Sight Shape Reconstruction" (Carnegie Mellon and University of Toronto). The StyleGAN paper, "A Style-Based Generator Architecture for Generative Adversarial Networks," received a Best Paper Honorable Mention, and it went on to shape high-quality GAN image synthesis. The exact award day was not confirmed in the sources, so only the month is recorded.
