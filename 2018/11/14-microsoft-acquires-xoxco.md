# Microsoft agrees to acquire XOXCO, makers of Botkit and Howdy

- **Date:** 2018-11-14
- **Who:** Microsoft (Lili Cheng, corporate VP of conversational AI); XOXCO
- **Type:** acquisition

Microsoft announced an agreement to acquire XOXCO, an Austin studio behind the Botkit bot-development toolkit and the Howdy Slack meeting-scheduling bot. Terms were not disclosed. Microsoft framed the deal as bringing bot developer communities into its conversational AI work, and said it would keep supporting Howdy. It mattered as part of Microsoft's push to own developer tooling for chatbots and conversational AI.
