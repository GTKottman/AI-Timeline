# Nvidia Jarvis conversational AI framework enters open beta

- **Date:** 2020-10-05
- **Who:** Nvidia
- **Type:** software

Jarvis, first shown in May 2020, was put into open beta at Nvidia's GTC 2020 conference. It is a GPU-accelerated framework that gives developers pre-trained speech recognition, language understanding and speech synthesis pipelines for building conversational AI services. It was aimed at customer support and real-time transcription, and it packaged speech models that had been hard to deploy into a single toolkit.
