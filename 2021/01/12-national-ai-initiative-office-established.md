# White House OSTP establishes the National AI Initiative Office

- **Date:** 2021-01-12
- **Who:** White House Office of Science and Technology Policy (OSTP)
- **Type:** policy

On January 12, 2021, OSTP set up the National Artificial Intelligence Initiative Office to coordinate federal AI research, funding and standards work under the newly enacted National AI Initiative Act. The office gave the Act an operating body, which was needed to run the research-resource task force and the cross-agency programs the law created.
