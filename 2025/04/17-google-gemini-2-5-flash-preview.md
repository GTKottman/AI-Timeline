# Google releases Gemini 2.5 Flash in preview

- **Date:** 2025-04-17
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** model

Google released an early preview of Gemini 2.5 Flash through the Gemini API, Google AI Studio and Vertex AI. Google called it its first fully hybrid reasoning model, which lets developers switch thinking on or off and set a thinking budget. Gemini 2.5 Flash and 2.5 Pro became generally available on June 17, 2025, per SD Times.
