# DeepMind DeepNash masters Stratego in Science

- **Date:** 2022-12-01
- **Who:** DeepMind
- **Type:** research

DeepMind published DeepNash in Science, a model-free multi-agent reinforcement learning agent for Stratego, an imperfect-information game. DeepNash learns by self-play with a method called Regularised Nash Dynamics, with no tree search or opponent modeling. It won over 97% of games against existing Stratego bots and reached an 84% win rate against human experts on the Gravon platform, ranking in the top three for 2022.
