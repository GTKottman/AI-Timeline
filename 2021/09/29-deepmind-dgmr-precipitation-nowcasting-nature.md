# DeepMind publishes DGMR generative rain nowcasting model in Nature

- **Date:** 2021-09-29
- **Who:** DeepMind; UK Met Office
- **Type:** research

DeepMind and the UK Met Office published "Skilful precipitation nowcasting using deep generative models of radar" in Nature. The Deep Generative Model of Rain (DGMR) takes 20 minutes of radar history and produces probabilistic forecasts up to 90 minutes ahead. Expert meteorologists ranked it first for accuracy and usefulness in 89% of cases against two competing methods, a strong showing for a generative model in a physical forecasting task.
