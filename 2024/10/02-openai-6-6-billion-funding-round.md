# OpenAI closes $6.6 billion funding round at $157 billion valuation

- **Date:** 2024-10-02
- **Who:** OpenAI, Thrive Capital, Microsoft, Nvidia, SoftBank
- **Type:** funding

OpenAI closed a $6.6 billion funding round at a $157 billion post-money valuation, nearly double the roughly $80 billion valuation of February 2024. Thrive Capital led the round, with Microsoft, Nvidia, SoftBank, Khosla Ventures and Fidelity also taking part. Reports said investors could reclaim their money if OpenAI did not complete its move to a for-profit structure, which made the round partly a bet on that restructuring.
