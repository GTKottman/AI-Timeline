# Google releases Gemini 2.0 Flash Thinking Experimental (1219)

- **Date:** 2024-12-19
- **Who:** Google DeepMind (Jeff Dean announced)
- **Type:** model

Google released gemini-2.0-flash-thinking-exp-1219, an experimental model built on Gemini 2.0 Flash that generates an explicit thinking process before its answer. It was free in Google AI Studio and the Gemini API, with a 32k input token limit and text-only output. Jeff Dean described it as trained to use its thoughts to strengthen reasoning, and LMArena posted that it ranked first across categories at launch.
