# Genesis open-source physics engine and simulation platform launches

- **Date:** 2024-12-19
- **Who:** Genesis project (CMU, Stanford, MIT CSAIL, NVIDIA, Tsinghua and other labs)
- **Type:** software

Genesis launched as an open-source universal physics engine for robotics, with rigid body, MPM, SPH, FEM, PBD and fluid solvers, written in Python. The developers claim 10-80x speedups over GPU stacks such as Isaac Gym and MJX, though these are their own benchmarks. The team said a generative layer that turns natural-language prompts into multimodal simulation data would roll out later.
