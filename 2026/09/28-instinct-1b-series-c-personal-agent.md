# Instinct raises $1 billion Series C for personal AI agent

- **Date:** 2026-09-28
- **Who:** Instinct (founder Noah Shinn); investors Sequoia Capital, Benchmark and Coatue
- **Type:** funding

San Francisco startup Instinct raised $1 billion in a Series C at a reported $10 billion valuation, about four times the valuation of its $250 million Series B a month earlier. The company builds a personal agent that texts or calls on a user's behalf to book trips and tickets, buy groceries, and cancel subscriptions; it launched as an invite-only service in August 2026. One dealroom listing gives a $6.47 billion valuation, and the round does not disclose revenue or user counts.
