# Anthropic Constitutional AI: harmlessness from AI feedback

- **Date:** 2022-12-15
- **Who:** Anthropic
- **Type:** research

Anthropic posted Constitutional AI, a method that trains a model to be harmless using a written set of principles and AI-generated feedback, with less reliance on human labels for harmful content. The approach became a basis for Claude's training.
