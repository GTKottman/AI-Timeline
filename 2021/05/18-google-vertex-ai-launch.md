# Google launches Vertex AI, a unified managed ML platform

- **Date:** 2021-05-18
- **Who:** Google Cloud
- **Type:** product

Google announced general availability of Vertex AI at Google I/O on May 18, 2021. It merged Google Cloud's separate ML services, including AutoML and the AI Platform, into one UI and API for training, deploying and monitoring models. Google also introduced MLOps tools for watching models in production. Google claimed about 80% fewer lines of code to train a model, a vendor figure.
