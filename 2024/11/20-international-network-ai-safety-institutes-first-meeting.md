# International Network of AI Safety Institutes holds first meeting in San Francisco

- **Date:** 2024-11-20
- **Who:** AI safety institutes and government-mandated offices from the US, UK, EU, Japan, Canada, Australia, South Korea, Singapore, France and Kenya
- **Type:** policy

The first meeting of the International Network of AI Safety Institutes took place in San Francisco on November 20 and 21, 2024, growing out of the Seoul commitments of May 2024. The network brings together government AI safety institutes across several countries to share safety-testing work. The European Commission gives the meeting dates as 20 to 21 November; a CSIS analysis had given 21 to 22 November as a pre-event schedule.
