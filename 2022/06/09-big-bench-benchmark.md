# BIG-bench: Beyond the Imitation Game benchmark

- **Date:** 2022-06-09
- **Who:** Google and a large group of collaborators
- **Type:** dataset

BIG-bench is a collaborative benchmark of 204 tasks written by many researchers to test what language models can do and where they fail. It gave the field a shared, broad evaluation suite that went beyond the older narrow benchmarks.
