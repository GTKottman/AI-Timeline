# Anthropic: Red Teaming Language Models to Reduce Harms

- **Date:** 2022-08-23
- **Who:** Anthropic
- **Type:** paper

Anthropic posted a study of red teaming for language models, comparing how harmful outputs scale across model sizes and training methods. It released a dataset of nearly 39,000 adversarial attacks for other researchers to use in safety work.
