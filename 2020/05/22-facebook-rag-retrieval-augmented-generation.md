# Facebook AI introduces retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)

- **Date:** 2020-05-22
- **Who:** Facebook AI Research, with University College London and New York University collaborators
- **Type:** paper

The arXiv paper "Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive NLP Tasks" combined a pretrained sequence-to-sequence generator with a neural retriever over a dense index of Wikipedia. The model drew on external documents while generating text, and it set top results on three open-domain question-answering tasks. RAG became a standard pattern for grounding language models in outside knowledge.
