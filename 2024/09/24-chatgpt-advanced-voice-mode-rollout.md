# OpenAI rolls out Advanced Voice Mode to paying ChatGPT users

- **Date:** 2024-09-24
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** product

OpenAI began rolling out Advanced Voice Mode to paying ChatGPT Plus and Team users, with Enterprise and Edu users to follow the next week. The release added five new voices and a redesigned on-screen interface. The feature had been demoed in May 2024, and it was first shown to a small invited group in July; the September rollout made real-time, speech-to-speech conversation available to a mass audience. Users in the EU, UK, Switzerland, Iceland, Norway and Liechtenstein had to wait.
