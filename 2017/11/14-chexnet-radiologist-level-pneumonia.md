# CheXNet: radiologist-level pneumonia detection on chest X-rays

- **Date:** 2017-11-14
- **Who:** Stanford ML Group (Pranav Rajpurkar, Andrew Ng et al.)
- **Type:** paper

CheXNet is a 121-layer convolutional network trained on the public ChestX-ray14 dataset of 112,120 frontal chest X-rays. It outputs the probability of pneumonia with a heatmap of the regions behind the prediction. The authors reported that it exceeded the average performance of four academic radiologists on their pneumonia test set.
