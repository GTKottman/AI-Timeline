# CVPR 2026 Best Paper: "Efficiently Reconstructing Dynamic Scenes One D4RT at a Time"

- **Date:** 2026-06-05
- **Who:** Oxford Visual Geometry Group with Google DeepMind and University College London (D4RT authors); CVPR 2026 organisers
- **Type:** competition

The CVPR 2026 Best Paper award went to D4RT, a model that predicts a scene's 3D geometry, camera poses, depth and correspondences across video frames for both moving and static scenes, selected from 4,089 accepted papers out of more than 16,000 submissions. CVPR also gave a Best Student Paper award to "Native and Compact Structured Latents for 3D Generation" (TRELLIS.2 is named in one source) and honorable mentions including SAM 3D. The date is the oral-session day shown on the CVPR listing (Fri 5 June) because the exact award-announcement day was not confirmed; the conference itself took place in June 2026.
