# Mozilla Releases Common Voice, Largest Public-Domain Transcribed Voice Dataset

- **Date:** 2019-02-28
- **Who:** Mozilla (Common Voice project)
- **Type:** dataset

Mozilla released a multi-language update to Common Voice, its open speech dataset, covering 18 languages with about 1,400 hours of transcribed voice clips from more than 42,000 people in the public domain. Mozilla said it was the largest publicly available transcribed voice dataset of its kind, and it was built from volunteer recordings. Open speech data like this lets researchers and small teams train speech recognition systems without licensed corpora.
