# Stability AI releases Stable Diffusion 3.5

- **Date:** 2024-10-22
- **Who:** Stability AI
- **Type:** model

Stable Diffusion 3.5 came as a family of three models: Large at 8B parameters, a distilled Large Turbo, and Medium at 2.5B. The open licence allowed use by many small businesses and researchers, keeping open image generation active after Stability's leadership change.
