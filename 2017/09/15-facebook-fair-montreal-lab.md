# Facebook opens AI research lab in Montreal

- **Date:** 2017-09-15
- **Who:** Facebook AI Research (FAIR), Joelle Pineau, McGill University, MILA, CIFAR, Université de Montréal
- **Type:** people

Facebook opened a Facebook AI Research lab in Montreal, led by McGill professor Joelle Pineau, starting with about 10 researchers and aiming to grow. The lab focused on reinforcement learning and dialogue systems, and Facebook pledged $7 million to Montreal's AI research community with partners CIFAR, MILA, McGill and Université de Montréal. It extended the FAIR network beyond the US and Paris and joined Microsoft and Google in building Canadian AI research hubs.
