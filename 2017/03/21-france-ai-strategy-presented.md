# France presents its national AI strategy, "France IA"

- **Date:** 2017-03-21
- **Who:** French government (Prime Minister's office, Ministry of Higher Education and Research, Ministry of Economy and Digital); presented at the Cité des Sciences et de l'Industrie in Paris with the President
- **Type:** policy

The French government presented its "France IA" strategy for developing AI technologies on March 21, 2017 at the Cité des Sciences in Paris. Seventeen working groups had met between January 20 and March 14, 2017 and produced about fifty proposals. The later Villani mission report, which ran from September 2017 to March 2018, was a separate effort.
