# Hugging Face launches Private Hub

- **Date:** 2022-08-03
- **Who:** Hugging Face
- **Type:** product

Hugging Face announced the Private Hub, an enterprise version of its public Hub for hosting models, datasets and Spaces privately. It could run as a managed SaaS service or on a company's own infrastructure. The launch came after the company's May 2022 Series C and was aimed at teams that wanted to keep model work inside their own walls.
