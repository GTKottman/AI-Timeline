# Facebook parent company renamed Meta

- **Date:** 2021-10-28
- **Who:** Meta (formerly Facebook), Mark Zuckerberg
- **Type:** other

At its Connect event on October 28, 2021, Mark Zuckerberg announced that Facebook's parent company would be renamed Meta, with the metaverse as its focus. The social apps Facebook, Instagram, Messenger and WhatsApp kept their names, and the corporate structure did not change. The rebrand matters for AI because the company's VR/AR and metaverse plans rely heavily on AI research and products.
