# US Commerce issues AI Diffusion interim final rule

- **Date:** 2025-01-13
- **Who:** US Bureau of Industry and Security (Department of Commerce)
- **Type:** policy

BIS issued the Framework for Artificial Intelligence Diffusion as an interim final rule, published in the Federal Register on January 15, 2025. It tightened export controls on advanced AI chips, added a control on the model weights of certain closed-weight AI models, and set country tiers with license requirements and exceptions for lower-risk destinations. Compliance was expected to start about 120 days after publication.
