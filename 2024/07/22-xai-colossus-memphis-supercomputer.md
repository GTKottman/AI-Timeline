# xAI brings Colossus 100,000-GPU supercomputer online in Memphis

- **Date:** 2024-07-22
- **Who:** xAI, Elon Musk, NVIDIA
- **Type:** hardware

xAI brought online Colossus, a supercomputer in South Memphis built from about 100,000 NVIDIA Hopper GPUs on Spectrum-X Ethernet, after a build reported at 122 days. It was built to train the Grok models, and the site was later reported to be expanding toward 200,000 GPUs. Aggregator quotes of Musk's post give July 22, 2024; the original post was not found. The figures come mostly from secondary blogs, not xAI.
