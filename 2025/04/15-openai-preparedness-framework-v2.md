# OpenAI Preparedness Framework version 2

- **Date:** 2025-04-15
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** policy

OpenAI published version 2 of its Preparedness Framework, the first update since 2023. It tracks biological and chemical capability, cybersecurity and AI self-improvement, and replaces the older levels with "high" and "critical" capability thresholds. It also says OpenAI may adjust its safety requirements if a rival lab ships a high-risk system without similar protections, which critics saw as a loosening of commitments.
