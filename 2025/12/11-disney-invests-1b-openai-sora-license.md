# Disney invests $1 billion in OpenAI with Sora character licensing deal

- **Date:** 2025-12-11
- **Who:** Disney; OpenAI
- **Type:** funding

Disney agreed to take a $1 billion equity stake in OpenAI and license more than 200 Disney, Marvel, Pixar and Lucasfilm characters for use in Sora videos. The agreement is three years long. It does not cover talent likenesses or voices.
