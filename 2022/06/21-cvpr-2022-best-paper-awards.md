# CVPR 2022 Best Paper Awards

- **Date:** 2022-06-21
- **Who:** CVPR 2022 (IEEE/CVF Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition); winners from ETH Zurich, University of Washington, Georgia Tech, and Czech Technical University
- **Type:** competition

CVPR 2022 in New Orleans gave its Best Paper Award to "Learning to Solve Hard Minimal Problems" by Petr Hruby, Timothy Duff, Anton Leykin and Tomas Pajdla. The conference also named "Dual-Shutter Optical Vibration Sensing" as a Best Paper honorable mention, "EPro-PnP" as the Best Student Paper, and "Ref-NeRF" as a Best Student Paper honorable mention. The awards marked the year's leading computer vision research, including neural radiance fields and learned pose estimation, at the main venue for the field.
