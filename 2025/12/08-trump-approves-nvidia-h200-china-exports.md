# Trump approves Nvidia H200 chip sales to approved Chinese customers

- **Date:** 2025-12-08
- **Who:** Trump administration (White House, Commerce Department); Nvidia
- **Type:** policy

On December 8, 2025 President Trump announced on Truth Social that the US would allow Nvidia H200 sales to approved Chinese customers, with a 25% fee on the chips. Blackwell and the upcoming Rubin chips were excluded. The Commerce Department was still finalizing terms, and reports later said the Bureau of Industry and Security formally approved the sales with a cap on China shipments relative to US sales. It mattered because it set the first real loosening of the AI-chip export line toward China at the frontier-GPU tier.
