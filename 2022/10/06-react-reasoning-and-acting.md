# ReAct: reasoning and acting in language models

- **Date:** 2022-10-06
- **Who:** Princeton University and Google Research
- **Type:** paper

ReAct prompts a language model to alternate reasoning steps with actions, such as queries to Wikipedia or an interactive environment. Interleaving thought and action reduced errors on question answering and interactive tasks, and the pattern became a basis for later LLM agents.
