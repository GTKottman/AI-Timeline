# Zhipu releases GLM-4.6

- **Date:** 2025-09-30
- **Who:** Zhipu AI / Z.ai
- **Type:** model

Zhipu AI (Z.ai) released GLM-4.6, an update to GLM-4.5 with a mixture-of-experts design of about 355 to 357 billion parameters, and a context window expanded from 128K to 200K tokens. It targets agentic coding and reasoning, and its weights were published on Hugging Face under the zai-org organisation. One guide dates the release to September 30, 2025, and a press item reports an MIT licence. The specs rest on aggregator listings and should be checked against Z.ai's own release notes.
