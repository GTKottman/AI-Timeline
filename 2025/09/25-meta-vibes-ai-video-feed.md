# Meta launches Vibes, a short-form feed of AI-generated videos

- **Date:** 2025-09-25
- **Who:** Meta
- **Type:** product

Meta launched Vibes, a feed of short AI-generated videos inside the Meta AI app and on meta.ai. Users make clips from text prompts or remix others' videos, then post to the Vibes feed, send them by DM, or cross-post to Instagram and Facebook. Alexandr Wang said the early version used models from Midjourney and Black Forest Labs while Meta kept building its own. Many users criticised it, and Meta said it would add more tools in the coming months.
