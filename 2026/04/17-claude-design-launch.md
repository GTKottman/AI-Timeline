# Anthropic launches Claude Design

- **Date:** 2026-04-17
- **Who:** Anthropic
- **Type:** product

Anthropic launched Claude Design in research preview for Pro, Max, Team and Enterprise subscribers. It creates visuals such as prototypes, slides and one-pagers from a prompt, which users refine by direct edits or follow-up requests. It is powered by Claude Opus 4.7.
