# New York governor signs RAISE Act on frontier AI safety

- **Date:** 2025-12-19
- **Who:** Governor Kathy Hochul; New York State legislature; frontier AI developers
- **Type:** policy

Governor Hochul signed the Responsible AI Safety and Education (RAISE) Act, which imposes transparency, safety and reporting duties on developers of large frontier AI models. The heaviest obligations apply to developers with more than $500 million in annual revenue. The signing was conditional on chapter amendments moving the law closer to California's model, and it came eight days after a federal executive order aimed at curbing state AI laws, setting up a federal-state conflict.
