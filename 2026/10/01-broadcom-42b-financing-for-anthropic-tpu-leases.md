# Broadcom to lend Anthropic up to $42 billion to lease TPU chips

- **Date:** 2026-10-01
- **Who:** Broadcom; Anthropic
- **Type:** funding

Reuters reported on October 1, 2026, citing Broadcom's IPO filing, that Broadcom will provide up to $42 billion in financing to Anthropic through convertible notes that could convert into Anthropic shares. Proceeds are restricted to Anthropic's leases of TPU capacity that Broadcom co-develops with Google, which cover about a third of a five-year, $125.2 billion commitment. No notes had been issued, and Anthropic does not expect a sale before its IPO. The filing flags potential conflicts of interest, since Broadcom is also the chip supplier.
