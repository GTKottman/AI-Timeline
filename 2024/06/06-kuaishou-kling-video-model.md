# Kuaishou unveils Kling text-to-video model

- **Date:** 2024-06-06
- **Who:** Kuaishou Technology
- **Type:** model

Kuaishou unveiled Kling, its proprietary text-to-video model, and opened it to limited testing. Kuaishou claimed clips up to two minutes long at 30 fps and up to 1080p, positioned as a rival to OpenAI's Sora. Kuaishou announced it on June 6, 2024.
