# Goodfire raises $150M Series B at $1.25B valuation

- **Date:** 2026-02-05
- **Who:** Goodfire (AI interpretability lab); B Capital (lead), with Juniper, Menlo, Lightspeed, Salesforce Ventures and others
- **Type:** funding

Goodfire, an interpretability research lab, announced a $150 million Series B at a $1.25 billion valuation, less than a year after its Series A. The money will fund frontier interpretability research, its core product and partnerships in AI agents and life sciences. The round shows investors paying for tools that explain what models are doing inside.
