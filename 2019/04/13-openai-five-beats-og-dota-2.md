# OpenAI Five beats OG 2-0 in Dota 2

- **Date:** 2019-04-13
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** competition

OpenAI Five won a best-of-three exhibition against Team OG, the reigning The International champions, sweeping 2-0 in San Francisco. Illusions and summoned units were disabled for the matches. OpenAI said the bots had trained continually for about 10 months, showing deep reinforcement learning working in a complex team game.
