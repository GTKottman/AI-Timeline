# Hinton's Forward-Forward algorithm paper

- **Date:** 2022-12-27
- **Who:** Geoffrey Hinton (University of Toronto)
- **Type:** research

Geoffrey Hinton posted the Forward-Forward algorithm, a way to train neural networks with two forward passes, one on real data and one on negative data, instead of backpropagation. It was a proposal for learning procedures that do not need a backward pass.
