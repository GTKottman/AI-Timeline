# Google DeepMind launches SynthID image watermarking

- **Date:** 2023-08-29
- **Who:** Google DeepMind; Google Cloud
- **Type:** software

Google DeepMind announced SynthID, a beta tool that embeds an imperceptible watermark in the pixels of AI-generated images, so they can be identified later. It was first offered to a limited group of testers using Imagen on Vertex AI, and the watermark was designed to survive filters, color changes and brightness adjustments. One report called it the first Big Tech public launch of such a watermark, after companies pledged to develop them at the White House in July.
