# Anthropic runs Super Bowl ads promising an ad-free Claude

- **Date:** 2026-02-04
- **Who:** Anthropic
- **Type:** product

Anthropic made its Super Bowl debut with ads promising Claude would stay free of advertising. It set up a public contrast with ad-supported chatbot plans and became a marker of how assistants would be monetised.
