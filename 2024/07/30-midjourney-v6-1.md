# Midjourney releases Version 6.1

- **Date:** 2024-07-30
- **Who:** Midjourney
- **Type:** model

Midjourney V6.1 improved image coherence, especially for arms, legs, hands and bodies, and made small details such as eyes more precise. It also improved text accuracy when words are included in prompts.
