# Figure announces Helix, a vision-language-action model for humanoid robots

- **Date:** 2025-02-20
- **Who:** Figure AI
- **Type:** model

Figure introduced Helix, a generalist vision-language-action model that controls a humanoid's whole upper body at high rate, including wrists, torso, head and fingers. Figure says two robots can run one model together on a shared task, and that the robots can pick up small household items they have never seen from natural-language prompts. The capability claims come from Figure's own announcement; independent evaluation was not found.
