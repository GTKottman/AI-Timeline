# Lift, Splat, Shoot: Encoding Images From Arbitrary Camera Rigs

- **Date:** 2020-08-13
- **Who:** Jonah Philion and Sanja Fidler
- **Type:** paper

"Lift, Splat, Shoot" was posted to arXiv on 13 August 2020. It encodes images from any camera rig by implicitly unprojecting each camera's features into a 3D bird's-eye-view grid, then pooling them into one top-down representation. That gives self-driving perception a single shared spatial output across arbitrary camera layouts.
