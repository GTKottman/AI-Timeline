# IBM unveils Telum, first processor with on-chip AI inference acceleration

- **Date:** 2021-08-23
- **Who:** IBM (IBM Z and LinuxOne mainframes)
- **Type:** hardware

IBM revealed the Telum processor at the Hot Chips 33 conference in August 2021. It was IBM's first processor with on-chip AI inference acceleration, pitched for real-time fraud detection inside transactions without sending data to off-platform AI. IBM said the chip took about three years to develop and that first Telum-based systems were planned for the first half of 2022. The performance claims were IBM's own.
