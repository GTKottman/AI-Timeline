# Intel Core Ultra (Meteor Lake) goes on sale with first consumer NPU

- **Date:** 2023-12-14
- **Who:** Intel
- **Type:** hardware

Intel's Core Ultra processors, code-named Meteor Lake, went on sale in laptops on 14 December 2023. They were Intel's first consumer chips with a dedicated neural processing unit (NPU) and used a tiled design with Foveros packaging and Arc graphics. It mattered as the start of the "AI PC" category, where on-device AI inference moved into mainstream laptops.
