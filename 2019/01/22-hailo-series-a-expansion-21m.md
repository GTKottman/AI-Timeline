# Hailo expands Series A to $21M led by Glory Ventures

- **Date:** 2019-01-22
- **Who:** Hailo; Glory Ventures
- **Type:** funding

Israeli deep learning chip startup Hailo expanded its Series A to $21 million, with Chinese venture firm Glory Ventures leading the increase. The money was meant to open China and Hong Kong while keeping its existing markets in Europe, North America, Japan and Korea. The company also launched a fast-track program for its Hailo-8 edge AI processor, one of several bets that deep learning inference would move onto cheap devices instead of the cloud.
