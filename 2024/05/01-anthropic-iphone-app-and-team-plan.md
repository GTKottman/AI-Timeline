# Anthropic launches a Claude iPhone app and a business tier

- **Date:** 2024-05-01
- **Who:** Anthropic
- **Type:** product

Anthropic released a free Claude app for iPhone and a Team business plan, at $30 per user per month with a minimum of five seats. It competed with ChatGPT's mobile app and enterprise offering. Team customers got a 200,000-token context window and admin and billing tools.
