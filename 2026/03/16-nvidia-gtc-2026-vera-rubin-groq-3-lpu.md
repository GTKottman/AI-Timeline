# Nvidia GTC 2026: Vera Rubin Platform and Groq 3 LPU Unveiled

- **Date:** 2026-03-16
- **Who:** Nvidia (Jensen Huang keynote, San Jose)
- **Type:** hardware

At its GTC keynote Nvidia unveiled the Vera Rubin rack-scale platform for agentic AI, pairing a new Vera CPU with the Rubin GPU, and its first chip built on the Groq technology it licensed in December 2025: the Groq 3 LPU, sold in a Groq 3 LPX rack of 256 LPUs. Nvidia pitches the combination as up to 35x higher inference throughput per megawatt, with Vera Rubin handling prefill and KV cache and the LPUs handling latency-sensitive decode. These performance figures are vendor claims, and sources disagree on shipping timing (reports say Q3 2026 for the LPU).
