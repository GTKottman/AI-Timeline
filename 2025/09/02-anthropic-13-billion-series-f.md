# Anthropic closes $13 billion Series F at $183 billion valuation

- **Date:** 2025-09-02
- **Who:** Anthropic; ICONIQ (lead), Fidelity, Lightspeed
- **Type:** funding

Anthropic closed a $13 billion Series F funding round at a $183 billion post-money valuation. ICONIQ led the round, with Fidelity Management & Research and Lightspeed Venture Partners as co-leads.
