# Google pauses Gemini image generation of people

- **Date:** 2024-02-22
- **Who:** Google; Sundar Pichai
- **Type:** product

Google suspended Gemini's ability to generate images of people after it produced historically inaccurate depictions. Pichai called the outputs "completely unacceptable" in an internal memo. The episode became a public case study in how tuning for diversity can fail, and it drove more attention to model evaluation and alignment.
