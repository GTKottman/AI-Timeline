# OpenAI introduces GPT-4o

- **Date:** 2024-05-13
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** model

GPT-4o is a single model that reasons across audio, vision and text, with real-time voice responses that are much faster than earlier ChatGPT voice modes. OpenAI offered it to free users as well as paid users, with paid users keeping up to five times the capacity limits. Full voice and video features were announced for later rollout. It pushed multimodal, low-latency assistants into everyday use.
