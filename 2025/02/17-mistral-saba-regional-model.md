# Mistral AI releases Saba, a 24B model for Arabic and South Asian languages

- **Date:** 2025-02-17
- **Who:** Mistral AI
- **Type:** model

Mistral Saba is a 24-billion-parameter model trained for the Middle East and South Asia, with strength in Arabic and South Indian languages such as Tamil and Malayalam. Mistral says it answers more accurately than models more than five times its size, and it can run on a single GPU at over 150 tokens per second. It is offered through the API and can be deployed inside a customer's own infrastructure. Critics noted that it was not released with open weights.
