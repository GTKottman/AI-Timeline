# Figure AI raises $675M at $2.6B valuation and partners with OpenAI

- **Date:** 2024-02-29
- **Who:** Figure AI; OpenAI; Microsoft; Nvidia; Jeff Bezos
- **Type:** funding

Humanoid robot startup Figure AI raised $675 million from a group including Microsoft, Nvidia, Intel, Jeff Bezos and the startup funds of Amazon and OpenAI, at a $2.6 billion valuation. The same period brought a partnership with OpenAI to give its robots language-model reasoning. It was a major bet that foundation models would move into physical robots.
