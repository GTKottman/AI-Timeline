# Stack Overflow temporarily bans ChatGPT-generated answers

- **Date:** 2022-12-00
- **Who:** Stack Overflow moderators
- **Type:** other

Stack Overflow's moderators put a temporary ban on posting answers generated by ChatGPT, saying average accuracy was too low and the flood of answers overwhelmed volunteer curation. It was an early sign that generative AI output was straining online knowledge communities.
