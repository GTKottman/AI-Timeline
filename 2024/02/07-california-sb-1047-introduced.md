# California Senator Scott Wiener introduces SB 1047

- **Date:** 2024-02-07
- **Who:** Sen. Scott Wiener; California Legislature
- **Type:** policy

SB 1047, the Safe and Secure Innovation for Frontier Artificial Intelligence Models Act, was introduced in the California Senate. It proposed safety testing and shutdown duties for developers of very large frontier models. It became the most contested US state-level AI bill of 2024 before its veto.
