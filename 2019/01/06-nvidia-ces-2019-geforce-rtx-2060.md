# Nvidia launches GeForce RTX 2060 at CES 2019

- **Date:** 2019-01-06
- **Who:** Nvidia (Jensen Huang)
- **Type:** hardware

At its CES 2019 press conference, Nvidia launched the GeForce RTX 2060 on its Turing architecture, with Tensor cores for AI-based features such as DLSS, alongside 40+ laptop models. Its main significance for AI was bringing Turing's deep-learning hardware to a consumer card. The press conference was led by Jensen Huang at the MGM Grand.
