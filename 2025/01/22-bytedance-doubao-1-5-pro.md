# ByteDance launches Doubao 1.5 Pro

- **Date:** 2025-01-22
- **Who:** ByteDance
- **Type:** model

ByteDance released Doubao-1.5-pro, a closed-source multimodal model that it presents as a resource-efficient training result. ByteDance's own benchmarks claim it beat GPT-4o, Gemini and Qwen on half of 14 tests; these are company figures, not independent evaluations. The release came days after DeepSeek and Moonshot launched reasoning models, part of a fast wave of Chinese frontier releases that month.
