# xAI announces Grok-1.5 with 128,000-token context

- **Date:** 2024-03-28
- **Who:** xAI
- **Type:** model

Grok-1.5 added stronger reasoning and a 128,000-token context window, up from 8,192 tokens in Grok-1. xAI positioned it as a step up from the open Grok-1 weights and the chatbot tier on X.
