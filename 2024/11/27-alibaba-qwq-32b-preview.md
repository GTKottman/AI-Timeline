# Alibaba releases QwQ-32B-Preview, an open reasoning model

- **Date:** 2024-11-27
- **Who:** Alibaba (Qwen team)
- **Type:** model

Alibaba's Qwen team released QwQ-32B-Preview, a roughly 32.5B-parameter reasoning model, as downloadable weights under a permissive Apache 2.0 license. Alibaba said it beat OpenAI's o1-preview on AIME and MATH, and it was among the first open models to rival o1 on reasoning. Alibaba's own post noted limits such as language switching and looping.
