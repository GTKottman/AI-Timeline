# Google Imagen Editor and EditBench

- **Date:** 2022-12-13
- **Who:** Google Research
- **Type:** research

Google posted Imagen Editor, a cascaded diffusion model that fine-tunes Imagen for text-guided inpainting, along with EditBench, a benchmark for evaluating inpainting edits on objects, attributes and scenes. Its training uses object detectors to propose masks instead of random masks, which the authors said improved text-image alignment. In human evaluation it was preferred over DALL-E 2 and Stable Diffusion. The paper was later accepted to CVPR 2023.
