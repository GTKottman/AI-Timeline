# Google unveils second-generation TPU as Cloud TPU

- **Date:** 2017-05-17
- **Who:** Google
- **Type:** hardware

At Google I/O, Google announced its second-generation Tensor Processing Unit, branded Cloud TPU. Unlike the first TPU, which only ran inference, the new chip handles neural network training as well, at up to 180 teraflops per device. Google also launched the TensorFlow Research Cloud, offering researchers free access to a cluster of 1,000 Cloud TPUs in exchange for sharing their work.
