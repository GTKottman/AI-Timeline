# ECCV 2020 Best Paper: RAFT Optical Flow

- **Date:** 2020-08-24
- **Who:** Zachary Teed and Jia Deng (Princeton University)
- **Type:** competition

The ECCV 2020 Best Paper award went to "RAFT: Recurrent All-Pairs Field Transforms for Optical Flow." RAFT extracts per-pixel features, builds all-pairs correlation volumes at several scales, and iteratively refines a flow field with a recurrent update. It reported 5.10% F1-all on KITTI, against 6.10% for the best prior published result, and 2.855 pixels end-point error on Sintel final. The award was given at the virtual conference; 2020-08-24 is the date of the Synced report, and Princeton's post is dated August 28.
