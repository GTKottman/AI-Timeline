# Nvidia and NIH release COVID-19 CT AI models

- **Date:** 2020-05-14
- **Who:** Nvidia, U.S. National Institutes of Health (NIH) Clinical Center
- **Type:** model

Nvidia and the NIH Clinical Center co-developed two AI models for chest CT scans under a cooperative research agreement: one for lung segmentation and one for COVID-19 classification. NIH radiologists did the labeling, and the models were built in under three weeks. Nvidia released them in Clara Imaging on the NGC software hub, and announced them with an expansion of the Clara healthcare platform.
