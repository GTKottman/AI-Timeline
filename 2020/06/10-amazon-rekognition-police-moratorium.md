# Amazon places one-year moratorium on police use of Rekognition

- **Date:** 2020-06-10
- **Who:** Amazon Web Services
- **Type:** policy

Amazon announced a one-year moratorium on police use of its Rekognition facial recognition service, saying it hoped Congress would use the time to write appropriate rules. Exceptions were allowed for anti-trafficking and missing-children organizations such as Thorn and NCMEC. The move reversed the company's earlier defence of the technology and became one of the most visible corporate limits on AI surveillance.
