# Facebook reports AI detects about 95% of removed hate speech and introduces RIO

- **Date:** 2020-11-19
- **Who:** Facebook (Meta) AI integrity teams
- **Type:** research

In its Community Standards Enforcement Report published November 19, 2020, Facebook said AI models detected about 95% of the hate speech it removed in Q3 2020, about 94.7% of 22.1 million removed items. The report also described RIO, a system that keeps content-review models current by training them on recent posts, and mentioned Linformer, an efficient-transformer technique. It showed content moderation at scale running on continually retrained models.
