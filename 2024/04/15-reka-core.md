# Reka launches Reka Core multimodal model

- **Date:** 2024-04-15
- **Who:** Reka AI
- **Type:** model

Reka announced Reka Core, its largest model at the time, a frontier-class multimodal language model that accepts text, image, video and audio input, with a 128K-token context window. Reka claimed it was comparable to GPT-4V on MMMU and ahead of Claude 3 Opus on an independently run human evaluation; these are the company's own results. A technical report covering Core, Flash and Edge followed on arXiv on April 18.
