# Ziff Davis sues OpenAI over training and attribution

- **Date:** 2025-04-24
- **Who:** Ziff Davis (owner of CNET and IGN); OpenAI
- **Type:** policy

Ziff Davis sued OpenAI in Delaware federal court, alleging copyright infringement, DMCA violations, unjust enrichment and trademark dilution. It says OpenAI's crawler ignored its robots.txt opt-outs, trained on its content, and stripped copyright management information. It seeks damages and destruction of models and datasets trained on its works.
