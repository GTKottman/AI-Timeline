# ScienceQA and "Learn to Explain" chain-of-thought paper

- **Date:** 2022-09-00
- **Who:** Pan Lu, Swaroop Mishra, Tony Xia and co-authors (UCLA and collaborators)
- **Type:** dataset

The authors released ScienceQA, about 21,000 multimodal multiple-choice science questions with lecture-style explanations, and paired it with chain-of-thought training. Their results showed chain-of-thought raised few-shot GPT-3 accuracy by 1.2 points and fine-tuned UnifiedQA by about 4 points. The paper was accepted to NeurIPS 2022. The exact arXiv day was not confirmed.
