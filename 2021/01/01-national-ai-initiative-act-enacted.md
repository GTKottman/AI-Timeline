# National Artificial Intelligence Initiative Act becomes law in the FY2021 NDAA

- **Date:** 2021-01-01
- **Who:** U.S. Congress; Public Law 116-283
- **Type:** policy

The National AI Initiative Act, placed in Division E of the FY2021 National Defense Authorization Act, became law on January 1, 2021. It created a coordinated federal program to accelerate AI research and application and a task force on a National AI Research Resource. Sources differ on whether the President signed it or Congress overrode a veto. One source puts the funding at nearly $6.5 billion over five years.
