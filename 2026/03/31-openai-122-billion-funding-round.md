# OpenAI Closes $122 Billion Funding Round at $852 Billion Valuation

- **Date:** 2026-03-31
- **Who:** OpenAI; Amazon ($50 billion commitment), Nvidia ($30 billion), SoftBank ($30 billion)
- **Type:** funding

OpenAI said it closed a record round of $122 billion in committed capital, lifting its post-money valuation to $852 billion. Amazon's $50 billion includes about $35 billion that depends on OpenAI going public or reaching a technology milestone. The round grew from the roughly $110 billion flagged in February, and OpenAI said it generates about $2 billion in monthly revenue while still losing money.
