# Microsoft releases three in-house MAI models for speech, voice and images

- **Date:** 2026-04-02
- **Who:** Microsoft (MAI Superintelligence team, led by Mustafa Suleyman)
- **Type:** model

Microsoft launched MAI-Transcribe-1 (speech-to-text in 25 languages), MAI-Voice-1 (speech generation with custom voices from a few seconds of audio) and MAI-Image-2 (image generation, now in Microsoft Foundry). They are the company's own models for these modalities, available through Microsoft Foundry and the MAI Playground, and are already being wired into Copilot features. Microsoft is reducing reliance on outside models for non-text tasks. The figures for speed and accuracy are company-reported.
