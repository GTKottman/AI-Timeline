# Workday acquires AI company Sana for $1.1 billion

- **Date:** 2025-09-16
- **Who:** Workday, Sana
- **Type:** acquisition

Workday agreed to buy Sana, an AI company behind the Sana Learn and Sana Agents products, for about $1.1 billion. The deal was announced at Workday Rising in San Francisco, and Workday plans to use Sana's technology for an employee-facing experience that combines knowledge, data, actions and learning. Sana's products serve over one million users across hundreds of enterprises, and closing was expected in Workday's fiscal Q4 2026.
