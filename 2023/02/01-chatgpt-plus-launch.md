# OpenAI launches ChatGPT Plus

- **Date:** 2023-02-01
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** product

OpenAI launched ChatGPT Plus, a $20-per-month pilot subscription that gave paying users access during peak times, faster responses and priority access to new features, while the free tier stayed available. The first rollout was limited to US customers from the waitlist, and OpenAI later extended it internationally. It was one of the first consumer subscription models for a large language model chatbot and set a pricing pattern that other AI assistants later copied.
