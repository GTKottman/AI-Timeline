# Slamcore raises $16M Series A

- **Date:** 2022-05-03
- **Who:** Slamcore; ROBO Global Ventures and Presidio Ventures (leads)
- **Type:** funding

Slamcore, a vision-based location and mapping startup for robots and consumer devices, announced a $16 million Series A on May 3, 2022, led by ROBO Global Ventures and Presidio Ventures. Strategic investors Samsung Ventures, Toyota Ventures and Yamato Holdings also joined. Its visual SLAM software lets low-cost cameras give robots real-time location and mapping.
