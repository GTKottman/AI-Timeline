# Nvidia releases Maxine, Jarvis and TAO AI tools at GTC 2021

- **Date:** 2021-04-12
- **Who:** NVIDIA
- **Type:** software

At its April 2021 GTC keynote Nvidia made several AI developer tools available. Maxine offered SDKs for video conferencing effects such as super-resolution, noise removal and virtual backgrounds, plus an AI face codec that Nvidia said could cut bandwidth up to tenfold versus H.264. Jarvis provided a framework for conversational AI services with speech recognition, translation and text-to-speech. TAO was a GUI-driven workflow framework for building enterprise AI. Nvidia's throughput claims were its own.
