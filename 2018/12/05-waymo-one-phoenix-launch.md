# Waymo launches Waymo One ride-hailing in Phoenix

- **Date:** 2018-12-05
- **Who:** Waymo (Alphabet)
- **Type:** product

Waymo opened Waymo One, a commercial self-driving ride-hailing service, in the Phoenix area, starting with a few hundred riders from its earlier Early Rider pilot. Vehicles had a safety driver on board, and the service covered about 100 square miles including Chandler, Tempe, Mesa and Gilbert. The launch was a milestone for deploying machine-learning driving systems to paying passengers.
