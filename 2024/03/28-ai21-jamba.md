# AI21 Labs releases Jamba, a Mamba-Transformer hybrid

- **Date:** 2024-03-28
- **Who:** AI21 Labs
- **Type:** model

AI21 released Jamba, the first production-grade model that mixes Mamba state-space layers with Transformer attention and mixture-of-experts layers. It has 52 billion total parameters, 12 billion active at inference, and a 256K-token context. AI21 released the weights openly under Apache 2.0 and claimed about 3x throughput on long contexts versus Mixtral 8x7B, a company figure. It showed a non-Transformer architecture could compete at production scale.
