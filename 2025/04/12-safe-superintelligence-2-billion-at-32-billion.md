# Safe Superintelligence reportedly raises $2 billion at $32 billion valuation

- **Date:** 2025-04-12
- **Who:** Safe Superintelligence (SSI), Ilya Sutskever; reportedly led by Greenoaks
- **Type:** funding

Ilya Sutskever's Safe Superintelligence reportedly raised $2 billion at a $32 billion valuation, according to a Financial Times report that TechCrunch carried on April 12, 2025. The company did not comment, and the round was reportedly led by Greenoaks, with Lightspeed and Andreessen Horowitz also named. SSI had no product or revenue at the time. The valuation was about six times its September 2024 level.
