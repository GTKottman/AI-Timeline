# California Assembly passes SB 1047

- **Date:** 2024-08-28
- **Who:** California State Assembly; Sen. Scott Wiener
- **Type:** policy

The California Assembly passed the amended SB 1047 in August 2024, sending it back to the Senate for a concurrence vote. It was the last legislative step before the governor's decision, which came in September with a veto. It drew opposition from parts of the tech industry.
