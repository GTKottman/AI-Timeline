# NVIDIA unveils Rubin CPX for massive-context inference

- **Date:** 2025-09-09
- **Who:** NVIDIA
- **Type:** hardware

NVIDIA unveiled Rubin CPX, a new class of GPU built for the context phase of inference, where very long inputs such as million-token coding sessions and generative video are processed. It sits in the Vera Rubin NVL144 CPX rack with Vera CPUs and Rubin GPUs, which NVIDIA says delivers 8 exaflops of AI compute and 100TB of fast memory, and it is expected to be available by the end of 2026. The chip signals that inference hardware is being split into separate prefill and generation stages.
