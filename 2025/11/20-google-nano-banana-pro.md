# Google launches Nano Banana Pro (Gemini 3 Pro Image)

- **Date:** 2025-11-20
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** model

Google released Nano Banana Pro, its Gemini 3 Pro-based image model, with 2K and 4K output, support for up to 14 reference images, and character consistency for up to 5 people. It was released as a preview model.
