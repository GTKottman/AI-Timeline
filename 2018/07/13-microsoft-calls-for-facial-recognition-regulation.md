# Microsoft asks Congress to regulate facial recognition

- **Date:** 2018-07-13
- **Who:** Microsoft (Brad Smith, President and Chief Legal Officer)
- **Type:** policy

In a blog post on July 13, 2018, Microsoft's Brad Smith called on Congress to set up a bipartisan, expert-led commission to draft rules for facial recognition. He cited risks of abuse and of government tracking people without their knowledge. He also said Microsoft had turned down customers over human rights risks and that its ICE contract was not used for facial recognition. It mattered as one of the first calls for AI regulation from a major tech company's leadership.
