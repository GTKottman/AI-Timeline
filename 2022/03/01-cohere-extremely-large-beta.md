# Cohere launches Extremely Large (beta) model

- **Date:** 2022-03-01
- **Who:** Cohere
- **Type:** model

Cohere released its Extremely Large (beta) model on March 1, 2022, under the identifier xlarge-20220301. Cohere's changelog says it was the company's largest generation model at the time and beat its previous large model on sentiment analysis, named entity recognition and common-sense reasoning in Cohere's internal benchmarks. The beta was capped at 1024 tokens and one generation per call.
