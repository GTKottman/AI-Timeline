# European Parliament adopts three resolutions on AI ethics, civil liability and intellectual property

- **Date:** 2020-10-20
- **Who:** European Parliament
- **Type:** policy

On October 20, 2020 the European Parliament adopted three non-binding resolutions asking the European Commission to propose EU legislation. One set out an ethical framework for AI, robotics and related technologies. Another called for a harmonised civil liability regime for AI, with strict liability for operators of high-risk systems. The third covered intellectual property rights in works created with AI. They fed into the Commission's AI legislative work that followed the February 2020 white paper.
