# Runway releases Gen-3 Alpha video model

- **Date:** 2024-06-17
- **Who:** Runway
- **Type:** model

Runway announced Gen-3 Alpha, the first model in its next generation of video foundation models, trained on video and images together on new large-scale infrastructure. It makes hyper-realistic 10-second clips from text or images, and Runway said it would power all of its image-to-video and text-to-video tools and reach all users within days. It shipped with new moderation and C2PA content-credential measures, which made it one of the strongest commercial video models of mid-2024.
