# DataRobot acquires Nutonian

- **Date:** 2017-05-23
- **Who:** DataRobot; Nutonian (Eureqa engine, developed at Cornell's AI lab)
- **Type:** acquisition

DataRobot announced on May 23, 2017 that it had acquired Nutonian, a Boston data science software company focused on time series modeling. Nutonian's Eureqa modeling engine came out of Cornell's AI lab and was used by companies such as Audi and NASA. Terms were not disclosed, and Fortune and Xconomy covered the deal as one AI startup buying another.
