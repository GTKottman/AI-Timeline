# Bletchley Declaration signed at UK AI Safety Summit

- **Date:** 2023-11-01
- **Who:** UK government; 28 countries and the European Union, including the US and China
- **Type:** policy

Countries attending the AI Safety Summit at Bletchley Park signed the Bletchley Declaration on frontier AI risk. The non-binding text committed signatories to identify shared AI safety risks through research, to build risk-based policies with transparency and accountability, and to set up international cooperation. Reported participants included the US, China, the UK and the EU.
