# Google puts Cloud AutoML Vision, Natural Language and Translation into beta

- **Date:** 2018-07-24
- **Who:** Google Cloud
- **Type:** product

At Google Cloud Next 2018 Google moved AutoML Vision, AutoML Natural Language and AutoML Translation into public beta. The suite uses transfer learning and neural architecture search so people with limited machine learning experience can train custom models from their own labeled data. It had been in alpha earlier in 2018 after its unveiling at I/O 2017. It mattered as a push to make custom model training a self-service cloud product.
