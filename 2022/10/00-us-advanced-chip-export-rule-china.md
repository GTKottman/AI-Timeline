# US export rule on advanced AI chips to China takes effect

- **Date:** 2022-10-00
- **Who:** US Department of Commerce (BIS); NVIDIA
- **Type:** policy

In October 2022 the US expanded export controls on advanced computing chips to China, which took in NVIDIA's data-center GPUs. NVIDIA answered with the A800, a China-compliant variant of the A100, showing how hardware export rules shape which AI chips reach which markets.
