# NVIDIA moves Vera Rubin to full production

- **Date:** 2026-05-00
- **Who:** NVIDIA
- **Type:** hardware

NVIDIA said its Vera Rubin platform had entered full production, positioned as the basis of an agentic AI factory. Deployment of Rubin systems followed in the autumn, and the platform became the next major AI accelerator generation after Blackwell.
