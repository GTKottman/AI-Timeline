# Moonshot releases Kimi K2 Thinking, an open-weights reasoning model

- **Date:** 2025-11-06
- **Who:** Moonshot AI
- **Type:** model

Moonshot released Kimi K2 Thinking, an open-weights mixture-of-experts model with 1 trillion total parameters and 32 billion active per token, under a modified MIT licence. Moonshot reports 44.9% on Humanity's Last Exam with tools and 60.2% on BrowseComp. Artificial Analysis scored it 67 on its Intelligence Index, the top score for an open-weights model at the time, but it used the most tokens of any model it tested. Benchmark claims against GPT-5 and Claude Sonnet 4.5 are Moonshot's own. A reported training cost of about $4.6 million came from a source CNBC cited and could not be verified.
