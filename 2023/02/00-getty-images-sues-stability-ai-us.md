# Getty Images files US lawsuit against Stability AI in Delaware

- **Date:** 2023-02-00
- **Who:** Getty Images
- **Type:** policy

Getty Images filed a US suit in Delaware accusing Stability AI of copying about 12 million copyrighted images to train Stable Diffusion for a competing business. It extended Getty's earlier UK action into the US courts.
