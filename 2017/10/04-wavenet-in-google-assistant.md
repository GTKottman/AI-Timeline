# WaveNet launches as the voice generator for Google Assistant

- **Date:** 2017-10-04
- **Who:** DeepMind, Google
- **Type:** product

DeepMind announced that an updated WaveNet now generates the Google Assistant voices for US English and Japanese on all platforms. The original WaveNet, from late 2016, was a research model too slow for consumer products; a year of speed and quality work made it deployable. It was one of the first large neural text-to-speech systems to run in a major consumer product.
