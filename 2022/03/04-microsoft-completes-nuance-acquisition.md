# Microsoft completes acquisition of Nuance Communications

- **Date:** 2022-03-04
- **Who:** Microsoft / Nuance
- **Type:** acquisition

Microsoft finished buying Nuance Communications, the speech recognition and clinical AI company behind Dragon dictation. The deal added large-scale speech and language AI assets to Microsoft's cloud and healthcare products.
