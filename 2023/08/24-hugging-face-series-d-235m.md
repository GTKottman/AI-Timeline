# Hugging Face raises $235M Series D at $4.5B valuation

- **Date:** 2023-08-24
- **Who:** Hugging Face; led by Salesforce Ventures with Google, Amazon, NVIDIA, Intel, AMD, Qualcomm, IBM
- **Type:** funding

Hugging Face announced a $235 million Series D at a $4.5 billion valuation, led by Salesforce Ventures with a broad group of chip, cloud and software investors. CEO Clement Delangue said the money would mainly fund hiring. The round made Hugging Face, the main hub for open models and datasets, one of the best-funded open-source AI companies.
