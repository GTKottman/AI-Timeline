# New York Times sues OpenAI and Microsoft over copyright

- **Date:** 2023-12-27
- **Who:** The New York Times; OpenAI; Microsoft
- **Type:** policy

The New York Times filed a federal copyright suit in Manhattan against OpenAI and Microsoft, alleging millions of its articles were used without permission to train ChatGPT and Bing Chat. It sought billions of dollars in damages. It was the first major news publisher to sue the ChatGPT developers, and it made training-data copyright a central legal fight for generative AI.
