# DeepSeek releases R1-Lite-Preview, a visible-reasoning model

- **Date:** 2024-11-20
- **Who:** DeepSeek
- **Type:** model

DeepSeek released R1-Lite-Preview, its first reasoning model, through its chat site with a "Deep Think" option that shows the model's chain of thought as it works. DeepSeek said it matched OpenAI's o1-preview on AIME and MATH, and independent write-ups described it as close to o1-preview. The launch had no model card or detailed architecture at first, and open weights and an API were promised later. It put a Chinese lab in direct competition with OpenAI's reasoning line.
