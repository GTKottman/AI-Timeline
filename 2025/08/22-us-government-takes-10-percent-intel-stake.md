# US government takes 10% stake in Intel

- **Date:** 2025-08-22
- **Who:** US government (President Donald Trump); Intel; CEO Lip-Bu Tan
- **Type:** funding

The US government will hold about 433 million Intel shares, around 10%, in exchange for CHIPS Act and Department of Defense funding Intel had been awarded. The stake comes without a board seat, and the government agreed to vote with the board except in limited cases. Intel warned the stake could prompt adverse reactions from international customers. It mattered because it made the US state a major shareholder in the leading domestic chipmaker that makes AI-relevant foundry capacity.
