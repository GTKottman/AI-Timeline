# AWS Inferentia Chips Become Generally Available as EC2 Inf1

- **Date:** 2019-12-03
- **Who:** Amazon Web Services
- **Type:** hardware

AWS made its custom Inferentia inference chip generally available through EC2 Inf1 instances, with up to 16 Inferentia chips per instance. AWS claimed up to 3x higher throughput and up to 40% lower cost per inference than its G4 GPU instances. Andy Jassy announced the service at AWS re:Invent in Las Vegas. It was one of the first cloud-provider inference chips offered to customers as a direct alternative to NVIDIA GPUs.
