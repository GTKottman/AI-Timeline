# Luma AI launches Dream Machine text-to-video model

- **Date:** 2024-06-12
- **Who:** Luma AI (Luma Labs)
- **Type:** product

Luma AI released Dream Machine, a text-to-video model that makes realistic clips from text prompts or still images, and opened it to the public for free testing. It was praised for how well it renders motion, and it was seen as a direct challenge to OpenAI's Sora. Within days it spread into a meme-remix trend on X, which showed how fast consumer video generation was moving.
