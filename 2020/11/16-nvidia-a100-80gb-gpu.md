# Nvidia unveils A100 80GB GPU and DGX Station A100

- **Date:** 2020-11-16
- **Who:** Nvidia
- **Type:** hardware

At SC20, Nvidia launched the A100 80GB, doubling the memory of the 40GB A100 to 80GB of HBM2e with over 2 TB/s of bandwidth. The announcement also brought DGX Station A100 and DGX A100 640GB systems built on it. The extra memory let larger models and datasets fit on one GPU.
