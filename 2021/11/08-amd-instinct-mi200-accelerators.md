# AMD launches Instinct MI200 accelerators for exascale systems

- **Date:** 2021-11-08
- **Who:** AMD (CDNA 2 architecture); MI250X used in the Frontier supercomputer
- **Type:** hardware

AMD announced the Instinct MI200 series on November 8, 2021, built on the CDNA 2 architecture. AMD's release calls it the first multi-die GPU and the first exascale-class GPU accelerator, with 128 GB of HBM2e memory. The MI250X was the GPU in the Frontier exascale supercomputer, which AMD designed with the US Department of Energy, Oak Ridge National Laboratory and HPE. Performance claims are AMD's own.
