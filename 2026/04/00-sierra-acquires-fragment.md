# Sierra acquires French startup Fragment

- **Date:** 2026-04-00
- **Who:** Sierra (Bret Taylor, Clay Bavor); Fragment (Olivier Moindrot, Guillaume Genthial)
- **Type:** acquisition

Sierra, the enterprise customer-service agent company, acquired Fragment, a Paris-based AI startup founded in 2023 by Guillaume Genthial and Olivier Moindrot, who join Sierra. Terms were undisclosed. It was Sierra's third acquisition of 2026, after Opera Tech and Receptive AI. The earliest report found is dated April 28, 2026, so the exact day is unconfirmed.
