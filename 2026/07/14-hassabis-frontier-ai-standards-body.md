# Hassabis proposes an independent frontier AI standards body

- **Date:** 2026-07-14
- **Who:** Demis Hassabis, Google DeepMind
- **Type:** policy

On July 14, 2026 Demis Hassabis published the essay "A Framework for Frontier AI and the Dawning of a New Age", proposing an industry-funded, federally overseen standards body modeled on FINRA. Frontier labs would submit their most advanced models for evaluation on cyber, biological, deception and agentic risks up to 30 days before release, voluntarily at first, with mandatory approval only once the evaluations prove reliable. Endorsements from other AI leaders reported by a secondary source could not be verified.
