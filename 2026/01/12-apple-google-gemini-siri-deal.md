# Apple and Google confirm multi-year Gemini deal for Siri and Apple Foundation Models

- **Date:** 2026-01-12
- **Who:** Apple; Google (Gemini)
- **Type:** other

Apple and Google confirmed a multi-year partnership in which Google's Gemini models become the base for Apple Foundation Models and the revamped Siri. Google said Apple Intelligence keeps running on Apple devices and Private Cloud Compute. Financial terms were not disclosed; press reports put the value at roughly $1 billion a year or about $5 billion total. It is a major sign that a frontier lab is supplying the models inside Apple's consumer products.
