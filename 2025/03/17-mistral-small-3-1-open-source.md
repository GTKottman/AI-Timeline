# Mistral releases Small 3.1 under Apache 2.0

- **Date:** 2025-03-17
- **Who:** Mistral AI
- **Type:** model

Mistral released Small 3.1, a 24-billion-parameter model under the Apache 2.0 license. It adds multimodal understanding and a 128k-token context window over Mistral Small 3. Mistral says it beats Gemma 3 and GPT-4o mini on its own benchmarks and runs at about 150 tokens per second. Base and instruction-tuned checkpoints were released.
