# Microsoft signs 20-year nuclear power deal to restart Three Mile Island unit

- **Date:** 2024-09-20
- **Who:** Microsoft, Constellation Energy
- **Type:** funding

Microsoft agreed to buy the full 835 MW output of Constellation's Three Mile Island Unit 1 for 20 years, to restart the plant (renamed the Crane Clean Energy Center) with a target of 2028. The deal was a 20-year power purchase agreement to supply Microsoft's data centers, and it was described as the first restart of its kind in US nuclear history. Regulatory approvals were still pending at announcement.
