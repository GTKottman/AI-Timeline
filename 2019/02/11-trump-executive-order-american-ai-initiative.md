# Trump signs Executive Order 13859 launching the American AI Initiative

- **Date:** 2019-02-11
- **Who:** White House / US government (President Donald Trump)
- **Type:** policy

Trump signed Executive Order 13859, "Maintaining American Leadership in Artificial Intelligence," which created the American AI Initiative. It set five lines of effort: increasing AI research investment, unleashing federal AI computing and data resources, setting AI technical standards, building America's AI workforce, and engaging with international allies. The order named no dollar figures.
