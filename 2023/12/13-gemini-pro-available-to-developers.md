# Google makes Gemini Pro available to developers and Cloud customers

- **Date:** 2023-12-13
- **Who:** Google; Google DeepMind
- **Type:** product

Gemini 1.0 Pro became available to developers and enterprise customers through Google AI Studio and Vertex AI, a week after the Gemini family was announced. It opened Google's new flagship model to outside builders for the first time.
