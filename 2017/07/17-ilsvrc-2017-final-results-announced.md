# ILSVRC 2017 final results announced, last ImageNet challenge

- **Date:** 2017-07-17
- **Who:** ImageNet team (Stanford / UNC / Princeton and others); classification winners were the SENet authors
- **Type:** competition

The official ILSVRC 2017 results were announced on 17 July 2017, and the ImageNet site says the workshop marked the last of the ImageNet Challenge competitions. The Squeeze-and-Excitation Networks (SENet) entry won classification with a top-5 error of 2.251%, a figure the SENet authors reported themselves. It mattered because it marked the end of the benchmark that had driven deep learning vision progress since 2012.
