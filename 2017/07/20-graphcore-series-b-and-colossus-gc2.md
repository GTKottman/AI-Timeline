# Graphcore raises $30 million Series B and details Colossus GC2 chip

- **Date:** 2017-07-20
- **Who:** Graphcore, Atomico (lead investor)
- **Type:** funding

Graphcore, a UK maker of AI accelerator chips, raised a $30 million Series B led by Atomico and detailed its first chip, the Colossus GC2, a 16 nm massively parallel mixed-precision processor. The company aimed at training and inference workloads with a different architecture from GPUs. The round was one of several large AI-chip funding rounds in 2017.
