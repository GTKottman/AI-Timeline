# Huawei unveils Ascend 910 and Ascend 310 AI chips

- **Date:** 2018-10-10
- **Who:** Huawei
- **Type:** hardware

At Huawei Connect in Shanghai, Huawei unveiled the Ascend 910, a 7nm data-center chip, and the Ascend 310, a 12nm low-power edge chip. It also introduced the CANN operator library, the MindSpore framework and a cloud ML platform. Huawei's own claims put the 910 above Nvidia's Tesla V100 and Google's TPU on deep-learning throughput, which were not independently verified. It mattered as a move by a phone maker into a full AI chip and software stack for enterprise and IoT, aimed at Nvidia, Intel and Qualcomm.
