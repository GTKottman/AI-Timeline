# UK ICO rules Royal Free broke data law in sharing patient data with DeepMind

- **Date:** 2017-07-00
- **Who:** UK Information Commissioner's Office, Royal Free NHS Trust, DeepMind (Google)
- **Type:** policy

The UK Information Commissioner's Office ruled that the Royal Free Hospital failed to comply with the Data Protection Act when it gave DeepMind records of 1.6 million patients to test the Streams app. DeepMind published a response saying it needed to do better and listing transparency measures. The case became a key early example of privacy rules limiting how AI companies use health data.
