# Hugging Face, Meta and Scaleway launch AI accelerator for European startups

- **Date:** 2024-06-25
- **Who:** Hugging Face; Meta; Scaleway
- **Type:** other

The three companies launched an accelerator program for European startups building products on open foundation models. It is based at Station F in Paris, with technical mentoring from Meta's FAIR research teams and access to Hugging Face and Scaleway tools and compute. It was meant to put open models into real products in Europe.
