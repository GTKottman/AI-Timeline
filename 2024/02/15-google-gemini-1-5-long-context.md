# Google DeepMind introduces Gemini 1.5 with one-million-token context

- **Date:** 2024-02-15
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** model

Gemini 1.5 is a multimodal model with a context window of up to one million tokens, far beyond the rest of the field at the time. Google said it could process long documents, hours of video and large codebases in one prompt. Long context became a major competitive axis for frontier models in 2024.
