# Reversible architectures for arbitrarily deep residual networks (arXiv)

- **Date:** 2017-09-12
- **Who:** Bo Chang and co-authors
- **Type:** paper

The paper "Reversible Architectures for Arbitrarily Deep Residual Neural Networks" (arXiv 1709.03698) proposed residual networks whose layers can be inverted, so intermediate activations need not be stored during backpropagation and memory stays constant with depth. The authors reported results on CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100 and STL-10, and the work was accepted at AAAI 2018. It mattered because it showed a route to very deep networks with lower memory cost.
