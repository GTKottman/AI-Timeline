# China's State Council releases New Generation AI Development Plan

- **Date:** 2017-07-20
- **Who:** State Council of the People's Republic of China
- **Type:** policy

China's State Council published a national strategy to make China the world leader in AI by 2030, with targets for AI research, industry and talent. It is one of the first national AI strategies and drove large government investment in AI research and companies in the following years.
