# Emad Mostaque steps down as Stability AI CEO

- **Date:** 2024-03-22
- **Who:** Emad Mostaque; Stability AI
- **Type:** people

Stability AI founder Emad Mostaque resigned as chief executive and from the board, saying he left to pursue decentralized AI. The company named COO Shan Shan Wong and CTO Christian Laforte interim co-CEOs. Coverage tied the exit to tensions with some of Stability's largest investors. The Stable Diffusion maker's announcement came late on March 22 US time.
