# Mistral releases Voxtral, its first open-weight audio model

- **Date:** 2025-07-15
- **Who:** Mistral AI
- **Type:** model

Mistral released Voxtral, its first open-weight speech model, in a 24B version for production and a 3B version for local and edge use. Both handle transcription and audio understanding for up to 30 to 40 minutes of audio across several major languages, under Apache 2.0. It mattered because it offered an open alternative to paid speech-recognition APIs.
