# Midjourney opens its image generator in open beta

- **Date:** 2022-07-12
- **Who:** Midjourney (David Holz)
- **Type:** product

Midjourney launched its text-to-image tool into open beta, letting anyone generate images from prompts through its Discord-based interface. The launch put a polished image generator in front of a large public audience.
