# ByteDance launches Seed 2.1 Pro and Seed 2.1 Turbo models

- **Date:** 2026-06-23
- **Who:** ByteDance Seed (Volcano Engine)
- **Type:** model

ByteDance announced Seed 2.1 Pro and Seed 2.1 Turbo on stage at its Volcano Engine FORCE conference on June 23, 2026, with commercial pricing live on June 24. Pro is the top model and powers the Doubao 2.1 Pro assistant, while Turbo is a faster, cheaper option for high-volume enterprise use. ByteDance claims Pro beats Claude Opus 4.6 on several benchmarks at much lower total cost, a vendor-reported claim not yet independently checked. It was announced alongside Seedance 2.5 and Seedream 5.0 Pro.
