# US government takes 10% stake in Intel

- **Date:** 2025-08-22
- **Who:** US government (President Donald Trump); Intel; CEO Lip-Bu Tan
- **Type:** funding

The US government will hold about 433 million Intel shares, around 10%, in exchange for CHIPS Act and Department of Defense funding Intel had been awarded. The stake comes without a board seat, and the government agreed to vote with the board except in limited cases. It mattered because it made the US state a major shareholder in the leading domestic chipmaker that makes AI-relevant foundry capacity.
