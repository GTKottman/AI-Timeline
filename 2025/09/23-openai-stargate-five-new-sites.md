# OpenAI, Oracle and SoftBank add five Stargate data center sites

- **Date:** 2025-09-23
- **Who:** OpenAI; Oracle; SoftBank (SB Energy)
- **Type:** other

OpenAI, Oracle and SoftBank announced five new US sites for Stargate, the AI infrastructure project, at the Abilene, Texas flagship. The sites are Shackelford County in Texas, Doña Ana County in New Mexico, a Midwest site later identified as Wisconsin, Lordstown in Ohio and Milam County in Texas. The companies said the expansion would bring Stargate to nearly 7 gigawatts of planned capacity and over $400 billion of investment over three years. OpenAI said the sites were picked from more than 300 proposals from over 30 states.
