# FTC blog: Aiming for truth, fairness, and equity in your company's use of AI

- **Date:** 2021-04-19
- **Who:** US Federal Trade Commission
- **Type:** policy

On 19 April 2021 the FTC published a business blog post, written by a staff attorney, warning companies that biased or overstated AI claims can count as unfair or deceptive practices under FTC law. It said the agency may act if firms do not police their own algorithms, and it built on the agency's 2020 guidance on AI and algorithms. Law firms read it as a signal that enforcement against biased AI systems was a live risk in the US. One firm noted that a recent Supreme Court ruling limited the FTC's remedies, though injunctions remained available.
