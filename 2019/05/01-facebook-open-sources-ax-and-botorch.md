# Facebook open-sources Ax and BoTorch

- **Date:** 2019-05-01
- **Who:** Facebook AI
- **Type:** software

At its F8 developer conference, Facebook AI open-sourced Ax, a general-purpose platform for managing and automating adaptive experiments, and BoTorch, a Bayesian optimization library built on PyTorch. The tools target data-efficient tuning problems such as hyperparameter search, and Facebook said it already used them on tasks ranging from Instagram back-end optimization to survey response rates. The first BoTorch release was made with Cornell University.
