# NVIDIA announces DRIVE PX Pegasus Level 5 autonomous platform

- **Date:** 2017-10-10
- **Who:** NVIDIA
- **Type:** hardware

At GTC Europe, NVIDIA announced DRIVE PX Pegasus, an autonomous driving computer rated at 320 INT8 TOPS, aimed at fully self-driving Level 5 vehicles. It was built on next-generation NVIDIA GPUs and targeted robotaxi makers. It extended NVIDIA's push from data center AI into automotive AI computing.
