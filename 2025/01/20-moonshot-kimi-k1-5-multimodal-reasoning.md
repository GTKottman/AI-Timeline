# Moonshot AI launches Kimi k1.5, a multimodal reasoning model

- **Date:** 2025-01-20
- **Who:** Moonshot AI
- **Type:** model

Moonshot AI launched Kimi k1.5, a model that accepts text and images and was trained for long-chain-of-thought reasoning, with a short-chain variant for concise answers. Moonshot's technical report says both variants match or beat OpenAI o1 and DeepSeek-R1 on reasoning benchmarks, a company claim. The Decoder reported the model on January 21, 2025, and on January 25 said the models had not yet been made publicly available and that Kimi.ai had opened free web access to all users. The launch day is not confirmed beyond these reports.
