# Google Acquires Bengaluru AI Startup Halli Labs

- **Date:** 2017-07-12
- **Who:** Google; Halli Labs (founder Pankaj Gupta)
- **Type:** acquisition

Google acquired Halli Labs, a two-month-old Bengaluru AI startup that had surfaced publicly in May, for an undisclosed sum. The team was to join Google's Next Billion Users group, which targets people in emerging markets, mainly India, who are not yet online or do not use English. It showed Google buying small AI teams to build products for new users.
