# Fal raises $140M Series D led by Sequoia

- **Date:** 2025-12-09
- **Who:** Fal (generative media inference platform); Sequoia (lead), Kleiner Perkins, NVentures, Alkeon Capital
- **Type:** funding

Fal announced a $140 million Series D for its real-time generative media infrastructure, led by Sequoia with Kleiner Perkins and NVIDIA's NVentures participating, plus new investment from Alkeon Capital. One outlet reported a $4.5 billion valuation; other coverage in these sources did not confirm it. The round signals investor appetite for inference platforms that serve image and video models to developers.
