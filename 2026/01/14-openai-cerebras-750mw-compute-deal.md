# OpenAI signs 750 MW low-latency inference deal with Cerebras

- **Date:** 2026-01-14
- **Who:** OpenAI; Cerebras
- **Type:** other

OpenAI announced a multi-year deal for 750 megawatts of low-latency inference compute from Cerebras, with capacity arriving in tranches through 2028 and workloads moved into OpenAI's inference stack in phases. OpenAI did not disclose terms; people familiar put it above $10 billion. The deal diversifies OpenAI's inference supply away from Nvidia GPUs toward a wafer-scale rival.
