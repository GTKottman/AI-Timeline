# Facebook expands its Montreal AI research lab

- **Date:** 2018-09-21
- **Who:** Facebook AI Research (FAIR), led in Montreal by Joelle Pineau
- **Type:** other

Facebook said it would expand its Montreal AI research lab into a workspace for up to 60 workers. Montreal International reported this as tripling the current staff, while a Financial Post headline said quadrupling, so the target multiplier is disputed. The lab was announced in September 2017 as Facebook's second AI research facility outside the U.S. The expansion added to FAIR's network of research hubs outside Silicon Valley.
