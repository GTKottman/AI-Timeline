# DeepMind begins work with US Department of Veterans Affairs on patient deterioration prediction

- **Date:** 2018-02-22
- **Who:** DeepMind (Google), US Department of Veterans Affairs
- **Type:** research

DeepMind announced a research partnership with the US Department of Veterans Affairs to use machine learning to predict patient deterioration, starting with acute kidney injury. The work was to analyse about 700,000 de-identified historical patient records. It extended DeepMind's healthcare work from the UK NHS to a US federal health system.
