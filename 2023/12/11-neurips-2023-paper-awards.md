# NeurIPS 2023 announces paper awards

- **Date:** 2023-12-11
- **Who:** NeurIPS program committee
- **Type:** competition

The NeurIPS 2023 conference ran in New Orleans from December 10 to 16. On December 11 the organisers named the Test of Time award and the outstanding papers in the main track and the datasets and benchmarks track. Winners included "Privacy Auditing with One (1) Training Run" and "Are Emergent Abilities of Large Language Models a Mirage?", the latter arguing that apparent emergent abilities depend on the metric chosen.
