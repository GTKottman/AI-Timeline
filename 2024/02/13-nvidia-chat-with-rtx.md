# Nvidia releases Chat with RTX local chatbot

- **Date:** 2024-02-13
- **Who:** Nvidia
- **Type:** software

Nvidia released Chat with RTX, a free app that runs a Mistral or Llama open-weight chatbot locally on Windows PCs with GeForce RTX 30 or 40 series GPUs with at least 8GB VRAM. It uses retrieval-augmented generation over a user's local files and YouTube transcripts, accelerated with TensorRT-LLM. It mattered as an early consumer example of a fully local, retrieval-based LLM app on a gaming GPU.
