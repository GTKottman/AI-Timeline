# Moonshot releases Kimi K2-0905

- **Date:** 2025-09-05
- **Who:** Moonshot AI
- **Type:** model

Moonshot AI released Kimi K2-0905, an update to its Kimi K2 0711 mixture-of-experts model with 1 trillion total parameters and 32 billion active per pass. The context window grew from 128k to 256k tokens, and the update targets multi-step agentic coding and frontend work such as web and 3D tasks. DataLearner lists the weights as MIT-licensed on Hugging Face; OpenRouter dates the release to September 4 while DataLearner and Vercel give September 5.
