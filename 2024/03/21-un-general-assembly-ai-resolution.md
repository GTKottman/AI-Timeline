# UN General Assembly adopts first resolution on AI

- **Date:** 2024-03-21
- **Who:** United Nations General Assembly (US-led draft, 120+ co-sponsors)
- **Type:** policy

The UN General Assembly adopted by consensus its first resolution on artificial intelligence, calling for AI systems that are safe, secure and trustworthy and that respect human rights. It also urged support for developing countries to benefit from AI. The text is non-binding, but it was the first global body-wide statement on AI governance and it set a shared baseline for later international talks.
