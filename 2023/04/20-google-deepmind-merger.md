# Google merges DeepMind and Google Brain into Google DeepMind

- **Date:** 2023-04-20
- **Who:** Google, Alphabet, DeepMind, Google Brain
- **Type:** people

Sundar Pichai announced that Google's two main AI research groups, DeepMind and Google Brain, would combine into one unit called Google DeepMind, led by Demis Hassabis as CEO. Jeff Dean moved to chief scientist, and a scientific board was created to oversee research. Pichai said pooling talent and compute would speed progress. It mattered because it reorganised Google's AI research around competing with OpenAI after ChatGPT.
