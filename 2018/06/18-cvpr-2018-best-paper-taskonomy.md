# CVPR 2018 awards Best Paper to Taskonomy

- **Date:** 2018-06-18
- **Who:** CVPR 2018 organizers; Taskonomy authors (Amir Zamir, Alexander Sax, William Shen, Leonidas Guibas, Jitendra Malik, Silvio Savarese); Total Capture authors (Hanbyul Joo, Tomas Simon, Yaser Sheikh)
- **Type:** competition

At the opening of CVPR 2018 in Salt Lake City, the organizers announced the main awards. The Best Paper went to "Taskonomy: Disentangling Task Transfer Learning," which maps how visual tasks transfer to one another. The Best Student Paper went to "Total Capture: A 3D Deformation Model for Tracking Faces, Hands, and Bodies." NVIDIA's SPLATNet, a sparse lattice network for point clouds, received a Best Paper honorable mention.
