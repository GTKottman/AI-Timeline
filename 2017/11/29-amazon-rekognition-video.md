# Amazon launches Rekognition Video for tracking people in video

- **Date:** 2017-11-29
- **Who:** Amazon Web Services
- **Type:** product

At re:Invent 2017, AWS launched Rekognition Video, an extension of its image-recognition service that detects and tracks people and objects in video stored in S3 and in live streams. It was announced alongside translation, transcription and comprehension services, extending AWS's managed AI APIs from still images to video. It made video analysis available as a pay-per-use cloud API.
