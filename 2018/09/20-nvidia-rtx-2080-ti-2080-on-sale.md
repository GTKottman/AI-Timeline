# NVIDIA's GeForce RTX 2080 Ti and RTX 2080 go on sale with Turing tensor cores

- **Date:** 2018-09-20
- **Who:** NVIDIA
- **Type:** hardware

NVIDIA's first Turing consumer cards, the GeForce RTX 2080 Ti and RTX 2080, reached the market on September 20, 2018, after their reveal in August. Each Turing streaming multiprocessor included tensor cores for deep learning alongside the new RT cores for ray tracing. The 2080 Ti launched at $999 with 11 GB of GDDR6 memory, bringing tensor-core hardware to a consumer price point for local training and inference. Turing was the same architecture family as NVIDIA's data-center GPUs, which made the consumer cards attractive to researchers and hobbyists.
