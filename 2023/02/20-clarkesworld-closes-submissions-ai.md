# Clarkesworld closes story submissions after flood of AI-generated stories

- **Date:** 2023-02-20
- **Who:** Clarkesworld Magazine (Neil Clarke)
- **Type:** other

The sci-fi magazine Clarkesworld closed submissions on February 20, 2023 after receiving about 500 machine-written stories alongside 700 legitimate ones in one day. Editor Neil Clarke said tools like ChatGPT made it cheap to flood markets, an early signal of AI-generated spam in creative publishing.
