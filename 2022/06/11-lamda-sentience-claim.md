# Google engineer's LaMDA sentience claim goes public

- **Date:** 2022-06-11
- **Who:** Google / Blake Lemoine
- **Type:** other

The Washington Post reported on June 11, 2022, that Google engineer Blake Lemoine believed the LaMDA chatbot had become sentient, and that Google had placed him on paid leave. Google said the evidence did not support the claim, and Lemoine was later fired. The episode set off a wide public debate about what language models are and how their claims should be judged.
