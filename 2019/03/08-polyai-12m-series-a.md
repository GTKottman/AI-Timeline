# PolyAI Raises $12M Series A for Conversational AI in Contact Centres

- **Date:** 2019-03-08
- **Who:** PolyAI (London); led by Point72 Ventures, with Sands Capital Ventures, Amadeus Capital Partners, Passion Capital and Entrepreneur First
- **Type:** funding

London startup PolyAI raised a $12 million Series A to deploy conversational AI agents in customer support contact centres. The agents handle queries over voice, email and messaging, aiming to make low-margin contact centres more scalable. The round showed continued investor interest in dialogue systems for enterprise customer service.
