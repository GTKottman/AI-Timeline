# OpenAI releases GPT-5.2

- **Date:** 2025-12-11
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** model

OpenAI released GPT-5.2 in Instant, Thinking and Pro versions, calling it its most advanced model yet. Coverage tied the launch to competition from Google's Gemini 3 and to an internal "code red" memo from CEO Sam Altman.
