# DeepSeek publishes DeepSeek-R1 paper on reasoning from reinforcement learning

- **Date:** 2025-01-22
- **Who:** DeepSeek-AI
- **Type:** paper

The DeepSeek-R1 paper showed that a language model can learn to reason through reinforcement learning alone, without human-written reasoning examples. The resulting model performed strongly on tasks with checkable answers such as math, coding and STEM problems. The authors also showed that its reasoning traces could be distilled into smaller models, a result widely reused in open-source work.
