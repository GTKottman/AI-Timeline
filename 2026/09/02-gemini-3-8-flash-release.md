# Google releases Gemini 3.8 Flash and a cyber variant

- **Date:** 2026-09-02
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** model

Google released Gemini 3.8 Flash, built on Gemini 3.7 Flash. A more permissively mitigated cybersecurity variant, 3.8 Flash Cyber, was offered to vetted partners through the Fairwind Program.
