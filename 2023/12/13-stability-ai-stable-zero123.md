# Stability AI releases Stable Zero123

- **Date:** 2023-12-13
- **Who:** Stability AI
- **Type:** model

Stability AI released Stable Zero123, a view-conditioned image model that generates novel views of an object from a single input image. It is built on Stable Diffusion 1.5 and trained on a filtered Objaverse set, and Stability reported about 40x faster training than Zero123-XL. It mattered as a building block for text-to-image-to-3D pipelines, released for non-commercial and research use.
