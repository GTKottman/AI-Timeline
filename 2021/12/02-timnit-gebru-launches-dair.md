# Timnit Gebru launches Distributed AI Research Institute (DAIR)

- **Date:** 2021-12-02
- **Who:** Timnit Gebru; funded by several foundations including MacArthur, Ford, Kapor Center and Open Society
- **Type:** other

On December 2, 2021, the first anniversary of her departure from Google, Timnit Gebru launched the Distributed AI Research Institute (DAIR), an independent AI research institute aimed at countering Big Tech's influence on AI. Coverage reports starting funding of about $3.7 million from several foundations. The launch marked a move of AI ethics research outside large tech companies.
