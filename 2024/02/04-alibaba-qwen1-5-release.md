# Alibaba releases Qwen1.5 model family

- **Date:** 2024-02-04
- **Who:** Alibaba Cloud (Qwen team)
- **Type:** model

Alibaba's Qwen team released Qwen1.5, a beta of its Qwen2 generation, with base and chat models from 0.5B up to 72B parameters plus a mixture-of-experts variant, all with 32K context support. Weights were published openly, though the training data and full training code were not released. It mattered as the main open-weight family from a Chinese lab at the time, and it gave developers a range of sizes from phone-scale to large models.
