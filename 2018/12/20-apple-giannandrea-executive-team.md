# Apple names John Giannandrea to its executive team

- **Date:** 2018-12-20
- **Who:** Apple (John Giannandrea)
- **Type:** people

Apple named John Giannandrea, who had joined in April 2018, a senior vice president on its executive team, with responsibility for machine learning and AI strategy across products. His remit covers Core ML, Create ML and Siri development. Putting AI leadership on the executive team signalled that machine learning was central to Apple's platform strategy.
