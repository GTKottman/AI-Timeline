# OpenAI launches Democratic Inputs to AI grant program

- **Date:** 2023-05-25
- **Who:** OpenAI (nonprofit arm)
- **Type:** research

OpenAI announced ten grants of $100,000 each for experiments in setting up democratic processes to decide what rules AI systems should follow, open to any applicant regardless of background. The initial experiments were not meant to be binding and were limited to model behaviour. It mattered as a public attempt to give outsiders a voice in model-behaviour rules. The January 2024 update reported that the awards went to 10 teams from nearly 1,000 applicants.
