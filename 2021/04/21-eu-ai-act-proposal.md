# European Commission proposes the AI Act

- **Date:** 2021-04-21
- **Who:** European Commission
- **Type:** policy

The European Commission proposed the Artificial Intelligence Act, a risk-based legal framework that sorts AI systems into unacceptable, high, limited and minimal risk tiers. It was the first comprehensive attempt by a major regulator to set binding rules for AI across sectors, and it shaped later debate on AI regulation worldwide.
