# ICML 2022 Test of Time award

- **Date:** 2022-07-20
- **Who:** ICML 2022 (Battista Biggio, Blaine Nelson, Pavel Laskov)
- **Type:** competition

ICML announced its 2022 Test of Time Award, announced in July 2022, to Biggio, Nelson and Laskov for their 2012 ICML paper "Poisoning Attacks against Support Vector Machines". The committee said it was one of the earliest and most influential works on poisoning attacks, which the community now studies widely.
