# CVPR 2021 best paper award goes to GIRAFFE

- **Date:** 2021-06-25
- **Who:** M. Niemeyer, A. Geiger (University of Tubingen / MPI-IS); CVPR 2021 organizers
- **Type:** competition

CVPR 2021 gave its best paper award to "GIRAFFE: Representing Scenes as Compositional Generative Neural Feature Fields" by Michael Niemeyer and Andreas Geiger. The method represents scenes as compositions of separately controllable objects, so object placement and identity can be changed in generated images. The University of Tubingen announced the award in a press release dated 25 June 2021.
