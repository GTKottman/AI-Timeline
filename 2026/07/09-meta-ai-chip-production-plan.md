# Meta plans to put its own AI chip into production

- **Date:** 2026-07-09
- **Who:** Meta
- **Type:** hardware

Reuters reported that Meta planned to put an AI chip into production in September and return to custom chips alongside Nvidia GPUs. It was a sign that large platforms were moving to in-house accelerators to cut compute costs.
