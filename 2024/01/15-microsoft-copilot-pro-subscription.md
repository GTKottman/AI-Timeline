# Microsoft launches Copilot Pro at $20 per month

- **Date:** 2024-01-15
- **Who:** Microsoft
- **Type:** product

Copilot Pro gave subscribers priority access to the latest OpenAI models, the Copilot GPT Builder and Image Creator from Designer for $20 per user per month. It was Microsoft's paid consumer tier for its AI assistant.
