# Intel Core Ultra (Meteor Lake) goes on sale with first consumer NPU

- **Date:** 2023-12-14
- **Who:** Intel
- **Type:** hardware

Intel's Core Ultra processors, code-named Meteor Lake, went on sale in laptops on 14 December 2023. They were Intel's first processors with an integrated neural processing unit (NPU) for AI acceleration on the PC. It mattered as the start of the "AI PC" category, where on-device AI inference moved into mainstream laptops.
