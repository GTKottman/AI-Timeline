# Google invests $75 million in A24 for DeepMind AI filmmaking research

- **Date:** 2026-06-23
- **Who:** Google, Google DeepMind, A24
- **Type:** other

Google is investing about $75 million in the independent studio A24 under a multiyear, nonexclusive research partnership with Google DeepMind to build AI production tools for filmmakers, such as storyboarding. The Wall Street Journal first reported the amount. The deal gives Google no access to A24's content library or data. Some outlets call it Alphabet's first equity stake in a film studio, which could not be confirmed.
