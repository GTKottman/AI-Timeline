# Gemini Deep Think reaches gold-medal standard at the International Mathematical Olympiad

- **Date:** 2025-07-21
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** competition

An advanced version of Gemini with Deep Think fully solved five of six problems at the 2025 International Mathematical Olympiad for 35 of 42 points, and IMO graders certified the result. It worked in natural language within the 4.5-hour exam limit, unlike DeepMind's earlier AlphaProof and AlphaGeometry systems, which needed formal translation and days of computation. It was a gold-medal result for an AI system on a major math competition.
