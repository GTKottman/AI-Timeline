# AMD agrees to acquire Silo AI for 665 million USD

- **Date:** 2024-07-10
- **Who:** AMD; Silo AI (Helsinki, Finland; CEO Peter Sarlin)
- **Type:** acquisition

AMD signed a definitive agreement to buy Silo AI, Europe's largest private AI lab, in an all-cash deal worth about 665 million USD. Silo AI had built custom models for clients such as Rolls-Royce, Nokia and Philips, and it developed the open multilingual LLMs Poro and Viking. AMD positioned the deal as a step toward end-to-end AI solutions on open standards, and the deal was expected to close in the second half of 2024.
