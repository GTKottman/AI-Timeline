# Baidu Create 2019: Apollo 5.0 and DuerOS 5.0 released

- **Date:** 2019-07-03
- **Who:** Baidu (Apollo autonomous driving platform; DuerOS voice assistant)
- **Type:** software

At Baidu Create in Beijing, Baidu released Apollo 5.0, adding 17 core capabilities to the Apollo Open Platform and two mass-production solutions for self-driving taxis and intelligent signal control on the Apollo Enterprise side. It also launched DuerOS 5.0 with full-duplex conversation that needs no wake word, and said DuerOS had passed 400 million devices. The release put Baidu's autonomous driving and voice stack under a single v5.0 version number.
