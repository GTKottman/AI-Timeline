# AlphaFold database expands to about 200 million protein structures

- **Date:** 2022-07-28
- **Who:** DeepMind and EMBL-EBI
- **Type:** dataset

DeepMind and EMBL's European Bioinformatics Institute added predicted structures for around 200 million proteins from about one million species to the AlphaFold Protein Structure Database. This covered almost every known protein at the time and made AI-predicted structures available to biologists in bulk.
