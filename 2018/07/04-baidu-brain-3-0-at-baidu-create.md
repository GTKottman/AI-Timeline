# Baidu launches Brain 3.0 at Baidu Create 2018

- **Date:** 2018-07-04
- **Who:** Baidu
- **Type:** product

At Baidu Create in Beijing (July 4-5, 2018) Baidu launched Baidu Brain 3.0, a platform of more than 110 AI capabilities, including facial recognition and natural language processing, with drag-and-drop tools for building AI apps. Baidu also said it would develop a high-performance AI chip for cloud to edge use, and showed an L4 autonomous bus. It mattered as a full-stack AI platform push from one of China's largest AI companies.
