# OpenAI paper: learning to summarize from human feedback

- **Date:** 2020-09-02
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** paper

The arXiv paper "Learning to summarize from human feedback" collected human comparisons between candidate summaries, trained a reward model on them, and fine-tuned a summarizer with reinforcement learning. The summaries it produced beat human reference summaries and larger supervised models, and transferred to news articles. It was an early large-scale demonstration of reinforcement learning from human feedback for language models.
