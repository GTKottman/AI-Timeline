# AI Futures Project publishes the AI 2027 scenario forecast

- **Date:** 2025-04-03
- **Who:** AI Futures Project
- **Type:** research

The AI Futures Project published AI 2027, a scenario forecast of how AI systems might grow more capable and automate AI research over the next few years, with a timeline of milestones through 2027. It gained wide attention among researchers and policymakers, and it fed the public debate on AI timelines and safety.
