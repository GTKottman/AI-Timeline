# Huawei Unveils Atlas 900, Its "Fastest AI Training Cluster"

- **Date:** 2019-09-18
- **Who:** Huawei (Huawei Connect 2019, Shanghai)
- **Type:** hardware

Huawei unveiled the Atlas 900 AI training cluster at Huawei Connect 2019 in Shanghai and said it trained ResNet-50 in 59.8 seconds, which it described as 10 seconds faster than the previous record. The cluster is built on Ascend 910 processors. The record figure is Huawei's own claim and was not independently verified in the sources.
