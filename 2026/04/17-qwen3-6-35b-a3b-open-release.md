# Alibaba releases open-weights Qwen3.6-35B-A3B

- **Date:** 2026-04-17
- **Who:** Alibaba Qwen team
- **Type:** model

Alibaba released Qwen3.6-35B-A3B, an Apache 2.0 open-weights mixture-of-experts model, while keeping its Qwen3.6-Plus flagship proprietary. It was another sign of Chinese labs mixing open and closed releases.
