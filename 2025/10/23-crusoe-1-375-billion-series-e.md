# Crusoe raises $1.375 billion Series E at over $10 billion valuation

- **Date:** 2025-10-23
- **Who:** Crusoe; Mubadala Capital and Valor Equity Partners (co-leads); NVIDIA, Fidelity, Salesforce Ventures
- **Type:** funding

AI data-center infrastructure company Crusoe announced an oversubscribed $1.375 billion Series E at a valuation above $10 billion, co-led by Mubadala Capital and Valor Equity Partners, with NVIDIA, Fidelity and Salesforce Ventures also participating. The money funds the build-out of AI compute capacity. Later reports of a larger round at about $30 billion were not confirmed by Crusoe in these sources.
