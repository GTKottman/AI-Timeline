# White House releases Blueprint for an AI Bill of Rights

- **Date:** 2022-10-04
- **Who:** White House Office of Science and Technology Policy (OSTP)
- **Type:** policy

The White House Office of Science and Technology Policy released the Blueprint for an AI Bill of Rights, a non-binding framework with five principles: safe and effective systems, protection from algorithmic discrimination, data privacy, notice and explanation, and human alternatives. It set out the US government's early position on how automated and AI systems should treat people, though it has no enforcement power.
