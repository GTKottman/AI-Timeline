# Google DeepMind unveils AlphaMissense for genetic variant classification

- **Date:** 2023-09-19
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** research

DeepMind announced AlphaMissense, an AlphaFold-based model that classifies single-letter DNA changes as likely benign or likely pathogenic, published in Science. At a precision threshold of 90%, it classified 89% of 71 million possible missense variants, against the roughly 0.1% that human experts had confirmed. DeepMind released the predictions for research and commercial use and open-sourced the model code.
