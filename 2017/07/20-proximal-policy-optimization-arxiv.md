# Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) algorithm posted to arXiv

- **Date:** 2017-07-20
- **Who:** John Schulman, Filip Wolski, Prafulla Dhariwal, Alec Radford, Oleg Klimov (OpenAI)
- **Type:** paper

The paper "Proximal Policy Optimization Algorithms" (arXiv 1707.06347) introduced PPO, a policy-gradient method that gets most of the stability of trust region policy optimization while being much simpler to implement. It alternates data collection with clipped surrogate-objective updates and beat other online policy gradient methods on robotic locomotion and Atari benchmarks. PPO became one of the most widely used reinforcement learning algorithms in later research and products.
