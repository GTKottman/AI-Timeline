# Self-Normalizing Neural Networks with SELU activations (arXiv)

- **Date:** 2017-06-08
- **Who:** Günter Klambauer, Thomas Unterthiner, Andreas Mayr, Sepp Hochreiter (Johannes Kepler University Linz)
- **Type:** paper

The paper (arXiv 1706.02515) proposed the SELU activation, which keeps neuron activations near zero mean and unit variance without explicit normalization layers. The authors used a fixed-point argument to show the self-normalizing property holds under noise, and reported results on 121 UCI tasks and the Tox21 dataset. It was accepted at NIPS 2017 and gave deep feed-forward networks a simpler normalization alternative.
