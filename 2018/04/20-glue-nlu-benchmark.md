# GLUE natural language understanding benchmark

- **Date:** 2018-04-20
- **Who:** Paper authors of arXiv:1804.07461 (GLUE)
- **Type:** dataset

GLUE is a multi-task benchmark and analysis platform for natural language understanding. It combines nine NLU tasks, a diagnostic test suite for specific linguistic phenomena, and an online platform for evaluating and comparing models. The multi-task design rewards sharing knowledge across tasks, which helps tasks with very little training data. It became one of the main leaderboards for general-purpose language models.
