# Run:AI raises $30 million Series B led by Insight Partners

- **Date:** 2021-01-26
- **Who:** Run:AI; led by Insight Partners, with TLV Partners and S-Capital
- **Type:** funding

Tel Aviv startup Run:AI announced a $30 million Series B on January 26, 2021, led by Insight Partners, bringing its total venture funding to $43 million. Run:AI builds an orchestration and virtualization layer that lets companies share and schedule GPU clusters for AI workloads. The company said it would use the money to expand quickly and to grow its team.
