# Mistral AI releases Saba, a 24B model for Arabic and South Asian languages

- **Date:** 2025-02-17
- **Who:** Mistral AI
- **Type:** model

Mistral Saba is a 24-billion-parameter model trained for the Middle East and South Asia, with strength in Arabic and South Indian languages such as Tamil and Malayalam. Mistral says it runs on a single GPU at over 150 tokens per second, is offered through its API and can be deployed inside a customer's own infrastructure. Its performance claims, including beating models more than five times its size, come from Mistral's own evaluation.
