# Google launches AI Mode, a conversational Gemini search interface

- **Date:** 2025-05-20
- **Who:** Google
- **Type:** product

Google launched AI Mode, a conversational interface in Google Search powered by Gemini, for follow-up questions and multi-step queries. It moved generative answers from a summary box into a full search mode. It was announced at Google I/O 2025.
