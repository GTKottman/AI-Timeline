# Google publishes Gemini 2.5 technical report

- **Date:** 2025-07-07
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** paper

The Gemini 2.5 technical report covers the Gemini 2.5 model family and its advances in reasoning, multimodal input, long context and agentic capabilities. It documents the models that Google had released since March, including Gemini 2.5 Pro.
