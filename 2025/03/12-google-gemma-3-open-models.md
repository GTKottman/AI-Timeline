# Google releases Gemma 3 open models in four sizes

- **Date:** 2025-03-12
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** model

Google released Gemma 3 as an open model family in 1B, 4B, 12B and 27B sizes, built on the research behind Gemini 2.0. The 4B, 12B and 27B models accept image and text input. The context window is 128K for the larger models and 32K for the 1B model. Google says the models support more than 140 languages.
