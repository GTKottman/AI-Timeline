# SenseTime raises $600M Series C led by Alibaba

- **Date:** 2018-04-09
- **Who:** SenseTime; Alibaba (lead), Temasek, Suning
- **Type:** funding

Chinese computer-vision company SenseTime announced a $600 million Series C led by Alibaba, with Temasek and Suning also taking part. Reported valuations were in the range of $3 billion to $4.5 billion, depending on the source. It followed a $410 million Series B in July 2017.
