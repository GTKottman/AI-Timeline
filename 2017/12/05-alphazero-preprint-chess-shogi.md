# AlphaZero preprint: superhuman chess and shogi by self-play

- **Date:** 2017-12-05
- **Who:** DeepMind (Google)
- **Type:** paper

DeepMind posted a preprint describing AlphaZero, a single algorithm that learned chess, shogi and Go from self-play, starting from random play and the game rules. It reached superhuman strength in chess and shogi within 24 hours and beat a top program in each game, including Stockfish in chess and Elmo in shogi. The paper showed that one general learning method could master several games.
