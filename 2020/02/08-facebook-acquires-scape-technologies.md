# Facebook acquires Scape Technologies, London computer vision startup

- **Date:** 2020-02-08
- **Who:** Facebook; Scape Technologies (London)
- **Type:** acquisition

Facebook quietly acquired Scape Technologies, a London computer vision startup, as shown in UK Companies House filings. Scape offered a visual positioning service that builds 3D maps in the cloud for location accuracy beyond GPS. TechCrunch estimated the price at about $40 million, though Facebook did not disclose it. Coverage pointed to augmented reality and robotics uses.
