# Anthropic hires OpenAI co-founder Durk Kingma

- **Date:** 2024-10-01
- **Who:** Anthropic; Durk Kingma
- **Type:** people

Durk Kingma, co-author of the Adam optimiser and the variational autoencoder and an OpenAI co-founder, joined Anthropic. It was a high-profile research hire from one frontier lab to another.
