# Google opens Project Genie world-model prototype to US Ultra subscribers

- **Date:** 2026-01-29
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** product

Google DeepMind rolled out Project Genie, an experimental prototype built on its Genie 3 world model, to US Google AI Ultra subscribers aged 18+. Users can generate interactive environments from text and images, explore them and remix other users' worlds, with simulations limited to 60 seconds and some input lag. Google framed it as a research prototype for feedback rather than a game engine.
