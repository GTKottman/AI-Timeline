# Baidu posts ERNIE 3.0 Titan, a 260-billion-parameter Chinese model

- **Date:** 2021-12-23
- **Who:** Baidu
- **Type:** model

Baidu posted the ERNIE 3.0 Titan paper to arXiv on December 23, 2021. The model has over 260 billion parameters and the authors describe it as the largest Chinese dense pretrained model to date, using knowledge-enhanced pretraining and an online distillation scheme. The authors report state-of-the-art results on 68 NLP datasets.
