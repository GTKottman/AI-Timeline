# Apple announces M1 chip with 16-core Neural Engine

- **Date:** 2020-11-10
- **Who:** Apple
- **Type:** hardware

Apple announced the M1, its first Apple-silicon chip for Mac, with a 16-core Neural Engine rated at about 11 trillion operations per second. Apple said machine learning tasks on the Mac ran up to 15 times faster, with work spread across the CPU, GPU and Neural Engine. The chip put dedicated ML acceleration into mainstream consumer laptops and desktops.
