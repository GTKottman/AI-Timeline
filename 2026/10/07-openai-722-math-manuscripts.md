# OpenAI uploads 722 math manuscripts, including Kakeya results

- **Date:** 2026-10-07
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** research

OpenAI uploaded 722 mathematical manuscripts to GitHub, grouped into 372 achievement families. Two of the papers claim stronger Kakeya results: a proof of the stronger version of the Kakeya problem in three dimensions and of the dimension conjecture in four dimensions. The results are not formalized and have not been independently verified.
