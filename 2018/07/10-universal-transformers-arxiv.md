# "Universal Transformers" posted to arXiv

- **Date:** 2018-07-10
- **Who:** Mostafa Dehghani, Stephan Gouws, Oriol Vinyals, Jakob Uszkoreit, Łukasz Kaiser (Google Brain and University of Amsterdam)
- **Type:** paper

The paper proposes a recurrent, parallel generalisation of the Transformer with per-position adaptive halting. It improved results on algorithmic tasks and language modelling over the standard Transformer. It was one of the early papers extending the architecture that BERT and later large language models built on.
