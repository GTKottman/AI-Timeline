# Facebook AI open-sources Pythia for vision and language

- **Date:** 2019-05-21
- **Who:** Facebook AI Research
- **Type:** software

Facebook AI Research released Pythia, an open-source PyTorch framework for multimodal vision-and-language models such as visual question answering and image captioning. It built on Facebook's winning entries in the 2018 VQA and VizWiz challenges and included reference models and datasets such as VQA, VizWiz and TextVQA. The goal was reproducible research in multimodal AI.
