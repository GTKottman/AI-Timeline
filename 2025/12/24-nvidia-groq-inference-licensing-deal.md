# NVIDIA strikes about $20 billion licensing and asset deal with Groq

- **Date:** 2025-12-24
- **Who:** NVIDIA; Groq
- **Type:** acquisition

NVIDIA agreed to license Groq's inference technology and buy assets for about $20 billion, and Groq founder Jonathan Ross and other executives moved to NVIDIA. Groq said it would stay independent. It was among the largest AI-chip deals of the year and an inference-focused move by NVIDIA.
