# Runway launches Aleph, an in-context video editing model

- **Date:** 2025-07-25
- **Who:** Runway
- **Type:** model

Runway launched Aleph, a video-to-video model that edits existing footage with instructions, such as adding or removing objects, generating new camera angles, and changing style or lighting. Runway's post says it went to all paid users, though some outlets reported a first rollout to enterprise and creative clients. It mattered as a move from text-to-video generation toward post-production editing with AI.
