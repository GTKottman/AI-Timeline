# xAI announces Grok-1.5 Vision

- **Date:** 2024-04-12
- **Who:** xAI
- **Type:** model

Grok-1.5V added image understanding to the Grok line, letting the model answer questions about documents, diagrams, charts, screenshots and photos. xAI called it its first multimodal model. It shipped first to early testers and existing Grok users.
