# UK ICO rules Royal Free broke data law in sharing patient data with DeepMind

- **Date:** 2017-07-03
- **Who:** UK Information Commissioner's Office, Royal Free NHS Trust, DeepMind (Google)
- **Type:** policy

The UK Information Commissioner's Office ruled that the Royal Free Hospital failed to comply with the Data Protection Act when it gave DeepMind records of about 1.6 million patients to test the Streams app, which flags acute kidney injury. The main finding was that patients were not properly told their data would be used for the test. The ICO required no penalty, and the Trust accepted its findings.
