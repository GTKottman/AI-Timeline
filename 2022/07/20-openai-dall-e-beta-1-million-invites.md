# OpenAI opens DALL-E beta to 1 million waitlisted users

- **Date:** 2022-07-20
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** product

OpenAI moved DALL-E from its research preview into a beta and began inviting 1 million people from its waitlist over the following weeks. New users received 50 free credits in their first month and 15 each month after, and beta users were allowed to use generated images commercially. The change moved text-to-image generation from a limited research preview into wide public use.
