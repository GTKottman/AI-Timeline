# Andrej Karpathy leaves Tesla as senior director of AI

- **Date:** 2022-07-13
- **Who:** Tesla / Andrej Karpathy
- **Type:** people

Andrej Karpathy, Tesla's senior director of AI and Autopilot Vision, announced in July 2022 that he was leaving the company after more than five years. He had led the computer-vision and neural-network work behind Tesla's self-driving system.
