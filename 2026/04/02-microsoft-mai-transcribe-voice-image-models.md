# Microsoft releases three in-house MAI models for speech, voice and images

- **Date:** 2026-04-02
- **Who:** Microsoft
- **Type:** model

Microsoft launched MAI-Transcribe-1 (speech-to-text in 25 languages), MAI-Voice-1 (speech generation with custom voices from a few seconds of audio) and MAI-Image-2 (image generation). The models are available through Microsoft Foundry and the MAI Playground. Speed and accuracy figures are company-reported.
