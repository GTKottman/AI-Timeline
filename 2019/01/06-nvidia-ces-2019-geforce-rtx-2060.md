# Nvidia launches GeForce RTX 2060 at CES 2019

- **Date:** 2019-01-06
- **Who:** Nvidia (Jensen Huang)
- **Type:** hardware

At its CES 2019 opening press conference, Nvidia launched the GeForce RTX 2060 on its Turing architecture, with Tensor cores for AI-based features such as DLSS, and announced more than 40 laptop models with Turing GPUs. The RTX 2060 was priced at $349. Its main significance for AI was bringing Turing's deep-learning hardware to a consumer card. Jensen Huang presented at the MGM Grand in Las Vegas.
