# Intel unveils Nervana NNP-T and NNP-I AI chips in production

- **Date:** 2019-11-12
- **Who:** Intel (Nervana group)
- **Type:** hardware

At its AI Summit in San Francisco, Intel showed its first purpose-built deep learning ASICs: the NNP-T1000 for training in data centers and the NNP-I1000 for inference. Intel said both were in production, with Baidu and Facebook named among early customers. It also announced a next-generation Movidius Myriad VPU for edge vision. The launch marked Intel's serious entry into dedicated AI accelerators against NVIDIA GPUs. Performance claims are vendor-stated.
