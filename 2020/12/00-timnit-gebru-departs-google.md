# Timnit Gebru forced out of Google's Ethical AI team

- **Date:** 2020-12 (exact day not confirmed)
- **Who:** Google; Timnit Gebru (former technical co-lead, Ethical AI team)
- **Type:** people

Google ended the employment of Timnit Gebru, technical co-lead of its Ethical AI team, after management asked her to withdraw a paper on the risks of large language models. The paper was "On the Dangers of Stochastic Parrots." Her dispute over whether she resigned or was fired became a major public controversy over research independence and diversity in AI labs.
