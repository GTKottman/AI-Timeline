# Mistral launches Le Chat mobile apps

- **Date:** 2025-02-06
- **Who:** Mistral AI
- **Type:** product

Mistral launched Le Chat apps for iOS and Android, free to use, alongside a Pro tier at $14.99 a month that offers faster responses and access to premium models, according to Maginative. AFP reported the launch on Thursday, February 6, 2025.
