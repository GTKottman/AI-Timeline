# Facebook AI Habitat Open-Sourced

- **Date:** 2019-06-14
- **Who:** Facebook AI
- **Type:** software

Facebook AI open-sourced AI Habitat, a simulation platform for training embodied agents such as virtual robots in photo-realistic 3D environments. It has two parts: Habitat-Sim, a fast 3D simulator, and Habitat-API, a library for defining and benchmarking tasks such as navigation, instruction following and question answering. It was released alongside Replica, a dataset of detailed 3D reconstructions of indoor spaces. Facebook's speed claims for the simulator came from its own materials and were not independently verified.
