# Nvidia agrees to acquire Arm from SoftBank for $40 billion

- **Date:** 2020-09-13
- **Who:** Nvidia, SoftBank Group, Arm
- **Type:** acquisition

Nvidia and SoftBank signed a definitive agreement for Nvidia to buy Arm Limited, the chip-design company whose licensed architecture runs most mobile and embedded processors, for about $40 billion. Nvidia paid $2 billion at signing with $10 billion in cash and 44.3 million shares due at closing, and it planned to keep Arm's Cambridge headquarters and brand. For AI, the deal aimed to combine GPU and CPU design for data-center and edge hardware, and it drew heavy regulatory scrutiny in the UK, EU, China and US.
