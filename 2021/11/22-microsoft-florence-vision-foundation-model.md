# Microsoft unveils Florence, a foundation model for computer vision

- **Date:** 2021-11-22
- **Who:** Microsoft Research (Florence team; Lu Yuan lead author)
- **Type:** model

Microsoft Research submitted Florence to arXiv on 22 November 2021 (arXiv 2111.11432). It is meant to extend visual representations from scenes to objects, from images to video, and from RGB to other modalities such as depth. The authors report new state-of-the-art results on most of 44 representative benchmarks, including few-shot, linear-probe and zero-shot transfer.
