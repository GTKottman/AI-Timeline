# Meta publishes Chameleon mixed-modal early-fusion model paper

- **Date:** 2024-05-16
- **Who:** Meta FAIR
- **Type:** paper

Meta published "Chameleon: Mixed-Modal Early-Fusion Foundation Models," describing models that tokenize images and text into one sequence and process them in a single transformer, trained on interleaved image-text data. The authors reported state-of-the-art image captioning and results that matched or exceeded Gemini Pro and GPT-4V in human judgments on mixed-modal long-form generation. It was posted to arXiv on May 16, 2024.
