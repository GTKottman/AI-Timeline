# California AB 489 bars AI from implying a healthcare license

- **Date:** 2025-10-11
- **Who:** Governor Gavin Newsom, California legislature
- **Type:** policy

California Governor Gavin Newsom signed AB 489, which restricts AI systems from implying they hold a professional healthcare license. It is one of a batch of California AI bills signed in early October 2025, alongside AB 979 on an AI component in the state's cybersecurity center.
