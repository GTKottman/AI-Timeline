# Google's "Looking to Listen" separates voices using video of the speaker

- **Date:** 2018-04-11
- **Who:** Google Research (Ariel Ephrat, Inbar Mosseri, Oran Lang and colleagues)
- **Type:** research

Google Research introduced an audio-visual model that pulls one speaker's voice out of a mix of overlapping voices by using the speaker's face in the video. The model is speaker-independent, so one trained network works across different people. The paper, "Looking to Listen at the Cocktail Party," beat audio-only separation when speech overlaps. It also introduced AVSpeech, a dataset of thousands of hours of web video used for training, and was presented at SIGGRAPH 2018.
