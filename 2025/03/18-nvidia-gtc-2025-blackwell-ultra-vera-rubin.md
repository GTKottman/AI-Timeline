# NVIDIA GTC 2025 keynote sets Blackwell Ultra and Vera Rubin roadmap

- **Date:** 2025-03-18
- **Who:** NVIDIA
- **Type:** hardware

At its GTC 2025 keynote in San Jose, Jensen Huang presented Blackwell Ultra for AI reasoning workloads, with the GB300 NVL72 rack pairing 72 Blackwell Ultra GPUs with 36 Grace CPUs, expected in the second half of 2025. He also gave the roadmap for Vera Rubin, the successor, with HBM4 memory, expected in the second half of 2026. Sources are mostly secondary, so the specifications and timing are as reported in coverage.
