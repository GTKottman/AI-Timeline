# Rad AI raises $8M seed round for radiology AI

- **Date:** 2020-08-01
- **Who:** Rad AI
- **Type:** funding

Rad AI, a startup building AI tools to assist radiologists with reporting, announced an $8 million seed round. It was one of the smaller but steady stream of 2020 healthcare AI investments that moved medical imaging models from research into clinical workflow software.
