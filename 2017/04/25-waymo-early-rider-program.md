# Waymo launches early rider program in Phoenix area

- **Date:** 2017-04-25
- **Who:** Waymo (Alphabet)
- **Type:** product

Waymo, Alphabet's self-driving car unit, opened a free early rider program to residents of the Phoenix area, including Chandler, Tempe, Mesa and Gilbert. Riders travelled in its autonomous vehicles with a test driver aboard. Waymo said it wanted feedback on how riders communicate with the vehicles, and it planned to grow its Chrysler Pacifica Hybrid fleet from 100 to about 600 vehicles.
