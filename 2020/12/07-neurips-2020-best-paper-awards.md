# NeurIPS 2020 best paper awards go to GPT-3 and two theory papers

- **Date:** 2020-12-07
- **Who:** NeurIPS 2020 organisers; winners from OpenAI (Tom B. Brown et al.), UC Berkeley (Derezinski, Khanna, Mahoney), and Celli, Marchesi, Farina, Gatti
- **Type:** competition

NeurIPS 2020 gave three Best Paper Awards. The winners were "Language Models are Few-Shot Learners" (GPT-3, 175 billion parameters), "Improved Guarantees and a Multiple-Descent Curve for Column Subset Selection and the Nystrom Method," and "No-Regret Learning Dynamics for Extensive-Form Correlated Equilibrium." A Test of Time award was also given. The GPT-3 award gave official recognition to the paper that set off the large-language-model era.
