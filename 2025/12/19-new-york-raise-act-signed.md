# New York governor signs RAISE Act on frontier AI safety

- **Date:** 2025-12-19
- **Who:** Governor Kathy Hochul; New York State legislature; frontier AI developers
- **Type:** policy

Governor Hochul signed the RAISE Act (S6953B/A6453B), which requires large frontier AI developers to publish safety protocols and report incidents to the state within 72 hours. It creates an oversight office in the Department of Financial Services and civil penalties of up to $1 million for a first violation and $3 million for later ones. The signing came with chapter amendments that move the law closer to California's, and about a week after a federal executive order aimed at curbing state AI laws.
