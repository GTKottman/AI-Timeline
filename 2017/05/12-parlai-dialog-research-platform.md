# Facebook AI Research releases ParlAI dialog research platform

- **Date:** 2017-05-12
- **Who:** Facebook AI Research
- **Type:** software

ParlAI is an open source Python platform for dialog research, released by Facebook AI Research in May 2017. Its first release supported more than 20 tasks, including SQuAD, bAbI and VQA, giving dialog researchers a shared framework and benchmark base. The paper describing it was presented at the EMNLP 2017 System Demonstrations track in September 2017.
