# IBM unveils watsonx AI and data platform

- **Date:** 2023-05-09
- **Who:** IBM
- **Type:** product

IBM announced watsonx at its Think conference, a platform with watsonx.ai for building and training models, watsonx.data for data storage, and watsonx.governance for monitoring and compliance. IBM also announced a collaboration with Hugging Face to bring open-source models into the enterprise platform. IBM expected watsonx.ai and watsonx.data to be ready in July, with watsonx.governance later in 2023. It mattered as IBM's enterprise-focused answer to generative AI, built around governance.
