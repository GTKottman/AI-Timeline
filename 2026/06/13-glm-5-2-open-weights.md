# Z.ai releases GLM-5.2 open-weight model with 1M-token context

- **Date:** 2026-06-13
- **Who:** Z.ai (Zhipu AI)
- **Type:** model

Z.ai released GLM-5.2 under an MIT licence on June 13, 2026, first to GLM Coding Plan subscribers, with Hugging Face weights reported around June 16. It has a 1M-token context, up from 200K in GLM-5.1, and about 753 billion total parameters (one review says 744B) with roughly 40 billion active. Early third-party figures were reported as 81.0 or 82.7 on Terminal-Bench 2.1 and 62.1 on SWE-bench Pro. One guide says Z.ai published no official benchmark suite at launch.
