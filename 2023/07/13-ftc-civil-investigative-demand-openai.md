# FTC civil investigative demand to OpenAI over ChatGPT

- **Date:** 2023-07-13
- **Who:** US Federal Trade Commission; OpenAI
- **Type:** policy

The FTC sent OpenAI a 20-page Civil Investigative Demand, reported publicly on July 13, 2023. It asked how OpenAI handles risks such as false or defamatory outputs and personal-data exposure, what training data was used, and how many people were affected by OpenAI's March 2023 security incident. It was an early US federal enforcement step against a generative-AI company, under existing consumer-protection law.
