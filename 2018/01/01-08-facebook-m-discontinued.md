# Facebook shuts down M, its AI and human personal assistant

- **Date:** 2018-01-08
- **Who:** Facebook
- **Type:** product

Facebook announced it was discontinuing M, a Messenger personal assistant that combined AI with human trainers and was launched in 2015. The shutdown showed the difficulty of scaling assistants that depended on human-in-the-loop work and helped shift Facebook toward narrower AI features and bots.
