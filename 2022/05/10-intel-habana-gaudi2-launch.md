# Intel launches Habana Gaudi2 AI accelerator

- **Date:** 2022-05-10
- **Who:** Intel (Habana Labs)
- **Type:** hardware

Habana Labs, Intel's data-center AI unit, released the Gaudi2 training accelerator along with the Greco inference chip, unveiled at Intel's May 2022 event. Gaudi2 was pitched as a lower-cost alternative to NVIDIA GPUs for training large models. It became the main non-NVIDIA training accelerator Intel was putting forward for AI workloads.
