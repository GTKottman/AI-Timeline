# VGGT wins CVPR 2025 Best Paper

- **Date:** 2025-06-13
- **Who:** Jianyuan Wang, Minghao Chen, Nikita Karaev, Andrea Vedaldi, Christian Rupprecht, David Novotny (Oxford VGG and Meta AI)
- **Type:** competition

CVPR and the IEEE Computer Society announced VGGT as the sole Best Paper of CVPR 2025, chosen unanimously. VGGT is a feed-forward network that estimates core 3D scene properties such as camera poses and depth from hundreds of views in one pass. It mattered because it was a top conference award for a fast learned alternative to classic structure-from-motion.
