# Monica launches Manus, an autonomous general AI agent

- **Date:** 2025-03-06
- **Who:** Monica (Butterfly Effect Technology)
- **Type:** product

Chinese startup Monica launched Manus, an agent that runs in the cloud to plan and carry out multi-step tasks without constant supervision, as an invite-only beta. Wikipedia lists the release as March 6, 2025, though one account says a preview appeared on the evening of March 5. One secondary explainer says Manus integrates models including Anthropic's Claude 3.5 Sonnet and fine-tuned Qwen models; the company has not been checked on this.
