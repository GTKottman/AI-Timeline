# AMD and Anthropic agree on up to 2 GW of MI450 GPUs

- **Date:** 2026-07-00
- **Who:** AMD; Anthropic
- **Type:** hardware

AMD and Anthropic announced a strategic partnership to deploy up to 2 gigawatts of AMD Instinct MI450 GPUs, with AMD investing up to $5 billion. It was a major move to diversify Anthropic's accelerator supply beyond NVIDIA.
