# Google unveils PaLM 2 at I/O 2023

- **Date:** 2023-05-10
- **Who:** Google
- **Type:** model

At Google I/O, Google unveiled PaLM 2, a next-generation language model with stronger multilingual, reasoning and coding skills in four sizes. It was already powering dozens of Google products, and it was the base for Bard's upgrade and Google's Gemini-era stack.
