# Alibaba Qwen team releases open-weight Qwen-Image

- **Date:** 2025-08-04
- **Who:** Alibaba Qwen team
- **Type:** model

Alibaba's Qwen team released Qwen-Image, a 20-billion-parameter multimodal diffusion transformer for image generation and editing under Apache 2.0, available on Hugging Face and Qwen Chat. It is known for rendering text inside images, in both alphabetic and logographic scripts such as Chinese. It mattered as a strong open image model with reliable text rendering.

One aggregator lists 2025-08-31; the 4 August date comes from aggregator sources, and no official dated announcement was found.
