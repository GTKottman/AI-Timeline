# Character.AI raises $150M Series A at $1B valuation

- **Date:** 2023-03-23
- **Who:** Character.AI (led by Andreessen Horowitz)
- **Type:** funding

Character.AI, founded by former Google researchers, raised a $150M Series A led by Andreessen Horowitz at a valuation of about $1 billion. Reuters reported the company had no revenue at the time. The money was earmarked for more compute, a more capable model, new features and a larger engineering team. It mattered as a sign that investors would fund consumer chatbot companies with personality-driven characters before any revenue model existed.
