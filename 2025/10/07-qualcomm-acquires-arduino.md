# Qualcomm acquires Arduino

- **Date:** 2025-10-07
- **Who:** Qualcomm, Arduino
- **Type:** acquisition

Qualcomm agreed to acquire Arduino, the open-source hardware and software platform used by millions of developers, to extend its reach into robotics, IoT and edge AI. The price was not disclosed. Qualcomm said the Arduino brand and its open-source approach would stay, and the deal came with the Arduino UNO Q, built on the Qualcomm Dragonwing platform with an AI focus. Some outlets date the announcement October 8.
