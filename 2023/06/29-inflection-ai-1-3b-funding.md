# Inflection AI raises $1.3 billion led by Microsoft and NVIDIA

- **Date:** 2023-06-29
- **Who:** Inflection AI; Microsoft, NVIDIA, Reid Hoffman, Bill Gates, Eric Schmidt
- **Type:** funding

Inflection AI, maker of the Pi chatbot, announced $1.3 billion in new funding led by Microsoft and NVIDIA, bringing its total to $1.525 billion. Reports put the valuation at about $4 billion. The company said it was building a cluster of 22,000 NVIDIA H100 GPUs with CoreWeave and NVIDIA, and it pitched its Inflection-1 model as a rival to models from OpenAI, Google and Meta.
