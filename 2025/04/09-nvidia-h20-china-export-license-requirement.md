# US requires licence for Nvidia H20 exports to China, Nvidia takes $5.5B charge

- **Date:** 2025-04-09
- **Who:** US Commerce Department; NVIDIA
- **Type:** policy

The US Commerce Department told NVIDIA that exports of its H20 chip to China would need a licence, a requirement that also covers AMD's MI308 and similar chips. NVIDIA's SEC filing said it expected up to about $5.5 billion in charges tied to H20 inventory and purchase commitments. On April 14, the government told NVIDIA the licence requirement would stay in place indefinitely, citing the risk of diversion to Chinese supercomputers.
