# DeepSeek releases DeepSeek-OCR, which compresses text into vision tokens

- **Date:** 2025-10-20
- **Who:** DeepSeek
- **Type:** model

DeepSeek released DeepSeek-OCR as an open-source model that tests optical compression: long text is rendered as an image and decoded from a small number of vision tokens. The paper reports about 97% decoding precision at up to 10x compression, falling to about 60% at 20x. The model pairs a DeepEncoder with a 3-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts decoder. The paper is listed with an October 21, 2025 date by one database, so the release date is approximate.
