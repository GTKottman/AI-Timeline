# Google releases Gemma 3 open models in four sizes

- **Date:** 2025-03-12
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** model

Google released Gemma 3 as an open model family in 1B, 4B, 12B and 27B sizes, built on the research behind Gemini 2.0. The 4B to 27B models accept image and text input. Google said the 27B model was the strongest model that runs on a single accelerator, citing preliminary LMArena human-preference results. Sources disagree on language coverage, with figures of over 35 and over 140 languages. Google also reported more than 100 million Gemma downloads and over 60,000 community variants.
