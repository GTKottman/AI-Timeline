# Deepfake Detection Challenge winners announced

- **Date:** 2020-06 (exact day not confirmed)
- **Who:** Facebook, Partnership on AI, and partners; more than 2,000 participants
- **Type:** competition

The Deepfake Detection Challenge, which ran on Kaggle from December 2019 to 31 May 2020, ended with winners who spotted deepfakes with about 82.6% accuracy on a 100,000-video public dataset. More than 2,000 participants submitted over 35,000 models, sharing a $1 million prize pool. The winning models mostly used EfficientNet, and the top entries used augmentations that blended fake and real faces.
