# Hopfield Networks is All You Need links modern Hopfield nets to attention

- **Date:** 2020-07-16
- **Who:** Ramsauer et al. (Johannes Kepler University Linz and collaborators)
- **Type:** paper

The arXiv paper "Hopfield Networks is All You Need" introduced a continuous-state Hopfield network that stores very many patterns and retrieves one in a single update. The authors showed that this update rule is the attention mechanism used in transformers. They added "Hopfield layers" to deep networks, which performed well on several benchmarks.
