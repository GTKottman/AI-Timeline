# Microsoft invests $1 billion in OpenAI

- **Date:** 2019-07-22
- **Who:** Microsoft, OpenAI
- **Type:** funding

Microsoft agreed to invest $1 billion in OpenAI to support building artificial general intelligence. OpenAI said Microsoft would become its exclusive cloud provider, and the two would jointly develop Azure AI supercomputing technologies. The deal came as OpenAI moved to its capped-profit structure, and it gave OpenAI the compute it needed to train much larger models.
