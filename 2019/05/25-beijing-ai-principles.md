# Beijing AI Principles

- **Date:** 2019-05-25
- **Who:** Beijing Academy of Artificial Intelligence (BAAI), with Peking University, Tsinghua University, two Chinese Academy of Sciences institutes, and an industry alliance including Baidu, Alibaba and Tencent
- **Type:** policy

The Beijing AI Principles were unveiled by BAAI and partner institutions as a national-level AI ethics framework. They cover research and development, use, governance and long-term planning, and call for AI that benefits humanity and nature, with fairness, transparency, diversity and no data monopolies. Sources differ on the date (Xinhua and China Daily say May 25; CMS and New America say May 28). The principles arrived in a wave of Chinese AI guidance that month and called for international cooperation on AI governance.
