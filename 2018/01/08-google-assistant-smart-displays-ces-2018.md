# Google Assistant smart displays shown at CES 2018

- **Date:** 2018-01-08
- **Who:** Google, Lenovo, JBL, LG, Sony
- **Type:** product

Google showed the first smart displays with Google Assistant at CES 2018, adding a screen to the voice assistant so it could answer with visual results. Lenovo announced an 8-inch model at $200 and a 10-inch model at $250, and JBL, LG and Sony also showed devices. It was an early step toward multimodal assistant hardware competing with Amazon's Echo Show.
