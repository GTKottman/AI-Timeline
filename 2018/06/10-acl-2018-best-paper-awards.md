# ACL 2018 names its best long and short papers

- **Date:** 2018-06-10
- **Who:** Association for Computational Linguistics (ACL 2018 organizing committee)
- **Type:** other

The ACL 2018 organizing committee announced three best long papers and two best short papers ahead of the 56th Annual Meeting in Melbourne. Long-paper winners: "Finding syntax in human encephalography with beam search" (Hale, Dyer, Kuncoro, Brennan); "Learning to Ask Good Questions" (Rao, Daume III); and "Let's do it 'again'" (Cianflone et al.). Short-paper winners: "Know What You Don't Know: Unanswerable Questions for SQuAD" (Rajpurkar, Jia, Liang) and "'Lighter' Can Still Be Dark" (Winn, Muresan). It mattered as the field's signal of which language-model and reading-comprehension ideas were judged most important that year.
