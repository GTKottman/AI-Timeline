# OpenAI closes $40 billion funding round at a $300 billion valuation

- **Date:** 2025-03-31
- **Who:** OpenAI, SoftBank, Microsoft, Coatue, Altimeter, Thrive Capital
- **Type:** funding

OpenAI announced that it would raise up to $40 billion in a round led by SoftBank Group at a $300 billion post-money valuation, almost double its October 2024 valuation of $157 billion. Microsoft, Coatue, Altimeter and Thrive Capital also took part. SoftBank's commitments were staged, with its later tranche contingent on OpenAI completing its move to a for-profit structure by the end of the year.
