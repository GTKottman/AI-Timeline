# LMSYS releases Vicuna open-source chatbot

- **Date:** 2023-03-30
- **Who:** LMSYS (UC Berkeley, CMU, Stanford, UC San Diego researchers)
- **Type:** model

Vicuna is a LLaMA model fine-tuned on user conversations shared from ShareGPT, released by the LMSYS team with a reported training cost of about $300. The team's GPT-4-judged comparison claimed over 90% of ChatGPT's quality, while stating that the test was not rigorous. It mattered as one of the first cheap, widely shared demonstrations that a small fine-tuned open model could feel close to ChatGPT. Licence terms were reported inconsistently across sources, so check the current release before relying on it.
