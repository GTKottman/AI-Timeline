# Anthropic publishes Constitutional Classifiers against universal jailbreaks

- **Date:** 2025-02-03
- **Who:** Anthropic (Safeguards Research Team)
- **Type:** research

Anthropic introduced Constitutional Classifiers, safeguards trained from explicit constitutional rules that defend a model against universal jailbreaks. Its prototype held up to thousands of hours of human red-teaming, at the cost of high overrefusal and extra compute, and an updated version cut that overhead. Anthropic also ran a public jailbreak challenge and recommended complementary defenses, since new attacks may appear.
