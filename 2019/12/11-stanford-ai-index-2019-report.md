# Stanford HAI releases the 2019 AI Index report

- **Date:** 2019-12-11
- **Who:** Stanford Institute for Human-Centered AI (HAI); AI Index steering committee (chaired by Yoav Shoham)
- **Type:** research

The third annual AI Index report tracked the state of AI across research output, data sets, and economic and policy trends, with about three times as many data sets as the 2018 edition. HAI also released a Global AI Vibrancy Tool and an arXiv monitor. It became a standard reference for measuring AI progress.
