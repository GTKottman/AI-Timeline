# Stanford AI Index Report 2022

- **Date:** 2022-03-16
- **Who:** Stanford Institute for Human-Centered AI (AI Index steering committee, co-chaired by Jack Clark)
- **Type:** other

Stanford HAI released the fifth annual AI Index, about 230 pages covering research output, technical performance, ethics, the economy, education and policy. New material included a survey of robotics researchers, AI legislation data from 25 countries and technical ethics metrics. It became one of the main reference datasets people cite for the state of AI. Sources differ by a day, with HAI's own seminar page giving March 16.
