# Stanford AI Index 2024 report published

- **Date:** 2024-04-15
- **Who:** Stanford Institute for Human-Centered AI (HAI), with the Hoover Institution
- **Type:** research

Stanford HAI released the 2024 AI Index, a 502-page annual data report covering AI research output, training costs, industry investment and policy. It added a chapter on AI's effects on science and medicine and new estimates of frontier model training costs. It is one of the most cited yearly references on the state of the field.
