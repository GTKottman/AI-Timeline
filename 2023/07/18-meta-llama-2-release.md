# Meta releases Llama 2 for research and commercial use

- **Date:** 2023-07-18
- **Who:** Meta AI
- **Type:** model

Meta released Llama 2, models from 7 to 70 billion parameters, free for research and most commercial use under a license that limits very large companies. Its license made it one of the widely used open-weight base models of 2023 and fed a large open-source ecosystem.
