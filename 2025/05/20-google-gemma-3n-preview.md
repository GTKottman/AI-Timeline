# Google previews Gemma 3n, a mobile-first open model

- **Date:** 2025-05-20
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** model

Google's developer blog announced an early preview of Gemma 3n, the first open model built on a new architecture that Google said also powers the next generation of Gemini Nano. Google said it can run on as little as 2 GB of RAM, and that its Per-Layer Embeddings technique reduces RAM use. Coverage says it handles text, images, audio and video.
