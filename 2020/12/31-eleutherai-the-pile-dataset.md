# The Pile: an 800 GB diverse text dataset for language modeling

- **Date:** 2020-12-31
- **Who:** EleutherAI
- **Type:** dataset

The arXiv paper "The Pile" described an English text corpus of about 825 GiB assembled from 22 diverse subsets, many from academic and professional sources. The authors reported that GPT-2 and GPT-3 performed poorly on many of its components, and that models trained on the Pile beat those trained on Raw CC and CC-100. The dataset became a widely used open pretraining corpus for open-source language models.
