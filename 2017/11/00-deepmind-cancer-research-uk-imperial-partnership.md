# DeepMind partners with Cancer Research UK Imperial Centre on mammography ML

- **Date:** 2017-11-00
- **Who:** DeepMind (Google), Cancer Research UK Imperial Centre
- **Type:** research

DeepMind announced a research partnership with the Cancer Research UK Centre at Imperial College London to apply machine learning to mammography. It was one of DeepMind's first medical imaging collaborations beyond its NHS kidney-injury work.
