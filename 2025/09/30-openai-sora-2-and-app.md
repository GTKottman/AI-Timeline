# OpenAI releases Sora 2 and a Sora social app

- **Date:** 2025-09-30
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** model

OpenAI released Sora 2, its video and audio generation model, together with a social app built around AI-generated video. Copyright and likeness questions followed quickly, and OpenAI changed its opt-in rules for copyrighted material within days.
