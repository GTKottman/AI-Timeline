# Microsoft will not sell facial recognition to US police until national law exists

- **Date:** 2020-06-11
- **Who:** Microsoft (Brad Smith)
- **Type:** policy

Microsoft said it would not sell its facial recognition technology to U.S. police departments until a national law, grounded in human rights, governed the technology. It was the third large vendor in one week to limit police use after IBM and Amazon. Critics such as the ACLU argued the position was lobbying for weak rules that could still legitimize police use, so the pause was read as conditional rather than a moratorium.
