# UK regulator Ofcom opens investigation into X over Grok deepfakes

- **Date:** 2026-01-12
- **Who:** Ofcom; X; xAI
- **Type:** policy

Ofcom opened a formal investigation into X under the Online Safety Act over sexualised deepfakes created with Grok's image feature. Ofcom had contacted X on January 5 and asked it to explain its protective steps by January 9.
