# OpenLLaMA: permissively licensed LLaMA reproduction

- **Date:** 2023-06-07
- **Who:** OpenLM Research
- **Type:** model

OpenLLaMA released final 1-trillion-token 3B and 7B models on June 7, 2023, with a 13B model following on June 15. They reproduce Meta's LLaMA recipe on the RedPajama dataset and are released under Apache 2.0 for weights and the EasyLM training framework. This gave developers a commercially usable LLaMA-style base model when LLaMA's weights were restricted to research use.
