# Graphcore launches second-generation IPU (Colossus Mk2 GC200)

- **Date:** 2020-07-15
- **Who:** Graphcore
- **Type:** hardware

Graphcore unveiled the Colossus Mk2 GC200, a 7nm chip with 59.4 billion transistors, 1,472 cores and 900MB of in-processor memory, along with the IPU-Machine M2000 system that holds four GC200 chips. Graphcore said systems could scale to 64,000 IPUs; that figure, like its performance claims, is the company's own. The chip was positioned directly against Nvidia's A100.
