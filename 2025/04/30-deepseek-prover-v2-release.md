# DeepSeek releases Prover-V2 for Lean 4 theorem proving

- **Date:** 2025-04-30
- **Who:** DeepSeek
- **Type:** model

DeepSeek released DeepSeek-Prover-V2 in 7B and 671B sizes. The 671B model is built on DeepSeek-V3-Base and targets formal theorem proving in Lean 4. The paper reports an 88.9% pass ratio on MiniF2F-test and 49 of 658 PutnamBench problems solved; DeepSeek also released the ProverBench benchmark.
