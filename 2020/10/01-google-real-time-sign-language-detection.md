# Google research: real-time sign language detection for video calls

- **Date:** 2020-10-01
- **Who:** Google Research, with Bar-Ilan University and University of Zurich (Amit Moryossef and colleagues)
- **Type:** research

Google published a lightweight model that detects when a person is signing so that video conferencing tools can treat the signer as the active speaker. It runs on optical-flow features from pose estimation, reached about 80% accuracy with a linear classifier and up to 91% with a recurrent model, and ran in under 4 ms per frame. A browser demo was shown at ECCV 2020, and the work showed how cheap pose-based models could make calls accessible to deaf users.
