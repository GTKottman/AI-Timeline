# Seoul AI Summit ends with the Seoul Declaration on AI safety

- **Date:** 2024-05-21
- **Who:** Governments of the EU, US, UK, Korea and others
- **Type:** policy

Leaders from the EU, the US, the UK, Japan, Korea and other countries signed the Seoul Declaration for Safe, Innovative and Inclusive AI, following the 2023 Bletchley summit. Ministers also agreed a statement, and Korea announced an AI safety institute. It kept intergovernmental AI safety talks on a regular schedule.
