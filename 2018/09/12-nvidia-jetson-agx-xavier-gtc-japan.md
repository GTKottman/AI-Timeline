# NVIDIA unveils Jetson AGX Xavier, a 32-TOPS module for autonomous machines

- **Date:** 2018-09-12
- **Who:** NVIDIA, with Japanese adopters including FANUC, Komatsu, Musashi Seimitsu, Kawada and Yamaha Motor
- **Type:** hardware

At GTC Japan, NVIDIA announced the Jetson AGX Xavier, an embedded AI computer for robots and autonomous machines rated at up to 32 TOPS, with power modes from 10 W to 30 W. Its developer kit was listed at $2,499. NVIDIA named industrial adopters for next-generation autonomous machines, and its DRIVE AGX Xavier was shown for self-driving cars. It put a high-throughput inference computer in front of robotics and industrial developers.
