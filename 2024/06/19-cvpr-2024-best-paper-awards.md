# CVPR 2024 announces best paper awards

- **Date:** 2024-06-19
- **Who:** CVPR 2024 (Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition conference), IEEE Computer Society
- **Type:** competition

At the CVPR 2024 opening session in Seattle, organizers named 10 best paper winners from more than 11,500 submissions, double the number of awards given in 2023. Winners included "Generative Image Dynamics" (animating a still picture into motion) and "Rich Human Feedback for Text-to-Image Generation", a dataset for training image generation from human feedback. The Best Student Paper honors went to "Mip-Splatting" and "BioCLIP", a vision foundation model for the tree of life.
