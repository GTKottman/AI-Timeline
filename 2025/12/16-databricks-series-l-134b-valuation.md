# Databricks raises over $4B Series L at $134B valuation

- **Date:** 2025-12-16
- **Who:** Databricks; led by Insight Partners, Fidelity and J.P. Morgan Asset Management, with a16z and others
- **Type:** funding

Databricks announced a Series L of more than $4 billion at a $134 billion valuation. The company said it passed a $4.8 billion revenue run-rate, growing more than 55% year over year, and reported positive trailing-twelve-month free cash flow. Funds are earmarked for building AI apps and agents, product work, employee liquidity and AI acquisitions. These figures are company-reported and not audited.
