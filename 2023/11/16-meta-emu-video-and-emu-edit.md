# Meta unveils Emu Video and Emu Edit research models

- **Date:** 2023-11-16
- **Who:** Meta AI
- **Type:** model

Meta announced Emu Video, a text-to-video model that first generates an image from the prompt and then a video from the text and image, producing 512x512, four-second clips at 16 frames per second. It also announced Emu Edit, an image and video editing model driven by text instructions. Meta reported that raters preferred Emu Video over Make-A-Video by 96% on quality and 85% on faithfulness. Both were research models built on the Emu image model shown at Connect in September.
