# Perplexity launches publishers' program with ad revenue sharing

- **Date:** 2024-07-30
- **Who:** Perplexity AI
- **Type:** product

Perplexity launched a publishers' program that shares advertising revenue with partner outlets whose content appears in its answers. Launch partners included Time, Fortune and Der Spiegel. It was an attempt to answer publisher complaints about AI search reusing their reporting.
