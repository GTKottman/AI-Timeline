# Black Forest Labs raises 31 million USD seed round

- **Date:** 2024-08-05
- **Who:** Black Forest Labs (founded by former Stability AI researchers Robin Rombach, Patrick Esser and Andreas Blattmann); led by Andreessen Horowitz
- **Type:** funding

Black Forest Labs, a Freiburg company founded by former Stability AI developers, announced a 31 million USD seed round led by Andreessen Horowitz, with angel investors and General Catalyst and Mätch VC also named. The company planned to use the money to expand globally. The round set up the lab that later released the FLUX image models, and the sources here do not give a valuation for it.
