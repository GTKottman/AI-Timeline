# Timnit Gebru launches Distributed AI Research Institute (DAIR)

- **Date:** 2021-12-02
- **Who:** Timnit Gebru; funded by MacArthur, Ford, Kapor Center, Open Society and Rockefeller foundations
- **Type:** other

On December 2, 2021, the first anniversary of her removal from Google, Timnit Gebru launched the Distributed AI Research Institute (DAIR), an independent, community-rooted AI research institute. It started with about $3.7 million from several foundations, as a fiscally sponsored project of Code for Science & Society. DAIR's first project was a study of apartheid-era land use using South African aerial imagery. Its founding marked a shift of AI ethics research outside large tech companies.
