# Flood-filling networks cut connectomics error by an order of magnitude

- **Date:** 2018-07-16
- **Who:** Google Research with the Max Planck Institute of Neurobiology
- **Type:** research

Google and Max Planck researchers introduced flood-filling networks, a recurrent network that traces neurons through 3D electron microscopy volumes. The authors reported an order-of-magnitude accuracy gain over earlier deep learning pipelines on a zebra finch songbird brain dataset. The TensorFlow code was open-sourced, which helped make mapping brain wiring a more automated, machine-learning-driven task.
