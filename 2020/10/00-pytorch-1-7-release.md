# PyTorch 1.7 released with CUDA 11 and new FFT and distributed APIs

- **Date:** 2020-10 (exact day not confirmed; the release post does not show its date)
- **Who:** PyTorch / Facebook AI
- **Type:** software

PyTorch 1.7 added official CUDA 11 support, a NumPy-compatible torch.fft module, and major updates to distributed data parallel and RPC training. It also brought prototype support for Nvidia A100 and TF32, and prototype distributed training on Windows. Its torchvision and torchaudio updates added TorchScript-compatible transforms and new speech and text-to-speech pipelines.
