# Amazon fall 2019 event: Echo Studio speaker

- **Date:** 2019-09-25
- **Who:** Amazon (Alexa, Echo, Ring, Eero)
- **Type:** product

At its September 25, 2019 devices event Amazon introduced a large Echo lineup, including the Echo Studio, a $199.99 speaker with five directional speakers and Dolby Atmos support, which Amazon called its best-sounding Echo to date. It was pushed further into everyday home hardware with a built-in smart home hub. Echo Studio details come from Amazon's press release and launch coverage.
