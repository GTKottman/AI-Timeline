# European Parliament adopts the AI Act in plenary

- **Date:** 2024-03-13
- **Who:** European Parliament
- **Type:** policy

Parliament approved the final text of the AI Act with 523 votes in favour, 46 against and 49 abstentions, in a named roll-call vote. It was the main legislative vote that completed the deal reached in December 2023. Amnesty International said the Act failed to ban abusive AI technologies.
