# Anthropic releases Claude Opus 5.5

- **Date:** 2026-09-22
- **Who:** Anthropic
- **Type:** model

Anthropic released Claude Opus 5.5, the first model of the Claude 5.5 family. Anthropic said it costs 40% less to operate than Claude Opus 5, and that its per-token prices are about 20% lower.
