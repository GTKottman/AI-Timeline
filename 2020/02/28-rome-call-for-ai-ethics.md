# Rome Call for AI Ethics signed by Microsoft, IBM, FAO and Italy

- **Date:** 2020-02-28
- **Who:** Pontifical Academy for Life; Microsoft (Brad Smith); IBM (John Kelly III); FAO; Italian government
- **Type:** policy

At a Vatican event, Microsoft, IBM, the UN Food and Agriculture Organization and the Italian government signed the Rome Call for AI Ethics. It sets six principles, including transparency, inclusion, responsibility, impartiality, reliability, security and privacy, which it calls "Algor-ethics." It was an early industry-and-religious commitment to ethical AI design, signed the day before Italy's COVID-19 lockdown. Cisco and others signed later.
