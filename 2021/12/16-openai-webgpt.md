# OpenAI releases WebGPT

- **Date:** 2021-12-16
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** research

OpenAI presented WebGPT, a GPT-3 model that searches and browses the web through a text browser to answer open-ended questions, citing its sources. It was trained with imitation learning and then human feedback, and its answers were preferred over human demonstrators in many cases. It is a precursor to browsing in ChatGPT.
