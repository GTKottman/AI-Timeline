# Anthropic announces $124 million Series A

- **Date:** 2021-05-28
- **Who:** Anthropic; Dario Amodei (CEO), Daniela Amodei (President); lead Jaan Tallinn
- **Type:** funding

Anthropic, a safety-focused AI lab founded by former OpenAI staff Dario and Daniela Amodei, raised a $124 million Series A in May 2021. Jaan Tallinn led the round, with participation from James McClave, Dustin Moskovitz, Eric Schmidt and others, and it came almost entirely from tech founders and philanthropists rather than traditional VCs. The company said it aimed to build reliable, interpretable and steerable AI systems. Trackers date the round to May 28; the valuation is a secondary-market estimate, not confirmed by Anthropic.
