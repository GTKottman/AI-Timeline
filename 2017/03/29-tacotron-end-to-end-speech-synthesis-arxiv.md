# Tacotron: end-to-end text-to-speech from characters (arXiv)

- **Date:** 2017-03-29
- **Who:** Yuxuan Wang, RJ Skerry-Ryan and colleagues (Google)
- **Type:** paper

Google's Tacotron paper (arXiv 1703.10135) proposed a neural network that turns raw characters directly into speech spectrograms, replacing the separate front end, acoustic model and vocoder pipeline of older systems. It reported a 3.82 mean opinion score on US English, above a production parametric system on naturalness. It mattered as the starting point for the Tacotron line of models and later end-to-end speech synthesis.
