# EU High-Level Expert Group publishes draft AI ethics guidelines for consultation

- **Date:** 2018-12-18
- **Who:** High-Level Expert Group on Artificial Intelligence (AI HLEG), for the European Commission
- **Type:** policy

The expert group published a draft of its ethics guidelines for trustworthy AI for public comment, after the Commission's April 2018 AI strategy. The consultation closed on 1 February 2019 with more than 500 comments. The guidelines were later revised and finalised in April 2019.
