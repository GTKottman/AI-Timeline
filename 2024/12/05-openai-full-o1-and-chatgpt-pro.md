# OpenAI launches full o1 model and $200 ChatGPT Pro tier

- **Date:** 2024-12-05
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** product

On the first day of its "12 Days of OpenAI" livestreams, OpenAI released the full version of o1, its reasoning model, replacing the September 2024 preview. OpenAI said it was faster and made 34% fewer major errors on hard problems. It also launched ChatGPT Pro at $200 a month, with unlimited access to the top models and an o1 "pro mode" that spends more compute on hard questions.
