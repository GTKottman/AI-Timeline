# Meta releases V-JEPA 2 video world model

- **Date:** 2025-06-11
- **Who:** Meta AI (FAIR)
- **Type:** model

Meta released V-JEPA 2, a video-trained world model that predicts how the physical world responds to actions, with code and three new benchmarks for physical reasoning from video. Meta says it was trained on robot data from the DROID dataset and deployed on a robot arm in new environments, where it completes tasks such as reaching, grasping and pick-and-place from goal images. It is presented as an open step toward models that plan by predicting physical outcomes.
