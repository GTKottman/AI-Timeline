# NIST releases AI Risk Management Framework concept paper

- **Date:** 2021-12-14
- **Who:** NIST (US National Institute of Standards and Technology)
- **Type:** policy

NIST released a concept paper for its AI Risk Management Framework, dated December 13 and published December 14, 2021, for public comment. It drew on a July 2021 request for information and an October 2021 workshop. The proposal laid out a core of functions, profiles and tiers for managing AI risks, and comments were due January 25, 2022. The framework that followed became AI RMF 1.0 in January 2023.
