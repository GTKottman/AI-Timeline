# Apple unveils Apple Intelligence at WWDC 2024

- **Date:** 2024-06-10
- **Who:** Apple
- **Type:** product

Apple announced Apple Intelligence as a built-in layer of iOS 18, iPadOS 18 and macOS Sequoia. It came with Apple's own on-device and server foundation models and a Private Cloud Compute design for heavier requests, plus an opt-in ChatGPT integration powered by GPT-4o. It was the largest push of generative AI into Apple's consumer platforms.
