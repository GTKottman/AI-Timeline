# MIT-IBM Watson AI Lab announced with $240M over ten years

- **Date:** 2017-09-07
- **Who:** IBM, MIT
- **Type:** funding

IBM committed $240 million over ten years to create the MIT-IBM Watson AI Lab with MIT, with roughly 100 AI scientists, professors and students working across IBM's Cambridge research site and the MIT campus. Research scope covered AI hardware, software and algorithms, applications in health care and cybersecurity, and the economic and ethical effects of AI. It was one of the largest university-industry AI research commitments of 2017.
