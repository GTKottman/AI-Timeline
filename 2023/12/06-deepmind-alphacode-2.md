# DeepMind unveils AlphaCode 2 competitive programming system

- **Date:** 2023-12-06
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** research

DeepMind unveiled AlphaCode 2, a competitive-programming system built on a fine-tuned version of Gemini Pro, the same day Google announced Gemini. Google said it beat an estimated 85% of competitors on average across a subset of Codeforces contests, versus roughly 50% for the original AlphaCode. It was a research result rather than a product, and it showed that a general model tuned on contest data could compete with human programmers.
