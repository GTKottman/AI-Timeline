# Microsoft launches Copilot Pro at $20 per month

- **Date:** 2024-01-15
- **Who:** Microsoft
- **Type:** product

Copilot Pro gave subscribers priority access to newer OpenAI models, GPT Builder, Copilot Labs and higher-resolution Designer images for $20 a month. It was Microsoft's first paid consumer tier for its AI assistant.
