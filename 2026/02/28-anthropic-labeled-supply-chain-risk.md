# Anthropic labelled a supply chain risk and barred from federal contracts

- **Date:** 2026-02-28
- **Who:** US federal government; Anthropic
- **Type:** policy

Reports said Anthropic had been labelled a supply chain risk and barred from federal contracts after refusing Pentagon demands. The designation was formalised in March and later challenged in court.
