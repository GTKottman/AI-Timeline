# AMD and OpenAI announce 6-gigawatt Instinct GPU partnership

- **Date:** 2025-10-06
- **Who:** AMD; OpenAI
- **Type:** hardware

AMD and OpenAI agreed to deploy 6 gigawatts of AMD Instinct GPUs, starting with 1 GW of MI450 chips in the second half of 2026. As part of the deal, AMD issued OpenAI a warrant for up to 160 million AMD shares, which vests as deployment milestones are met and could give OpenAI up to about 10% of AMD. OpenAI continues to work with NVIDIA and Broadcom, according to Reuters as reported by The Decoder.
