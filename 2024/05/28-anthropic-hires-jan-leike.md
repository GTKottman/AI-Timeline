# Anthropic hires former OpenAI alignment lead Jan Leike

- **Date:** 2024-05-28
- **Who:** Anthropic; Jan Leike
- **Type:** people

Jan Leike, who left OpenAI in May 2024 over concerns that safety had lost priority, joined Anthropic to continue alignment research. It was one of the most closely watched researcher moves of the year.
