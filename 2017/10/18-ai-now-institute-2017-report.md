# AI Now Institute publishes its 2017 annual report

- **Date:** 2017-10-18
- **Who:** AI Now Institute, New York University
- **Type:** research

The AI Now Institute, an interdisciplinary research centre at NYU, published its second annual report on the social implications of AI. It set out the institute's policy and accountability agenda covering bias, labour, and the public use of automated decision systems. The report marked the institute's move from a symposium series to a standing research body.
