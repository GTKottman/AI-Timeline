# Apple acquires Dublin voice AI startup Voysis

- **Date:** 2020-04-03
- **Who:** Apple; Voysis (Dublin voice AI startup)
- **Type:** acquisition

Bloomberg reported on April 3, 2020 that Apple had bought Voysis, a voice AI company whose software let shopping apps understand spoken requests and narrow product searches. Apple declined to give details, in its usual line about buying smaller companies. Reports said the team's natural language work could feed Siri's understanding. A Voysis co-founder said in 2018 that its trained system could run in as little as 25 MB of memory, which would let it run on a device.
