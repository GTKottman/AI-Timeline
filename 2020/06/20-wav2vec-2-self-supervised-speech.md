# wav2vec 2.0 self-supervised speech recognition framework

- **Date:** 2020-06-20
- **Who:** Facebook AI (Baevski, Zhou, Mohamed, Auli)
- **Type:** paper

The arXiv paper "wav2vec 2.0: A Framework for Self-Supervised Learning of Speech Representations" pretrained on unlabeled audio and then fine-tuned on a small amount of transcribed speech. It beat leading semi-supervised systems, and reached usable recognition with about ten minutes of labeled speech. The approach cut the labeled-data needs of speech recognition.
