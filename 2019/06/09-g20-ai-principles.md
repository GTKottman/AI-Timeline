# G20 adopts human-centred AI principles

- **Date:** 2019-06-09
- **Who:** G20 (ministerial statement on trade and the digital economy)
- **Type:** policy

The G20 adopted a set of AI principles based on the OECD's May 2019 recommendation, as part of a ministerial statement on trade and the digital economy. They extended the OECD text to the major economies, including China, the US, and the EU. The principles were non-binding, but they gave the OECD framework wider government backing.
