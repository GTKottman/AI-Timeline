# iFlytek unveils Spark Desk cognitive large model

- **Date:** 2023-05-06
- **Who:** iFlytek
- **Type:** model

iFlytek's CEO Liu Qingfeng unveiled SparkDesk, a ChatGPT-style cognitive large model for Chinese and multilingual use, to be integrated into iFlytek's learning devices, transcription, office and car products. Testing had opened to selected users on April 20. It mattered as one of the first Chinese models pitched at a specific vertical set of products rather than general chat. Public release came later, after government approval.
