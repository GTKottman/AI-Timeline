# Decagon raises $250M at $4.5B valuation

- **Date:** 2026-01-28
- **Who:** Decagon (AI customer-support agents); Coatue and Index Ventures led
- **Type:** funding

Decagon, which builds AI agents for customer support, raised $250 million at a $4.5 billion valuation, roughly tripling its prior valuation. The round was led by Coatue and Index Ventures. It shows investors pricing enterprise AI agents for customer service as a major category.
