# Meta releases DINOv2 vision foundation model

- **Date:** 2023-04-17
- **Who:** Meta AI
- **Type:** model

Meta open-sourced DINOv2, a self-supervised computer vision model trained on unlabeled images that gives strong features without task-specific fine-tuning. The family ranges from ViT-S (21M parameters) to ViT-g (1.1B), and it transfers to classification, segmentation, depth estimation and image retrieval. Meta released it with an interactive demo and a new fairness benchmark called FACET. It mattered as a general-purpose visual backbone that many later vision systems adopted.
