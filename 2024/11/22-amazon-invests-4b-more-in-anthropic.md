# Amazon invests another $4 billion in Anthropic and names AWS primary training partner

- **Date:** 2024-11-22
- **Who:** Amazon, Anthropic
- **Type:** funding

Amazon announced a further $4 billion investment in Anthropic, bringing its total to $8 billion while keeping a minority stake. Anthropic named Amazon Web Services its "primary training partner" and said it would use AWS chips to train and deploy future foundation models. The deal tied one of the leading AI labs more tightly to a single cloud and chip provider. Bloomberg described it as following an earlier $4 billion investment that year.
