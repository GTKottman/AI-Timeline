# Meta brings Meta AI to Meta Quest 3 as a mixed-reality assistant

- **Date:** 2024-07-23
- **Who:** Meta
- **Type:** product

Meta introduced Meta AI on Meta Quest 3, letting the assistant see and reason about the user's mixed-reality surroundings. It extended the Llama-based assistant from phones and glasses into headsets.
