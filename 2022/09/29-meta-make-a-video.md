# Meta Make-A-Video: text-to-video without text-video data

- **Date:** 2022-09-29
- **Who:** Meta AI
- **Type:** model

Meta introduced Make-A-Video, which turns text-to-image models into text-to-video generators without needing paired text-video training data. It was an early public demonstration of AI-generated short video from a text prompt.
