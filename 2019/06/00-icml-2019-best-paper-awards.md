# ICML 2019 Best Paper Awards

- **Date:** 2019-06-00
- **Who:** ICML 2019 organizers (Long Beach, California); award papers from Google Brain Zurich, Max Planck Institute, ETH Zurich, University of Cambridge and PROWLER.io
- **Type:** other

ICML 2019 gave its Best Paper Award to "Challenging Common Assumptions in the Unsupervised Learning of Disentangled Representations," a joint paper from Max Planck Institute, ETH Zurich and Google Brain Zurich. A second Best Paper went to "Rates of Convergence for Sparse Variational Gaussian Process Regression" from Cambridge and PROWLER.io, and seven Best Paper Honourable Mentions were also given. The conference accepted about 750 papers. The exact award day was not confirmed in the sources, so only the month is recorded.
