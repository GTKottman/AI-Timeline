# Inspur AI Research announces Yuan 1.0 Chinese language model

- **Date:** 2021-10-21
- **Who:** Inspur AI Research
- **Type:** model

Inspur AI Research publicly announced Yuan 1.0 on October 21, 2021, after unveiling it at a Beijing workshop in late September. The GPT-3-style model had 245.7 billion parameters and was trained on about 5 TB of Chinese text across 2,128 GPUs. Inspur called it the largest language model in the world at that point and claimed that human reviewers struggled to tell its outputs from human writing. Those claims were the company's own.
