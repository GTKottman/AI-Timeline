# Stability AI names Prem Akkaraju CEO and closes new investment

- **Date:** 2024-06-25
- **Who:** Stability AI; Prem Akkaraju; Sean Parker; Greycroft
- **Type:** funding

Stability AI appointed Prem Akkaraju as chief executive and closed a rescue investment round led by investors including Greycroft, Coatue, Lightspeed and Sound Ventures. Sean Parker joined as executive chairman. It was a stabilising step for a company that had lost its founder-CEO three months earlier.
