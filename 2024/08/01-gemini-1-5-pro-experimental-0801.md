# Google releases Gemini 1.5 Pro experimental "0801" and tops LMArena

- **Date:** 2024-08-01
- **Who:** Google / Google DeepMind
- **Type:** model

Google released an experimental version of Gemini 1.5 Pro, named 0801, for testing in Google AI Studio and the Gemini API. It reached first place on the LMArena chatbot leaderboard with a reported score of 1300, ahead of GPT-4o at 1286 and Claude 3.5 Sonnet at 1271, and it also topped the vision leaderboard. Commentators noted it was still experimental and did not lead the coding or hard-prompt categories, and the 1300 score is a reported figure from secondary sources. The name suggests an August 1 release, but the sources give only "August 2024," so the day is taken from the model name and should be checked.
