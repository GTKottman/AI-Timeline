# Allen Institute for AI launches Iconary, a drawing and guessing game with humans

- **Date:** 2019-02-05
- **Who:** Allen Institute for Artificial Intelligence (AI2)
- **Type:** research

AI2 launched Iconary, a Pictionary-style game in which an AI partner, AllenAI, draws and guesses together with a human player. Sketches are turned into clip-art icons with computer vision, and a database of words and their relationships helps the program guess the phrase. Researchers presented it as a testbed for common sense and collaborative play between people and AI.
