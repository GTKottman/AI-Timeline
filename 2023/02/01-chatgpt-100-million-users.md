# UBS estimates ChatGPT reached 100 million monthly users

- **Date:** 2023-02-01
- **Who:** UBS analysts (estimate based on Similarweb data)
- **Type:** product

A UBS study estimated that ChatGPT had about 100 million monthly active users in January 2023, about two months after its public launch, and called it the fastest-growing consumer application in history. The figure was an estimate, not an official OpenAI number, and it showed strong early consumer demand for generative AI.
