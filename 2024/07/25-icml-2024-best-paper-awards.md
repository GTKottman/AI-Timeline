# ICML 2024 best paper awards at Vienna conference

- **Date:** 2024-07-25
- **Who:** ICML 2024 (International Conference on Machine Learning), Stanford AI Lab, UTS, others
- **Type:** competition

ICML 2024 ran in Vienna from July 21 to 27, and its best paper awards went to several papers, including "Discrete Diffusion Modeling by Estimating the Ratios of the Data Distribution" by Aaron Lou, Chenlin Meng and Stefano Ermon, and a paper on LLM debate. The Test of Time award was announced in the same round. Only some winners were confirmed from the sources used.
