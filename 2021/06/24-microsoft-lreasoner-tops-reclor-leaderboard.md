# Microsoft LReasoner tops the ReClor logical reasoning leaderboard

- **Date:** 2021-06-24
- **Who:** Microsoft Research Asia, Natural Language Computing Group
- **Type:** research

Microsoft Research Asia announced that LReasoner was the top-ranked system on the ReClor leaderboard. ReClor is a reading-comprehension benchmark of logical reasoning questions drawn from admission tests such as the LSAT and GMAT. LReasoner extracts logical symbols and expressions from the passage, extends them with equivalence-based inference, and matches them against the answer options. It mattered because pure language models were weak on explicit logical reasoning, and a symbolic step on top of them moved the benchmark.
