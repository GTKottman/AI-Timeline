# AWS launches SageMaker Studio Lab

- **Date:** 2021-12-01
- **Who:** Amazon Web Services (AWS)
- **Type:** product

AWS announced Amazon SageMaker Studio Lab at re:Invent 2021 as a free service for learning and experimenting with machine learning. It provides a JupyterLab environment with CPU and GPU compute and 15 GB of persistent storage, and sign-up needs only an email address, with no AWS account or credit card. It lowered the cost of hands-on ML work for students and hobbyists.
