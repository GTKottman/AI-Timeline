# 28 Fields Medalists criticise AI companies over unsolved-problem benchmarks

- **Date:** 2026-09-11
- **Who:** Fields Medal laureates; AI companies
- **Type:** other

Twenty-eight Fields Medalists published an open letter criticising AI companies for using unsolved mathematical problems as publicity benchmarks, after OpenAI's claims on the Navier-Stokes problem. It put pressure on how AI math results are reported.
