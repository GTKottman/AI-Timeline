# Trump orders federal agencies to stop using Anthropic; Pentagon designates it a supply chain risk

- **Date:** 2026-02-27
- **Who:** US federal government (Trump; Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth); Anthropic
- **Type:** policy

On February 27, 2026, President Trump directed federal agencies to stop using Anthropic's technology. Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth announced that the Pentagon was designating Anthropic a supply chain risk, effective immediately, after Anthropic refused to drop its usage safeguards.
