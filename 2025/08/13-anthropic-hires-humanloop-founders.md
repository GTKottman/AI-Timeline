# Anthropic hires Humanloop co-founders and team

- **Date:** 2025-08-13
- **Who:** Anthropic; Humanloop (Raza Habib, Peter Hayes, Jordan Burgess)
- **Type:** acquisition

Anthropic hired the three co-founders and about a dozen engineers and researchers from Humanloop, a prompt-management and LLM-evaluation startup, in an acqui-hire. An Anthropic spokesperson said Anthropic did not acquire Humanloop's assets or IP, and terms were not shared. Coverage tied the move to Anthropic's enterprise push. It mattered as a notable talent deal in the race for enterprise AI tooling staff.
