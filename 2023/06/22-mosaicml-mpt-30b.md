# MosaicML MPT-30B open-source LLMs

- **Date:** 2023-06-22
- **Who:** MosaicML (later part of Databricks)
- **Type:** model

MosaicML released MPT-30B Base, Instruct and Chat as open-source models with an 8k-token context, claiming it beat the original GPT-3 on standard academic benchmarks with about a sixth of the parameters. It was described as the first LLM trained on NVIDIA H100 GPUs. The release followed MPT-7B, whose family the company said had passed 3.3 million downloads, and came days before Databricks agreed to buy the company.
