# Intel launches Habana Gaudi2 AI accelerator

- **Date:** 2022-05-10
- **Who:** Intel (Habana Labs)
- **Type:** hardware

At Intel Vision on May 10, 2022, Habana Labs launched Gaudi2, its second-generation training processor, and Greco, the successor to the Goya inference chip. Gaudi2 was pitched as a lower-cost alternative to NVIDIA GPUs for training large models. Habana's claim of twice the training throughput of NVIDIA's A100-80GB on ResNet-50 and BERT is a vendor claim.
