# Jasper raises $125M Series A at $1.5B valuation

- **Date:** 2022-10-18
- **Who:** Jasper (AI writing assistant); led by Insight Partners with Bessemer, IVP, Foundation Capital and others
- **Type:** funding

Jasper, an AI copywriting product built on large language models, announced a $125 million Series A at a $1.5 billion valuation. The round closed about six weeks before ChatGPT's launch, and it was one of the biggest raises for a generative-text application that year.
