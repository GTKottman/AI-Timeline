# IBM launches Watson AIOps at virtual Think Digital event

- **Date:** 2020-05-05
- **Who:** IBM (IBM Research, Watson)
- **Type:** product

At its virtual Think Digital conference, IBM launched Watson AIOps, which uses machine learning to detect, diagnose and respond to IT infrastructure anomalies in real time without manual triage. A separate, similar offering was planned for telecom firms. IBM also highlighted Watson Assistant for Citizens, a free COVID-19 service for overwhelmed government call centers. IBM Research's director said AI sat at the top of the company's investment list alongside cloud and quantum.
