# DeepSeek quietly releases DeepSeek-V3-0324 under MIT licence

- **Date:** 2025-03-24
- **Who:** DeepSeek
- **Type:** model

DeepSeek posted an updated DeepSeek-V3 checkpoint, V3-0324, to Hugging Face without a formal announcement. The company cited gains in reasoning, front-end web coding, and Chinese writing. Coverage says it shipped under the MIT license. One source reports benchmark jumps such as GPQA from 59.1 to 68.4, but these figures come from secondary sites and should be checked against the model card. Sources disagree on the total parameter count, reported as 671 or 685 billion.
