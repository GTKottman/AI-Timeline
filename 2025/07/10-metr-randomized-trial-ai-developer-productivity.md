# METR randomized trial finds AI tools slowed experienced open-source developers

- **Date:** 2025-07-10
- **Who:** METR
- **Type:** research

METR ran a randomized trial with experienced open-source developers and found they took 19% longer on tasks when using early-2025 AI coding tools. The result was a rare controlled measurement of real-world productivity effects, and it fed debate about AI-assisted coding claims.
