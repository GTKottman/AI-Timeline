# OpenAI DevDay 2025: Apps SDK, AgentKit and GPT-5 Pro in the API

- **Date:** 2025-10-06
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** product

At DevDay in San Francisco, OpenAI announced the Apps SDK, which lets developers build apps that run inside ChatGPT conversations. Launch partners included Canva, Booking.com, Coursera, Expedia, Figma, Spotify and Zillow, and one source says the Model Context Protocol underlies the integration. It also launched AgentKit, a toolkit for building and deploying agents with a visual Agent Builder, and ChatKit for embedding chat in other apps. GPT-5 Pro, Sora 2 and Sora 2 Pro became available in the API, and two cheaper real-time voice models were announced. Altman cited more than 800 million weekly ChatGPT users. Sources are secondary.
