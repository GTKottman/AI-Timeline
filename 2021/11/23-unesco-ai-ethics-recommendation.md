# UNESCO adopts Recommendation on the Ethics of AI

- **Date:** 2021-11-23
- **Who:** UNESCO
- **Type:** policy

UNESCO's member states adopted the Recommendation on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence, described as the first global standard on AI ethics. It set out values and policy areas, including human rights, transparency and data governance, for countries to follow.
