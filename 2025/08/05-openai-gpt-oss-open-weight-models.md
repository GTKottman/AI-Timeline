# OpenAI releases gpt-oss-120b and gpt-oss-20b open-weight models

- **Date:** 2025-08-05
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** model

OpenAI released gpt-oss-120b and gpt-oss-20b, open-weight reasoning models under the Apache 2.0 licence. OpenAI says the 120b model nearly matches o4-mini on core reasoning benchmarks and runs on a single 80 GB GPU, while the 20b model needs about 16 GB of memory.
