# Google and DeepMind expand AI research in Paris

- **Date:** 2018-03-29
- **Who:** Google; DeepMind (Alphabet); INRIA; Ecole Polytechnique
- **Type:** other

Google announced an expanded AI research presence in Paris, and DeepMind announced a Paris office at the same time. The teams were to work on fundamental AI and on applications in health, science and the arts. Google also described an academic partnership with INRIA, sponsorship of an AI chair at Ecole Polytechnique, and funding for French academic research.
