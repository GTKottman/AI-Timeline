# NVIDIA publishes StyleGAN2, improving GAN image quality

- **Date:** 2019-12-03
- **Who:** NVIDIA (Tero Karras, Samuli Laine, Miika Aittala, Janne Hellsten, Jaakko Lehtinen, Timo Aila)
- **Type:** model

The StyleGAN2 paper, "Analyzing and Improving the Image Quality of StyleGAN," fixed the droplet-like artifacts in the original StyleGAN and redesigned its normalization and training. The result was markedly more realistic synthetic faces and a new benchmark for generative image models. It became a common base for later GAN research.
