# Intel launches Iris Xe MAX, its first Xe discrete GPU, with Deep Link

- **Date:** 2020-10-31
- **Who:** Intel
- **Type:** hardware

Intel announced the Iris Xe MAX, its first discrete GPU based on the Xe architecture, with the Xe-LP design from its 11th Gen Core mobile chips. Its Deep Link technology lets the integrated GPU, discrete GPU and CPU share workloads, including inference and rendering, under one software framework, with dynamic power sharing. The first laptops named were the Acer Swift 3x, Asus VivoBook Flip TP470 and Dell Inspiron 15 7000 2-in-1. Performance figures were Intel's own claims.
