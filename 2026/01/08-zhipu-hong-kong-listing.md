# Zhipu AI lists in Hong Kong

- **Date:** 2026-01-08
- **Who:** Zhipu AI (Z.ai)
- **Type:** funding

Zhipu AI began trading on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange on January 8, 2026, at a fixed offer price of HK$116.20 that raised about HK$4.3 billion. The retail tranche was oversubscribed about 1,159 times. The stock opened higher and closed the day up about 13%, according to SCMP, and it was one of the first public listings of a Chinese frontier-model lab.
