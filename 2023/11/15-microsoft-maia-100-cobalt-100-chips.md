# Microsoft unveils Maia 100 AI accelerator and Cobalt 100 CPU

- **Date:** 2023-11-15
- **Who:** Microsoft; OpenAI
- **Type:** hardware

Microsoft unveiled Azure Maia 100, a 5nm AI accelerator for training and running AI workloads, and Cobalt 100, a 128-core Arm-based server CPU, at Ignite. The chips were due to roll out in 2024, initially powering services such as Microsoft Copilot and Azure OpenAI Service. OpenAI reviewed the Maia design to tune it for large language models. It signaled that a major cloud provider was building its own AI silicon to reduce reliance on GPUs.
