# DeepMind launches Ethics & Society research unit

- **Date:** 2017-10-03
- **Who:** DeepMind (Google)
- **Type:** research

DeepMind launched DeepMind Ethics & Society, a research unit to study the social and ethical impact of AI. It combines full-time DeepMind staff with independent Fellows, and it was planned to grow to about 25 people within 12 months.
