# PanGu-Alpha: 200-billion-parameter Chinese language model paper

- **Date:** 2021-04-26
- **Who:** PanGu-Alpha authors (Chinese team, built on Huawei's MindSpore framework)
- **Type:** model

The PanGu-Alpha paper on arXiv described Chinese autoregressive language models of up to 200 billion parameters, trained with automatic parallel computation on the MindSpore framework. It was among the largest Chinese-language models released in 2021 and showed that Chinese groups were training GPT-3-scale models on domestic software stacks.
