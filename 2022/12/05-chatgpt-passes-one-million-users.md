# ChatGPT passes one million users in about five days

- **Date:** 2022-12-05
- **Who:** OpenAI / Sam Altman
- **Type:** product

Sam Altman said on December 5, 2022 that ChatGPT had reached one million users, days after its November 30 launch. The fast uptake showed consumer demand for chat-based AI and pushed rivals and investors to move quickly.
