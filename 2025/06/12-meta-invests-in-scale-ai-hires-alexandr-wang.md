# Meta invests $14.3 billion in Scale AI and hires its CEO

- **Date:** 2025-06-12
- **Who:** Meta; Scale AI; Alexandr Wang
- **Type:** funding

Meta invested $14.3 billion for a 49% non-voting stake in Scale AI, the data-labeling company, and brought in its CEO Alexandr Wang to lead Meta's superintelligence effort. It was one of the largest AI deals of the year and a move to strengthen Meta's data and talent position.
