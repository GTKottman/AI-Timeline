# Google DeepMind releases Veo 3.1 and Veo 3.1 Fast

- **Date:** 2025-11-17
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** model

Google DeepMind released Veo 3.1 and a faster variant, extending its video model with better audio-visual output and control. It kept Google at the front of text-to-video generation alongside Luma, Runway and OpenAI.
