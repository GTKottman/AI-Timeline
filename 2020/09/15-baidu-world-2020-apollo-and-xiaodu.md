# Baidu World 2020 shows Apollo autonomous driving, Duxiaoxiao assistant and Baidu Brain 6.0

- **Date:** 2020-09-15
- **Who:** Baidu
- **Type:** product

Baidu held Baidu World 2020 on September 15 under the theme "Intelligence of Everything." Its Apollo autonomous driving platform showed driving functions in a livestream and 5G remote driving. Baidu also released the Duxiaoxiao virtual assistant app and upgraded its Baidu Brain open AI platform to version 6.0.
