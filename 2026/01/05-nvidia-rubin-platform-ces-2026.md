# NVIDIA unveils Rubin AI supercomputer platform at CES 2026

- **Date:** 2026-01-05
- **Who:** NVIDIA
- **Type:** hardware

NVIDIA presented the Rubin platform at CES 2026 as a six-chip AI platform: the Vera CPU, Rubin GPU, NVLink 6 switch, ConnectX-9 SuperNIC, BlueField-4 DPU and Spectrum-6 Ethernet switch. NVIDIA said Rubin is in full production, and that it would cut the cost of generating tokens to about one-tenth of the previous platform.
