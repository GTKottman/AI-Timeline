# Graphcore raises $222 million Series E at $2.77 billion valuation

- **Date:** 2020-12-29
- **Who:** Graphcore; led by Ontario Teachers' Pension Plan, with Fidelity International and Schroders joining
- **Type:** funding

Graphcore, the UK maker of the Intelligence Processing Unit, raised $222 million in a Series E round at a $2.77 billion post-money valuation. Total funding passed $710 million, and the money was earmarked for global expansion and the next generation of IPU silicon, systems and software. The round was dated December 29, 2020 on Graphcore's own post, though some outlets ran it in January 2021.
