# Anthropic raises the price of Claude 3.5 Haiku

- **Date:** 2024-11-04
- **Who:** Anthropic
- **Type:** product

Anthropic raised the API price of Claude 3.5 Haiku to $1 per million input tokens and $5 per million output tokens. That is four times the price of its predecessor, Claude 3 Haiku, at $0.25 and $1.25. The change showed the cost pressure on small models even as competition for low-cost inference intensified.
