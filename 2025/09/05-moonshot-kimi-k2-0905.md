# Moonshot releases Kimi K2-0905

- **Date:** 2025-09-05
- **Who:** Moonshot AI
- **Type:** model

Moonshot AI released Kimi K2-0905, an update to its Kimi K2 0711 mixture-of-experts model with 1 trillion total parameters and 32 billion active per pass. The context window grew from 128k to 256k tokens, and the update improves agentic coding. The Hugging Face model card lists the weights under a modified MIT licence. OpenRouter dates the release to September 4, while other aggregators give September 5.
