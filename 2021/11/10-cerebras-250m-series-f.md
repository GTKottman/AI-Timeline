# Cerebras raises $250 million Series F at over $4 billion valuation

- **Date:** 2021-11-10
- **Who:** Cerebras Systems; led by Alpha Wave Ventures (Edge Capital) and Abu Dhabi Growth Fund
- **Type:** funding

Cerebras Systems, maker of the wafer-scale CS-2 AI system, raised a $250 million Series F at a post-money valuation above $4 billion on November 10, 2021. Returning investors included Benchmark, Coatue, Altimeter and Eclipse. The round came as Cerebras moved from a single system toward cloud partnerships and a product portfolio, and it lifted the company's total funding to about $720 to $750 million by its own and secondary reports.
