# Ego4D egocentric video dataset and benchmarks released

- **Date:** 2021-10-13
- **Who:** Ego4D consortium (led by Kristen Grauman)
- **Type:** dataset

The Ego4D paper introduced a dataset of 3,670 hours of daily-life first-person video from many countries, with a suite of benchmarks for episodic memory, forecasting and interaction. It gave AI researchers a large common dataset for training models that see the world from a wearer's point of view.
