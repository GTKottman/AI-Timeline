# DeepSeek officially releases V4-Pro (0813)

- **Date:** 2026-08-12
- **Who:** DeepSeek
- **Type:** model

DeepSeek released V4-Pro 0813 as the official version of its Pro model, after the April 2026 preview. It has 1.6T total and 49B active parameters with a 1M-token context window, and completes the official V4 pair with V4-Flash.
