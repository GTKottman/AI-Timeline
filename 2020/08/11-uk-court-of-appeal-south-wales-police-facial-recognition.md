# UK Court of Appeal rules police live facial recognition unlawful

- **Date:** 2020-08-11
- **Who:** England and Wales Court of Appeal, South Wales Police, Ed Bridges (Liberty)
- **Type:** policy

In R (Bridges) v CC South Wales, the Court of Appeal found that South Wales Police's use of live automated facial recognition was not in accordance with the law, and that the force had not checked whether the software was biased by race or gender. The court did not ban the technology, but the ruling set legal requirements that UK police forces had to meet before deploying it. It was one of the first appellate judgments on AI-based biometric surveillance anywhere.
