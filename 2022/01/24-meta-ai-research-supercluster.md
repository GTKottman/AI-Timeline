# Meta announces AI Research SuperCluster (RSC)

- **Date:** 2022-01-24
- **Who:** Meta AI
- **Type:** hardware

Meta announced the AI Research SuperCluster, built on NVIDIA A100 GPUs, and said it had already begun training large NLP and computer vision models on it. Meta reported that RSC trains large-scale NLP models about three times faster than its earlier setup. The first phase had 760 DGX A100 systems (6,080 GPUs), with the full build of about 16,000 GPUs planned for mid-2022.
