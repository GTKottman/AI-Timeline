# Hugging Face acquires Pollen Robotics

- **Date:** 2025-04-14
- **Who:** Hugging Face; Pollen Robotics
- **Type:** acquisition

Hugging Face acquired Pollen Robotics, the French maker of the open-source humanoid Reachy 2, in its first move into hardware. Pollen's co-founders and about 20 staff moved to Hugging Face, and the company planned to sell Reachy 2 and open-source its designs over time. The price was not disclosed.
