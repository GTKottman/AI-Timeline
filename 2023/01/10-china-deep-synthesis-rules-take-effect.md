# China deep synthesis rules take effect

- **Date:** 2023-01-10
- **Who:** Cyberspace Administration of China (CAC), with MIIT and the Ministry of Public Security
- **Type:** policy

China's Provisions on the Administration of Deep Synthesis of Internet-Based Information Services took effect on January 10, 2023. The rules cover synthetic media made with generative models, including text, images, audio, video and virtual scenes, and set duties such as user consent for biometric editing, content labeling and algorithm auditing. It was among the first binding national rules aimed specifically at generative AI, and it set the regulatory frame later used for China's generative AI measures. Sources disagree on the issue date, so the effective date is the one used here.
