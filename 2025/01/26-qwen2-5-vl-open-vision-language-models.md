# Alibaba Qwen releases Qwen2.5-VL open vision-language models

- **Date:** 2025-01-26
- **Who:** Alibaba Qwen team
- **Type:** model

Alibaba's Qwen team released Qwen2.5-VL in 3B, 7B and 72B sizes, each with base and instruction-tuned variants, on Hugging Face and ModelScope. A 32B variant followed on March 24, 2025.
