# Aurora to acquire Uber's self-driving unit Advanced Technologies Group

- **Date:** 2020-12-07
- **Who:** Aurora Innovation; Uber Technologies (Uber ATG); Uber CEO Dara Khosrowshahi
- **Type:** acquisition

On December 7, 2020, Uber announced it would sell its Advanced Technologies Group (ATG), its self-driving unit, to Aurora Innovation. Aurora took on a majority of Uber ATG's roughly 1,200 employees, and Uber took a stake of about 26 percent in Aurora with a $400 million investment. Reuters reported the deal valued ATG at $4 billion, down from $7.25 billion a year earlier. Aurora's first product is self-driving trucking, with light vehicles and ride-hailing to follow. The deal showed one of the largest self-driving programs consolidating into a smaller company during the pandemic downturn.
