# DreamerV2 reaches human-level Atari play with discrete world models

- **Date:** 2020-10-05
- **Who:** Google Research and collaborators (Hafner et al.)
- **Type:** research

The arXiv paper "Mastering Atari with Discrete World Models" introduced DreamerV2, a reinforcement learning agent that learns behaviours by imagining outcomes in a latent world model trained separately from its policy. The authors report it is the first agent to reach human-level play across the 55-game Atari benchmark through this approach. It showed that model-based reinforcement learning could compete with the best model-free agents.
