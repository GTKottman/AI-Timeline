# Ilya Sutskever founds Safe Superintelligence Inc.

- **Date:** 2024-06-19
- **Who:** Ilya Sutskever; Daniel Levy; Daniel Gross
- **Type:** other

Ilya Sutskever announced Safe Superintelligence Inc. (SSI) with Daniel Levy and Daniel Gross, a company whose only product goal is safe superintelligence. It was founded weeks after his exit from OpenAI and set up one of the best-funded pure safety-focused labs.
