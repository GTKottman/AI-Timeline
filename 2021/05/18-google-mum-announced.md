# Google introduces MUM multitask model

- **Date:** 2021-05-18
- **Who:** Google
- **Type:** model

Google introduced MUM (Multitask Unified Model), a model meant to understand information across many languages and modalities such as text and images, and to answer complex questions that normally need several searches. It was presented as a successor to BERT for search.
