# CVPR 2017 best paper award for DenseNet

- **Date:** 2017-07-00
- **Who:** Gao Huang, Zhuang Liu, Laurens van der Maaten, Kilian Q. Weinberger (Cornell / Tsinghua / Facebook AI Research)
- **Type:** competition

CVPR 2017 in Honolulu gave a best paper award to "Densely Connected Convolutional Networks" (DenseNet), where each layer takes the feature maps of all earlier layers as input. The conference also honored "Learning from Simulated and Unsupervised Images through Adversarial Training" in the same session. The award made DenseNet a widely adopted vision architecture; the exact award day was not found in the sources.
