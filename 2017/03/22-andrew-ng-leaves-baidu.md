# Andrew Ng leaves Baidu as chief scientist

- **Date:** 2017-03-22
- **Who:** Andrew Ng; Baidu (AI Group under VP Haifeng Wang)
- **Type:** people

Andrew Ng announced in a Medium post on March 21, 2017 that he was leaving Baidu, where he had been chief scientist leading about 1,300 AI staff. MIT Technology Review and CNBC reported it on March 22, with Baidu's stock falling. Ng stayed until the end of April, and Baidu moved his teams into an AI Group under Haifeng Wang. It was a setback for Baidu's deep learning push, and Ng went on to start his own AI ventures.
