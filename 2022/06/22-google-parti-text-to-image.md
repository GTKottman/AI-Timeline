# Google Parti: autoregressive text-to-image generation

- **Date:** 2022-06-22
- **Who:** Google Research
- **Type:** model

Google introduced Parti, a text-to-image model that generates images autoregressively as sequences of tokens, scaled up to 20B parameters. Its paper showed that scaling this approach gave steady gains in photorealism and in following detailed prompts.
