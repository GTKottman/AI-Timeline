# Nvidia GTC 2018: Tesla V100 32GB, DGX-2 and DRIVE Constellation

- **Date:** 2018-03-28
- **Who:** Nvidia
- **Type:** hardware

At GTC 2018 in San Jose, Nvidia doubled the memory of its Tesla V100 data-center GPU to 32GB and unveiled the DGX-2, which links 16 V100s through the new NVSwitch fabric. It also announced DRIVE Constellation, a cloud simulator for testing autonomous vehicles, and the Quadro GV100. These products gave deep learning labs and self-driving developers much larger models and faster training hardware.
