# Google Research announces the iNaturalist 2018 species classification challenge

- **Date:** 2018-03-09
- **Who:** Google Research, iNaturalist and Visipedia
- **Type:** competition

The iNaturalist 2018 Challenge asked teams to classify species from photos across more than 8,000 categories of plants, animals and fungi. It came with over 450,000 training images and a long-tailed class distribution, so rare species had few examples. It ran as a flagship challenge at the FGVC5 workshop at CVPR 2018.
