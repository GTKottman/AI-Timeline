# Google introduces AdaNet, a TensorFlow framework for AutoML ensembles

- **Date:** 2018-10-30
- **Who:** Google AI
- **Type:** software

Google AI introduced AdaNet, a lightweight TensorFlow framework that searches for neural network architectures and learns to combine strong ones into a high-quality ensemble with little expert input. Its objective balances training performance against generalization, which removes the need for a hold-out validation set, and it comes with learning guarantees. It made AutoML ensembling available as an open library.
