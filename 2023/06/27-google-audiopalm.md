# Google AudioPaLM: speech and text in one language model

- **Date:** 2023-06-27
- **Who:** Google Research / Google DeepMind
- **Type:** model

AudioPaLM combined the PaLM 2 text model with the AudioLM audio model into one multimodal system. It handles speech recognition, text-to-speech and speech-to-speech translation, and it can keep a speaker's voice in translated output. Google's coverage claimed it beat OpenAI's Whisper on translation benchmarks. The date is the press-coverage date seen; the paper (arXiv 2306.12925) was not opened.
