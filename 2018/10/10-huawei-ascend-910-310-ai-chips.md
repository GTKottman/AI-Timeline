# Huawei unveils Ascend 910 and Ascend 310 AI chips

- **Date:** 2018-10-10
- **Who:** Huawei
- **Type:** hardware

At Huawei Connect in Shanghai, Huawei unveiled the Ascend 910, a 7nm data-center chip, and the Ascend 310, a low-power edge chip (12nm per Synced and DatacenterDynamics). It also introduced the CANN operator library, the MindSpore framework and a cloud ML platform. Huawei gave the 910 planned specs of up to 256 teraFLOPS at FP16; these were planned figures, not independent benchmarks. It mattered as a move by a phone maker into a full AI chip and software stack, aimed at Nvidia and Qualcomm.
