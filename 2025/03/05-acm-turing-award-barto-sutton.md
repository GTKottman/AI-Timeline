# ACM names Barto and Sutton 2024 Turing Award recipients for reinforcement learning

- **Date:** 2025-03-05
- **Who:** Association for Computing Machinery; Andrew Barto and Richard S. Sutton
- **Type:** people

The ACM announced that Andrew Barto and Richard S. Sutton would receive the 2024 Turing Award for their foundational work on reinforcement learning. The award is computing's highest honor.
