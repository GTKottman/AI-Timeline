# Microsoft LReasoner tops the ReClor logical reasoning leaderboard

- **Date:** 2021-06-24
- **Who:** Microsoft Research Asia, Natural Language Computing Group
- **Type:** research

Microsoft Research Asia announced that LReasoner was the top-ranked system on the ReClor leaderboard. ReClor is a reading-comprehension benchmark of logical reasoning questions drawn from admission tests such as the LSAT and GMAT. LReasoner recognizes logical symbols and expressions in the passage and uses them to find the answer. It mattered because pure language models were weak on explicit logical reasoning, and a symbolic step on top of them moved the benchmark.
