# Adobe introduces Firefly and Canva launches AI design assistants

- **Date:** 2023-03-00
- **Who:** Adobe; Canva
- **Type:** product

Adobe introduced Firefly, its generative image model family for creative tools, and Canva launched AI design assistants in the same month. March 2023 was when many established software companies began shipping their own generative AI products.
