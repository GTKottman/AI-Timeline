# TensorFlow 1.10.0 released

- **Date:** 2018-08-07
- **Who:** Google (TensorFlow team)
- **Type:** software

TensorFlow 1.10.0 was released on August 7, 2018, after release candidates on July 24 and August 1. Distributions and Bijectors moved from tf.contrib.distributions into TensorFlow Probability, with the contrib version deprecated. tf.data gained initial Bigtable integration, and prebuilt binaries were built against NCCL 2.2 and no longer bundle NCCL, which required multi-GPU users to upgrade. It mattered as the release that began separating probabilistic modeling into its own library.
