# Google unveils Lumiere text-to-video diffusion model

- **Date:** 2024-01-24
- **Who:** Google Research
- **Type:** model

Google researchers published Lumiere, a text-to-video diffusion model built on a Space-Time U-Net that generates the full clip in one pass instead of stitching keyframes and upsampling. Coverage described 80-frame outputs, compared with 14 to 25 frames for Stability's Stable Video Diffusion, and listed image-to-video, stylized generation, inpainting and cinemagraphs. Google did not release it publicly, but the paper set a new bar for temporally consistent video generation.
