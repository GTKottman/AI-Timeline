# ACL 2025 outstanding paper: "All That Glitters is Not Novel"

- **Date:** 2025-07-00
- **Who:** Tarun Gupta and Danish Pruthi (Indian Institute of Science)
- **Type:** competition

The Association for Computational Linguistics awarded an Outstanding Paper at ACL 2025, held in Vienna from 27 July to 1 August, to "All That Glitters is Not Novel: Plagiarism in AI-Generated Research." The paper studies plagiarism in AI-written research. It mattered as a top language-model research award focused on AI-generated scientific writing.

Exact award day not confirmed from the sources; the full main-track list was not confirmed.
