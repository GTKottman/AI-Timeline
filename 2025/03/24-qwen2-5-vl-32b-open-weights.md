# Qwen2.5-VL-32B open-weight vision model

- **Date:** 2025-03-24
- **Who:** Alibaba Qwen team
- **Type:** model

Alibaba's Qwen team released Qwen2.5-VL-32B-Instruct, a 32-billion-parameter vision-language model tuned with reinforcement learning. Third-party coverage says it beats the 72B Qwen2.5-VL on MMMU and MathVista; no Qwen team figures were found to confirm this. Release dates differ by source, though most listings give March 24, 2025.
