# US CHIPS and Science Act signed into law

- **Date:** 2022-08-09
- **Who:** US Congress and President Joe Biden
- **Type:** policy

President Joe Biden signed the CHIPS and Science Act, which funds semiconductor manufacturing and research, including artificial intelligence research. Officials framed it as part of an AI and technology competition with China.
