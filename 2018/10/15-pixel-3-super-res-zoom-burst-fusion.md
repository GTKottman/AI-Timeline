# Pixel 3 Super Res Zoom merges burst frames for sharper digital zoom

- **Date:** 2018-10-15
- **Who:** Google Research (Pixel camera team)
- **Type:** product

Google Research introduced Super Res Zoom on the Pixel 3, a digital zoom that merges many burst frames into one higher-resolution image instead of upscaling a single crop. It relies on natural hand tremor and small deliberate movements of the optical stabilizer to capture slightly offset frames. The post said its detail was roughly competitive with 2x optical zoom on many other phones. It was another case of learned burst processing replacing extra camera hardware.
