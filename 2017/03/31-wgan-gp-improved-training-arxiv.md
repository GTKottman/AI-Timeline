# Improved Training of Wasserstein GANs: gradient penalty (arXiv)

- **Date:** 2017-03-31
- **Who:** Ishaan Gulrajani and co-authors
- **Type:** paper

The paper (arXiv 1704.00028) replaced the weight clipping used in the original WGAN with a gradient penalty on the critic, giving WGAN-GP. The change made GAN training stable across many architectures, including 101-layer ResNets and discrete-data language models, with little tuning. It became a standard GAN training recipe and was published at NIPS 2017.
