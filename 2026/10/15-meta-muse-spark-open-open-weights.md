# Meta releases Muse Spark Open with open weights

- **Date:** 2026-10-15
- **Who:** Meta (Alexandr Wang)
- **Type:** model

Meta released Muse Spark Open on October 15, 2026, an 816-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with 42 billion active parameters and a 1M-token context, with weights on Hugging Face. It is the first open-weight release from the Spark line, which launched closed in April. It ships under the Muse Model License, which has commercial limits and is not an open-source licence. Alexandr Wang called it the most capable US open-weight model to date; benchmarks so far are mostly Meta's own.
