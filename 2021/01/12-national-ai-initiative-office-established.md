# White House OSTP establishes the National AI Initiative Office

- **Date:** 2021-01-12
- **Who:** White House Office of Science and Technology Policy (OSTP)
- **Type:** policy

On January 12, 2021, OSTP set up the National Artificial Intelligence Initiative Office under the National AI Initiative Act of 2020. The office is meant to oversee the national AI strategy and act as the federal hub for AI coordination with the private sector and academia. Michael Kratsios, then the US chief technology officer, called it a central hub for national AI research and policy.
