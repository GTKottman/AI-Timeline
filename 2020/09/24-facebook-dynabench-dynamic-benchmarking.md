# Facebook AI launches Dynabench for dynamic AI benchmarking

- **Date:** 2020-09-24
- **Who:** Facebook AI Research (Douwe Kiela, Adina Williams)
- **Type:** software

Dynabench is a platform where humans and models work together to write harder test examples, and the benchmark is re-built each round as models get better. Its premise was that static benchmarks saturate quickly, and that measuring how often human testers can fool a model is a better signal of progress than a fixed leaderboard score.
