# Nvidia agrees to acquire Hugging Face for about $13 billion

- **Date:** 2026-09-03
- **Who:** Nvidia; Hugging Face
- **Type:** acquisition

Nvidia agreed to buy the open AI model platform Hugging Face for roughly $12.9 billion, with about $11.9 billion going to Hugging Face shareholders and up to $1 billion in retention equity for employees who join Nvidia. Jensen Huang wrote that Hugging Face would remain an open platform where developers can choose their own models, frameworks, clouds and inference providers. The deal is pending regulatory approval and is expected to close in the first half of 2027. Reported annualized revenue of about $150 million makes the price a steep multiple of sales.
