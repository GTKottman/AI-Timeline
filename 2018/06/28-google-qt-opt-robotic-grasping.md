# Google's QT-Opt reaches 96% grasp success with distributed deep Q-learning

- **Date:** 2018-06-28
- **Who:** Google Brain Robotics
- **Type:** research

Google introduced QT-Opt, a scalable deep reinforcement-learning method for vision-based robotic manipulation, built on distributed optimization. Training used more than 580,000 real grasp attempts collected by seven robots. The closed-loop grasping policy reached a 96% success rate on objects it had not seen in training. It showed deep RL working at robot-fleet scale.
