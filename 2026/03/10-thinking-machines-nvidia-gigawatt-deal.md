# Thinking Machines Lab signs Nvidia gigawatt compute deal with Nvidia investment

- **Date:** 2026-03-10
- **Who:** Thinking Machines Lab, NVIDIA
- **Type:** funding

Thinking Machines Lab agreed to a multi-year partnership with NVIDIA to deploy at least one gigawatt of Vera Rubin systems for training its models, starting early 2027. NVIDIA also made a significant equity investment, with financial terms undisclosed. The deal shows frontier-model labs locking in gigawatt-scale compute through chip vendors that are also investors.
