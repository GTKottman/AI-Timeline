# Intel unveils Nervana Neural Network Processor (NNP) family

- **Date:** 2017-10-17
- **Who:** Intel (Nervana), Facebook (collaborator)
- **Type:** hardware

Intel announced its Nervana Neural Network Processor family, custom ASICs for deep learning training, and said first silicon would ship to customers before the end of 2017. Intel said it collaborated with Facebook on the design. It was part of a wave of dedicated AI accelerators competing with NVIDIA GPUs and Google TPUs.
