# OpenAI launches the GPT Store

- **Date:** 2024-01-10
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** product

OpenAI opened the GPT Store, a marketplace where users can publish and find custom ChatGPT GPTs. It turned custom assistants into a distribution channel and was OpenAI's first marketplace for developer-built AI apps.
