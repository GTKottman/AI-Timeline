# Cohere and Aleph Alpha announce combination backed by two governments

- **Date:** 2026-04-24
- **Who:** Cohere, Aleph Alpha, Schwarz Group
- **Type:** acquisition

Cohere announced it would acquire Aleph Alpha, the German AI company, in a deal framed around sovereign AI and backed by the Canadian and German governments. Schwarz Group committed US$600 million to lead the merged company's Series E. The combined company keeps the Cohere name with dual headquarters. A definitive merger agreement was signed later, on September 16, 2026, and remains subject to approval.
