# Meta releases Llama 3.3 70B open model

- **Date:** 2024-12-06
- **Who:** Meta AI
- **Type:** model

Meta released Llama 3.3 70B, an open-weights text model with a 128K-token context window, trained on more than 15 trillion tokens. Meta said it performed close to the much larger Llama 3.1 405B on many tasks, and some benchmarks showed it ahead. Meta pitched it for cheaper deployment and for synthetic data generation.
