# White House OMB issues draft AI regulation principles memo

- **Date:** 2020-01-07
- **Who:** White House Office of Management and Budget (OMB), director Russell Vought
- **Type:** policy

OMB issued a draft memorandum, "Guidance for Regulation of Artificial Intelligence Applications," setting ten principles for federal agencies regulating private-sector AI. Principles included trustworthiness and public participation, and the memo aimed to avoid over-regulation. It built on the February 2019 AI executive order. It was a draft that invited public comment, with agencies to report on alignment within 180 days.
