# Meta cuts about 600 roles in Superintelligence Labs

- **Date:** 2025-10-22
- **Who:** Meta, Meta Superintelligence Labs, Alexandr Wang
- **Type:** people

Meta cut about 600 roles in its AI Superintelligence Labs, roughly a fifth of the division, affecting FAIR and the AI infrastructure and product teams but not the TBD Lab. Chief AI Officer Alexandr Wang's internal memo framed the cuts as improving efficiency and reducing bureaucracy, and affected staff were encouraged to apply for internal roles. It came months after Meta's large talent-hiring spree for the lab.
