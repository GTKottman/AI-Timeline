# AMD unveils Instinct MI350 series at Advancing AI 2025

- **Date:** 2025-06-12
- **Who:** AMD
- **Type:** hardware

At its Advancing AI 2025 event AMD launched the Instinct MI350 series, the MI355X with 288GB HBM3E and 8TB/s bandwidth and the MI350X, and previewed the MI400 Helios rack. It also launched ROCm 7.0 in preview and opened the AMD Developer Cloud. The performance multiples were AMD's own claims against Nvidia's B200 and GB200, but it mattered as the clearest second-source AI accelerator launch against Nvidia at the time.
