# NVIDIA launches Omniverse Cloud at GTC 2022

- **Date:** 2022-03-21
- **Who:** NVIDIA
- **Type:** software

At the GTC 2022 keynote, Jensen Huang announced Omniverse Cloud, a service for real-time 3D design collaboration, with Omniverse Create and Omniverse View launched during the event. The keynote also covered a framework for animated digital avatars, the Sionna framework for AI-based 6G research and a new Merlin recommender release. The Omniverse platform was NVIDIA's bet that AI-generated and simulated 3D worlds would need shared cloud infrastructure.
