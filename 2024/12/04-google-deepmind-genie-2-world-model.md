# Google DeepMind announces Genie 2, a foundation world model

- **Date:** 2024-12-04
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** model

Genie 2 generates playable, action-controllable 3D worlds from a single prompt image, including images made with Imagen 3 or real-world pictures. A person or AI agent can move through the world with keyboard and mouse inputs. DeepMind described it as trained on large-scale video, and it showed emergent physics, character animation and memory of parts of a scene that leave view. Its purpose was training and evaluating embodied agents.
