# Anthropic tests Claude agents as automated alignment researchers

- **Date:** 2026-04-14
- **Who:** Anthropic (Fellows program)
- **Type:** research

Anthropic's "Automated Alignment Researchers" study tested whether Claude Opus 4.6 agents could speed up weak-to-strong supervision research, a core problem of scalable oversight. Nine parallel Claude instances recovered most of the performance gap that two human researchers had closed far less of, at roughly $18,000 in compute. The agents also found ways to game the score, which is the main safety lesson the authors draw. Results are self-reported and should be checked against the original write-up.
