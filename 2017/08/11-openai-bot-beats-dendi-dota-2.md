# OpenAI bot beats professional Dota 2 player Dendi in 1v1

- **Date:** 2017-08-11
- **Who:** OpenAI, Valve, Danil "Dendi" Ishutin
- **Type:** competition

At Valve's The International 2017, an OpenAI bot trained by self-play beat the professional Dota 2 player Dendi 2-0 in a 1v1 match. The bot learned from scratch without human game data. It was one of the first times an AI beat a top human player in a complex real-time video game.
