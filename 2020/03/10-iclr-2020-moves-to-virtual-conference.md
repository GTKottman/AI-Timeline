# ICLR 2020 cancelled in person and moved to a virtual conference

- **Date:** 2020-03-10
- **Who:** ICLR 2020 organizers (International Conference on Learning Representations)
- **Type:** other

Organizers announced that the in-person ICLR 2020, planned for Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, was cancelled because of COVID-19 travel restrictions, and that the conference would run as a virtual event from April 26 onward. Accepted papers were presented through pre-recorded talks, with live Q&A and video calls on Zoom and Slido. It was one of the first major machine learning conferences to switch fully online in the pandemic, a format most of the field then adopted.
