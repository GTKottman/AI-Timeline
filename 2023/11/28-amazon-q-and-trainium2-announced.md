# AWS unveils Amazon Q assistant and Trainium2 chip at re:Invent

- **Date:** 2023-11-28
- **Who:** Amazon Web Services
- **Type:** product

At re:Invent 2023 in Las Vegas, AWS CEO Adam Selipsky unveiled Amazon Q, a generative AI assistant for work, with initial applications for AWS developers in preview. The keynote also announced the Trainium2 AI training chip and Graviton4. Trainium2 was reported to offer up to 4x faster training than the first-generation Trainium. The date is the keynote day, which sources place on November 28.
