# Reflection AI unveils Beam, its first open-weight model

- **Date:** 2026-10-05
- **Who:** Reflection AI (Nvidia-backed)
- **Type:** model

Reflection AI introduced Beam, a 501-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with 23 billion parameters active per inference, built for coding, reasoning and agentic work. The company says Beam matches leading Chinese open models on advanced reasoning at lower inference cost, while conceding that Kimi K3 is still stronger in absolute terms. Beam was in final red-teaming, with weights and developer artifacts promised later in October. The sources checked did not confirm that the weights had been published, and the performance claims are the company's own.
