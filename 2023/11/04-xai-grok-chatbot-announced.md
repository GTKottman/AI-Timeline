# Elon Musk's xAI announces Grok chatbot

- **Date:** 2023-11-04
- **Who:** xAI (Elon Musk)
- **Type:** model

Elon Musk announced Grok, the first model from his new AI company xAI, through a post on X. The chatbot was offered first to a limited group of US users and was planned for X Premium+ subscribers. xAI positioned it as a rival to ChatGPT and Google Bard with some humor in its replies, modeled on The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy. Sources differ on whether the release was November 3 or 4.
