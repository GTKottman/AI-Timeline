# AI21 Labs raises $64M

- **Date:** 2022-07-12
- **Who:** AI21 Labs; led by Ahren Innovation Capital Fund (per SiliconANGLE)
- **Type:** funding

Israeli language-model company AI21 Labs announced a $64 million round on July 12, 2022, which TechCrunch and TechTarget put at a valuation of about $664 million. The company positioned itself as a competitor to OpenAI for enterprise text generation, and it cited Jurassic-X, a system that pairs language models with external tools. Sources differ on the total raised to date.
