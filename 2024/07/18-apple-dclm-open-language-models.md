# Apple releases DCLM open language models with open data

- **Date:** 2024-07-18
- **Who:** Apple (with University of Washington, Toyota Research Institute, Stanford and others)
- **Type:** model

Apple released DCLM (DataComp for Language Models), a set of fully open language models with weights, training code and datasets, including DCLM-7B and DCLM-1B on Hugging Face. The 7B model was trained on 2.5 trillion tokens of Common Crawl data curated by the DataComp team, and Apple said it beat Mistral-7B on benchmarks with far less compute. The exact release day varies by source (one Apple post says July 18, a third-party database says July 15), so check the date against the Hugging Face release before relying on it.
