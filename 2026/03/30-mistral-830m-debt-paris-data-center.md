# Mistral raises $830 million in debt for Paris data center

- **Date:** 2026-03-30
- **Who:** Mistral AI; lenders Bpifrance, BNP Paribas, Credit Agricole CIB, HSBC, La Banque Postale, MUFG, Natixis CIB
- **Type:** funding

Mistral took $830 million in debt to buy about 13,800 Nvidia GB300 GPUs for a data center at Bruyeres-le-Chatel south of Paris, with operations targeted for the end of June 2026. It is the company's first debt raise and is for infrastructure, not an equity round. The deal shows a European lab financing its own compute to reduce dependence on hyperscaler capacity. The date comes from one report; other outlets did not give a day.
