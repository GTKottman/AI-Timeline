# Google I/O 2022: LaMDA 2 and AI Test Kitchen

- **Date:** 2022-05-11
- **Who:** Google (Sundar Pichai)
- **Type:** product

At Google I/O 2022, Pichai introduced LaMDA 2, its most conversational model yet, with demos in which it answered follow-up questions on topics it was not specifically trained on. Google also opened AI Test Kitchen, a limited program where a selected community could try LaMDA. Pichai called the demos "quick sketches" and acknowledged that the models still make factual errors.
