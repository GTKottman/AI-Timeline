# Google DeepMind announces Genie 2, a foundation world model

- **Date:** 2024-12-04
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** model

Genie 2 generates playable, action-controllable 3D worlds from a single prompt image, including images made with Imagen 3 or real photos. A person or AI agent can move through the world with keyboard and mouse inputs. DeepMind described it as an autoregressive latent diffusion model trained on video, and it showed emergent physics, character animation and memory of parts of a scene that leave view. It was released as a limited research preview for training and testing embodied agents.
