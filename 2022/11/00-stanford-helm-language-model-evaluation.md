# Stanford HELM: Holistic Evaluation of Language Models

- **Date:** 2022-11-00
- **Who:** Stanford Center for Research on Foundation Models and HAI
- **Type:** research

Stanford CRFM published HELM, a benchmark and toolkit that evaluated 30 language models across 42 scenarios and 7 metrics under one standard setup, and released all raw prompts and completions publicly. The authors said earlier evaluations covered 17.9% of the core scenarios and HELM raised that to 96%. Instruction-tuned models led, and a consistent gap separated limited-access and open models. The exact day of the arXiv posting was not confirmed.
