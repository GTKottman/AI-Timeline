# Google Duet AI for Workspace generally available

- **Date:** 2023-08-29
- **Who:** Google
- **Type:** product

Google made Duet AI for Google Workspace generally available as an add-on for Business, Enterprise, Education and Frontline editions. It adds generative-AI help in Gmail, Docs, Meet and Chat, including meeting assistance, document and conversation summaries, a Chat assistant and drafting suggestions, with a 30-day trial for Enterprise. It was the first broad paid rollout of Google's generative AI in its office suite.
