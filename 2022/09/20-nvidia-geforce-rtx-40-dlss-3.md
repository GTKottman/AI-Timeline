# NVIDIA unveils GeForce RTX 40 series and DLSS 3

- **Date:** 2022-09-20
- **Who:** NVIDIA
- **Type:** hardware

At its GTC 2022 event NVIDIA unveiled the GeForce RTX 40 series, the first GPUs on the Ada Lovelace architecture, led by the RTX 4090. It also announced DLSS 3, which uses AI to generate whole frames as well as upscale them, and it is exclusive to RTX 40 cards. The AI frame generation was the headline feature, and NVIDIA's performance claims were its own pending independent tests.
