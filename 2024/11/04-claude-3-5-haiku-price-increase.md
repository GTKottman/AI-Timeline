# Anthropic raises the price of Claude 3.5 Haiku

- **Date:** 2024-11-04
- **Who:** Anthropic
- **Type:** product

Anthropic increased the API price of Claude 3.5 Haiku, which had been launched in the cheapest tier. The change showed the cost pressure on small models even as competition for low-cost inference intensified.
