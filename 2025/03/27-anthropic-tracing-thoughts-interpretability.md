# Anthropic publishes circuit-tracing research on how Claude thinks

- **Date:** 2025-03-27
- **Who:** Anthropic
- **Type:** research

Anthropic published two interpretability papers, "Circuit Tracing: Revealing Computational Graphs in Language Models" and "On the Biology of a Large Language Model". They use attribution graphs to follow the internal steps of Claude 3.5 Haiku. Reported findings include a shared concept space across languages, planning several words ahead when writing rhyming poetry, and parallel computation paths for mental arithmetic.
