# Cloudflare blocks AI crawlers by default and launches Pay Per Crawl

- **Date:** 2025-07-01
- **Who:** Cloudflare
- **Type:** product

Cloudflare began blocking AI crawlers by default for its customer sites and launched Pay Per Crawl, a marketplace where publishers can charge AI companies for access to their content. It was a major infrastructure-level move on how AI models get training and retrieval data from the web.
