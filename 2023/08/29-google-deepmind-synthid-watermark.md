# Google DeepMind launches SynthID image watermarking

- **Date:** 2023-08-29
- **Who:** Google DeepMind; Google Cloud
- **Type:** software

Google DeepMind and Google Cloud launched SynthID as a beta, a tool that embeds an imperceptible watermark in the pixels of AI-generated images so they can be identified later. It was offered to a small group of Vertex AI customers using the Imagen text-to-image model. DeepMind said the watermark stays detectable after filters, colour changes and lossy JPEG compression.
