# CVPR 2026 Best Paper: "Efficiently Reconstructing Dynamic Scenes One D4RT at a Time"

- **Date:** 2026-06-05
- **Who:** Oxford Visual Geometry Group with Google DeepMind and University College London (D4RT authors); CVPR 2026 organisers
- **Type:** competition

The CVPR 2026 Best Paper award went to D4RT, a model that predicts a scene's 3D geometry, camera poses, depth and correspondences across video frames for both moving and static scenes. CVPR announced the awards at its welcome and awards ceremony on June 5, 2026, selecting from 4,089 accepted papers out of 16,092 submissions. The Best Student Paper award went to "Native and Compact Structured Latents for 3D Generation".
