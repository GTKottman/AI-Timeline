# NVIDIA unveils Rubin AI supercomputer platform at CES 2026

- **Date:** 2026-01-00
- **Who:** NVIDIA
- **Type:** hardware

NVIDIA presented its Rubin platform as an AI supercomputer built from six co-designed chips, aimed at lowering the cost of training and serving large models. Rubin became the successor to Blackwell and the main hardware story for 2026 data centres. The exact announcement day is not confirmed in this record.
