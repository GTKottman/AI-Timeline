# Washington signs SB 6280 facial recognition law

- **Date:** 2020-03-31
- **Who:** Washington State Governor Jay Inslee; Washington State Legislature; ACLU of Washington (critic); Microsoft (supporter)
- **Type:** policy

Governor Jay Inslee signed SB 6280, which regulates government use of facial recognition by state and local agencies, with one section vetoed. The law bars ongoing surveillance with the technology, requires a search warrant for using it to gather evidence of serious crime, and requires agencies to publish accountability reports and test systems for accuracy and bias. It took effect July 1, 2021 and was widely cited as an early template for US state rules on government face recognition, while the ACLU of Washington said it did not go far enough.
