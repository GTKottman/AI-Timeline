# NVIDIA unveils DGX Cloud at GTC 2023

- **Date:** 2023-03-21
- **Who:** NVIDIA
- **Type:** hardware

At its GTC 2023 keynote, NVIDIA unveiled DGX Cloud, a service offering dedicated AI supercomputing clusters on DGX systems with NVIDIA AI Enterprise software, aimed at training large generative models. The keynote also covered H100 NVL and Grace Hopper systems. Coverage differs on which cloud providers launched first, with Oracle Cloud Infrastructure and Microsoft Azure both named. It mattered because it moved NVIDIA from selling chips to renting full AI training stacks directly.
