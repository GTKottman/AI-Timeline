# Google expands electronic health records deep learning work with Intermountain Healthcare

- **Date:** 2019-01-22
- **Who:** Google AI with Intermountain Healthcare (Utah)
- **Type:** research

Google said it was expanding its 2018 work on deep learning for electronic health records with Intermountain Healthcare. The earlier paper showed that models trained on medical records could predict hospital outcomes such as length of stay and unexpected readmission after discharge. The new step started with tests on historical records under strict privacy and security rules. It was planned to explore predictions in settings from primary care to a telehealth critical care unit.
