# Waymo and Lyft agree to collaborate on self-driving cars

- **Date:** 2017-05-14
- **Who:** Waymo (Alphabet); Lyft
- **Type:** other

Waymo and Lyft announced on May 14, 2017 that they would collaborate on developing self-driving technology and products, with Lyft expected to help Waymo expand its real-world testing through its ride-hailing network. Reports said the deal was non-exclusive, so Lyft could keep its self-driving partnership with General Motors. It was a rival move to Uber's self-driving push, which was then in legal dispute with Waymo over trade secrets.
