# Figure 01 humanoid robot demo with OpenAI models

- **Date:** 2024-03-13
- **Who:** Figure (robotics startup), OpenAI
- **Type:** product

Figure posted a video of its Figure 01 humanoid robot holding a conversation and explaining its actions, using OpenAI's vision-language model for high-level reasoning and speech while Figure's own neural networks handled low-level motor control. The partnership came with a $675 million funding round that valued Figure at $2.6 billion. It showed general-purpose language models being connected to physical robot bodies.
