# Intel agrees to acquire Mobileye for $15.3 billion

- **Date:** 2017-03-13
- **Who:** Intel, Mobileye
- **Type:** acquisition

Intel announced it would buy Mobileye, an Israeli maker of vision systems for driver assistance and self-driving cars, for about $15.3 billion in cash ($63.54 per share). Intel put the market for vehicle systems, data and services at up to $70 billion by 2030. Coverage called it the largest acquisition of an Israeli tech company.
