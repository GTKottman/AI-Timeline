# Black Knight acquires HeavyWater, an AI mortgage-document startup

- **Date:** 2018-06-04
- **Who:** Black Knight; HeavyWater (University City, team of 14)
- **Type:** acquisition

Mortgage services firm Black Knight announced on a Monday in early June 2018 that it had acquired HeavyWater, a 14-person company whose AI platform, a virtual assistant, processes financial documents for mortgage companies. Terms were not in the coverage. It mattered as an example of AI document automation being bought into a large regulated industry rather than built in-house.
