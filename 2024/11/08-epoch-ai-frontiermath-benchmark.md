# Epoch AI releases FrontierMath, a hard expert-level math benchmark

- **Date:** 2024-11-08
- **Who:** Epoch AI (with mathematicians including IMO gold medalists)
- **Type:** dataset

Epoch AI released FrontierMath, a set of hundreds of original, expert-written research-level math problems built to replace saturated benchmarks such as MATH and GSM8K. The problem writers included 14 International Mathematical Olympiad gold medalists and a Fields Medal recipient. At launch, models including GPT-4o and Gemini 1.5 Pro solved under 2 percent of the problems. The benchmark set a new bar for measuring advanced mathematical reasoning.
