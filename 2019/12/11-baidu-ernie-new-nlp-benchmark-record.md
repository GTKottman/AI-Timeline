# Baidu's ERNIE pretrained model reports a new NLP benchmark record

- **Date:** 2019-12-11
- **Who:** Baidu
- **Type:** model

Baidu announced that its ERNIE pretrained language model reached a macro-average score of 90.1 on the GLUE benchmark, the first model to clear 90 on the leaderboard, ahead of Microsoft's MT-DNN-SMART and Google's T5. The work followed ERNIE 1.0 earlier in 2019 and ERNIE 2.0. Baidu said search quality improved after it adopted the model.
