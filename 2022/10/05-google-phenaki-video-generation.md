# Google Phenaki generates long videos from text stories

- **Date:** 2022-10-05
- **Who:** Google Brain with University of Michigan and UCL researchers
- **Type:** research

Google published Phenaki, a model that generates variable-length video from a sequence of text prompts, so one clip can follow a story across several prompts. It uses a causal video tokenizer and a bidirectional masked transformer conditioned on text. The authors said it was the first paper to study video generation from time-variable prompts.
