# Mythic launches an AI chip with a $9M Series A

- **Date:** 2017-03-22
- **Who:** Mythic (formerly Isocline); Series A led by Draper Fisher Jurvetson, with Lux Capital, Data Collective and AME Cloud Ventures
- **Type:** hardware

Austin startup Mythic launched a chip and software that run computer vision and voice control on local devices without a cloud connection, the size of a small shirt button. The launch came with a roughly $9 million Series A led by Draper Fisher Jurvetson.
