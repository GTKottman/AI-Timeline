# Level AI $20M Series B

- **Date:** 2022-03-01
- **Who:** Level AI; led by Battery Ventures (Neeraj Agrawal)
- **Type:** funding

Level AI, which applies machine learning to customer-service conversations, announced a $20 million Series B on March 1, 2022, led by Battery Ventures. Coverage reports that ENIAC and Village Global also invested. It is a small round that shows venture money flowing into applied conversational AI in early 2022.
