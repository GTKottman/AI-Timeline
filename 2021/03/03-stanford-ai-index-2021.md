# Stanford releases 2021 AI Index report

- **Date:** 2021-03-03
- **Who:** Stanford Institute for Human-Centered AI (HAI)
- **Type:** research

Stanford HAI published the 2021 AI Index, an annual data report on AI research, technical performance, economy, education, ethics, diversity and policy. The 2021 edition expanded its data sources and reported that private AI investment rose substantially in 2020 while the number of newly funded AI companies fell for a third straight year. Drug discovery drew the largest share of private investment. The index became a standard reference for tracking AI progress.
