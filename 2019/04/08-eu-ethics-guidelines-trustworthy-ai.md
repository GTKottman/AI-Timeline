# EU High-Level Expert Group publishes Ethics Guidelines for Trustworthy AI

- **Date:** 2019-04-08
- **Who:** European Commission High-Level Expert Group on AI
- **Type:** policy

The EU's expert group published its final Ethics Guidelines for Trustworthy AI after a consultation that drew more than 500 comments on the December 2018 draft. The guidelines define trustworthy AI as lawful, ethical and robust, and set out seven requirements, including human agency, privacy and accountability. The text is non-binding but became a reference point for later European AI policy.
