# Mira Murati leaves OpenAI as CTO

- **Date:** 2024-09-25
- **Who:** Mira Murati; OpenAI; Bob McGrew; Barret Zoph
- **Type:** people

OpenAI chief technology officer Mira Murati announced she was stepping down, and chief research officer Bob McGrew and research VP Barret Zoph left the same day. It was one of the largest senior departures in OpenAI's history, weeks after the o1 release.
