# Meta launches Vibes, a short-form feed of AI-generated videos

- **Date:** 2025-09-25
- **Who:** Meta
- **Type:** product

Meta launched Vibes, a feed of short AI-generated videos inside the Meta AI app and on meta.ai. Users make clips from text prompts or remix others' videos, then post to the Vibes feed, send them by DM, or cross-post to Instagram and Facebook. Early versions reportedly used models from Midjourney and Black Forest Labs while Meta builds its own. TechCrunch called it a feed of mostly AI-generated "slop," and many users criticised it.
