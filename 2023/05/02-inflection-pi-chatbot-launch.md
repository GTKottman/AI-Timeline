# Inflection AI launches Pi chatbot

- **Date:** 2023-05-02
- **Who:** Inflection AI (Mustafa Suleyman, Reid Hoffman, Karen Simonyan)
- **Type:** product

Inflection AI, a startup founded by former DeepMind leaders and LinkedIn co-founder Reid Hoffman, released Pi, a conversational chatbot built for emotionally intelligent, supportive dialogue. Suleyman said it was deliberately narrower than general-purpose assistants, which he argued made it safer. The company had raised $225 million in a 2022 seed round, and the product showed a consumer chatbot built around a conversational persona rather than tasks. Reuters reported the release on May 3, saying it was announced on Tuesday.
