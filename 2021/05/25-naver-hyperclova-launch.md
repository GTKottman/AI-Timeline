# Naver unveils HyperCLOVA, Korea's hyperscale language model

- **Date:** 2021-05-25
- **Who:** Naver
- **Type:** model

Naver unveiled HyperCLOVA at its online NAVER AI NOW conference on May 25, 2021. The company put the model at 204 billion parameters, above GPT-3's 175 billion, and said it was trained on a large Korean-language corpus. Demos showed chat, product copy generated from keywords and document summarization. The launch was one of the first hyperscale language models built for a non-English language.
