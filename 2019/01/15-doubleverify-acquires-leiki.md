# DoubleVerify acquires Helsinki AI firm Leiki

- **Date:** 2019-01-15
- **Who:** DoubleVerify; Leiki
- **Type:** acquisition

DoubleVerify, a brand-safety and ad-measurement company, announced it had acquired Leiki, a Helsinki artificial intelligence company that analyzes text in more than 40 languages. The deal was completed on December 27, 2018 as an all-cash-and-stock offer, for an undisclosed sum.
