# Mistral AI launches Mistral Large and partners with Microsoft

- **Date:** 2024-02-26
- **Who:** Mistral AI; Microsoft
- **Type:** model

Mistral released Mistral Large, its flagship model for businesses, alongside the latency- and cost-optimised Mistral Small and a chat app. Microsoft invested about $16 million in Mistral, and Azure became the first distribution partner for its models. The deal tied a European open-weight lab to a major cloud and signalled that Mistral would also sell closed models.
