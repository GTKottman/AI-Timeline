# Cohere and Aleph Alpha announce combination backed by two governments

- **Date:** 2026-04-24
- **Who:** Cohere, Aleph Alpha, Schwarz Group
- **Type:** acquisition

Cohere announced it would acquire Aleph Alpha, the German AI company, in a deal framed around sovereign AI. Reports put the deal at about $20 billion, citing people familiar with the terms. Schwarz Group plans to invest about 500 million euros (roughly US$600 million) and lead Cohere's upcoming Series E. The combined company keeps the Cohere name with dual headquarters, and the deal remains subject to shareholder and regulatory approval.
