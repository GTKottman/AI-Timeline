# Google opens Cloud TPU Pods to the public in beta

- **Date:** 2019-05-07
- **Who:** Google Cloud
- **Type:** hardware

At Google I/O 2019 Google made Cloud TPU v2 and v3 Pods publicly available in beta. Google said the v3 pods are liquid-cooled and deliver more than 100 petaFLOPs of computing power at lower numerical precision. Outside users could now train very large models on that scale without building their own supercomputer.
