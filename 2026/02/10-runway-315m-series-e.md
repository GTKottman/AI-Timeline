# Runway raises $315M Series E at $5.3B valuation

- **Date:** 2026-02-10
- **Who:** Runway (AI video and world models); General Atlantic (lead), with Nvidia, Adobe Ventures, AMD Ventures and Fidelity
- **Type:** funding

Runway raised $315 million in a Series E led by General Atlantic, valuing the AI video company at about $5.3 billion, nearly double its prior mark. The company said it will use the funds to pre-train next-generation world models and expand into new products and industries. The round signals investor focus shifting from video generation toward world-model research.
