# Hundreds of AI leaders sign Statement on AI Risk

- **Date:** 2023-05-30
- **Who:** Center for AI Safety; signatories incl. Geoffrey Hinton, Sam Altman, Demis Hassabis
- **Type:** other

Hundreds of AI researchers, executives and public figures signed a one-sentence Statement on AI Risk saying mitigating extinction risk from AI should be a global priority alongside pandemics and nuclear war. It put AI safety in mainstream headlines and in policy discussions.
