# Black Forest Labs releases FLUX.2

- **Date:** 2025-11-25
- **Who:** Black Forest Labs
- **Type:** model

Black Forest Labs released FLUX.2, its next image-generation family after FLUX.1, with variants including a FLUX.2+ model. It continued the open-and-API image model competition with better text rendering and editing.
