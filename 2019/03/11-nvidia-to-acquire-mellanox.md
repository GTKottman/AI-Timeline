# Nvidia agrees to acquire Mellanox for $6.9 billion

- **Date:** 2019-03-11
- **Who:** Nvidia, Mellanox Technologies
- **Type:** acquisition

Nvidia agreed to buy Mellanox, an interconnect maker for data-center networking, for about $6.9 billion in enterprise value at $125 per share. The deal was Nvidia's largest acquisition to date. Mellanox's high-speed networking is used to link GPUs in AI and HPC clusters, so the deal tied Nvidia's accelerators more closely to the networking that large training systems depend on.
