# Meta releases Llama 3 in 8B and 70B sizes

- **Date:** 2024-04-18
- **Who:** Meta AI
- **Type:** model

Llama 3 came in 8B and 70B parameter sizes, pretrained on about 15 trillion tokens, and was released with open weights. Meta claimed it beat comparable open models at its sizes. The release set a new baseline for open-weight LLMs and was rolled out alongside the Meta AI assistant on Facebook and WhatsApp.
