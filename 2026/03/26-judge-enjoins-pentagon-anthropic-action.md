# Federal judge issues temporary injunction against Pentagon action on Anthropic

- **Date:** 2026-03-26
- **Who:** US federal court (Judge Rita Lin, N.D. California); Anthropic; Pentagon
- **Type:** policy

Judge Rita Lin issued a preliminary injunction against the Pentagon's supply chain risk designation of Anthropic, calling it likely contrary to law and arbitrary and capricious. The order also temporarily halted President Trump's directive for federal agencies to stop using Anthropic's technology.
