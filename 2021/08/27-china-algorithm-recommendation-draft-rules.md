# China's Cyberspace Administration releases draft algorithmic recommendation rules

- **Date:** 2021-08-27
- **Who:** Cyberspace Administration of China (CAC)
- **Type:** policy

The CAC published the draft "Internet Information Service Algorithmic Recommendation Management Provisions" for public comment on August 27, 2021, with comments accepted until September 26. It would require recommendation providers to let users decline algorithmic recommendations and to file algorithms that have public-opinion or social-mobilisation capabilities. DigiChina's English translation of the draft is the main English text.
