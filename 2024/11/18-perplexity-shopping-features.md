# Perplexity adds Shopping features backed by Amazon and Nvidia

- **Date:** 2024-11-18
- **Who:** Perplexity AI
- **Type:** product

Perplexity added Shopping features to its AI search, with investment and support from Amazon and Nvidia. It pushed AI search into product discovery and commerce, where incumbents like Google and Amazon compete directly.
