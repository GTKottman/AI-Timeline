# NVIDIA unveils Clara platform for medical-instrument AI

- **Date:** 2018-09-12
- **Who:** NVIDIA
- **Type:** product

NVIDIA unveiled Clara at GTC Japan in Tokyo, a platform for building AI into medical imaging and instruments. Its Clara AGX hardware is based on the Xavier AI computing module and Turing GPUs, and the platform includes the Clara SDK, a set of GPU-accelerated libraries. NVIDIA's blog said the platform was available to early-access partners. It mattered because it pushed GPU-based AI from research toward medical instrument pipelines.
