# Midjourney releases V6 alpha image model

- **Date:** 2023-12-21
- **Who:** Midjourney
- **Type:** model

Midjourney V6 alpha was trained from scratch over nine months and improved text rendering and literal prompt following. It was a major visual-quality step for a model used widely by artists and designers, and it made in-image text usable for the first time in its line.
