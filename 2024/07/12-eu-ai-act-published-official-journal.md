# EU AI Act published in the Official Journal

- **Date:** 2024-07-12
- **Who:** European Union (Official Journal)
- **Type:** policy

Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, the AI Act, was published in the Official Journal of the EU. Publication started the countdown to entry into force on August 1, 2024.
