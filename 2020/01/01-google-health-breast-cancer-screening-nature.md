# Google Health AI matches radiologists in breast cancer screening (Nature)

- **Date:** 2020-01-01
- **Who:** Google Health (DeepMind), Northwestern Medicine, Imperial College London, Cancer Research UK, NHS
- **Type:** research

A deep learning system published in Nature detected breast cancer on screening mammograms about as well as expert radiologists. It was tested on US and UK screening data, cutting false positives by 5.7% (US) and 1.2% (UK) and false negatives by 9.4% (US) and 2.7% (UK). Google stated that clinical validation and regulatory approval were still needed. It was a high-profile case of a medical imaging model compared against human experts on real screening outcomes.
