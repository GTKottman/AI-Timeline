# Google releases Gemini 3.7 Flash

- **Date:** 2026-08-13
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** model

Google released Gemini 3.7 Flash, which it called its most intelligent workhorse model yet for coding and agents. It follows Gemini 3.6 Flash by about three weeks.
