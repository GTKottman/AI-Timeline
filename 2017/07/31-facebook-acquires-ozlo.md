# Facebook Acquires AI Assistant Startup Ozlo

- **Date:** 2017-07-31
- **Who:** Facebook (Messenger team); Ozlo
- **Type:** acquisition

Facebook acquired Ozlo, a Palo Alto startup building conversational AI assistants, to improve its Messenger virtual assistant M. Most of Ozlo's roughly 30 staff were expected to join the Messenger team, and Ozlo shut down its apps and API services. Financial terms were not disclosed. It mattered because M had struggled to answer questions without human help, and Facebook added dedicated natural language talent to fix that.
