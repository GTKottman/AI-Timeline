# Anthropic publishes persona vectors for tracking and steering model personality

- **Date:** 2025-08-01
- **Who:** Anthropic (Anthropic Fellows Program)
- **Type:** research

Anthropic researchers identified directions in a model's internal activations that track traits such as evil, sycophancy and hallucination, calculated as the difference in average activations between trait and non-trait responses. Injecting the vectors changed behaviour, and the authors proposed using them to monitor personality shifts during training and deployment and to flag training data that would cause them. The experiments used open models of 7 to 8 billion parameters, so scaling was unproven. It mattered as an interpretability method for watching and steering character traits.
