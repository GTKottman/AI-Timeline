# Apple acquires Dublin voice AI startup Voysis

- **Date:** 2020-04-03
- **Who:** Apple; Voysis (Dublin, founded 2012 by Noel Ruane and Peter Cahill)
- **Type:** acquisition

Bloomberg reported that Apple had bought Voysis, a voice AI company whose software let shopping apps understand spoken requests and narrow product searches. Apple declined to give details, in its usual line about buying smaller companies. Commentators expected the team's natural language work to feed Siri's understanding, and Voysis had said its trained models could run in about 25 MB on a device, so they did not need the cloud.
