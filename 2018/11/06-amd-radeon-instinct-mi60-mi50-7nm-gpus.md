# AMD unveils Radeon Instinct MI60 and MI50, its first 7nm datacenter GPUs

- **Date:** 2018-11-06
- **Who:** AMD
- **Type:** hardware

AMD announced the Radeon Instinct MI60 and MI50, which it called the world's first 7nm datacenter GPUs, aimed at deep learning, HPC and cloud. The MI60 was listed at 29.5 FP16 teraflops for training and 59 INT8 teraops for inference, with 32 GB of HBM2 memory; the MI50 has 16 GB. AMD said the cards came with an updated ROCm software platform. It was AMD's direct push into the datacenter AI accelerator market against NVIDIA.
