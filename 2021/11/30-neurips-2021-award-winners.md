# NeurIPS 2021 announces outstanding paper, test-of-time and datasets awards

- **Date:** 2021-11-30
- **Who:** NeurIPS 2021 organizers; winners include Bubeck and Sellke, Abel et al., Agarwal et al., Koch et al.
- **Type:** competition

NeurIPS announced six Outstanding Paper Awards, a Test of Time Award and two Best Paper Awards in the new Datasets and Benchmarks track on November 30, 2021, ahead of the December conference. Named winners included Bubeck and Sellke's "A Universal Law of Robustness via Isoperimetry," Abel et al.'s "On the Expressivity of Markov Reward," and Agarwal et al.'s "Deep Reinforcement Learning at the Edge of the Statistical Precipice." The Test of Time Award went to Hoffman, Blei and Bach's 2010 online LDA paper, and a datasets best paper was "Reduced, Reused and Recycled: The Life of a Dataset in Machine Learning Research."
