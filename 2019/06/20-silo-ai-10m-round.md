# Silo.AI raises EUR 10 million

- **Date:** 2019-06-20
- **Who:** Silo.AI (Helsinki); Business Finland Growth Engine program and unnamed angel investors
- **Type:** funding

Helsinki-based Silo.AI, which describes itself as the largest private AI services provider in the Nordics, raised EUR 10 million to grow its team and expand into the European market. Funding came from Business Finland's Growth Engine program and a number of unnamed angel investors. Tech.eu reported the round on 20 June 2019.
