# Cohere raises $125M Series B

- **Date:** 2022-02-15
- **Who:** Cohere (Aidan Gomez, Ivan Zhang, Nick Frosst); led by Tiger Global with Radical Ventures, Index Ventures and Section 32
- **Type:** funding

Toronto-based Cohere, which sells API access to large language models, announced a $125 million Series B on February 15, 2022. Tiger Global led the round, with Radical Ventures, Index Ventures and Section 32 also investing. The money was earmarked for platform development, team growth and a new Palo Alto office, which BetaKit reports would be run by Bill MacCartney.
