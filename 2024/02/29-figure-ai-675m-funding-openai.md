# Figure AI raises $675M at $2.6B valuation and partners with OpenAI

- **Date:** 2024-02-29
- **Who:** Figure AI; OpenAI; Microsoft; Nvidia; Jeff Bezos
- **Type:** funding

Humanoid robot startup Figure AI raised a $675 million Series B at a $2.6 billion valuation. Investors included Microsoft, the OpenAI Startup Fund, Nvidia, Jeff Bezos, Intel Capital and Amazon's Industrial Innovation Fund. The same announcement set up a collaboration with OpenAI to build language-model reasoning into its robots. It was a major bet that foundation models would move into physical robots.
