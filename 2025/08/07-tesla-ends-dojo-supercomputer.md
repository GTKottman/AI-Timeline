# Tesla shuts down Dojo supercomputer project

- **Date:** 2025-08-07
- **Who:** Tesla; Elon Musk; Peter Bannon; Ganesh Venkataramanan
- **Type:** hardware

Tesla ended its Dojo AI training supercomputer project and reassigned the team, with Musk calling Dojo "an evolutionary dead end" and saying Tesla would focus on its AI5 and AI6 chips. Around 20 Dojo staff left for DensityAI, a startup led by Dojo's former chip lead. It mattered because Tesla had pitched Dojo as key to self-driving training, and the move shifted its compute toward Nvidia and Samsung hardware.
