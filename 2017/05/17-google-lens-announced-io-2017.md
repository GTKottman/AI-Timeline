# Google announces Google Lens at I/O 2017

- **Date:** 2017-05-17
- **Who:** Google
- **Type:** product

Google first announced Google Lens at I/O 2017 as a camera-based tool that recognizes objects, text and places and can act on them. It used computer vision and the Google Assistant to answer questions about what the camera sees. It later launched on Pixel 2 phones in October 2017.
