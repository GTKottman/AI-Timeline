# IBM Project Debater holds its first public debate with humans

- **Date:** 2018-06-18
- **Who:** IBM Research (Project Debater); debaters Noa Ovadia and Dan Zafrir
- **Type:** research

On June 18, 2018 in San Francisco, IBM's Project Debater argued against two Israeli human debaters. It took the side for government-funded space exploration against Noa Ovadia, and argued for telemedicine against Dan Zafrir. The system built its arguments from a large corpus of newspapers and journals, and a snap poll found most of the audience felt it enriched their knowledge more than the human did. It mattered as a public demonstration of AI building open-domain spoken argument, though IBM acknowledged the system sometimes made mistakes.
