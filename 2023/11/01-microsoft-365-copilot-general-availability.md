# Microsoft 365 Copilot becomes generally available to enterprises

- **Date:** 2023-11-01
- **Who:** Microsoft
- **Type:** product

Microsoft 365 Copilot became generally available to enterprise customers on November 1, 2023, at $30 per user per month, for Microsoft 365 E3 and E5 customers. At launch, buyers had to purchase at least 300 licenses; Microsoft dropped that minimum in January 2024. It turned the AI assistant from a preview into a paid, workplace-wide product.
