# Tesla AI Day 2022 shows Optimus humanoid prototype

- **Date:** 2022-09-30
- **Who:** Tesla (Elon Musk)
- **Type:** product

At Tesla's AI Day, Musk and staff showed a bipedal humanoid "rough development robot," later named Optimus, walking on stage. Footage showed it picking up a box while still tethered, and Musk said it had run without a tether for the first time that night. It was the first public look at Tesla applying its vehicle AI work to general-purpose robots.
