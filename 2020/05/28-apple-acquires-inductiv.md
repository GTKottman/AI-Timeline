# Apple acquires Waterloo data-error AI startup Inductiv

- **Date:** 2020-05-28
- **Who:** Apple; Inductiv (Waterloo, Ontario; co-founded by Ihab Ilyas, Theodoros Rekatsinas and Christopher Re)
- **Type:** acquisition

Bloomberg reported that Apple had bought Inductiv, a startup whose machine learning finds and corrects errors in datasets, with its engineers reportedly moving to work on Siri's training data. Apple confirmed the deal in its standard line about buying smaller companies. Inductiv traced back to the open-source HoloClean project, so the deal brought data-cleaning research into Apple's speech and assistant pipelines. The price was not disclosed.
