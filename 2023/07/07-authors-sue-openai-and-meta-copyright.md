# Sarah Silverman and authors sue OpenAI and Meta over training data

- **Date:** 2023-07-07
- **Who:** Sarah Silverman, Christopher Golden, Richard Kadrey; defendants OpenAI and Meta Platforms
- **Type:** other

Comedian Sarah Silverman and authors Christopher Golden and Richard Kadrey filed proposed class actions in the US District Court for the Northern District of California. They allege OpenAI and Meta trained their models on copyrighted books, citing ChatGPT summaries of the plaintiffs' books and Meta's use of a dataset containing Silverman's book. The claims include copyright infringement and DMCA violations, and the suits were among the first big US lawsuits over generative-AI training data.
