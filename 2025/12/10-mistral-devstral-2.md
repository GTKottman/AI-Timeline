# Mistral releases Devstral 2 and Devstral Small 2 coding models

- **Date:** 2025-12-10
- **Who:** Mistral AI
- **Type:** model

Mistral released Devstral 2, a 123B dense coding model, and Devstral Small 2, a 24B model that can run locally. Together with a CLI, they targeted agentic software engineering in open weights.
