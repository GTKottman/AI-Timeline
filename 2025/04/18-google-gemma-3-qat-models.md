# Google Gemma 3 QAT quantization-aware models

- **Date:** 2025-04-18
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** model

Google released quantization-aware trained (QAT) versions of Gemma 3 that keep high quality at 4-bit weights, so they run on consumer GPUs. Google said the 27B model runs on an RTX 3090, and its int4 size dropped from 54 GB to 14.1 GB; the 12B model dropped from 24 GB to 6.6 GB. The size figures are Google's own.
