# NVIDIA to supply 260,000 GPUs to South Korea

- **Date:** 2025-10-31
- **Who:** NVIDIA; South Korea's Ministry of Science and ICT; Samsung, SK Group, Hyundai Motor Group, Naver Cloud
- **Type:** hardware

At the APEC summit in Gyeongju, NVIDIA said it would supply 260,000 of its most advanced chips to South Korea. The deal was arranged with the Ministry of Science and ICT and includes AI factories built by Samsung, SK Group, Hyundai Motor Group and Naver Cloud, with about 50,000 GPUs each for the first three and more than 60,000 for Naver. Some GPUs were also earmarked for public-sector projects such as national AI models and a national AI data centre. Financial terms were not disclosed, and an estimate by the Chosun Daily put the value at about 14.8 trillion won.
