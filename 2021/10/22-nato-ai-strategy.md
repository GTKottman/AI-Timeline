# NATO adopts its first AI strategy

- **Date:** 2021-10-22
- **Who:** NATO
- **Type:** policy

NATO released its first AI strategy, with principles for responsible use of AI in defence and security. It set out how member states would develop and use AI in military settings, which made AI governance a formal part of alliance policy.
