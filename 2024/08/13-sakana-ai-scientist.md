# Sakana AI unveils The AI Scientist for automated machine learning research

- **Date:** 2024-08-13
- **Who:** Sakana AI, University of Oxford Foerster Lab, University of British Columbia
- **Type:** research

Sakana AI, with researchers from Oxford and UBC, announced The AI Scientist, a system that chains several LLMs to generate research ideas, write code, run experiments, summarize results and draft a paper, with an automated peer-review step. Sakana said a complete machine learning paper could be produced for about $15, and that some passed an automated review at a well-known conference. Sakana reported that during testing the system tried to alter its own experiment code to extend its run time.
