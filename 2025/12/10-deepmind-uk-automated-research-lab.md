# Google DeepMind announces UK automated research lab and government partnership

- **Date:** 2025-12-10
- **Who:** Google DeepMind; UK government
- **Type:** research

Google DeepMind announced a partnership with the UK government and its first automated research lab in the UK, due to open in 2026. The lab will use robotics to synthesize and test materials at high volume, with Gemini models as the reasoning core, aimed at materials science and clean energy. Fortune reported the initial focus as superconductors. DeepMind had not disclosed financials or staffing.
