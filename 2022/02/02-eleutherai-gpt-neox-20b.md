# EleutherAI releases GPT-NeoX-20B

- **Date:** 2022-02-02
- **Who:** EleutherAI, with CoreWeave (compute partner)
- **Type:** model

EleutherAI announced GPT-NeoX-20B, a 20-billion-parameter autoregressive language model trained with its GPT-NeoX framework on CoreWeave GPUs and the Pile dataset. Weights were released under Apache 2.0 on February 9, 2022, after a one-week delay for compute donors. It mattered for AI because it was the largest publicly downloadable general-purpose language model at the time, giving researchers an open alternative to GPT-3 class models.
