# MTEB: Massive Text Embedding Benchmark

- **Date:** 2022-10-13
- **Who:** Niklas Muennighoff, Nouamane Tazi, Loic Magne, Nils Reimers (Hugging Face and collaborators)
- **Type:** dataset

The MTEB paper introduced a benchmark of 8 embedding task types across 56-58 datasets in 112 languages, with 33 models evaluated. No single embedding method led on every task, which the authors took as evidence that no universal embedding approach yet existed. The public leaderboard became the standard place to compare text embedding models, and the paper was published at EACL 2023.
