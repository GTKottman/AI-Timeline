# China issues Ethics Norms for the New Generation of AI

- **Date:** 2021-09-25
- **Who:** China's Ministry of Science and Technology; National New Generation AI Governance Professional Committee
- **Type:** policy

On September 25, 2021 China issued ethics norms for the new generation of AI, applying to anyone involved in AI management, R&D, supply or use. It sets six general principles and 18 specific ethical requirements, including that humans keep full autonomy in decision-making. AGORA describes the document as proposed and not yet enacted, so treat it as non-binding guidance.
