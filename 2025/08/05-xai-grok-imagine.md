# xAI launches Grok Imagine image generator with a "spicy mode"

- **Date:** 2025-08-05
- **Who:** xAI
- **Type:** product

xAI launched Grok Imagine, an image and video generator in Grok that included a "spicy mode" for sexualized output. It was limited to paying subscribers. The launch drew criticism over content safety and was part of xAI's push to offer generative media in its app.
