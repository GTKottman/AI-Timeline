# Montreal Declaration for a Responsible Development of AI is officially launched

- **Date:** 2018-12-04
- **Who:** Universite de Montreal with the Fonds de recherche du Quebec and partners including CIRANO
- **Type:** policy

The Montreal Declaration for a Responsible Development of Artificial Intelligence was officially launched in Montreal on December 4, 2018. It is a set of ethical principles for AI development, described as the culmination of more than a year of work, research and consultations with citizens. The declaration was an early national-level attempt to set out responsible AI principles outside the big tech companies.
