# Perplexity releases R1 1776, an uncensored post-trained DeepSeek-R1

- **Date:** 2025-02-18
- **Who:** Perplexity AI
- **Type:** model

R1 1776 is an open-weights version of DeepSeek-R1 that Perplexity post-trained to remove censorship on topics restricted by the Chinese government. Secondary coverage says its reasoning performance stayed close to the base R1 on multiple benchmarks. The date of February 18 comes from a secondary report citing Perplexity's post; other listings give February 19 or 20.
