# Microsoft DeBERTa 1.5B tops SuperGLUE human baseline

- **Date:** 2021-01-06
- **Who:** Microsoft Research (Pengcheng He, Xiaodong Liu, Jianfeng Gao, Weizhu Chen)
- **Type:** research

Microsoft's 1.5-billion-parameter DeBERTa scored 89.9 on the SuperGLUE macro-average, against a human baseline of 89.8, the first single model to pass that line. An ensemble reached 90.3, and the team reported a GLUE macro-average of 90.8. Google's T5 with Meena followed at 90.2 within days. The result showed that the NLU benchmark was close to saturation, and the team said it would release the code and models.
