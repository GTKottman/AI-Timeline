# Facebook AI Research releases Blender open-domain chatbot

- **Date:** 2020-04-29
- **Who:** Facebook AI Research
- **Type:** model

Facebook AI Research introduced Blender, an open-domain chatbot that the company said beat Google's Meena in conversational quality. It combined large-scale pretraining with blended skills such as empathy and personality in its training. The largest model had 9.4 billion parameters, and human evaluators preferred Blender over Meena in the comparisons Facebook reported.
