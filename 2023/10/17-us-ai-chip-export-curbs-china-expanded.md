# US expands AI chip export curbs on China

- **Date:** 2023-10-17
- **Who:** US Department of Commerce, Bureau of Industry and Security; Nvidia
- **Type:** policy

The Bureau of Industry and Security expanded licensing requirements for advanced AI chips sent to China and to countries or firms likely to resell to China, including companies incorporated in Macau, and added due diligence rules. The rules covered Nvidia's China-specific A800 and H800 chips and also the A100, H100 and L40S, and added Chinese firms Biren and Moore Threads to a restricted list. Nvidia said the curbs took effect on October 23, earlier than the 30-day deadline. It tightened the hardware supply that Chinese AI labs could legally buy.
