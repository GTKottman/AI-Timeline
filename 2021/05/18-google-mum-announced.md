# Google introduces MUM multitask model

- **Date:** 2021-05-18
- **Who:** Google
- **Type:** model

Google introduced MUM (Multitask Unified Model), a model meant to understand information across many languages and modalities such as text and images, and to answer complex questions that normally need several searches. Google said it was 1,000 times more powerful than BERT.
