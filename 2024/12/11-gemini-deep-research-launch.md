# Google launches Deep Research in Gemini Advanced

- **Date:** 2024-12-11
- **Who:** Google
- **Type:** product

Google launched Deep Research in the Gemini app for Gemini Advanced subscribers. The agent drafts a multi-step research plan that the user can edit, searches the web repeatedly, and writes a report with sources that can be exported to Google Docs. It first ran on Gemini 1.5 Pro, and TechCrunch reported it was limited to paying subscribers. Google later said the Gemini 2.0 Flash Thinking experimental model improved its quality and efficiency.
