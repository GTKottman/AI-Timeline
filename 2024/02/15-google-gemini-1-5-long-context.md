# Google DeepMind introduces Gemini 1.5 with one-million-token context

- **Date:** 2024-02-15
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** model

Gemini 1.5 Pro shipped with a standard 128,000-token context window. A limited group of developers and enterprise customers could try a window of up to one million tokens in private preview. Google said this could handle long documents, hours of video and large codebases in one prompt. Long context became a major competitive axis for frontier models in 2024.
