# Nvidia agrees to acquire Mellanox for $6.9 billion

- **Date:** 2019-03-11
- **Who:** Nvidia, Mellanox Technologies
- **Type:** acquisition

Nvidia announced it will buy Mellanox, an interconnect maker for data-center networking, for $125 per share in cash, a total enterprise value of about $6.9 billion. Mellanox's high-speed networking is used to link GPUs in AI and HPC clusters, so the deal tied Nvidia's accelerators more closely to the networking that large training systems depend on. Nvidia's 10-Q gives March 10, 2019 as the signing date of the merger agreement.
