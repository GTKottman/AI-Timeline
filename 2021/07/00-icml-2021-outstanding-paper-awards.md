# ICML 2021 outstanding paper awards

- **Date:** 2021-07-00
- **Who:** ICML 2021 organizers; Google Brain researchers (Vicol, Metz, Sohl-Dickstein; Grathwohl, Swersky, Hashemi, Duvenaud, Maddison)
- **Type:** competition

ICML 2021 gave its outstanding paper award to "Unbiased Gradient Estimation in Unrolled Computation Graphs with Persistent Evolution" by Paul Vicol, Luke Metz and Jascha Sohl-Dickstein, which introduced Persistent Evolution Strategies for cheaper gradient estimates in long unrolled optimization. An honorable mention went to "Oops I took a gradient: Scalable sampling for discrete distributions" by Google Brain researchers. ICML also gave a Test of Time Award to Welling and Teh for the 2011 SGLD paper. The exact day of the award announcement during the July conference was not confirmed.
