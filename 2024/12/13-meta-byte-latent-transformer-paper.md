# Meta publishes Byte Latent Transformer paper

- **Date:** 2024-12-13
- **Who:** Meta FAIR, University of Washington, University of Chicago
- **Type:** paper

"Byte Latent Transformer: Patches Scale Better Than Tokens" (arXiv:2412.09871, submitted December 13, 2024) introduces a byte-level language model that groups bytes into dynamically sized patches based on next-byte entropy. The authors report it matches tokenization-based LLM performance at scale up to 8B parameters in a FLOP-controlled study, with better inference efficiency and robustness to noisy input. A secondary summary notes that BPE is better at small budgets.
