# AudioSet: 2 million human-labelled YouTube sound clips released

- **Date:** 2017-03-30
- **Who:** Google Research (Jort F. Gemmeke, Daniel P. W. Ellis and team)
- **Type:** dataset

Google announced AudioSet on March 30, 2017: an ontology of 527 sound event categories and over 2 million human-labelled 10-second clips from YouTube. The AudioSet site now lists 632 classes and 2,084,320 clips. Segments were nominated from YouTube metadata and content search, then checked by human raters. The release included clip URLs, the labels judged present and precomputed audio features.
