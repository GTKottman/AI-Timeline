# Nobel Prize in Chemistry awarded for protein design and AlphaFold

- **Date:** 2024-10-09
- **Who:** David Baker; Demis Hassabis; John Jumper (Google DeepMind)
- **Type:** people

David Baker received half the chemistry prize for computational protein design. Demis Hassabis and John Jumper of Google DeepMind shared the other half for AlphaFold's protein structure prediction. It was a major recognition of AI-driven science.
