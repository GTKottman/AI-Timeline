# ICML 2026 Outstanding Paper Awards (Seoul)

- **Date:** 2026-07-05
- **Who:** ICML 2026 (held July 6 to 11 at COEX, Seoul); Zanlin Ni, Shenzhen Wang, Yang Yue and co-authors; Fan Chen, Sinho Chewi, Constantinos Daskalakis, Alexander Rakhlin; Mnih et al. (Test of Time)
- **Type:** competition

ICML 2026 named two Outstanding Papers, both on diffusion models: "The Flexibility Trap: Rethinking the Value of Arbitrary Order in Diffusion Language Models" and "High-Accuracy Sampling for Diffusion Models and Log-Concave Distributions." The awards also included an Outstanding Position Paper, on how alignment work can be repurposed as a censor's toolkit, and a Test of Time award to Mnih et al.'s 2016 "Asynchronous Methods for Deep Reinforcement Learning." The date is the award-announcement date given in secondary coverage that links to the ICML blog post of 2026/07/05; one source's count of award winners differs.
