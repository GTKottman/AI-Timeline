# Meta releases LLaMA foundation models to researchers

- **Date:** 2023-02-24
- **Who:** Meta AI
- **Type:** model

Meta AI introduced LLaMA, a family of language models from 7 to 65 billion parameters, released to researchers under a non-commercial license. Its weights leaked soon after, and LLaMA became the base for a large wave of open-source fine-tunes such as Alpaca.
