# Cohere raises $500M at a $5.5B valuation

- **Date:** 2024-07-22
- **Who:** Cohere; Cisco; AMD; Fujitsu
- **Type:** funding

Cohere raised $500 million from investors including Cisco, AMD and Fujitsu at a $5.5 billion valuation. It was one of the largest rounds for an enterprise-focused LLM company outside the big US labs.
