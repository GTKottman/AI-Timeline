# Stability AI announces Stable Cascade

- **Date:** 2024-02-13
- **Who:** Stability AI
- **Type:** model

Stability AI announced Stable Cascade, a text-to-image model built on a three-stage cascade in a highly compressed latent space, which it said made training and fine-tuning practical on consumer GPUs. Stability said the model supports training, fine-tuning, ControlNet and LoRA. It mattered because it offered a new architecture for open image generation, with claimed large cost and speed gains over Stable Diffusion models of similar size.
