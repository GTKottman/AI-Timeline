# Sam Altman reinstated as OpenAI CEO with new board

- **Date:** 2023-11-22
- **Who:** OpenAI (Sam Altman, Greg Brockman, Bret Taylor, Larry Summers, Adam D'Angelo); Microsoft (Satya Nadella)
- **Type:** people

OpenAI agreed in principle to bring Sam Altman back as CEO, with a new initial board of Bret Taylor as chair, Larry Summers and Adam D'Angelo, the only returning director from the previous board. Greg Brockman returned as president. Altman said he looked forward to returning with Satya Nadella's support and to building on the partnership with Microsoft. The reinstatement came about five days after the firing, after pressure from staff who threatened to follow Altman to Microsoft.
