# HPE announces shipment of its first NVIDIA Grace Blackwell system

- **Date:** 2025-02-13
- **Who:** HPE; NVIDIA
- **Type:** hardware

HPE announced that it had shipped its first NVIDIA Grace Blackwell system, the GB200 NVL72 rack with 72 Blackwell GPUs and 36 Grace CPUs, cooled directly by liquid. The announcement did not say how many units had shipped or who the first customers were.
