# Google launches Veo 3.1 Lite video model at lower price

- **Date:** 2026-03-31
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** product

Veo 3.1 Lite is a budget video-generation tier for developers, priced at $0.05 per second for 720p and $0.08 per second for 1080p in the paid Gemini API and Google AI Studio tiers. Google says its per-second cost is under half that of Veo 3.1 Fast. Lower video-generation prices make short AI video cheap enough for high-volume production use.
