# PyTorch moves to the independent PyTorch Foundation

- **Date:** 2022-09-00
- **Who:** Meta AI and the Linux Foundation
- **Type:** software

Meta announced in September 2022 that PyTorch would be governed by the independent PyTorch Foundation under the Linux Foundation. Moving governance away from one company was meant to help the framework's broader community of contributors.
