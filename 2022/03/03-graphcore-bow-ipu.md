# Graphcore announces Bow IPU with wafer-on-wafer technology

- **Date:** 2022-03-03
- **Who:** Graphcore (built with TSMC)
- **Type:** hardware

Graphcore announced the Bow IPU on March 3, 2022, its third-generation IPU and the first built with TSMC's 3D wafer-on-wafer stacking. Graphcore claims up to 40% higher performance on real AI workloads, mainly from a higher clock speed, and up to 16% better performance per watt, at the same price and with no software changes. These are vendor "up to" figures.
