# Adept raises $350M Series B

- **Date:** 2023-03-14
- **Who:** Adept AI (co-led by General Catalyst and Spark Capital)
- **Type:** funding

Adept announced a $350M Series B, reportedly valuing the company at at least $1 billion. The company said the money would go to productizing its work on ACT-1, a large model trained to take actions across software tools such as browsers and business apps. The round showed investor appetite for AI agents that operate software rather than only generate text.
