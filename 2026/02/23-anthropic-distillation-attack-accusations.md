# Anthropic accuses DeepSeek, Moonshot and MiniMax of distillation attacks

- **Date:** 2026-02-23
- **Who:** Anthropic; DeepSeek; Moonshot AI; MiniMax
- **Type:** research

Anthropic said three Chinese labs had run distillation attacks on Claude, using large numbers of fraudulent accounts to extract model behaviour. It was a public dispute over whether model outputs can be used to train rival models, and it prompted calls for export and access controls.
