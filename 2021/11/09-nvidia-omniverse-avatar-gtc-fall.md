# Nvidia shows Omniverse Avatar and Replicator at GTC Fall 2021

- **Date:** 2021-11-09
- **Who:** NVIDIA; Omniverse, Maxine SDK
- **Type:** product

At the GTC Fall 2021 keynote on 9 November 2021, NVIDIA introduced Omniverse Avatar and Omniverse Replicator, along with Project Tokkio, Maxine and DRIVE Concierge demos. An AI avatar of Jensen Huang, nicknamed "Toy Jensen", appeared in the keynote, built on Omniverse. Omniverse Avatar targeted interactive AI characters such as customer-support agents and in-vehicle assistants, and Replicator targeted synthetic training data.
