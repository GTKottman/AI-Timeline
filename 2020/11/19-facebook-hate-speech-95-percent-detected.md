# Facebook says AI proactively detected about 95% of hate speech it removed

- **Date:** 2020-11-19
- **Who:** Facebook (Meta)
- **Type:** research

Facebook's Community Standards Enforcement Report, published November 19, 2020, covered July to September 2020. It said Facebook took action on 22.1 million pieces of hate speech, about 95% of which were found proactively by its systems (94.7% in some coverage). It also reported global hate speech prevalence of 0.10% to 0.11% of content views, the first time the company had reported that figure.
