# Stargate AI infrastructure project announced at the White House

- **Date:** 2025-01-21
- **Who:** OpenAI, SoftBank, Oracle, MGX; announced by President Trump
- **Type:** funding

OpenAI, SoftBank, Oracle and MGX announced the Stargate Project, a plan for up to $500 billion of US AI infrastructure over several years,. The announcement was made publicly at the White House.
