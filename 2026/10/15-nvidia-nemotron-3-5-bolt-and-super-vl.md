# NVIDIA releases Nemotron 3.5 Bolt 74B-A9B and Nemotron 3.5 Super VL

- **Date:** 2026-10-15
- **Who:** NVIDIA
- **Type:** model

NVIDIA released Nemotron 3.5 Super VL, a 120B vision model with a 1M-token context, and Nemotron 3.5 Bolt 74B-A9B on October 15, 2026. Bolt is a 74-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with about 9 billion active parameters and a 256K context, built to run in 24 GB of memory with native 2-bit quantization for local agent work. NVIDIA said Bolt E4B, a 4 GB edge model, would follow on October 23. NVIDIA's leaderboard claims are vendor figures.
