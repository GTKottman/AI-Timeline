# NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin partner production systems at Computex

- **Date:** 2022-05-24
- **Who:** NVIDIA; nearly three dozen partners (including Advantech, AAEON, Basler, Connect Tech, Stereolabs)
- **Type:** hardware

At Computex 2022 in Taipei, NVIDIA and its partners announced the first production systems built on the Jetson AGX Orin module, including servers, edge appliances, industrial PCs and carrier boards. NVIDIA said Jetson AGX Orin production modules would be available from July and Orin NX modules from September. The 32GB Jetson AGX Orin module was reported as available by August 2022.
