# Google's Soft Actor-Critic learns robot skills from real-world experience

- **Date:** 2019-01-18
- **Who:** Google AI (Tuomas Haarnoja, Sergey Levine) with UC Berkeley
- **Type:** research

Google AI explained Soft Actor-Critic (SAC), a deep reinforcement learning method, as a practical way to train robots directly from real-world experience. The post said real robot tasks could be solved in a few hours using one set of hyperparameters across environments, where many earlier methods needed days or weeks of robot data. The approach was first announced in December 2018 on the BAIR blog. The related paper, "Soft Actor-Critic Algorithms and Applications," followed on January 29, 2019.
