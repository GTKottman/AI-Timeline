# OpenAI opens limited preview of GPT-5.6 Sol, Terra and Luna

- **Date:** 2026-06-26
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** model

OpenAI launched its GPT-5.6 family, Sol, Terra and Luna, in a limited preview on June 26, 2026, with access restricted at the US government's request during a security review of the models' cyber capabilities. Sol is the flagship, Terra the balanced everyday model and Luna the fast, low-cost tier, reported at $5/$30, $2.50/$15 and $1/$6 per million input/output tokens. The broad public release followed on July 9.
