# Mistral releases Magistral, its first reasoning model

- **Date:** 2025-06-10
- **Who:** Mistral AI
- **Type:** model

Mistral AI introduced Magistral, its first reasoning model, in two versions. Magistral Small was a 24-billion-parameter open-weight model under Apache 2.0, and Magistral Medium was an enterprise version in preview through Le Chat and the API. Mistral said both showed traceable multi-step reasoning across several languages.
