# Andrew Ng launches deeplearning.ai

- **Date:** 2017-06-26
- **Who:** Andrew Ng
- **Type:** people

Andrew Ng, after leaving Baidu's AI group, launched deeplearning.ai, a new AI company and education venture, and promised more announcements soon. The move gave one of the field's best-known teachers a platform for deep learning courses and training. It mattered because Ng's courses reached a very large audience of new practitioners.
