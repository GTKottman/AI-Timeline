# Google's structured method learns depth and ego-motion from monocular video

- **Date:** 2018-11-27
- **Who:** Google Research
- **Type:** research

Google described an unsupervised method that learns depth and camera motion from a single camera. It models the scene in 3D, with moving objects and the camera each given their own motion. The post said it outperforms state-of-the-art approaches on the KITTI and Cityscapes urban driving datasets, and added an online refinement step that keeps learning while collecting new data. It mattered for robots and cars that must judge distance without a stereo rig.
