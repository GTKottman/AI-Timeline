# Ai2 releases Tulu 3, a fully open post-training recipe and model family

- **Date:** 2024-11-21
- **Who:** Allen Institute for AI (Ai2)
- **Type:** model

Ai2 released Tulu 3, a family of post-trained language models in 8B and 70B sizes built on Llama 3.1, together with its training data, data mixes, recipes, code and evaluation framework. Ai2 claimed the 70B model matched or beat proprietary models from OpenAI and others on math, instruction following and chat benchmarks; those are its own numbers. The technical report followed on arXiv days later. Full release of the post-training stack made the process reproducible for outside researchers.
