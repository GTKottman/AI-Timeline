# PyTorch 1.9 released

- **Date:** 2021-06-15
- **Who:** PyTorch (Meta AI open-source team and community)
- **Type:** software

PyTorch 1.9 shipped with over 3,400 commits from 398 contributors. torch.linalg became stable, torch.special was added as a scientific computing API, and TorchElastic moved into core for fault-tolerant distributed training.
