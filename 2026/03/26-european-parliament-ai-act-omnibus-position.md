# European Parliament adopts position on AI Act digital omnibus delay

- **Date:** 2026-03-26
- **Who:** European Parliament (MEPs)
- **Type:** policy

MEPs adopted their negotiating position on the Digital Omnibus for AI by 569 votes to 45, with 23 abstentions. The position moves the high-risk deadline to 2 December 2027 and the sectoral-product rules to 2 August 2028. It followed committee approval on March 18 and set up the trilogue that later produced a provisional deal.
