# Bletchley Declaration signed at UK AI Safety Summit

- **Date:** 2023-11-01
- **Who:** UK government; 28 countries and the European Union, including the US and China
- **Type:** policy

Countries attending the AI Safety Summit at Bletchley Park published the Bletchley Declaration on frontier AI risk on the summit's opening day. The non-binding text, signed by 28 countries and the EU, committed signatories to identify shared AI safety risks through research and to build risk-based national policies. Named signatories included the US, China, the UK and the EU.
