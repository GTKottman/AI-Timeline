# DeepSeekMath open model for mathematical reasoning

- **Date:** 2024-02-05
- **Who:** DeepSeek
- **Type:** paper

DeepSeek posted the paper "DeepSeekMath: Pushing the Limits of Mathematical Reasoning in Open Language Models" on arXiv on 5 February 2024. It describes a 7B-parameter open model specialised for mathematical reasoning, trained on a large math-focused corpus. It mattered because it showed a small open model could reach strong math-benchmark scores, narrowing the gap with closed systems.
