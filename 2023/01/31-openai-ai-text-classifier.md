# OpenAI releases AI text classifier to detect AI-written text

- **Date:** 2023-01-31
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** product

OpenAI released a classifier meant to tell human-written text from AI-written text, catching only 26% of AI text in its own tests with a 9% false-positive rate. It was withdrawn on July 20, 2023 for low accuracy, which showed how hard reliable AI-text detection is.
