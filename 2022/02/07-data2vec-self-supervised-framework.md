# data2vec: one self-supervised learning method for speech, vision and language

- **Date:** 2022-02-07
- **Who:** Meta AI
- **Type:** research

data2vec uses the same learning method to train models on speech, images and text, instead of a different recipe per modality. Showing one framework works across all three was a step toward more general self-supervised learning.
