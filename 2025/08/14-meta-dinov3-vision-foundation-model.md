# Meta releases DINOv3 self-supervised vision model

- **Date:** 2025-08-14
- **Who:** Meta AI
- **Type:** model

Meta announced DINOv3, a self-supervised vision model scaled to 7B parameters on 1.7B images, with smaller distilled models and ConvNeXt variants, and released training code and pretrained backbones. Meta reported 86.6 mean IoU on PASCAL VOC segmentation against 83.1 for DINOv2. Licence terms are reported differently across sources, so they should be checked against the licence text. It mattered as a general-purpose frozen visual backbone for segmentation and depth tasks.
