# ConvNeXt: A ConvNet for the 2020s

- **Date:** 2022-01-10
- **Who:** Meta AI (FAIR) and UC Berkeley researchers
- **Type:** paper

Researchers modernized a standard ResNet with Transformer-inspired design choices and produced ConvNeXt, a family of purely convolutional image models. The paper argued that plain convolutional networks, given modern training and architecture tweaks, could compete with Vision Transformers, which were then the focus of most vision research.
