# Facebook AI publishes XLM-R, a 100-language masked language model

- **Date:** 2019-11-05
- **Who:** Facebook AI (Alexis Conneau, Kartikay Khandelwal and colleagues)
- **Type:** model

XLM-R is a transformer masked language model trained on filtered CommonCrawl text covering 100 languages, with more than two terabytes of data. It reported about 14.6% average XNLI accuracy and 13% average MLQA F1 gains over multilingual BERT, with the largest gains on low-resource languages. The paper also documented the "curse of multilinguality," where adding languages at fixed capacity eventually hurts performance. XLM-R became a common base for cross-lingual NLP systems.
