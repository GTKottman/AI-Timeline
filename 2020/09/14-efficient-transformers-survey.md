# Efficient Transformers: A Survey

- **Date:** 2020-09-14
- **Who:** Google Research (Tay, Dehghani, Bahri, Metzler)
- **Type:** paper

The arXiv survey "Efficient Transformers: A Survey" reviewed the many proposed variants of the Transformer that cut computation and memory cost. It grouped these "X-formers" across domains so researchers could compare them. It became a standard reference for the efficient-attention line of work.
