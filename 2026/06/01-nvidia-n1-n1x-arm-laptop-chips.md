# NVIDIA unveils N1 and N1X Arm laptop chips at Computex

- **Date:** 2026-06-01
- **Who:** NVIDIA (OEM partners reported: Dell, Lenovo, ASUS, MSI, HP, Microsoft)
- **Type:** hardware

NVIDIA showed its first Arm-based PC system-on-chips, N1 and N1X, in Jensen Huang's Computex 2026 keynote in Taipei. Reports describe a flagship pairing a 20-core Arm CPU with a Blackwell GPU and large unified LPDDR5X memory, with laptops from partners expected before the 2026 holidays. It matters because it puts NVIDIA's GPU and AI stack into Windows-on-Arm laptops against Qualcomm's Snapdragon X line. Specs came from pre-event leaks and no independent benchmarks were available at reveal.
