# President Biden signs Executive Order 14110 on safe, secure and trustworthy AI

- **Date:** 2023-10-30
- **Who:** White House (President Joe Biden)
- **Type:** policy

President Biden signed an executive order setting federal requirements for safety testing, reporting of large training runs, watermarking guidance and AI workforce measures. It was the broadest US federal AI policy action to date.
