# Mistral AI raises 1.7 billion euros in Series C led by ASML

- **Date:** 2025-09-09
- **Who:** Mistral AI; ASML (lead), DST Global, a16z, Bpifrance, General Catalyst, Index Ventures, Lightspeed, Nvidia
- **Type:** funding

Mistral AI announced a 1.7 billion euro Series C at an 11.7 billion euro post-money valuation, led by Dutch chip-equipment maker ASML. DST Global joined, and existing investors including Andreessen Horowitz, Bpifrance, General Catalyst, Index Ventures, Lightspeed and Nvidia also took part. Sources disagree on ASML's exact contribution, which Sifted had put at 1.3 billion euros before the announcement, so the euro figures are the reliable ones.
