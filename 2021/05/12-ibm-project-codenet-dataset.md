# IBM releases Project CodeNet dataset for AI coding

- **Date:** 2021-05-12
- **Who:** IBM Research, MIT-IBM Watson AI Lab
- **Type:** dataset

IBM announced Project CodeNet in May 2021 at its Think 2021 conference. It is a dataset of about 14 million code samples, roughly 500 million lines in more than 55 languages, drawn from Aizu and AtCoder programming-contest submissions, with runtime and memory data attached. IBM and the MIT-IBM Watson AI Lab published a paper and released the materials on GitHub, framing it as an ImageNet-style benchmark for AI that works on code, such as translation between programming languages. The Register's coverage is dated 12 May 2021.
