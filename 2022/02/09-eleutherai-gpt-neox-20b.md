# EleutherAI releases GPT-NeoX-20B weights

- **Date:** 2022-02-09
- **Who:** EleutherAI, with CoreWeave (compute partner)
- **Type:** model

EleutherAI released the full GPT-NeoX-20B weights on February 9, 2022, under Apache 2.0, one week after a hosted version became available on February 2 through a CoreWeave partner API. The 20-billion-parameter autoregressive model was trained with EleutherAI's GPT-NeoX framework on CoreWeave A100 GPUs and the Pile dataset. EleutherAI calls it a research artifact and advises careful evaluation before production use.
