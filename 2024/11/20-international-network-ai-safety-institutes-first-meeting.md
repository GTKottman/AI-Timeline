# International Network of AI Safety Institutes holds first meeting in San Francisco

- **Date:** 2024-11-20
- **Who:** AI safety institutes from the US, UK, EU, Japan, Canada, Australia, South Korea, Singapore, France and Kenya
- **Type:** policy

The first meeting of the International Network of AI Safety Institutes took place in San Francisco, growing out of the Seoul commitments of May 2024. Members signed a mission statement, and the group reviewed results from its first joint safety-testing exercise. Coverage also reported more than $11 million for research on AI-generated content. The network made government AI safety testing a shared, cross-border practice. The European Commission gives the meeting dates as 20 to 21 November; CSIS gives 21 to 22 November.
