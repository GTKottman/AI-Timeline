# Intel Acquires Habana Labs

- **Date:** 2019-12-16
- **Who:** Intel Corporation, Habana Labs (Israel)
- **Type:** acquisition

Intel announced it had acquired Habana Labs, an Israeli maker of programmable deep learning accelerators, for about $2 billion. Habana's Goya inference processor and Gaudi training processor were its core products. Habana became a separate business unit under Intel's Data Platforms Group, and Intel framed the deal as a way to strengthen its AI portfolio in a market it expected to exceed $25 billion by 2024.
