# ICLR 2026 Outstanding Paper Awards

- **Date:** 2026-04-00
- **Who:** ICLR 2026 (International Conference on Learning Representations); Anthony W. Lin (MPI-SWS)
- **Type:** competition

The ICLR 2026 Outstanding Paper award went to "Transformers are Inherently Succinct" by Anthony W. Lin of MPI-SWS, per an MPI-SWS news item dated April 28, 2026. That item says only two of more than 5,000 accepted papers received the award this year. The second winner could not be confirmed from the sources checked, and the conference and award-announcement dates are also unconfirmed.
