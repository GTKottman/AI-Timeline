# NVIDIA announces DGX GH200 AI supercomputer

- **Date:** 2023-05-30
- **Who:** NVIDIA
- **Type:** hardware

NVIDIA announced the DGX GH200, which links 256 Grace Hopper Superchips through NVLink Switch so they act as one giant GPU with about 144 TB of shared memory and roughly one exaflop. NVIDIA planned to offer the design to hyperscalers such as Google Cloud, Meta and Microsoft. Coverage differs on availability timing. It mattered as a memory-scale architecture aimed at giant generative AI and recommender models.
