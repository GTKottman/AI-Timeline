# Amazon Alexa launches in Ireland

- **Date:** 2018-02-01
- **Who:** Amazon
- **Type:** product

Amazon launched Alexa in Ireland, extending the voice assistant to another English-speaking market in Europe. It was part of Amazon's rollout of Alexa across new countries through late 2017 and early 2018.
