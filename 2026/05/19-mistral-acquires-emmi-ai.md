# Mistral AI acquires Austrian physics-AI startup Emmi AI

- **Date:** 2026-05-19
- **Who:** Mistral AI, Emmi AI
- **Type:** acquisition

Mistral AI acquired Linz-based Emmi AI, a physics-AI startup spun out of NXAI in 2024 that builds models for engineering tasks such as airflow, heat transfer and material stress. Reuters reported the deal on May 19; the price was not disclosed, and one unconfirmed report puts it in the hundreds of millions of euros. Emmi's co-founders and more than 30 researchers join Mistral's science and applied-AI teams, and Linz becomes a Mistral office.
