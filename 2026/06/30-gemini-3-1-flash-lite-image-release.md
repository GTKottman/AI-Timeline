# Google releases Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite Image (Nano Banana 2 Lite)

- **Date:** 2026-06-30
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** model

Google released Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite Image, marketed as Nano Banana 2 Lite, the fastest and cheapest tier of its Nano Banana image line. It is based on Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite.
