# Apple unveils Gemini-based Siri AI at WWDC 2026

- **Date:** 2026-06-08
- **Who:** Apple, Google (Gemini)
- **Type:** product

At its June 8 WWDC keynote, Apple unveiled a rebuilt assistant, Siri AI, built in partnership with Google's Gemini models. It ships as a standalone app across iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch and Vision Pro, with on-screen awareness, personal-context search and synced conversation history. A developer beta arrived at the keynote, a public beta is due in July, and general availability comes with iOS 27 in the fall. One source reports no initial EU or China launch. Deal terms reported in the press were not part of Apple's announcement.
