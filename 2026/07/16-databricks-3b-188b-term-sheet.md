# Databricks signs term sheet for $3 billion round at $188 billion valuation

- **Date:** 2026-07-16
- **Who:** Databricks, Coatue Management (lead)
- **Type:** funding

According to Wall Street Journal reporting, Databricks signed a term sheet on July 16, 2026 for a $3 billion round valuing the company at $188 billion, with existing investor Coatue leading. The money is meant to fund acquisitions and expand products including the Genie AI assistant and Unity AI Gateway. The round had not closed in the reports, and the valuation is about 40% above its earlier round near $134 billion. An earlier report of a $10 billion raise is not supported by the sources found.
