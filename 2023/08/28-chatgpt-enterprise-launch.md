# OpenAI launches ChatGPT Enterprise

- **Date:** 2023-08-28
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** product

OpenAI launched ChatGPT Enterprise, a business tier with enterprise-grade security and privacy, unlimited higher-speed GPT-4 access, longer context windows and advanced data analysis. OpenAI said it would not use customer prompts or data to train its models and that it was SOC 2 compliant. Customization on company data was promised later. It was OpenAI's first product aimed directly at large organizations, and it moved ChatGPT from a consumer chatbot into workplace software.
