# OpenAI releases Sora video generation to ChatGPT subscribers

- **Date:** 2024-12-09
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** product

OpenAI moved its Sora text-to-video model out of research preview and made it available at sora.com to ChatGPT Plus and Pro subscribers. The launch version, Sora Turbo, generated clips of up to 20 seconds at up to 1080p on the Pro tier, with shorter, lower-resolution limits on Plus. It could animate still images and extend existing videos. It was not offered in the UK, Switzerland or the European Economic Area at launch. It put high-quality AI video in front of paying ChatGPT users.
