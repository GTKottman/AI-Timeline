# Microsoft asks Congress to regulate facial recognition

- **Date:** 2018-07-13
- **Who:** Microsoft (Brad Smith, President and Chief Legal Officer)
- **Type:** policy

In a blog post on July 13, 2018, Microsoft's Brad Smith called on Congress to set up a bipartisan, expert-led commission to assess and draft rules for facial recognition, arguing that elected governments should set the rules. He cited risks of abuse and of government tracking people without their knowledge. He also said Microsoft's ICE contract was not being used for facial recognition. It mattered as one of the first calls for AI regulation from a major tech company's leadership.
