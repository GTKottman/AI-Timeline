# sketch-rnn: neural representation of sketch drawings (arXiv)

- **Date:** 2017-04-11
- **Who:** David Ha, Douglas Eck (Google Brain)
- **Type:** paper

The paper "A Neural Representation of Sketch Drawings" (arXiv 1704.03477) introduced sketch-rnn, a recurrent model that generates vector stroke sequences of common objects, trained on the Quick, Draw! dataset of crude human drawings. It handled both unconditional generation and conditional completion of sketches. It mattered because it modeled drawings as sequences of strokes rather than pixels and opened a line of generative sketch research.
