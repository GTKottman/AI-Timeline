# Uber AI Labs' Go-Explore solves Montezuma's Revenge without human demonstrations

- **Date:** 2018-11-26
- **Who:** Uber AI Labs (Joel Lehman, Jeff Clune, Kenneth Stanley, Adrien Ecoffet)
- **Type:** research

Uber AI Labs published Go-Explore, an algorithm for hard-exploration games. It saves promising states it has already reached, returns to them, explores further, and then turns the best routes into a robust policy with imitation learning. Without domain knowledge it scored over 35,000 points on Montezuma's Revenge, more than three times the prior state of the art. The post also said it scored above zero on Pitfall, which no learning algorithm had done before. The arXiv paper followed in January 2019.
