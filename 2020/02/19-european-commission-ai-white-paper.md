# European Commission publishes White Paper on Artificial Intelligence

- **Date:** 2020-02-19
- **Who:** European Commission
- **Type:** policy

The European Commission released "On Artificial Intelligence - A European approach to excellence and trust," a white paper that set out policy options for AI uptake and risk management. It favored a risk-based approach with sector- and application-specific assessments rather than blanket sectoral rules or bans. It was released alongside a European data strategy and a report on safety and liability for AI, and opened public consultation until 31 May 2020; it was the first step toward the EU's later AI legislation.
