# CVPR 2023 best paper awards

- **Date:** 2023-06-21
- **Who:** CVPR 2023 awards committee; winning teams incl. Gupta and Kembhavi; Hu, Li and collaborators
- **Type:** competition

At CVPR 2023 in Vancouver, Best Paper went to "Visual Programming: Compositional visual reasoning without training" (Gupta and Kembhavi) and to "Planning-oriented Autonomous Driving" (Hu, Li and team). The two winners were a training-free compositional reasoning paper and an end-to-end driving paper.
