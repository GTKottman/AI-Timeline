# Google Claims Quantum Supremacy with Sycamore

- **Date:** 2019-10-23
- **Who:** Google AI Quantum (John Martinis, Sergio Boixo and team)
- **Type:** research

Google published in Nature "Quantum supremacy using a programmable superconducting processor," reporting that its 53-working-qubit Sycamore chip sampled random quantum circuits in about 200 seconds. Google estimated the same task would take about 10,000 years on a state-of-the-art classical supercomputer. IBM publicly disputed the claim, arguing the result was not proof of quantum "supremacy" in a practical sense. The result matters for AI because quantum hardware is a candidate accelerator for machine learning and optimization.
