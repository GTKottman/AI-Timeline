# G20 adopts human-centred AI principles

- **Date:** 2019-06-09
- **Who:** G20 (ministerial statement on trade and the digital economy)
- **Type:** policy

The G20 Trade and Digital Economy Ministers, meeting in Tsukuba, Japan, adopted a set of non-binding G20 AI Principles as part of their joint ministerial statement. The principles draw on the OECD's May 2019 Recommendation on AI and were the first AI principles agreed by the G20. They gave the OECD framework wider government backing across the major economies.
