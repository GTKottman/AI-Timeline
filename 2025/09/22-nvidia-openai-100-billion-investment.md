# Nvidia plans up to $100 billion investment in OpenAI

- **Date:** 2025-09-22
- **Who:** Nvidia; OpenAI
- **Type:** funding

Nvidia announced plans to invest up to $100 billion in OpenAI, tied to at least 10 gigawatts of data-center capacity. The deal drew concern about circular financing, where a chip supplier funds its own customer.
