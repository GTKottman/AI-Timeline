# MLPerf Inference v0.5 results published

- **Date:** 2019-11-06
- **Who:** MLPerf (industry benchmarking consortium); submitters including NVIDIA, Dell, Intel
- **Type:** competition

The first MLPerf Inference benchmark round posted results covering five benchmarks across three tasks: two object detection (COCO), two image classification (ImageNet) and one translation (WMT English-German). Vendors including NVIDIA, Dell (Tesla T4 servers) and Intel (Nervana NNP-I and VNNI) submitted results. It gave the industry a shared, vendor-neutral way to compare inference hardware and software. Vendor claims should be checked against mlperf.org, which was not reached directly.
