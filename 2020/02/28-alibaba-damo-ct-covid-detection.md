# Alibaba DAMO Academy announces AI that flags COVID-19 on chest CT scans

- **Date:** 2020-02-28
- **Who:** Alibaba DAMO Academy (Alibaba's research arm); Alibaba Cloud
- **Type:** product

Alibaba's DAMO Academy announced an AI system that analyzes chest CT scans to separate COVID-19 from ordinary viral pneumonia. Alibaba said it was trained on data from more than 5,000 confirmed cases, reached up to 96 percent accuracy and returned results in about 20 seconds. It was first deployed at Qiboshan Hospital in Zhengzhou. The accuracy figures were company claims, and no independent validation was seen in coverage.
