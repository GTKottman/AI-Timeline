# Mobileye unveils EyeQ Ultra autonomous driving chip at CES 2022

- **Date:** 2022-01-04
- **Who:** Mobileye (Intel)
- **Type:** hardware

At CES 2022 Mobileye announced the EyeQ Ultra, a system-on-chip it calls its most advanced, built specifically for autonomous driving and rated at 176 TOPS. Mobileye said first silicon was expected at the end of 2023 and automotive-grade production in 2025. It mattered for AI because it is dedicated inference hardware for self-driving perception and planning in vehicles.
