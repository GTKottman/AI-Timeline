# Ofcom says chatbot content falls under the Online Safety Act

- **Date:** 2024-11-09
- **Who:** Ofcom (UK); Character.AI
- **Type:** policy

Ofcom, the UK online safety regulator, said content produced by chatbots that impersonate real and fictional people falls under the Online Safety Act 2023. It followed reports of user-made bots imitating murdered and bereaved people on Character.AI, and it made the UK one of the first regulators to state chatbot output is covered by platform duties.
