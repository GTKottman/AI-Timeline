# NVIDIA's GeForce RTX 2080 Ti and RTX 2080 go on sale with Turing tensor cores

- **Date:** 2018-09-20
- **Who:** NVIDIA
- **Type:** hardware

NVIDIA's first Turing consumer cards, the GeForce RTX 2080 Ti and RTX 2080, were revealed in August. The cards were due on sale September 20, 2018, but AnandTech reported that RTX 2080 Ti general availability was pushed back a week to September 27. Each Turing streaming multiprocessor included tensor cores for deep learning alongside RT cores for ray tracing. The 2080 Ti launched at $999 with 11 GB of GDDR6 memory, bringing tensor-core hardware to a consumer price point for local training and inference.
