# New York City public schools ban ChatGPT on school networks

- **Date:** 2023-01-03
- **Who:** NYC Department of Education
- **Type:** policy

New York City, the largest US school district, blocked students and teachers from using ChatGPT on district networks and devices, citing safety and accuracy concerns. The ban was reversed in May 2023, and it became an early example of schools reacting to generative AI.
