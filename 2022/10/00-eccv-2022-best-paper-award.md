# ECCV 2022 best paper award

- **Date:** 2022-10-00
- **Who:** European Conference on Computer Vision (ECCV) 2022 program committee; winning team from the University of Wisconsin-Madison (X. Zhen, Z. Meng, R. Chakraborty, V. Singh)
- **Type:** other

The ECCV 2022 Best Paper Award went to "On the Versatile Uses of Partial Distance Correlation in Deep Learning." The conference was held in October 2022 in Tel Aviv. The university reported the winning paper was picked from more than 5,000 entries.
