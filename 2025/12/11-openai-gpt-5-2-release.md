# OpenAI releases GPT-5.2

- **Date:** 2025-12-11
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** model

OpenAI released GPT-5.2 in Instant, Thinking and Pro versions, a response to competitive pressure from Google's Gemini 3. OpenAI pitched it at professional knowledge work and coding, and the release was part of a fast cadence of GPT-5.x updates.
