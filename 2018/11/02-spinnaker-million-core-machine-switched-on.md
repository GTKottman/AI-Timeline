# SpiNNaker brain-inspired machine reaches one million cores and is switched on

- **Date:** 2018-11-02
- **Who:** University of Manchester (SpiNNaker project, funded by EPSRC and later the EU Human Brain Project)
- **Type:** hardware

The University of Manchester switched on a SpiNNaker machine with one million processor cores for the first time. SpiNNaker is a neuromorphic computer built from many small chips that exchange spikes, meant to simulate spiking neural networks in real time. The project was described as more than ten years in construction. Its developers said it could model more biological neurons in real time than any other machine, with a longer-term goal of around a billion neurons.
