# ByteDance Seed releases Seed-OSS-36B open-source model

- **Date:** 2025-08-20
- **Who:** ByteDance Seed team
- **Type:** model

ByteDance's Seed team released Seed-OSS-36B, a 36B-parameter open model under Apache 2.0 with a 512K-token context window, trained on 12 trillion tokens. It lets users set a thinking budget to control reasoning length, and comes in base and instruct variants. It mattered as a large, permissively licensed model from one of China's biggest platforms.
