# Meta expands Ray-Ban Meta glasses with Meta AI vision

- **Date:** 2024-04-23
- **Who:** Meta
- **Type:** product

Meta announced new Ray-Ban Meta glasses styles and began a beta rollout of Meta AI with Vision to the glasses in the US and Canada, so the assistant could answer questions about what the wearer was looking at. It was an early consumer deployment of a multimodal assistant on wearable hardware.
