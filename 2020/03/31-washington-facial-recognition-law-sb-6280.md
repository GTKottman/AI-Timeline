# Washington signs SB 6280 facial recognition law

- **Date:** 2020-03-31
- **Who:** Washington State Governor Jay Inslee; Washington State Legislature; ACLU of Washington (critic)
- **Type:** policy

Governor Jay Inslee signed SB 6280, which regulates government use of facial recognition by state and local agencies, with a partial veto. The veto removed Section 10, which would have created a legislative task force on the technology. The law takes effect July 1, 2021. The ACLU of Washington said the regulations were insufficient.
