# Character.ai ends open-ended chat for users under 18

- **Date:** 2025-10-29
- **Who:** Character.ai
- **Type:** product

Character.ai announced it would end open-ended chat for users under 18 by November 25, after lawsuits over teen harm. It was one of the first major chatbot services to restrict minors' open conversations.
