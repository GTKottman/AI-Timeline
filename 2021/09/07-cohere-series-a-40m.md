# Cohere raises $40M Series A led by Index Ventures

- **Date:** 2021-09-07
- **Who:** Cohere; Index Ventures (lead); Radical Ventures; Section 32; individual AI researchers
- **Type:** funding

Toronto NLP startup Cohere announced a US$40 million Series A led by Index Ventures, with Index partner Mike Volpi joining its board. The round took total funding to about US$50 million according to BetaKit. It signalled investor appetite for commercial large language model APIs as a business separate from the big cloud labs.
