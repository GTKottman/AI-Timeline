# Google Minerva: language model for quantitative reasoning

- **Date:** 2022-06-29
- **Who:** Google Research
- **Type:** model

Google presented Minerva, a large language model first trained on general text and then trained further on technical and math content. It solved many quantitative reasoning problems, showing that specialized training data could push language models into science and math.
