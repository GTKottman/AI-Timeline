# Universal Music Group settles with Udio and licenses a new AI music platform

- **Date:** 2025-10-29
- **Who:** Universal Music Group; Udio
- **Type:** other

Universal Music Group settled its copyright suit against Udio and agreed a license for a new licensed AI music creation platform.
