# Safe Superintelligence raises $1 billion at a reported $5 billion valuation

- **Date:** 2024-09-04
- **Who:** Safe Superintelligence Inc.; Sequoia; a16z; DST Global; SV Angel
- **Type:** funding

Sutskever's Safe Superintelligence raised $1 billion from SV Angel, DST Global, Sequoia and Andreessen Horowitz. The company did not disclose its valuation, and Reuters sources put it at $5 billion. The money was for computing power and hiring, and it showed how much investors would back a research-only lab.
