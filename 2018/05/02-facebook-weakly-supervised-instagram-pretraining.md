# Facebook pretrains vision models on billions of Instagram hashtags

- **Date:** 2018-05-02
- **Who:** Facebook AI Research and Applied Machine Learning
- **Type:** paper

The paper "Exploring the Limits of Weakly Supervised Pretraining" (arXiv:1805.00932) trained convolutional networks to predict hashtags on up to 3.5 billion public Instagram images. A model trained on a one-billion-image version reached 85.4% top-1 accuracy on ImageNet, which Engadget reported as beating the previous best of 83.1%. It showed that noisy social-media labels at very large scale can be a practical pretraining source for image recognition.
