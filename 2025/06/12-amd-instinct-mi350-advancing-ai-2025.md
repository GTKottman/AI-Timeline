# AMD unveils Instinct MI350 series at Advancing AI 2025

- **Date:** 2025-06-12
- **Who:** AMD
- **Type:** hardware

At its Advancing AI 2025 event in San Jose, AMD launched the Instinct MI350 series, the MI355X with 288 GB of memory and the MI350X, and previewed the MI400 for its Helios rack. It also released ROCm 7.0 and made the AMD Developer Cloud widely available. AMD's performance multiples are its own claims, compared with Nvidia's previous-generation chips.
