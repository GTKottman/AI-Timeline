# Google AI and NOAA use a CNN to detect humpback whale calls in 15 years of recordings

- **Date:** 2018-10-29
- **Who:** Google AI Perception with NOAA's Pacific Islands Fisheries Science Center
- **Type:** research

Google AI Perception worked with NOAA to detect humpback whale calls in about 15 years of underwater Pacific recordings. The model treats spectrograms as images and uses a ResNet-50 architecture. It identified whale presence in test clips with over 90% precision and 90% recall, and the seasonal patterns it found matched known humpback migration behavior. It was an example of computer vision applied to sound for ocean conservation.
