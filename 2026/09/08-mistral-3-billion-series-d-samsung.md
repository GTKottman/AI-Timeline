# Mistral AI Raises €3 Billion Series D Led by Samsung

- **Date:** 2026-09-08
- **Who:** Mistral AI; led by Samsung Electronics, with Scaleup Europe Fund (EQT), PSG Equity, Advent, BlackRock funds and the Grand Duchy of Luxembourg
- **Type:** funding

Mistral announced a €3 billion Series D at a post-money valuation above €21 billion, which coverage calls the largest equity round ever completed by a European tech company. The money is for data centres and compute, and the CEO said owned compute should roughly double in five years. The round follows a €1.7 billion Series C led by ASML in September 2025 and a $830 million debt raise in March 2026. Samsung's stake (estimated near €1 billion by one outlet) is not confirmed by the companies.
