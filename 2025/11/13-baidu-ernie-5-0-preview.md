# Baidu unveils ERNIE 5.0 preview at Baidu World

- **Date:** 2025-11-13
- **Who:** Baidu
- **Type:** model

Baidu unveiled ERNIE 5.0 at Baidu World 2025 as a natively omni-modal foundation model that models text, images, audio and video together for both understanding and generation. A preview was opened to the public through ERNIE Bot and to enterprises through Baidu's Qianfan cloud platform. Baidu compared it with Gemini, GPT-5 and DeepSeek, aiming to show it remained competitive with the leading US models rather than to claim top scores everywhere.
