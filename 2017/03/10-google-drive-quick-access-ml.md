# Quick Access in Google Drive uses machine learning

- **Date:** 2017-03-10
- **Who:** Google (Drive team; post by Sandeep Tata)
- **Type:** product

Google Research blog post describing Drive's Quick Access, which uses deep neural networks on Drive activity and Calendar meetings to show the documents a user is likely to need on the Drive home screen. Google said it got people to their documents in about half the time of manual browsing or searching. The feature had launched in 2016 and opened to all Drive users on web, Android and iOS.
