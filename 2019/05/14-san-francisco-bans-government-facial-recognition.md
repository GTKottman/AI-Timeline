# San Francisco becomes first US city to ban government facial recognition

- **Date:** 2019-05-14
- **Who:** San Francisco Board of Supervisors (sponsor: Supervisor Aaron Peskin)
- **Type:** policy

San Francisco's Board of Supervisors voted 8-1 to ban police and other city agencies from using facial recognition technology. It was the first such ban by a US city. The ban was part of a Stop Secret Surveillance ordinance that requires departments to get board approval for surveillance tools. It started a national debate on government use of face recognition AI.
