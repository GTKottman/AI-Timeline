# Judge rules Anthropic's training on books is fair use, but piracy is not

- **Date:** 2025-06-23
- **Who:** Judge William Alsup; Anthropic (Bartz v. Anthropic)
- **Type:** policy

Judge William Alsup ruled that training Claude on lawfully acquired books was fair use, but keeping pirated copies in a central library was not. The split ruling was one of the first US court decisions on AI training and copyright. It led to a later settlement of at least $1.5 billion.
