# DreamBooth: personalize diffusion models for a subject

- **Date:** 2022-08-25
- **Who:** Google Research and Boston University
- **Type:** paper

DreamBooth fine-tunes a text-to-image diffusion model on a few photos of one subject, so the model can place that subject in new scenes and styles. It became a common method for personalized image generation.
