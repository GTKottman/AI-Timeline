# OpenAI launches ChatGPT Projects for organising chats and files

- **Date:** 2024-12-13
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** product

ChatGPT Projects, announced on Day 7 of OpenAI's "12 Days" event, lets users group chats, files and custom instructions in one folder. Projects started rolling out during the livestream to Plus, Pro and Team users first, with Enterprise and Edu users promised access in January 2025. Coverage noted that the design resembles Claude's project folders.
