# Nvidia launches Alpamayo open models for autonomous driving at CES

- **Date:** 2026-01-05
- **Who:** Nvidia; Mercedes-Benz named as first adopter
- **Type:** model

At CES 2026 Nvidia introduced the Alpamayo family: open autonomous-driving models, a simulation framework (AlpaSim) and a dataset of more than 1,700 hours of driving. The flagship is a roughly 10B-parameter vision-language-action model that outputs its reasoning along with the driving trajectory. Nvidia positioned it as a large model developers fine-tune rather than one that runs directly in cars, with Mercedes-Benz as the first expected user in the CLA. Outlets differ on the model name (Alpamayo 1 vs. Alpamayo R1).
