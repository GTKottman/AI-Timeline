# Huawei moves up Ascend 960DT training chip and unveils Atlas 960 SuperPoD

- **Date:** 2026-09-17
- **Who:** Huawei (David Wang Tao, rotating chairman)
- **Type:** hardware

At Huawei Connect 2026 in Shanghai, Huawei said its Ascend 960DT training chip is now due in Q1 2027, about three quarters earlier than planned, with the 960PR inference chip listed for Q3 2027 and Ascend 970 and 980 for 2028 and 2029. It also unveiled the Atlas 960 SuperPoD cluster with an upgraded UnifiedBus interconnect, which one report says scales to 4,096 NPUs. Huawei Cloud is to start commercial Ascend 950 cluster service in China on September 30 and go global on November 30. Sources differ on how far the 960DT date moved, and the coming announcements were timed days before a planned Trump-Xi meeting.
