# Google upgrades Sound Search music recognition with on-device technology

- **Date:** 2018-09-14
- **Who:** Google
- **Type:** product

Google upgraded its server-side Sound Search music recognizer with technology from Now Playing, the on-device recognizer launched on Pixel 2 in 2017. To cover a library of tens of millions of songs while staying fast, it used a larger neural network, denser fingerprint embeddings and popularity-weighted matching thresholds. It showed how on-device audio models and cloud-scale matching could be combined.
