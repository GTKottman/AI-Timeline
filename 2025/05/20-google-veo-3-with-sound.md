# Google DeepMind announces Veo 3 video model with synchronized sound

- **Date:** 2025-05-20
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** model

Google DeepMind announced Veo 3 at Google I/O, a video model that generates synchronized dialogue and sound effects along with the picture. Adding native audio was a major step beyond silent AI video clips. It was announced in the same week as AI Mode.
