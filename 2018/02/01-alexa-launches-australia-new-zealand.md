# Amazon Alexa launches in Australia and New Zealand

- **Date:** 2018-02-01
- **Who:** Amazon
- **Type:** product

Amazon launched Alexa in Australia and New Zealand, with the Echo, Echo Dot and Echo Plus shipping in early February 2018. Amazon had announced the launch on January 18. It included an Alexa Australia and New Zealand edition with local knowledge and skills built by local developers. It extended Amazon's international rollout, which had started with Japan in November 2017.
