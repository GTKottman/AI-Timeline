# Trump revokes Biden's 2023 AI executive order

- **Date:** 2025-01-20
- **Who:** President Donald Trump, White House
- **Type:** policy

Executive Order 14148 on January 20, 2025 revoked dozens of Biden-era executive actions, including EO 14110, the 2023 AI executive order. Sources disagree on the date of the follow-on AI order that replaced it (reported as January 23), and agencies' status on the 100-plus actions taken under EO 14110 was left unclear. The revocation came one day before the Stargate announcement, which is already recorded separately in this timeline.
