# Alibaba Qwen releases Qwen2.5-1M long-context models

- **Date:** 2025-01-26
- **Who:** Alibaba Qwen team
- **Type:** model

The Qwen2.5-1M technical report extends context length to 1 million tokens. Alibaba open-weighted Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct-1M and Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct-1M, plus an inference framework with sparse attention and chunked prefill that Alibaba says gives 3x to 7x prefill speedups at 1M tokens. Alibaba reports the 14B model beats GPT-4o-mini on long-context tasks.
