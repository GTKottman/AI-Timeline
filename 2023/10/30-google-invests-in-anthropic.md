# Google to invest up to $2 billion in Anthropic

- **Date:** 2023-10-30
- **Who:** Google (Alphabet); Anthropic
- **Type:** funding

Google agreed to invest up to $2 billion in Anthropic, the maker of the Claude assistant, adding to the $4 billion Amazon committed earlier that year. Reports put Google's stake at about ten percent. The deal was another sign that the big cloud providers were backing rival frontier labs as well as building their own models.
