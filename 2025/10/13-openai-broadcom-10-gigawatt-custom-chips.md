# OpenAI and Broadcom announce 10 GW of custom AI accelerators

- **Date:** 2025-10-13
- **Who:** OpenAI; Broadcom
- **Type:** hardware

OpenAI and Broadcom announced a collaboration to build 10 gigawatts of custom AI accelerators. OpenAI designs the accelerators and systems, and Broadcom develops and deploys them, with racks targeted to begin in the second half of 2026 and finish by the end of 2029. The companies did not disclose financial terms, though the Wall Street Journal described the deal as worth multiple billions of dollars. Some outlets wrote "up to" 10 GW, while OpenAI's own headline uses the plain figure.
