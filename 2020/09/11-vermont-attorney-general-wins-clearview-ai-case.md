# Vermont court lets state lawsuit against Clearview AI proceed

- **Date:** 2020-09-11
- **Who:** Vermont Attorney General (T.J. Donovan), Clearview AI, Vermont Superior Court
- **Type:** policy

A Vermont court denied Clearview AI's motion to dismiss the state's suit over its collection and sale of images of Vermonters, under the Consumer Protection Act and the Data Broker Law. The court rejected Clearview's arguments on the First Amendment, Section 230, vagueness and standing, so the case could proceed. The attorney general's office announced the ruling on September 11, 2020.
