# MiniMax releases M2.5 agent model

- **Date:** 2026-02-13
- **Who:** MiniMax
- **Type:** model

MiniMax released M2.5, a successor in its M2 line aimed at agentic and office work. Reported figures include 80.2% on SWE-Bench Verified and 76.3% on BrowseComp; these are company-reported, and third-party runs with other scaffolds report lower SWE-Bench scores. Open weights were posted on ModelScope.
