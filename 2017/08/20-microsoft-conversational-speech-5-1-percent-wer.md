# Microsoft Reports 5.1% Word Error Rate on Switchboard, Human Parity Claim

- **Date:** 2017-08-20
- **Who:** Microsoft (Xuedong Huang and speech team)
- **Type:** research

Microsoft announced a conversational speech recognition system with a 5.1% word error rate on the NIST 2000 Switchboard evaluation set, which it said matched professional human transcribers. The system used a CNN-BLSTM acoustic model and character-based and dialog-aware LSTM language models for rescoring, and cut the error rate about 12% from the previous year. It mattered because it was a widely cited claim of human parity in speech recognition, though Microsoft cautioned that noisy far-field speech remained hard.
