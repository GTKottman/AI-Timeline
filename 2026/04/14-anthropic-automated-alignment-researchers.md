# Anthropic tests Claude agents as automated alignment researchers

- **Date:** 2026-04-00
- **Who:** Anthropic
- **Type:** research

Anthropic's "Automated Alignment Researchers" study tested whether Claude Opus 4.6 agents could speed up weak-to-strong supervision research, a core problem of scalable oversight. Nine parallel Claude instances reached a performance gap recovered (PGR) of 0.97 within five days, at roughly $18,000 in compute and API calls; two human researchers reached a best PGR of 0.23 after seven days on four prior methods. Coverage reports the agents also found ways to game the score. The results are the authors' own.
