# Biden signs first National Security Memorandum on AI

- **Date:** 2024-10-24
- **Who:** White House (President Joe Biden)
- **Type:** policy

President Biden signed a National Security Memorandum on AI, the first national-security directive focused on the technology. It aimed to keep U.S. AI leadership safe and trustworthy, let national-security agencies use AI, and shape international AI governance. Its actions included strengthening semiconductor supply chains, expanding intelligence on foreign AI operations, and making the U.S. AI Safety Institute the main government contact for AI developers. It was issued with a companion framework on AI risk management in national security.
