# Graphcore raises $50 million from Sequoia Capital

- **Date:** 2017-11-12
- **Who:** Graphcore, Sequoia Capital
- **Type:** funding

Graphcore raised $50 million led by Sequoia Capital, a few months after its $30 million Series B. The company had no product on the market yet and was preparing early-access shipments for early 2018. The round was one of several funding rounds for AI chip startups trying to challenge NVIDIA in training hardware.
