# Nvidia ships GeForce RTX 3080 and RTX 30 series on Ampere

- **Date:** 2020-09-17
- **Who:** Nvidia
- **Type:** hardware

Nvidia's GeForce RTX 3070, RTX 3080 and RTX 3090 went on sale on 17 September 2020 after being unveiled around 1 September. The cards used the Ampere microarchitecture, the same family as the A100 data-center GPU. They put Ampere tensor cores in consumer hands, which made desktop GPUs a practical option for local deep-learning work.
