# Trump executive order 13960 on trustworthy AI in federal government

- **Date:** 2020-12-03
- **Who:** President Donald Trump; US federal agencies
- **Type:** policy

On December 3, 2020, President Trump signed Executive Order 13960, "Promoting the Use of Trustworthy Artificial Intelligence in the Federal Government," published in the Federal Register on December 8, 2020. It sets nine principles federal agencies must follow when they build or use AI: lawfulness, purposefulness, accuracy, safety, understandability, traceability, monitoring, transparency and accountability. Agencies must inventory their AI use cases and report on them. National security systems and AI embedded in commercial products are exempt. It was an early government-wide rulebook for AI use, tying public trust to how the state deploys AI.
