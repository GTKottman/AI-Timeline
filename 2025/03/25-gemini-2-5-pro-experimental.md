# Google releases Gemini 2.5 Pro Experimental, its first 2.5-series model

- **Date:** 2025-03-25
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** model

Google released Gemini 2.5 Pro Experimental, a thinking model for complex tasks, in Google AI Studio and, for Gemini Advanced subscribers, in the Gemini app. Google said it ranked first on the LMArena human-preference leaderboard by a wide margin and led math and science benchmarks such as GPQA and AIME 2025.
