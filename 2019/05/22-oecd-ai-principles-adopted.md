# OECD adopts Principles on Artificial Intelligence

- **Date:** 2019-05-22
- **Who:** OECD Council; 42 adhering countries
- **Type:** policy

The OECD Council adopted the first intergovernmental AI principles at its Ministerial Council Meeting. They set five values-based principles, including inclusive growth, human-centred values, transparency, robustness and accountability, plus five policy recommendations. The principles are non-binding, and 42 countries signed on, including non-OECD partners. The G20 later drew on them.
