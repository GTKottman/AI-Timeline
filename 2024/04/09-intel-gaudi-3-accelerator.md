# Intel introduces Gaudi 3 AI accelerator

- **Date:** 2024-04-09
- **Who:** Intel
- **Type:** hardware

At Intel Vision 2024, Intel introduced Gaudi 3, its AI accelerator for training and inference, positioned against Nvidia's H100. It is built from two compute dies on TSMC's 5-nm process and was aimed at enterprise AI clusters, showing Intel's push back into data-center AI accelerators.
