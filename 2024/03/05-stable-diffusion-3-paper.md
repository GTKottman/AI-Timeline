# Stability AI publishes Stable Diffusion 3 paper on arXiv

- **Date:** 2024-03-05
- **Who:** Stability AI
- **Type:** paper

The paper "Scaling Rectified Flow Transformers for High-Resolution Image Synthesis" described Stable Diffusion 3, built on a multimodal diffusion transformer with rectified flow. It documented the architecture that later Stable Diffusion models build on.
