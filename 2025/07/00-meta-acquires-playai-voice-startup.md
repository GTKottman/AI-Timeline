# Meta acquires voice AI startup PlayAI

- **Date:** 2025-07-00
- **Who:** Meta; PlayAI
- **Type:** acquisition

Meta acquired PlayAI, a voice AI startup known for the PlayDialog multi-turn text-to-speech model, and the whole team joined Meta under Johan Schalkwyk. An internal memo tied the work to AI Characters, Meta AI, wearables and audio creation. Terms were not disclosed. It mattered as part of Meta's push on voice in its Superintelligence Labs build-out.

Exact day not confirmed from the sources (Bloomberg first reported; coverage from July 2025).
