# NeurIPS 2024 announces best paper awards

- **Date:** 2024-12-10
- **Who:** NeurIPS conference (Neural Information Processing Systems)
- **Type:** competition

The NeurIPS 2024 organisers announced their best paper awards. The main-track best paper went to "Visual Autoregressive Modeling: Scalable Image Generation via Next-Scale Prediction" by Keyu Tian and colleagues, which predicts images from coarse to fine scale and competes with diffusion models. A Datasets and Benchmarks track award also went to a benchmark built from data gathered in 75 countries. Two other main-track papers were named runners-up.
