# Databricks acquires Einblick

- **Date:** 2024-01-30
- **Who:** Databricks, Einblick (founded by researchers from MIT and Brown)
- **Type:** acquisition

Databricks acquired the team behind Einblick, a natural-language, AI-native data science notebook, for an undisclosed sum. Databricks said it would bring Einblick's AI-native approach into its platform and tie it to its Unity Catalog. It was reported as Databricks' fourth acquisition in about 12 months.
