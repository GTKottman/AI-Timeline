# Kuaishou releases Kling 1.6 video model

- **Date:** 2024-12-19
- **Who:** Kuaishou (Kling AI)
- **Type:** model

Kuaishou announced Kling 1.6, an AI video model, with a Standard tier at up to 720p and a Pro tier at up to 1080p. The Pro tier adds first-and-last-frame conditioning for 5-second clips. Kuaishou reports a 195% overall improvement rate over Kling 1.5 in its own internal evaluation.
