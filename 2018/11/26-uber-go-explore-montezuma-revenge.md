# Uber AI Labs' Go-Explore solves Montezuma's Revenge without human demonstrations

- **Date:** 2018-11-26
- **Who:** Uber AI Labs (Adrien Ecoffet, Joost Huizinga, Joel Lehman, Kenneth Stanley, Jeff Clune)
- **Type:** research

Uber AI Labs published Go-Explore, an algorithm for hard-exploration games. It saves promising states it has already reached, returns to them, explores further, and then turns the best routes into a robust policy with imitation learning. Without domain knowledge it scored over 35,000 points on Montezuma's Revenge, more than three times the prior state of the art, and it was the first to score above zero on Pitfall. The blog announced the result under the headline "Montezuma's Revenge Solved by Go-Explore." The arXiv paper followed in January 2019.
