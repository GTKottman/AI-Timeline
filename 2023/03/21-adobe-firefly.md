# Adobe unveils Firefly, its generative AI model family for creative tools

- **Date:** 2023-03-21
- **Who:** Adobe
- **Type:** product

Adobe introduced Firefly, a family of creative generative AI models first focused on generating images and text effects. Adobe said Firefly would be built into its Creative Cloud, Document Cloud, Experience Cloud and Adobe Express apps, and that its first model was designed for commercial use.
