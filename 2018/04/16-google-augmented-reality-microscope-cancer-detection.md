# Google shows an augmented reality microscope that flags cancer in real time

- **Date:** 2018-04-16
- **Who:** Google Research (with pathology collaborators)
- **Type:** research

Google presented a prototype Augmented Reality Microscope that projects machine learning results into the field of view of an ordinary light microscope. It can be retrofitted onto existing microscopes using low-cost parts, without needing digitized whole-slide images. Two models ran on it: one detected breast cancer metastases in lymph node tissue, and one detected prostate cancer, outlining detected tumor regions in green. Google first showed the work at the AACR meeting, and it was later published in Nature Medicine.
