# Apple Intelligence ships with iOS 18.1 and macOS 15.1

- **Date:** 2024-10-28
- **Who:** Apple
- **Type:** product

iOS 18.1 and macOS 15.1 shipped with Apple Intelligence fully available on iPhone, iPad and Mac, after a developer beta from late July. It was the first public release of Apple's on-device and private-cloud generative features.
