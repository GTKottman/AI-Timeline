# Cerebras breaks ground on Condor Galaxy 3 AI supercomputer

- **Date:** 2024-03-13
- **Who:** Cerebras Systems; G42
- **Type:** hardware

Cerebras started building Condor Galaxy 3, an AI supercomputer built from its wafer-scale chips and rated at up to 8 exaFLOPs. It was part of a multi-site cluster for training large models and a sign that wafer-scale hardware was being scaled into real data centres.
