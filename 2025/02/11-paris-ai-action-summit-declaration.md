# Paris AI Action Summit declaration signed by 62 countries

- **Date:** 2025-02-11
- **Who:** France (summit host); 62 countries, the African Union Commission and the EU
- **Type:** policy

The Paris AI Action Summit ended with a declaration on inclusive and sustainable AI signed by 62 countries, the African Union Commission and the EU. The United States and the United Kingdom declined to sign. France announced about 109 billion euros of private AI investment at the same event.
