# OpenAI shuts down the Sora video app

- **Date:** 2026-04-26
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** product

OpenAI discontinued the Sora video app, and later ended developer access to the Sora API in September. It was a rare shutdown of a flagship generative product.
