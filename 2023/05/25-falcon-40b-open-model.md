# TII releases Falcon 40B open-source model

- **Date:** 2023-05-25
- **Who:** Technology Innovation Institute (Abu Dhabi)
- **Type:** model

TII released Falcon 40B, a 40-billion-parameter model trained on one trillion tokens of RefinedWeb data, with weights distributed as an open-source package. TII first described it as open for research and commercial use, though sources disagree on the licence, and reports say TII moved to Apache 2.0 in June 2023. It mattered as one of the largest openly released models of the period and a sign that Gulf states were entering the open model race.
