# xAI closes $6 billion Series C funding

- **Date:** 2024-12-23
- **Who:** xAI; investors including a16z, BlackRock, Fidelity, Kingdom Holding, Lightspeed, MGX, Morgan Stanley, QIA, Sequoia, Valor and Vy Capital, plus NVIDIA and AMD
- **Type:** funding

xAI announced it had closed a $6 billion Series C to speed up its infrastructure and product work. Strategic investors NVIDIA and AMD took part alongside financial backers. Secondary reports put the valuation at about $45 billion, although the figure varied across reports. According to the Financial Times, only investors who had backed the previous round could take part.
