# Perplexity adds Shopping features to AI search

- **Date:** 2024-11-18
- **Who:** Perplexity AI
- **Type:** product

Perplexity added Shopping features to its AI search, with product cards, a "Snap to Shop" visual search tool and a "Buy with Pro" option for US Pro subscribers. Coverage named Jeff Bezos and Nvidia among its backers. It pushed AI search into product discovery and commerce, where incumbents like Google and Amazon compete directly.
