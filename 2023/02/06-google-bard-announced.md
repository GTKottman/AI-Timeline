# Google announces Bard, a conversational AI service built on LaMDA

- **Date:** 2023-02-06
- **Who:** Google / Sundar Pichai
- **Type:** product

Google CEO Sundar Pichai announced Bard, an experimental chatbot powered by the LaMDA dialogue model, opened first to trusted testers. It was Google's direct answer to ChatGPT and was rolled out with limited early access.
