# Nvidia passes $1 trillion market value

- **Date:** 2023-05-30
- **Who:** Nvidia
- **Type:** other

Nvidia's market capitalization crossed $1 trillion on May 30, 2023, making it the first chipmaker and the first US chipmaker to reach that value. The rise followed an earnings report with a revenue forecast more than 50 percent above analyst expectations, driven by demand for its AI accelerators. CNN tied the rise directly to booming demand for its AI chips.
