# Baidu launches Brain 3.0 at Baidu Create 2018

- **Date:** 2018-07-04
- **Who:** Baidu
- **Type:** product

At Baidu Create in Beijing (July 4-5, 2018) Baidu launched Baidu Brain 3.0, a set of more than 110 AI capabilities built around multimodal deep semantic understanding for conversational AI in Chinese. Baidu also said it would develop a high-performance AI chip for cloud to edge use, covering data centers, public clouds and autonomous vehicles, and it showed an L4 autonomous bus. Over 7,300 developers and partners attended. It mattered as a full-stack AI platform push from one of China's largest AI companies.
