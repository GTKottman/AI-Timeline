# Prometheus raises $12 billion Series B at $41 billion valuation

- **Date:** 2026-06-11
- **Who:** Prometheus (co-led by Jeff Bezos and Vik Bajaj); investors include JPMorgan, BlackRock, Goldman Sachs, DST Global, Arch Venture Partners
- **Type:** funding

Physical-AI startup Prometheus announced a $12 billion Series B at a valuation of about $41 billion on June 11, 2026. Bezos calls the goal an "artificial general engineer" for designing and manufacturing physical products, and says the company is not building robots. The money is mainly for compute, and the company has about 150 employees. Most details come from company statements and a CNBC interview with Bezos, so the valuation is company-reported.
