# Anthropic suspends Claude Fable 5 access after US security concerns

- **Date:** 2026-06-12
- **Who:** Anthropic; US government
- **Type:** policy

Anthropic suspended access to Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5 after US authorities raised national-security concerns. Access was restored in early July after a partial reversal in late June, showing how governments now gate frontier model access.
