# Amazon unveils Nova foundation models at re:Invent

- **Date:** 2024-12-03
- **Who:** Amazon / AWS
- **Type:** model

At AWS re:Invent, Amazon launched its first in-house family of foundation models, Nova: Micro (text), Lite and Pro (multimodal), and Premier (the most capable, due later), plus Nova Canvas for images and Nova Reel for video. The models were offered through Amazon Bedrock and replaced the older Titan lineup. Amazon claimed Lite and Pro matched or beat GPT-4o mini and GPT-4o on most of the benchmarks it chose.
