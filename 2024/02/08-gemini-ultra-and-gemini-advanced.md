# Google releases Gemini 1.0 Ultra and launches Gemini Advanced

- **Date:** 2024-02-08
- **Who:** Google; Google DeepMind
- **Type:** model

Gemini 1.0 Ultra, Google's largest Gemini model, became available to users through a paid Gemini Advanced tier in the Google One AI Premium plan. Bard and Duet AI were folded under the Gemini brand. It was Google's first paid chatbot subscription aimed directly at matching ChatGPT Plus.
