# Google Gemini Diffusion text and code model

- **Date:** 2025-05-20
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** model

Google DeepMind showed Gemini Diffusion at Google I/O, an experimental model that writes text and code by refining noise into output, the way its image and video models work, instead of predicting one token at a time. Its demo was reported at about 1,500 to 2,000 tokens per second, with coding performance comparable to Gemini 2.0 Flash-Lite. Access was limited to a waitlist, and outside researchers noted that few details were published.
