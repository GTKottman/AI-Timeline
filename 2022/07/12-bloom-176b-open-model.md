# BLOOM: 176B open-access multilingual language model

- **Date:** 2022-07-12
- **Who:** BigScience workshop (coordinated by Hugging Face)
- **Type:** model

The BigScience collaboration released BLOOM, a 176-billion-parameter open-access language model trained on the Jean Zay supercomputer in France. It covers 46 natural languages and 13 programming languages and was released with its training data documentation, making a model of this size available to the public.
