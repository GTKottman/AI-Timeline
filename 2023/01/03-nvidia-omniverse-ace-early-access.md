# NVIDIA opens Omniverse ACE early access for AI avatars

- **Date:** 2023-01-03
- **Who:** NVIDIA (partner example: Ready Player Me)
- **Type:** product

Announced around CES 2023, NVIDIA opened early access to Omniverse Avatar Cloud Engine (ACE), a suite of cloud-native AI microservices for building avatars and virtual assistants. Its NVIDIA Tokkio reference application was expanding, with early partners building customer-service avatars for telecom, banking and similar industries. The launch matters because it packaged speech, animation and conversational AI as deployable services for interactive digital humans, not only as research demos.
