# AMD completes acquisition of Xilinx

- **Date:** 2022-02-14
- **Who:** AMD / Xilinx
- **Type:** acquisition

AMD closed its roughly all-stock purchase of Xilinx, the maker of FPGAs and adaptive chips. The combination put Xilinx's programmable accelerator hardware, used for AI inference at the edge and in data centers, inside AMD's CPU and GPU business.
