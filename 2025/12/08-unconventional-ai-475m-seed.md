# Unconventional AI raises $475M seed at $4.5B valuation

- **Date:** 2025-12-08
- **Who:** Unconventional AI (founded by Naveen Rao, former Databricks AI head); investors a16z and Lightspeed led, with Lux, DCVC and others
- **Type:** funding

Unconventional AI, a new startup building energy-efficient computing hardware for AI, announced a $475 million seed round at a $4.5 billion valuation. Bloomberg described the company as two months old. Rao said this is the first portion of a financing that could reach $1 billion. DatacenterDynamics reports the company is building an analog chip fabbed in silicon, a bet that AI compute needs a different hardware design than GPUs.
