# Google launches Live Transcribe, free real-time captions for Android

- **Date:** 2019-02-04
- **Who:** Google
- **Type:** product

Google announced Live Transcribe, a free Android service that captions conversations in real time in more than 70 languages. It can be started from the accessibility icon. To save data, an on-device speech detector manages when the cloud speech recognizer is connected, and it pairs the captions with a loudness indicator. It put speech recognition into a free accessibility tool for deaf and hard-of-hearing users.
