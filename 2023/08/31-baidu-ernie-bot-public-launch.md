# Baidu opens ERNIE Bot to the general public in China

- **Date:** 2023-08-31
- **Who:** Baidu
- **Type:** product

Baidu opened its ERNIE Bot chatbot to the general public in China after regulatory approval under the new generative-AI measures that took effect August 15. It had been in limited access since March. Baidu said the app reached the top of Apple's free charts in China within 12 hours, and it argued that mass use would give valuable human feedback. It was among the first Chinese generative-AI services cleared for public release.
