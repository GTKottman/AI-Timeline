# Together releases GPT-JT open source model

- **Date:** 2022-11-29
- **Who:** Together (togethercomputer)
- **Type:** model

Together released GPT-JT-6B as open source, a fine-tune of EleutherAI's GPT-J-6B on 3.53 billion tokens. The team said it was trained with a decentralized algorithm over 1 Gbps links, cutting communication demand from 633 TB to 12.7 TB per machine. On the RAFT benchmark it came close to much larger models such as InstructGPT davinci v2, which made it a proof point for low-cost, distributed model training.
