# China issues Interim Measures for Generative AI Services

- **Date:** 2023-07-13
- **Who:** Cyberspace Administration of China and six other regulators
- **Type:** policy

China's Cyberspace Administration and six other national regulators issued Interim Measures regulating public-facing generative AI services, requiring security assessments and content controls. The rules took effect on August 15, 2023 and were among the first national generative AI regulations.
