# Huawei unveils Ascend 910 AI training chip and MindSpore framework

- **Date:** 2019-08-23
- **Who:** Huawei
- **Type:** hardware

Huawei launched the Ascend 910, its flagship AI training processor, rated at 256 TFLOPS in FP16 and 512 TOPS in INT8. It was launched together with MindSpore, Huawei's AI computing framework, and Huawei said the pairing trained models about twice as fast as TensorFlow-based setups (a claim not independently verified). It showed China building its own AI accelerator stack.
