# Meta plans to start production of its Iris AI chip in September

- **Date:** 2026-07-09
- **Who:** Meta
- **Type:** hardware

A Reuters report, citing an internal Meta memo, said Meta plans to start manufacturing its custom AI chip, code-named Iris, from September 2026. The chip is meant to supplement the GPUs Meta buys from Nvidia and AMD, not replace them.
