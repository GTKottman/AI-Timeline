# NVIDIA launches TITAN RTX desktop GPU at NeurIPS 2018

- **Date:** 2018-12-03
- **Who:** NVIDIA
- **Type:** hardware

NVIDIA unveiled the TITAN RTX, a Turing-architecture desktop GPU, at NeurIPS in Montreal. It was rated at 130 teraflops of deep learning performance, with 24GB of GDDR6 memory, 576 Tensor Cores and NVLink for pairing two cards, priced at US$2,499. It was pitched at AI researchers and data scientists as a desktop alternative to data-center accelerators, continuing NVIDIA's habit of launching TITAN cards at the same conference.
