# Nvidia unveils A100 80GB GPU and DGX Station A100

- **Date:** 2020-11-16
- **Who:** Nvidia
- **Type:** hardware

At SC20, Nvidia launched the A100 80GB, doubling the memory of the 40GB A100 to 80GB of HBM2e with over 2 TB/s of bandwidth. The same announcement brought DGX A100 and DGX Station A100 systems that use it, and Dell and HPE planned HGX A100 systems for 2021. The extra memory let larger models and datasets fit on one GPU, which mattered for the large-model training of that period.
