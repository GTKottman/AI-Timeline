# "In-Datacenter Performance Analysis of a Tensor Processing Unit" posted to arXiv

- **Date:** 2017-04-16
- **Who:** Google (Jouppi et al.)
- **Type:** paper

Google posted a paper analyzing its first-generation TPU in its own datacenters, against contemporary CPUs and GPUs on production neural network inference workloads. The TPU was reported at about 15x to 30x the speed and 30x to 80x the TOPS per Watt of those alternatives. The paper was presented at ISCA 2017.
