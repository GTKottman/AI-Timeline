# China's regulator publishes draft generative AI measures

- **Date:** 2023-04-11
- **Who:** Cyberspace Administration of China (CAC)
- **Type:** policy

The CAC released draft rules for generative AI services offered to the public in mainland China. The draft required providers to verify users' real identities, address data protection and training-data quality, and file algorithms and security assessments with the regulator. Public comment closed May 10, 2023. It mattered as one of the first national rulebooks aimed specifically at chatbots and image generators rather than only deepfakes.
