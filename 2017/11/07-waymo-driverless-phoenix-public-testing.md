# Waymo runs fully driverless cars in Phoenix and opens early rides to the public

- **Date:** 2017-11-07
- **Who:** Waymo (Alphabet)
- **Type:** other

Waymo said a subset of its Phoenix-area fleet had been operating in fully autonomous mode with no safety driver since mid-October, and that members of the public signed up for early rides in a suburb of about 100 square miles. It was one of the first public deployments of a self-driving taxi service without a human driver in the vehicle. Early riders were still in cars with safety operators in some reports, so the milestone was a public service step rather than a general release.
