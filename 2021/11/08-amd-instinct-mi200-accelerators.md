# AMD launches Instinct MI200 accelerators for exascale systems

- **Date:** 2021-11-08
- **Who:** AMD (CDNA 2 architecture); MI250X used in the Frontier supercomputer
- **Type:** hardware

AMD announced the Instinct MI200 series on November 8, 2021, built on the CDNA 2 architecture. It was described as the company's first exascale-class GPU family and the first multi-die GPU for HPC and AI, with up to 128 GB of HBM2e memory. The flagship MI250X was tied to the Frontier supercomputer, and HPE offered it in its Cray EX systems. AMD's performance claims were its own.
