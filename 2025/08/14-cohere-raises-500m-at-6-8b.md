# Cohere raises $500 million at $6.8 billion valuation

- **Date:** 2025-08-14
- **Who:** Cohere; Radical Ventures and Inovia Capital (leads); AMD Ventures, Nvidia, Salesforce Ventures, PSP Investments
- **Type:** funding

Cohere raised $500 million at a $6.8 billion valuation, up from $5.5 billion a year earlier, led by Radical Ventures and Inovia Capital with participation from AMD Ventures, Nvidia and Salesforce Ventures. The round also brought hires of former Meta and Uber executives. It mattered as a large raise for an enterprise-focused model company competing with OpenAI and Anthropic.
