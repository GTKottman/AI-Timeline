# Meta releases OPT-175B openly to researchers

- **Date:** 2022-05-03
- **Who:** Meta AI
- **Type:** model

Meta AI released OPT-175B, a 175-billion-parameter language model with its pretrained weights, training code and smaller baseline models, under a noncommercial research license. Giving researchers access to a model of GPT-3 size was a rare step, and it made large-model study possible outside a few companies.
