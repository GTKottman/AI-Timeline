# OpenAI publishes GLIDE text-guided diffusion model

- **Date:** 2021-12-20
- **Who:** OpenAI (Nichol, Dhariwal, Ramesh, Shyam, Mishkin, McGrew, Sutskever, Chen)
- **Type:** model

OpenAI posted the GLIDE paper (arXiv 2112.10741) on December 20, 2021. Its 3.5-billion-parameter text-conditional diffusion model with classifier-free guidance was preferred by human judges over DALL-E: 87% of the time for photorealism and 69% for caption similarity, even though DALL-E used CLIP reranking. The model could also be fine-tuned for text-driven inpainting and editing, and OpenAI released a smaller model trained on filtered data with code and weights on GitHub. The paper was later published at ICML 2022.
