# Databricks raises over $4B Series L at $134B valuation

- **Date:** 2025-12-16
- **Who:** Databricks; led by Insight Partners, Fidelity and J.P. Morgan Asset Management, with a16z and others
- **Type:** funding

Databricks announced a Series L of more than $4 billion at a $134 billion valuation, up about 34% from its prior mark. The company reported a revenue run-rate above $4.8 billion with growth over 55%. Funds are earmarked for building AI apps and agents, product work, employee liquidity and AI acquisitions, making it one of the largest private AI-data-platform rounds of the year.
