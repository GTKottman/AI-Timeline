# ByteDance launches Doubao LLM family and cuts API prices

- **Date:** 2024-05-15
- **Who:** ByteDance (Volcano Engine)
- **Type:** product

ByteDance launched the Doubao large-model family at a Beijing event, with at least eight versions from the top-tier Doubao Pro to the entry-level Doubao Lite, and priced the API far below rivals. The cuts started a price war across China's model market, pushing competitors such as Alibaba Cloud to lower their own rates. It showed that low-cost serving at scale was becoming a competitive weapon.
