# Waymo launches early rider program in Phoenix area

- **Date:** 2017-04-00
- **Who:** Waymo (Alphabet)
- **Type:** product

Waymo, Alphabet's self-driving car unit, opened an early rider program for residents of Tempe, Mesa, Gilbert and Chandler in the Phoenix area, letting the public ride in its autonomous vehicles with a test driver aboard. Waymo said it would add 500 Chrysler Pacifica vans to the fleet. The program gave the company a large real-world dataset of driving sensor data for training its perception and planning models.
