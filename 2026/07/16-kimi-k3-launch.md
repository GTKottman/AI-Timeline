# Moonshot launches Kimi K3, a 2.8-trillion-parameter model

- **Date:** 2026-07-16
- **Who:** Moonshot AI
- **Type:** model

Moonshot launched Kimi K3 on July 16, 2026 as a 2.8-trillion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with native vision and a 1-million-token context, live in Kimi products and the API. Moonshot had promised weights and a technical report for July 27; sources report the Hugging Face release on July 27 (one says July 26). One launch summary lists $3 per million uncached input tokens and $15 per million output tokens. Benchmarks are Moonshot's own, and Moonshot says K3 still trails Claude Fable 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol overall.
