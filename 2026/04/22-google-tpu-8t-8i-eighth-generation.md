# Google unveils eighth-generation TPUs, split into 8t and 8i

- **Date:** 2026-04-22
- **Who:** Google Cloud, Google DeepMind
- **Type:** hardware

At Cloud Next 2026 Google announced TPU 8t for training and TPU 8i for inference, splitting its accelerator line by workload. TPU 8t pods scale to 9,600 chips and 121 exaflops, and Google claims about 2.8x better price-performance; TPU 8i is aimed at agent inference with more on-chip SRAM. Google positions the chips as an alternative to Nvidia, alongside new Nvidia Vera Rubin instances. Availability dates conflict across sources.
