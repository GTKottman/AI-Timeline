# Google DeepMind unveils AlphaProteo for protein binder design

- **Date:** 2024-09-06
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** research

AlphaProteo is a DeepMind model that designs new protein binders for a given target structure and chosen binding sites. DeepMind reported it was the first AI tool to design a successful VEGF-A binder and claimed higher experimental success rates and binding affinities of three to 300 times better than existing methods on the seven targets tested. The team said it would share the work cautiously and with biosecurity experts. These figures are DeepMind's own claims.
