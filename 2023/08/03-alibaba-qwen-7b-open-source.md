# Alibaba open-sources Qwen-7B and Qwen-7B-Chat

- **Date:** 2023-08-03
- **Who:** Alibaba Cloud (Tongyi Qianwen)
- **Type:** model

Alibaba open-sourced Qwen-7B, a 7-billion-parameter model pretrained on about 2.2 trillion tokens of Chinese and English text and code, plus a chat-tuned Qwen-7B-Chat. The license is free for most commercial use, with companies above 100 million monthly active users needing permission. Alibaba's own figures put it at the top of Chinese benchmarks in its size class. The date is the coverage date seen; the exact release day was not checked.
