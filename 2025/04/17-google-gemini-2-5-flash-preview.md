# Google releases Gemini 2.5 Flash in preview

- **Date:** 2025-04-17
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** model

Google released a preview of Gemini 2.5 Flash, a faster hybrid-reasoning model with a 1 million token context window and multimodal input. One source gives April 17 as the preview date. A LongTermWiki entry instead says it was previewed on May 20 at Google I/O, so the exact first-preview date is not settled by the sources checked. Flash later reached general availability with Gemini 2.5 Pro on June 17, 2025.
