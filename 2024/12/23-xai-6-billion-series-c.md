# xAI closes $6 billion Series C funding

- **Date:** 2024-12-23
- **Who:** xAI; investors including a16z, BlackRock, Fidelity, Kingdom Holding, Morgan Stanley and Sequoia, plus NVIDIA and AMD
- **Type:** funding

xAI announced it had closed a $6 billion Series C to speed up its infrastructure and product work. NVIDIA and AMD took part as strategic investors alongside financial backers. Reports put the valuation between about $40 billion and $50 billion, with $45 billion tied to a public filing. The company said the money would go toward infrastructure and new products.
