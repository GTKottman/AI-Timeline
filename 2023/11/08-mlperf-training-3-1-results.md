# MLCommons publishes MLPerf Training v3.1 results

- **Date:** 2023-11-08
- **Who:** MLCommons; NVIDIA; Intel
- **Type:** competition

MLCommons released MLPerf Training v3.1 results on November 8, 2023. NVIDIA's Eos system, built from 10,752 H100 GPUs, trained GPT-3 (175B) in 3.9 minutes and remained the fastest. Intel reported a 2x gain for Gaudi2 from FP8 software, finishing the GPT-3 run in 153.58 minutes on 384 accelerators, and a 20.2-minute Stable Diffusion run on 64 accelerators. Intel's comparison to H100 used its own normalized numbers.
