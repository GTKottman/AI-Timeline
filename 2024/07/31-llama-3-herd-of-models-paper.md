# "The Llama 3 Herd of Models" paper posted to arXiv

- **Date:** 2024-07-31
- **Who:** Meta AI
- **Type:** paper

Meta published its technical report describing data, architecture, training and evaluation for the Llama 3 model family, including the 405B model. It gave the open community the most detailed account yet of how a frontier-scale open model was trained.
