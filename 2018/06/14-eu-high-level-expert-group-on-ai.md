# European Commission names High-Level Expert Group on AI

- **Date:** 2018-06-14
- **Who:** European Commission; members from universities and companies including Google, IBM, SAP, Nokia Bell Labs, Bosch and BMW
- **Type:** policy

The European Commission appointed its High-Level Expert Group on Artificial Intelligence and launched the European AI Alliance. The group was picked from nearly 500 applications, and it was tasked with recommendations on AI policy, ethical, legal and socio-economic questions, and drafting AI ethics guidelines. Its first meeting was on June 27, 2018. The date is from the Commission's news item; one repost is dated June 25. It mattered as the start of the EU's official AI ethics guidance work.
