# European Commission proposes AI Liability Directive

- **Date:** 2022-09-28
- **Who:** European Commission
- **Type:** policy

The European Commission published its proposal for an AI Liability Directive, published alongside a revised Product Liability Directive. The proposal sets uniform rules for fault-based civil claims for damage caused by AI systems, lets courts order disclosure of evidence about high-risk AI systems, and creates a rebuttable presumption of causality. It was designed to complement the AI Act, which was still in negotiation.
