# Beijing Academy of AI unveils Wu Dao 1.0

- **Date:** 2021-03-23
- **Who:** Beijing Academy of Artificial Intelligence (BAAI); led by Tang Jie (Tsinghua University)
- **Type:** model

BAAI introduced Wu Dao 1.0, presented as China's first homegrown super-scale intelligent model system, built by more than 100 researchers from several Chinese universities and institutes. It included four models, among them Wen Yuan, described as China's largest pretraining language model at the time. Coverage framed it as China's answer to GPT-3. Wu Dao 2.0, with 1.75 trillion parameters, followed in June 2021.
