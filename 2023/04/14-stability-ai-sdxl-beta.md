# Stability AI releases Stable Diffusion XL beta

- **Date:** 2023-04-14
- **Who:** Stability AI
- **Type:** model

Stability AI made Stable Diffusion XL available in beta through its API and DreamStudio. The model had about 2.3 billion parameters, against roughly 900 million for earlier versions, and the company said it produced more detailed, photorealistic images than Stable Diffusion 2.1, and added image-to-image, inpainting and outpainting. Access was limited to API and DreamStudio customers while Stability gathered feedback, with an open-source release planned. It was the forerunner of the SDXL 1.0 open release in July.
