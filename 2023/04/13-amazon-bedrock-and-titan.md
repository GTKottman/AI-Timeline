# Amazon launches Bedrock and Titan foundation models

- **Date:** 2023-04-13
- **Who:** Amazon Web Services
- **Type:** product

AWS announced Amazon Bedrock, a serverless service for building generative AI apps through an API, launched in limited preview. Bedrock offered models from AI21 Labs, Anthropic and Stability AI alongside AWS's own Titan family, which included a text-generation LLM and an embeddings model. It mattered as Amazon's entry into hosted foundation models and a multi-vendor model marketplace on a major cloud.
