# Microsoft releases VALL-E neural codec language model for zero-shot TTS

- **Date:** 2023-01-05
- **Who:** Microsoft Research
- **Type:** paper

Microsoft's paper 'Neural Codec Language Models are Zero-Shot Text to Speech Synthesizers' (arXiv 2301.02111) treated speech synthesis as language modeling over neural codec tokens. It cloned a voice from a three-second sample and scaled training to about 60K hours of speech.
