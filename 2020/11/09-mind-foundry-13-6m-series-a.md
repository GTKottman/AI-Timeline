# Mind Foundry raises $13.6M Series A for Oxford machine learning software

- **Date:** 2020-11-09
- **Who:** Mind Foundry (Oxford University spinout); led by Aioi Nissay Dowa Insurance, with the University of Oxford, Oxford Sciences Innovation, Parkwalk Advisors and Oxford Technology and Innovations EIS Fund
- **Type:** funding

Mind Foundry, a machine learning software spinout of the University of Oxford, raised a $13.6M Series A on November 9, 2020. It sells ML software for data analytics to enterprises. The round was led by Aioi Nissay Dowa Insurance (ANDI) of MS&AD Insurance Group, whose UK subsidiary Insure The Box had first run a proof of concept with the platform.
