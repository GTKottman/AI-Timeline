# Black Knight acquires HeavyWater, an AI mortgage-document startup

- **Date:** 2018-06-04
- **Who:** Black Knight, Inc. (NYSE: BKI); HeavyWater (Philadelphia)
- **Type:** acquisition

Mortgage services firm Black Knight announced on June 4, 2018 that it had acquired HeavyWater, a Philadelphia-based provider of AI and machine learning to financial services. Its product, AIVA, reads and interprets financial documents to help lenders verify income, assets and insurance coverage, tasks that are traditionally manual. Black Knight's 10-K says the deal closed on May 31, 2018, and it planned to fold AIVA into its origination products. Terms were not in the coverage. It mattered as an example of AI document automation being bought into a large regulated industry rather than built in-house.
