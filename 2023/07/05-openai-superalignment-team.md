# OpenAI launches Superalignment team

- **Date:** 2023-07-05
- **Who:** OpenAI (Ilya Sutskever, Jan Leike)
- **Type:** research

OpenAI announced a Superalignment team co-led by Ilya Sutskever and Jan Leike, and committed 20 percent of its secured compute to solving the problem of aligning superintelligent AI within four years. The team was to build a roughly human-level automated alignment researcher and work on scalable training, validation of alignment models, and stress testing. The post argued that current methods such as RLHF rely on human supervision that may fail once AI exceeds human intelligence.
