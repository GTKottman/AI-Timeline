# Databricks to acquire MosaicML for about $1.3 billion

- **Date:** 2023-06-26
- **Who:** Databricks; MosaicML
- **Type:** acquisition

Databricks signed a definitive agreement to acquire MosaicML, the generative-AI platform behind the open-source MPT models, for about $1.3 billion including retention packages. The deal brought a large-model training team and MPT-30B into Databricks' data platform. It was one of the first big acquisitions of an LLM-training startup by a data-infrastructure company.
