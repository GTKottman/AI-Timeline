# DeepSeek releases R1-Lite-Preview, a visible-reasoning model

- **Date:** 2024-11-20
- **Who:** DeepSeek
- **Type:** model

DeepSeek released R1-Lite-Preview, its first reasoning model, through its chat site with a "Deep Think" option that shows the model's chain of thought as it works. DeepSeek said it reached o1-preview-level performance on AIME and MATH, a company-reported result that secondary coverage reported as beating o1-preview on AIME. The launch had no model card or detailed architecture at first, and open-source models and an API were promised later.
