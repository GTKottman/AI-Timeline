# Google unveils second-generation TPU as Cloud TPU

- **Date:** 2017-05-17
- **Who:** Google
- **Type:** hardware

At Google I/O, Google announced its second-generation Tensor Processing Unit, branded Cloud TPU. Unlike the first TPU, which only ran inference, the new chip also handled neural network training, at up to 180 teraflops per chip. Google also launched the TensorFlow Research Cloud, giving researchers free access to a cluster of 1,000 TPUs.
