# SpaceX agrees to acquire Anysphere, maker of Cursor, for $60 billion

- **Date:** 2026-06-16
- **Who:** SpaceX (merged with xAI), Anysphere (Cursor)
- **Type:** acquisition

SpaceX announced it will buy Anysphere, the company behind the Cursor AI coding tool, for $60 billion in an all-stock deal, exercising an option it had secured in April. The announcement came days after SpaceX's Nasdaq debut, and closing is expected in the third quarter of 2026 pending regulatory approval. Reuters reported Cursor at about $2.6 billion in annualized business revenue, and the deal is seen as strengthening xAI's position in AI coding. All sources are secondary news coverage.
