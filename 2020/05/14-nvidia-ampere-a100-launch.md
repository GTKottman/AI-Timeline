# Nvidia unveils Ampere architecture and A100 GPU

- **Date:** 2020-05-14
- **Who:** Nvidia
- **Type:** hardware

Nvidia announced the Ampere architecture and the A100 data-center GPU during its GTC 2020 keynote, which Jensen Huang delivered from his kitchen. The A100 had more than 54 billion transistors on a 7 nm process and could be partitioned into up to seven GPU instances, and Nvidia said it delivered up to 20x AI performance over the prior generation. The DGX A100 system with eight A100s launched the same day, and the chip became a standard for AI training clusters.
