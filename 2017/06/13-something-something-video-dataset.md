# Something-Something Video Dataset Introduced

- **Date:** 2017-06-13
- **Who:** Twenty Billion Neurons (Twenty BN); Raghav Goyal and colleagues
- **Type:** dataset

The Something-Something dataset was posted to arXiv (1706.04261) in June 2017 and presented at ICCV 2017. It contains about 108,499 short clips of 2 to 6 seconds, each labeled with a textual description of the objects and actions shown, across 174 caption templates. The authors built it to test whether networks can learn physical common sense from video, arguing that most labeled video datasets focus on high-level concepts rather than physical detail.
