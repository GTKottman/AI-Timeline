# NVIDIA releases Llama-3.1-Nemotron-70B-Instruct

- **Date:** 2024-10-15
- **Who:** NVIDIA
- **Type:** model

NVIDIA released Llama-3.1-Nemotron-70B-Instruct, a customized version of Meta's Llama 3.1 70B tuned to make responses more helpful. NVIDIA claimed it beat GPT-4o and Claude 3.5 Sonnet on Arena Hard, with scores of 85 against 79.3 and 79.2. Those scores are NVIDIA's own figures. The model showed a large chip maker shipping a competitive open instruct model built on another lab's base.
