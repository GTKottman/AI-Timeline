# DeepMind's AlphaStar beats professional StarCraft II players 10-1

- **Date:** 2019-01-24
- **Who:** DeepMind
- **Type:** competition

AlphaStar beat professional StarCraft II players Dario "TLO" Wünsch and Grzegorz "MaNa" Komincz, winning 10 of 11 games. It was the first AI to beat top pros at StarCraft II, a real-time strategy game with imperfect information and a huge action space. The result extended game-playing AI results past Go and Dota 2 into a harder strategy domain.
