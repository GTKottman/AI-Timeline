# InstructPix2Pix: edit images from written instructions

- **Date:** 2022-11-17
- **Who:** UC Berkeley researchers
- **Type:** paper

InstructPix2Pix is a diffusion model that edits an input image from a written instruction such as a change of style or object. It made image editing a matter of plain-language commands instead of masks and manual tools.
