# Andrew Ng launches deeplearning.ai

- **Date:** 2017-06-23
- **Who:** Andrew Ng
- **Type:** people

Andrew Ng, after leaving Baidu's AI group, announced deeplearning.ai, a new AI company and education venture. TechCrunch reported on June 23, 2017 that the announcement had only a logo, a domain name and a footnote pointing to an August launch date. The move gave one of the field's best-known teachers a platform for deep learning courses and training. It mattered because Ng's courses reached a very large audience of new practitioners.
