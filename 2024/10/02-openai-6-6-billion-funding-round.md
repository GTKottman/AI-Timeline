# OpenAI closes $6.6 billion funding round at $157 billion valuation

- **Date:** 2024-10-02
- **Who:** OpenAI, Thrive Capital, Microsoft, Nvidia, SoftBank
- **Type:** funding

OpenAI closed a $6.6 billion funding round at a $157 billion post-money valuation, up from $80 billion nine months earlier. Thrive Capital led the round, with participation from Microsoft, Nvidia, SoftBank, Khosla Ventures, Altimeter, Fidelity and MGX. Reports said part of the funding was structured to convert to equity only if OpenAI moved to a profit-driven structure, which made the round a bet on the company's restructuring as well as its growth.
