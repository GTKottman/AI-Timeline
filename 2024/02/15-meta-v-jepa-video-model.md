# Meta releases V-JEPA video model

- **Date:** 2024-02-15
- **Who:** Meta (FAIR), Yann LeCun's research group
- **Type:** model

Meta released V-JEPA, a self-supervised video model that learns by predicting hidden parts of footage in an abstract representation space rather than reconstructing pixels. It is pre-trained on unlabeled video, and labelled data is used only afterward to adapt it to specific tasks. It mattered as a concrete step toward the joint-embedding world-model approach that LeCun has argued for, released for non-commercial research use.
