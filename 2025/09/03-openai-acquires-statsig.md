# OpenAI acquires Statsig for about $1.1 billion

- **Date:** 2025-09-03
- **Who:** OpenAI, Statsig (Vijaye Raji)
- **Type:** acquisition

OpenAI agreed to buy Statsig, a product experimentation platform for A/B testing, feature flags and real-time decisioning, in an all-stock deal reported at about $1.1 billion. Statsig's founder and CEO Vijaye Raji became OpenAI's Chief Technology Officer of Applications, leading product engineering for ChatGPT and Codex. The deal was aimed at speeding up how fast OpenAI ships generative AI products.
