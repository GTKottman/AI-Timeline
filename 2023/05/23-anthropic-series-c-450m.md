# Anthropic raises $450M Series C led by Spark Capital

- **Date:** 2023-05-23
- **Who:** Anthropic (led by Spark Capital; participants include Google, Salesforce Ventures, Sound Ventures and Zoom Ventures)
- **Type:** funding

Anthropic announced a $450 million Series C led by Spark Capital, which the company said would fund building next-generation AI assistants. Anthropic did not disclose its valuation. It mattered as one of the largest AI startup rounds of 2023 and a sign that Claude was being backed as a serious rival to OpenAI.
