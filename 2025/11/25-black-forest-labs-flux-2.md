# Black Forest Labs releases FLUX.2

- **Date:** 2025-11-25
- **Who:** Black Forest Labs
- **Type:** model

Black Forest Labs released FLUX.2, its second-generation image generation and editing family. The open-weight FLUX.2 [dev] variant was made available on Cloudflare Workers AI the same day.
