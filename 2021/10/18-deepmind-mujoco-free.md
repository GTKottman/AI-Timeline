# DeepMind makes MuJoCo physics simulator free

- **Date:** 2021-10-18
- **Who:** DeepMind; MuJoCo (developed by Emo Todorov, University of Washington)
- **Type:** software

DeepMind announced that the MuJoCo physics simulator, previously a paid commercial product that cost roboticists hundreds to thousands of dollars a year, would be free. It released a precompiled library first, with the full source to follow. MuJoCo became a standard tool for robotics and reinforcement learning research, so the change lowered the cost barrier for that work.
