# DeepMind publishes AlphaFold structure predictions for COVID-19 proteins

- **Date:** 2020-08-04
- **Who:** DeepMind
- **Type:** research

DeepMind released computational structure predictions for several SARS-CoV-2 proteins using its AlphaFold system, made public for researchers. The release applied an AI structure predictor to a pandemic problem and was an early example of AlphaFold being used on biology beyond its benchmark.
