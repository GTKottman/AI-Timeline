# Google to let Project Maven military AI contract lapse

- **Date:** 2018-06-01
- **Who:** Google (Google Cloud, Diane Greene); US Department of Defense (Project Maven)
- **Type:** policy

On June 1, 2018 Google told cloud staff it would let its Project Maven contract with the Pentagon expire in March 2019 with no follow-on work, according to reports of an internal meeting led by Diane Greene. Maven used AI to analyze drone video for the military. More than 4,000 employees had signed a letter against the work and at least a dozen resigned, and Greene reportedly said the backlash had hurt the company. It mattered because it showed employee pressure can change how an AI company takes military contracts, days before Google published its AI principles.
