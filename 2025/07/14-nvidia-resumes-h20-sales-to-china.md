# NVIDIA says it will resume H20 chip sales to China

- **Date:** 2025-07-14
- **Who:** NVIDIA; US government
- **Type:** policy

NVIDIA said it would resume sales of its H20 AI chip to China, after the April export licence requirement had blocked them. It said the US government had assured it that licences would be granted and that it hoped to start deliveries soon. It also introduced a new compliant GPU for the Chinese market. It mattered because it reopened a large AI chip market for NVIDIA. Some outlets reported the statement on 2025-07-15 because of the time difference.
