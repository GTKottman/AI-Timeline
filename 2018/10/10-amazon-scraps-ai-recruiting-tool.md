# Reuters reports Amazon scrapped AI recruiting tool that favoured men

- **Date:** 2018-10-10
- **Who:** Amazon (machine-learning recruiting team); reported by Reuters (Jeffrey Dastin)
- **Type:** other

Reuters reported that Amazon had built a resume-screening model since 2014 that rated candidates from one to five stars, and that it penalised resumes containing "women's" and downgraded graduates of all-women's colleges. The model learned bias from ten years of mostly male hiring data, and the team was disbanded. Amazon said recruiters never relied on the scores alone. The case became a standard example of training-data bias in hiring AI.
