# Zhipu AI lists in Hong Kong

- **Date:** 2026-01-08
- **Who:** Zhipu AI (Z.ai)
- **Type:** funding

Zhipu AI began trading on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange on January 8, 2026, at a fixed offer price of HK$116.20 that raised about HK$4.3 billion. The listing was heavily oversubscribed in retail subscriptions, reported at about 1,160 times. It was one of the first public listings of a Chinese frontier-model lab, and it set a public valuation benchmark for its rivals.
