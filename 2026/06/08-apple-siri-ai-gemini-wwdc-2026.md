# Apple unveils Gemini-based Siri AI at WWDC 2026

- **Date:** 2026-06-08
- **Who:** Apple, Google (Gemini)
- **Type:** product

At its June 8 WWDC keynote, Apple unveiled a rebuilt assistant called Siri AI, reported to be built with Google's Gemini models. It ships as a standalone app across iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch and Vision Pro, with on-screen awareness, personal-context search and synced conversation history. Reports say a developer beta arrived immediately, a public beta follows in July, and general availability comes in the fall with iOS 27, with no initial EU or China launch. The deal terms reported in the press are not confirmed by Apple's own announcement.
