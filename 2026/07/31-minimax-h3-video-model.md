# MiniMax launches H3 omni-modal 2K video model

- **Date:** 2026-07-31
- **Who:** MiniMax
- **Type:** model

MiniMax launched H3 on July 31, 2026, a unified omni-modal video model that takes text, image, video and audio input and outputs up to 15 seconds of 2K video with native stereo sound. It is the third generation of MiniMax's video line after Hailuo 01 and 02, and it is live in the MiniMax API as MiniMax-H3 and in the Hailuo app. One source reports a price of about 0.8 CNY per second at 2K. MiniMax says it plans to publish the weights, but open-weight status is reported inconsistently and the technical report was not published as of August 1.
