# Moonshot AI releases open-weight Kimi K2.5

- **Date:** 2026-01-27
- **Who:** Moonshot AI
- **Type:** model

Moonshot released Kimi K2.5, an open-weight natively multimodal model that handles text, images and video, with about 1T total and 32B active parameters, 384 experts and a 256K context. Moonshot describes it as trained on 15 trillion mixed visual and text tokens and supporting up to 100 parallel sub-agents. Benchmark claims are self-reported; the license is a modified MIT with a branding clause for very large commercial users. It joined the Chinese open-weight wave competing with US closed frontier models.
