# Google I/O 2018: Duplex, Lens and AI across products

- **Date:** 2018-05-08
- **Who:** Google
- **Type:** product

At Google I/O, Google unveiled Duplex, an AI system that carried out natural phone conversations to book a hair salon appointment and a restaurant table. It also moved Google Lens into the camera apps, added Smart Compose to Gmail, brought a camera-based Visual Positioning System to Maps, and launched ML Kit for developers. Duplex drew strong attention for how human-like its voice was, which later led to a disclosure rule for bots.
