# Samsung unveils Bixby 2.0 at its developer conference

- **Date:** 2017-10-18
- **Who:** Samsung Electronics
- **Type:** product

At the Samsung Developer Conference in San Francisco, Samsung unveiled Bixby 2.0, which it called a reinvention of its assistant as a control hub for connected devices. The Bixby SDK began in a beta program with select partners, with a public launch to follow. It was meant to let apps and devices plug into Samsung's assistant.
