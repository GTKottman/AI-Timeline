# Meta announces Movie Gen video and audio generation models

- **Date:** 2024-10-04
- **Who:** Meta AI
- **Type:** model

Meta announced Movie Gen, a family of generative models: a 30-billion-parameter video model that generates up to 16-second clips from text or images and edits existing footage, and a 13-billion-parameter audio model that adds synced sound effects and background music. The models are built on Llama 3. At announcement they were limited to Meta employees and outside partners, with a plan to bring them to Meta's platforms the next year. Meta's blind-test comparisons against Runway, OpenAI, ElevenLabs and Kling are its own claims.
