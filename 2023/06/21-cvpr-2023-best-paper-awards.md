# CVPR 2023 best paper awards

- **Date:** 2023-06-21
- **Who:** CVPR 2023 awards committee; winning teams incl. Gupta and Kembhavi; Hu, Li and collaborators
- **Type:** competition

At CVPR 2023 in Vancouver, Best Paper went to "Visual Programming: Compositional visual reasoning without training" (Gupta and Kembhavi) and to "Planning-oriented Autonomous Driving" (Hu, Li and team). The field's top vision conference chose a training-free compositional reasoning paper alongside an end-to-end driving paper, marking the year's shift toward large pretrained models and planning-aware perception.
