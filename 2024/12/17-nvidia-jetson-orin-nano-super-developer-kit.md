# NVIDIA launches Jetson Orin Nano Super Developer Kit

- **Date:** 2024-12-17
- **Who:** NVIDIA
- **Type:** hardware

NVIDIA launched the Jetson Orin Nano Super Developer Kit at $249, a lower price than the earlier $499 kit. NVIDIA rates it at 67 INT8 TOPS, about 70% more than before, with 102 GB/s of memory bandwidth and a 25 W power envelope. It targets low-cost edge generative AI development, and the gains come through a software (JetPack) update that also applies to existing Orin NX and Orin Nano modules.
