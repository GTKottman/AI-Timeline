# Senate votes 99-1 to strip AI law moratorium from budget bill

- **Date:** 2025-07-01
- **Who:** US Senate; Sens. Marsha Blackburn, Maria Cantwell, Ed Markey; Sen. Ted Cruz
- **Type:** policy

In the early hours of 2025-07-01 the Senate voted 99-1 to remove a ten-year ban on state AI regulation from the Republican budget reconciliation bill. Blackburn, Cantwell and Markey co-sponsored the amendment, and Cruz, who had championed the moratorium, voted against it. Sources say 17 Republican governors and 40 state attorneys general opposed the ban. It mattered because it kept US states free to write their own AI laws.
