# OpenAI opens limited preview of GPT-5.6 Sol, Terra and Luna

- **Date:** 2026-06-26
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** model

OpenAI launched its GPT-5.6 family, Sol, Terra and Luna, in a limited preview on June 26, 2026, for trusted partners and organisations via the API and Codex, not ChatGPT. Sol is the flagship, Terra the balanced everyday model and Luna the fast, low-cost tier, at $5/$30, $2.50/$15 and $1/$6 per million input/output tokens. The US government asked OpenAI to limit access while it evaluated the models' cyber capabilities. A broad release followed on July 9.
