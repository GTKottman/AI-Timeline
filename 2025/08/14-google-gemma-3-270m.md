# Google releases Gemma 3 270M, a small model for fine-tuning and on-device use

- **Date:** 2025-08-14
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** model

Google added a 270-million-parameter size to the Gemma 3 family for on-device use and task-specific fine-tuning. Of its 270M parameters, about 100M are Transformer blocks and 170M are embeddings for a large vocabulary. Google's official release log gives August 14, 2025. Ars Technica reported that it ran 25 conversations on a Pixel Tensor G4 using about 0.75% of the battery.
