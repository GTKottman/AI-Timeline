# OpenAI Scholars 2021 final projects

- **Date:** 2021-05-10
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** other

On 10 May 2021 OpenAI published the final projects of its 2021 Scholars class. The scholars had completed a six-month mentored program and produced open-source research projects on topics including AI safety, contrastive learning, generative modeling and scaling laws. It is a small, education-focused release, but it shows OpenAI running a structured program to bring new researchers into its work.
