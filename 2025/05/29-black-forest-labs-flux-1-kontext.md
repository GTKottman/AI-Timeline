# Black Forest Labs FLUX.1 Kontext

- **Date:** 2025-05-29
- **Who:** Black Forest Labs
- **Type:** model

Black Forest Labs announced FLUX.1 Kontext, a suite of models that generate and edit images from a text prompt and an input image, so edits can be made iteratively in context. The company released the open-weights FLUX.1 Kontext [dev] variant, a 12B-parameter editing model, on Hugging Face, under the FLUX.1 non-commercial license. The dev release came after the initial announcement.
