# Apple publishes MM1 multimodal LLM pre-training study

- **Date:** 2024-03-14
- **Who:** Apple AI/ML research
- **Type:** paper

Apple posted "MM1: Methods, Analysis and Insights from Multimodal LLM Pre-training" (arXiv 2403.09611), a systematic ablation of image encoders, vision-language connectors and data mixes for models up to 30 billion parameters. The paper found that image resolution and interleaved image-text data mattered most for multimodal performance. It was one of the first detailed public recipes from Apple on large models, though the models themselves were not released.
