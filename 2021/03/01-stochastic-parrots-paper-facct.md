# "On the Dangers of Stochastic Parrots" paper published at FAccT 2021

- **Date:** 2021-03-01
- **Who:** Emily M. Bender, Timnit Gebru, Angelina McMillan-Major, Margaret Mitchell (as Shmargaret Shmitchell)
- **Type:** paper

The paper "On the Dangers of Stochastic Parrots: Can Language Models Be Too Big?" was presented at the ACM FAccT 2021 conference (March 3-10, 2021, virtual). It argues that ever-larger language models carry environmental and financial costs, encode biases from web-scale data, and can produce fluent text without understanding. It urges teams to weigh costs first and to curate and document training datasets. The paper became one of the most-cited critiques of scaling and was central to the Google dispute that followed. The exact publication day is the one commentators give; conference dates are the firm anchor.
