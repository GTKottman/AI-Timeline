# Nvidia and NIH release COVID-19 CT AI models

- **Date:** 2020-05-14
- **Who:** Nvidia, U.S. National Institutes of Health (NIH) Clinical Center
- **Type:** model

Nvidia announced new AI models for tracking, testing and treating COVID-19, co-developed with the NIH Clinical Center under a research agreement. The models analysed chest CT scans, and the announcement came with an expansion of the Clara healthcare platform. It was one of the first large GPU-vendor efforts to put deep learning models directly into the pandemic response.
