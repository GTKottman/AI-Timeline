# Peltarion raises Series A to scale its operational AI platform

- **Date:** 2019-02-14
- **Who:** Peltarion; Euclidean Capital (lead), FAM, EQT Ventures
- **Type:** funding

Stockholm-based Peltarion raised a Series A of $20 million (one Swedish outlet reported €18 million), led by Euclidean Capital, the family office of hedge fund manager James Simons. Its "operational AI" platform covers data preprocessing, model building and deployment in a cloud service with a graphical interface. The round was meant to grow its team and expand into the US and other markets, in a field of companies selling AI development tools to non-specialists.
