# Apple researchers publish "The Illusion of Thinking" on reasoning model limits

- **Date:** 2025-06-07
- **Who:** Apple machine learning researchers
- **Type:** paper

The paper tested large reasoning models on controllable puzzles and found that their accuracy collapses beyond a certain complexity, even when they have spare token budget. It questioned how much "thinking" these models actually do.
