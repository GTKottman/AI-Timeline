# Chain-of-Thought Prompting paper (Wei et al.)

- **Date:** 2022-01-28
- **Who:** Google Research (Jason Wei and colleagues)
- **Type:** paper

The paper showed that giving a large language model a few examples that include step-by-step reasoning makes it solve arithmetic and commonsense problems far better. Chain-of-thought prompting became a standard technique for getting reasoning out of language models without any retraining.
