# Waymo plans a self-driving vehicle factory in the Detroit area

- **Date:** 2019-01-25
- **Who:** Waymo (Alphabet)
- **Type:** product

Waymo said it would set up a factory in the Detroit area to build its self-driving hardware into vehicles, starting with Chrysler Pacifica minivans. Waymo planned to spend about $13.6 million on a retrofit of a Detroit-area plant and hire up to 400 people. The plan came with a state incentive grant of up to $8 million approved by the Michigan Strategic Fund Board. Building its own production line was a step toward running a self-driving fleet at scale rather than retrofitting cars one at a time. Reports differ on the exact day, but the month is January 2019.
