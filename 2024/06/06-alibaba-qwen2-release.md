# Alibaba releases Qwen2 model family

- **Date:** 2024-06-06
- **Who:** Alibaba Cloud (Qwen team)
- **Type:** model

Qwen2 is a family of dense and mixture-of-experts language models released with open weights in several sizes. It was among the strongest open Chinese model families of 2024 and widened the open-model options for multilingual and coding tasks.
