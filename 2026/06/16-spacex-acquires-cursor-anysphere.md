# SpaceX agrees to acquire Anysphere, maker of Cursor, for $60 billion

- **Date:** 2026-06-16
- **Who:** SpaceX (merged with xAI), Anysphere (Cursor)
- **Type:** acquisition

SpaceX announced on June 16, 2026 that it will buy Anysphere, the company behind the Cursor coding tool, for $60 billion in an all-stock deal, exercising an option it had secured in April. The deal is described in an SEC Form 8-K dated June 16; Cursor shareholders receive SpaceX Class A shares. The announcement came days after SpaceX's June 12 Nasdaq debut, and closing is expected in the third quarter of 2026, subject to regulatory approval. Reuters reported Cursor at about $2.6 billion in annualized business revenue, while Forbes-sourced reporting cites a run rate above $4 billion; the two figures conflict.
