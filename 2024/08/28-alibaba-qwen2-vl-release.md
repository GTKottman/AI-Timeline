# Alibaba releases Qwen2-VL vision-language models

- **Date:** 2024-08-28
- **Who:** Alibaba Cloud (Qwen team)
- **Type:** model

Qwen2-VL added image and video understanding to the Qwen2 line in 2B and 7B sizes. It was a step toward open multimodal models competitive with Western vision-language systems.
