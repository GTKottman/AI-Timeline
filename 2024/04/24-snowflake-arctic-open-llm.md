# Snowflake launches Arctic open enterprise LLM

- **Date:** 2024-04-24
- **Who:** Snowflake
- **Type:** model

Snowflake launched Arctic, a mixture-of-experts LLM aimed at enterprise workloads such as SQL generation and instruction following, and released its weights under the Apache 2.0 license with its training research. Coverage describes about 480 billion total parameters with roughly 17 billion active per token, a figure from a secondary source. The release added another large open-weights model to the enterprise field.
