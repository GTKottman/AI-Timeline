# Waymo tells early riders in Phoenix they will get driverless rides

- **Date:** 2019-10-09
- **Who:** Waymo (Alphabet)
- **Type:** product

Waymo emailed members of its early rider program in the Phoenix suburbs to say rides with no safety driver behind the wheel were coming. The passengers would ride in autonomous Chrysler Pacifica minivans. It was a step from testing with safety drivers to public, driverless service, one of the first real-world deployments of a learned self-driving stack for paying riders. Report dates vary slightly, and the TechCrunch report is dated October 9, 2019.
