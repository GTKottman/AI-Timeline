# National Artificial Intelligence Initiative Act becomes law in the FY2021 NDAA

- **Date:** 2021-01-01
- **Who:** U.S. Congress
- **Type:** policy

The National AI Initiative Act, placed in Division E of the FY2021 National Defense Authorization Act, became law on January 1, 2021. It created a National AI Initiative to coordinate federal AI research, policy and application. Sources differ on whether the President signed the bill or Congress overrode a veto; one analysis puts the authorized funding at nearly $6.5 billion over five years.
