# Nvidia passes Microsoft and Apple as world's most valuable company

- **Date:** 2024-06-18
- **Who:** NVIDIA, Microsoft, Apple
- **Type:** other

On June 18, 2024 Nvidia's share price hit an all-time high, lifting its market value to about $3.3 trillion and past Microsoft and Apple to make it the most valuable publicly traded company. The lead was driven by demand for its AI GPUs, which were the main hardware for training and running frontier models. The milestone showed how much of the industry's spending was flowing into AI accelerators.
