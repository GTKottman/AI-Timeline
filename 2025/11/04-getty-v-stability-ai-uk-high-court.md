# UK High Court rejects Getty's secondary copyright claim against Stability AI

- **Date:** 2025-11-04
- **Who:** Getty Images; Stability AI; UK High Court
- **Type:** policy

The UK High Court rejected Getty Images' secondary copyright claim against Stability AI, finding only limited trade mark infringement.
