# NeurIPS 2019 Outstanding Paper Awards

- **Date:** 2019-12-08
- **Who:** NeurIPS 2019 organizing committee (Vancouver); winners include Diakonikolas, Gouleakis and Tzamos, and Microsoft's Lin Xiao (Test of Time)
- **Type:** other

At the opening of NeurIPS 2019 in Vancouver, the organizers announced the Outstanding Paper Award to "Distribution-Independent PAC Learning of Halfspaces with Massart Noise" by Ilias Diakonikolas, Themis Gouleakis and Christos Tzamos. The Outstanding New Directions Paper Award went to "Uniform convergence may be unable to explain generalization in deep learning" by Vaishnavh Nagarajan and J. Zico Kolter. The Test of Time Award went to Microsoft's Lin Xiao for "Dual Averaging Method for Regularized Stochastic Learning and Online Optimization."
