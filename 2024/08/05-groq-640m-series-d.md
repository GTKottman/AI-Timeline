# Groq raises $640M Series D at a $2.8B valuation

- **Date:** 2024-08-05
- **Who:** Groq; BlackRock Private Equity Partners
- **Type:** funding

Groq raised a $640 million Series D led by BlackRock Private Equity Partners at a valuation of $2.8 billion. The round funded its push to challenge Nvidia on AI inference hardware.
