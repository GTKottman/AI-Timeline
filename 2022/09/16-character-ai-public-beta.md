# Character.AI opens public beta

- **Date:** 2022-09-16
- **Who:** Character.AI (Noam Shazeer, Daniel De Freitas)
- **Type:** product

Character.AI, founded by former Google LaMDA engineers Noam Shazeer and Daniel De Freitas, made its chatbot service available to the public in September 2022. Users create and chat with custom characters built on the company's own large language model. The launch was an early consumer test of role-play and persona chat on large models.
