# NVIDIA releases Nemotron 3.5 Bolt 74B-A9B and Nemotron 3.5 Super VL

- **Date:** 2026-10-15
- **Who:** NVIDIA
- **Type:** model

NVIDIA expanded its Nemotron 3.5 family with Super VL, a 120B vision model, and Bolt 74B-A9B, a 74-billion-parameter model with about 9 billion active parameters and a 256K-token context. Bolt is built to run in 24 GB of memory with native 2-bit quantization for local agent work, and it launched with llama.cpp, vLLM, Ollama, Unsloth and LM Studio support. Bolt E4B, a 4 GB edge model, was scheduled for October 23. NVIDIA's claims of first place among open models on PinchBench and second on BrowseComp are vendor figures. Sources differ on the licence, which one listing gives as OpenMDW 1.1.
