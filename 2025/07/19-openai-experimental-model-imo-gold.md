# OpenAI experimental model reaches gold-medal level at IMO 2025

- **Date:** 2025-07-19
- **Who:** OpenAI (Alexander Wei, Noam Brown)
- **Type:** competition

OpenAI said an experimental reasoning model solved five of six problems at the 2025 International Mathematical Olympiad under exam conditions, scoring 35 of 42 points, which graders counted as gold level. The model was a research prototype, not a public product, and was not officially entered in the competition. Google DeepMind's Gemini Deep Think also scored gold, certified by the IMO. It mattered as a second lab reaching gold-medal performance on olympiad maths.
