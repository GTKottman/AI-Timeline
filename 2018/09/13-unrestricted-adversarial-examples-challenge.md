# Google Brain launches the Unrestricted Adversarial Examples Challenge

- **Date:** 2018-09-13
- **Who:** Google Brain
- **Type:** competition

Google Brain announced a community challenge on robust classification that goes beyond small, norm-bounded perturbations. Defenders build classifiers that avoid confident mistakes, while attackers submit arbitrary images, and human labelers check the attacker images. The bird-or-bicycle task was meant to test whether defenses hold against unrestricted attacks rather than only the narrow threat models studied so far.
