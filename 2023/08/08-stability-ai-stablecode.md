# Stability AI StableCode: open-source coding LLM

- **Date:** 2023-08-08
- **Who:** Stability AI
- **Type:** model

Stability AI released StableCode, its first coding LLM, in three variants: a base completion model with 4K context, an instruction-tuned model, and a long-context model handling up to 16,000 tokens. The base model was trained on The Stack v1.2 and all three were posted to Hugging Face. It was aimed at everyday programmers and at learners.
