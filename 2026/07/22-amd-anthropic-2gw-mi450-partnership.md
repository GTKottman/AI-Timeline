# AMD and Anthropic agree to deploy up to 2 GW of AMD Instinct MI450 GPUs

- **Date:** 2026-07-22
- **Who:** AMD; Anthropic
- **Type:** hardware

AMD and Anthropic announced a strategic partnership to deploy up to 2 gigawatts of AMD Instinct MI450 Series GPUs, with the first gigawatt starting in the first half of 2027. AMD committed to a strategic equity investment of up to $5 billion in Anthropic.
