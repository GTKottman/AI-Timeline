# Aurora to acquire Uber's self-driving unit Advanced Technologies Group

- **Date:** 2020-12-07
- **Who:** Aurora Innovation; Uber Technologies (Uber ATG)
- **Type:** acquisition

On December 7, 2020, Uber announced that it would sell its Advanced Technologies Group (ATG), its self-driving unit, to Aurora Innovation. Uber invested $400 million in Aurora and took a stake of about 26% on a fully diluted basis. Reuters, citing people familiar with the deal, reported that the deal valued ATG at $4 billion, down from $7.25 billion a year earlier.
