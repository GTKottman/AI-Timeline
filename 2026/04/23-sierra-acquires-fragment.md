# Sierra acquires French startup Fragment

- **Date:** 2026-04-23
- **Who:** Sierra (Bret Taylor), Fragment (Olivier Moindrot, Guillaume Genthial)
- **Type:** acquisition

Sierra, the enterprise customer-service agent company, acquired Fragment, a Y Combinator-backed French startup that connects LLMs securely to enterprise knowledge bases. The co-founders join Sierra and terms were undisclosed. It is one of Sierra's acquisitions of small AI teams to add agent capability.
