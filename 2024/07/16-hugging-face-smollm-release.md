# Hugging Face releases SmolLM small language models

- **Date:** 2024-07-16
- **Who:** Hugging Face
- **Type:** model

SmolLM is a family of small language models at 135M, 360M and 1.7B parameters, released with open weights. Its training corpus was released as the SmolLM-Corpus dataset. It was aimed at running capable models on laptops and phones.
