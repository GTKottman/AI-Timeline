# DeepSeek releases V3.1, a hybrid model with switchable thinking

- **Date:** 2025-08-20
- **Who:** DeepSeek
- **Type:** model

DeepSeek released V3.1, a 671-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with about 37 billion parameters active per token and a 128K context window. One model handles both reasoning and standard chat, switched through the chat template, with a Deep Thinking toggle in the app. DeepSeek says the thinking mode answers faster than R1-0528 at similar quality, and it used the UE8M0 FP8 format that it linked to upcoming domestic Chinese chips. eWeek reported 66.0% on SWE-bench Verified, from press coverage rather than DeepSeek's report. The Hugging Face date is August 20, and coverage dated August 21.
