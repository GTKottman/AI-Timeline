# GLM-130B: open bilingual English-Chinese language model

- **Date:** 2022-10-05
- **Who:** Tsinghua University (THUDM)
- **Type:** model

Researchers released GLM-130B, a 130-billion-parameter bilingual model trained on English and Chinese text. It was published with open weights and gave the research community a large Chinese-English model to study.
