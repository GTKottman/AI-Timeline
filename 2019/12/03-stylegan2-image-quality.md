# NVIDIA publishes StyleGAN2, improving GAN image quality

- **Date:** 2019-12-03
- **Who:** NVIDIA (Tero Karras, Samuli Laine, Miika Aittala, Janne Hellsten, Jaakko Lehtinen, Timo Aila)
- **Type:** model

The StyleGAN2 paper, "Analyzing and Improving the Image Quality of StyleGAN," analyzes characteristic artifacts of the original StyleGAN and proposes changes to its architecture and training. The authors redesigned generator normalization, revisited progressive growing, and added a path length regularizer. The result was markedly more realistic synthetic images from the same family of generators.
