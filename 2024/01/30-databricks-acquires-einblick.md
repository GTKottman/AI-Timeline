# Databricks acquires Einblick

- **Date:** 2024-01-30
- **Who:** Databricks, Einblick (founded by researchers from MIT and Brown)
- **Type:** acquisition

Databricks acquired Einblick, a Cambridge, Massachusetts startup building an AI-native, natural-language data science notebook, for an undisclosed sum. The deal was mainly a team acquisition, and Databricks said Einblick's tools would be tied into its data catalog (Unity Catalog). It mattered as one of a run of Databricks acquisitions aimed at putting AI-assisted analysis directly on enterprise data.
