# 25 Fields Medalists criticise AI companies over unsolved-problem benchmarks

- **Date:** 2026-09-11
- **Who:** Fields Medal laureates; AI companies
- **Type:** other

An open letter signed by 25 Fields Medalists criticised AI companies for using unsolved mathematical problems as publicity benchmarks. The letter said the goals of AI companies and the mathematical community are "severely misaligned." It followed OpenAI's September 8 claim that an unreleased model had solved the Navier-Stokes problem, which the mathematical community had not verified.
