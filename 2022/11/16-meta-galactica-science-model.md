# Meta Galactica: a large language model for science

- **Date:** 2022-11-16
- **Who:** Meta AI
- **Type:** model

Meta posted the Galactica paper, a large language model trained on scientific text to organize and reason about scientific knowledge. The paper's framing of the model as a tool for science drew sharp criticism over reliability, and the public demo was withdrawn soon after.
