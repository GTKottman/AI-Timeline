# US government orders suspension of Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5 access

- **Date:** 2026-06-12
- **Who:** Anthropic; US Commerce Department
- **Type:** policy

The Commerce Department issued an export control directive requiring that access to Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5 be suspended for foreign nationals, including Anthropic's foreign-national staff. Anthropic took both models offline for all users worldwide. Anthropic said access to its other models was unaffected.
