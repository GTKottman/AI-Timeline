# Google Pixel 3 launches Call Screen and Duplex on the phone

- **Date:** 2018-10-09
- **Who:** Google
- **Type:** product

At the Made by Google event, Google launched Pixel 3 with Call Screen, which uses the Assistant to answer unknown callers, ask who is calling, and show a live transcript. The screening voice identifies itself as an automated service, in line with Google's new policy for bots to disclose they are machines. Call Screen rolled out in the US first, with other Pixel devices to follow.
