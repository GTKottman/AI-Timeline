# NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1080 Ti launch

- **Date:** 2017-03-10
- **Who:** NVIDIA
- **Type:** hardware

NVIDIA's GeForce GTX 1080 Ti went on sale on March 10, 2017, after being announced in late February at GDC. It used the Pascal architecture with 11 GB of GDDR5X memory and about 3584 CUDA cores, and it was cheap enough to become a popular card for home deep learning experiments. Coverage put the launch price at $699.
