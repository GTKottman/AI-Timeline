# OpenAI announces o3 reasoning model preview

- **Date:** 2024-12-20
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** model

On the last day of its "12 Days of OpenAI" event, OpenAI announced o3 and o3-mini, its next reasoning models. o3 was shared only with safety and security testers at first, and invitations to apply for early access ran until January 10, 2025. The name skipped "o2" to avoid a trademark clash with the carrier O2.
