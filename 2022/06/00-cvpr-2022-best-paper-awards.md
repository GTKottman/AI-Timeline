# CVPR 2022 Best Paper Awards

- **Date:** 2022-06-00
- **Who:** CVPR 2022 awards committee (IEEE/CVF Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition), New Orleans
- **Type:** competition

The CVPR 2022 awards committee gave its Best Paper Award to "Learning to Solve Hard Minimal Problems" by Petr Hruby, Timothy Duff, Anton Leykin and Tomas Pajdla, from 2,064 accepted papers out of a record 8,161 submissions. The committee also named "Dual-Shutter Optical Vibration Sensing" a Best Paper honorable mention, "EPro-PnP" the Best Student Paper, and "Ref-NeRF" a Best Student Paper honorable mention. The exact June announcement day was not confirmed.
