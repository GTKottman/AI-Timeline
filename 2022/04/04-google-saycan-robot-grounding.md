# SayCan: grounding language models in robot affordances

- **Date:** 2022-04-04
- **Who:** Google Research and Everyday Robots
- **Type:** research

SayCan combines a large language model that proposes a task step with learned value functions that estimate whether the robot can actually do that step from where it is. It let a real robot follow open-ended spoken requests by checking language suggestions against its own skills.
