# Google publishes Fluid Annotation, a machine-assisted image labeling interface

- **Date:** 2018-10-22
- **Who:** Google AI (Jasper Uijlings and Vittorio Ferrari)
- **Type:** software

Fluid Annotation is a human-machine interface for labeling every object and background region in an image. It starts from a pre-trained semantic segmentation model (Mask R-CNN) and lets annotators refine its output. The post said it accelerates the creation of labeled datasets by a factor of 3x compared with traditional manual labeling, where labeling one COCO+Stuff image took 19 minutes. It mattered as a practical way to speed up building the labeled datasets that vision models depend on.
