# Alibaba releases open-weights Qwen3.6-35B-A3B

- **Date:** 2026-04-15
- **Who:** Alibaba Qwen team
- **Type:** model

Alibaba released Qwen3.6-35B-A3B, a sparse mixture-of-experts model with 35B total and about 3B active parameters, under the Apache 2.0 license.
