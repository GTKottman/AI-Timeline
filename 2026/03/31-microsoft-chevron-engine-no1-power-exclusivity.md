# Microsoft, Chevron and Engine No. 1 agree exclusivity for AI data center power

- **Date:** 2026-03-31
- **Who:** Microsoft, Chevron, Engine No. 1
- **Type:** other

The three companies entered an exclusivity agreement to explore power generation and supply for AI data centers, centered on a proposed West Texas natural gas plant expected to cost about $7 billion and produce around 2,500 MW. It is an exclusivity arrangement to explore the project, not a final supply contract. It shows hyperscalers turning to dedicated gas generation as grid capacity becomes the limit on AI buildout.
