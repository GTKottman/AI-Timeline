# Waymo gets California's first driverless testing permit

- **Date:** 2018-10-30
- **Who:** Waymo (Alphabet); California DMV
- **Type:** policy

The California DMV issued Waymo the state's first permit to test fully driverless cars on public roads, with no safety driver. The permit covered testing in Palo Alto, Mountain View, Los Altos, Los Altos Hills and Sunnyvale, with a fleet of about three dozen vehicles (reports said up to 40). Carrying members of the public needed a separate approval from the California Public Utilities Commission. It mattered as the first state permit for driverless testing without a human backup in California.
