# PyTorch 1.7 released with CUDA 11 and new FFT and distributed APIs

- **Date:** 2020-10-27
- **Who:** PyTorch / Facebook AI
- **Type:** software

PyTorch 1.7 added official CUDA 11 support, a NumPy-compatible torch.fft module, and major updates to distributed data parallel and RPC training. It also brought prototype support for Nvidia A100 and TF32, and prototype distributed training on Windows.
