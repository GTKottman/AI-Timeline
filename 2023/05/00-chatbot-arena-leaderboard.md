# LMSYS launches Chatbot Arena leaderboard

- **Date:** 2023-05-00
- **Who:** LMSYS (UC Berkeley, CMU, UCSD)
- **Type:** competition

LMSYS launched Chatbot Arena, where visitors send one prompt to two anonymous models, vote on the better answer, and the votes feed an Elo-style leaderboard. Its first public leaderboard, dated May 25, 2023, ranked GPT-4 first at 1225 Elo and Claude-v1 second at 1195, using about 27,000 votes. It mattered as a human-preference benchmark that became a widely cited reference for chatbot quality. The exact launch day in May was not confirmed from a primary source.
