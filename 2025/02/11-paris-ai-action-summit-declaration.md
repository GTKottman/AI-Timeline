# Paris AI Action Summit declaration signed by 62 countries

- **Date:** 2025-02-11
- **Who:** France (summit host); about 60 signatory countries (reports differ on the exact number)
- **Type:** policy

The Paris AI Action Summit ended with a declaration on inclusive and sustainable AI signed by about 60 countries, with reports giving counts from 60 to 64. The United States and the United Kingdom declined to sign.
