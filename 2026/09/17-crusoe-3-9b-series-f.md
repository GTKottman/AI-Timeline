# Crusoe raises $3.9 billion Series F at $30.9 billion valuation

- **Date:** 2026-09-17
- **Who:** Crusoe; lead investors Atreides Management, Mubadala Capital and Valor Equity Partners
- **Type:** funding

Crusoe, an AI infrastructure provider, announced a $3.9 billion Series F at a $30.9 billion post-money valuation, a threefold jump from its $10 billion Series E the year before. Founders Fund, GIC, NVIDIA, Qatar Investment Authority, Radical Ventures and TPG also took part. The company reports more than $140 billion in contracted value and over 6 GW of contracted capacity, with 1 GW operational, and is moving toward modular "Spark" data centers. Some outlets describe the $3.9 billion as a first close of a larger round, so the final total may differ.
