# Nvidia agrees to acquire Arm from SoftBank for $40 billion

- **Date:** 2020-09-13
- **Who:** Nvidia, SoftBank Group, Arm
- **Type:** acquisition

Nvidia and SoftBank agreed that Nvidia would buy Arm Limited, the chip-design company whose licensed architecture runs most mobile and embedded processors, in a deal valued at $40 billion. The consideration was about $12 billion in cash, including $2 billion at signing, and $21.5 billion in Nvidia stock. SoftBank could receive up to $5 billion more tied to Arm performance targets. The deal needed approval from UK, China, EU and US regulators.
