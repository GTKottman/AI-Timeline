# Google DeepMind publishes GenCast, an AI ensemble weather forecaster

- **Date:** 2024-12-04
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** research

GenCast, a diffusion-based ensemble model, was published in Nature on December 4, 2024. It outputs probabilistic 15-day forecasts and beat the ECMWF ENS system on most test cases, including extreme events such as hurricanes and heatwaves. It makes a 15-day forecast in about eight minutes on one Google Cloud TPU v5, where the physics-based system needs hours on a supercomputer. Outside experts noted it still depends on physics-based training data.
