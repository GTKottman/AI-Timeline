# NVIDIA launches NeMo LLM and BioNeMo LLM cloud services

- **Date:** 2022-09-20
- **Who:** NVIDIA
- **Type:** product

At its GTC keynote, NVIDIA launched the NeMo Large Language Model Service and the BioNeMo LLM Service as cloud APIs. Developers could adapt pretrained foundation models, including the 530B-parameter Megatron model, through prompt learning on NVIDIA-managed infrastructure, with early access starting in October. BioNeMo added language models for chemistry and biology aimed at drug discovery and protein work. It was an early example of a chip maker selling hosted large language model customization as a service.
