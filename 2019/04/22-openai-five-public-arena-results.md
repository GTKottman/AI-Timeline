# OpenAI Five wins 99.4% of public games in open online arena

- **Date:** 2019-04-22
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** competition

After beating OG, OpenAI opened OpenAI Five to the public for a four-day online event. A report published on April 22, 2019 said the bots had won 99.4% of their 42,729 public games. The result showed the system could beat a large field of human players, not just one professional team.
