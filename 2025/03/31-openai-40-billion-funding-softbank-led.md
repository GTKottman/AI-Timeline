# OpenAI closes $40 billion funding round at a $300 billion valuation

- **Date:** 2025-03-31
- **Who:** OpenAI, SoftBank, Microsoft, Coatue, Altimeter, Thrive Capital
- **Type:** funding

OpenAI finalised a roughly $40 billion round led by SoftBank at a $300 billion valuation, almost double its October 2024 valuation of $157 billion. The round included SoftBank's commitment, a syndicate, and participation from Microsoft, Coatue, Altimeter and Thrive Capital. Reports say about $18 billion was expected for the Stargate commitment. Sources disagree on the tranche timing, and SoftBank's share depended on OpenAI completing its restructuring as a for-profit.
