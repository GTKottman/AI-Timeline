# Google adds a prosody encoder to Tacotron for expressive speech

- **Date:** 2018-03-27
- **Who:** Google (Yuxuan Wang, RJ Skerry-Ryan and colleagues)
- **Type:** research

Google Research described its work on expressive speech synthesis with Tacotron, the end-to-end text-to-speech system. The team added a prosody encoder that turns a reference audio clip into a low-dimensional embedding of stress, intonation and timing, separate from the words and the speaker. Synthesis can then copy the prosody of a reference clip, even from a different speaker, or be steered with the embedding. The paper "Towards End-to-End Prosody Transfer for Expressive Speech Synthesis with Tacotron" was published at ICML 2018.
