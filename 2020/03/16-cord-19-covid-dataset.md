# CORD-19 COVID-19 Open Research Dataset released

- **Date:** 2020-03-16
- **Who:** Allen Institute for AI, Chan Zuckerberg Initiative, Georgetown University, Microsoft, US National Library of Medicine
- **Type:** dataset

The CORD-19 dataset of COVID-19 and coronavirus research papers was released as an open resource for text-mining and AI research. The initial release held over 29,000 scholarly articles, more than 13,000 of them with full text.
