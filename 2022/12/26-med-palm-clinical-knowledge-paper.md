# Google publishes 'Large Language Models Encode Clinical Knowledge' (Med-PaLM)

- **Date:** 2022-12-26
- **Who:** Google Research / DeepMind (Karan Singhal et al.)
- **Type:** paper

Google's arXiv paper introduced MultiMedQA and Med-PaLM, showing Flan-PaLM reached 67.6% on MedQA US-licensing-style questions, well above the prior best. It was an early major demonstration that large language models could approach medical exam competence.
