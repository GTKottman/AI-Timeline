# Apple unveils A13 Bionic with Neural Engine in iPhone 11

- **Date:** 2019-09-10
- **Who:** Apple
- **Type:** hardware

Apple announced the A13 Bionic for the iPhone 11 family, with a Neural Engine that runs machine learning models alongside the CPU and GPU. Apple also previewed Deep Fusion, a neural-network photo feature that runs on the A13 Bionic, at the event. It brought on-device neural processing to mainstream phones at scale.
