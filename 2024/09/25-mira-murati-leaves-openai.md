# Mira Murati leaves OpenAI as CTO

- **Date:** 2024-09-25
- **Who:** Mira Murati; OpenAI; Bob McGrew; Barret Zoph
- **Type:** people

OpenAI chief technology officer Mira Murati announced she was stepping down, and chief research officer Bob McGrew and research VP Barret Zoph left the same day. It was a large senior departure, weeks after the o1 release.
