# Colorado governor signs SB 26-189, replacing the 2024 AI Act

- **Date:** 2026-05-14
- **Who:** Governor Jared Polis, Colorado legislature
- **Type:** policy

SB 26-189 repeals and replaces the 2024 Colorado Artificial Intelligence Act before it could take effect, shifting high-risk AI and automated decision-making rules, including for employment decisions. It takes effect January 1, 2027. Most sources date the signing to May 14; one law-firm summary gives May 20, so the exact day is not fully settled.
