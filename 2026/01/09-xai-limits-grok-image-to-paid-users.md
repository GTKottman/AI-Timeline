# xAI limits Grok image generation to paid users after sexual deepfakes

- **Date:** 2026-01-09
- **Who:** xAI
- **Type:** product

xAI restricted Grok image generation to paying users after its tool was used to make non-consensual sexualised images. Regulators in Southeast Asia then blocked Grok in several countries, and the episode became a leading case for image-model safety rules.
