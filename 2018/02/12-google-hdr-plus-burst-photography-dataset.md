# Google releases the HDR+ Burst Photography Dataset

- **Date:** 2018-02-12
- **Who:** Google Research
- **Type:** dataset

Google published an archive of 3,640 full-resolution raw image bursts, 28,461 images in all, with intermediate and final HDR+ results. Researchers can use it to benchmark their own burst-merging and computational photography methods against Google's HDR+ pipeline. The data covers varied subjects, motion and dynamic range.
