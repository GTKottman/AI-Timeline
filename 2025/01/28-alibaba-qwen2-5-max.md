# Alibaba's Qwen team releases Qwen2.5-Max

- **Date:** 2025-01-28
- **Who:** Alibaba (Qwen team)
- **Type:** model

Alibaba's Qwen team released Qwen2.5-Max, a large mixture-of-experts model pretrained on more than 20 trillion tokens. The team said its instruct model beat DeepSeek V3 on Arena-Hard, LiveBench, LiveCodeBench and GPQA-Diamond. These are Qwen's own reported results.
