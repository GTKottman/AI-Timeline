# DeepMind Ithaca restores and dates ancient Greek inscriptions

- **Date:** 2022-03-09
- **Who:** DeepMind with Ca' Foscari University of Venice, University of Oxford and the Athens University of Economics and Business
- **Type:** research

DeepMind published Ithaca in Nature, a neural network that restores missing text in damaged ancient Greek inscriptions, estimates where they were found and dates them. Alone it restored text with about 62% accuracy and attributed locations with 71% accuracy. Historians' accuracy rose from 25% to 72% when they used it. The code and pre-trained models were released open source, and the paper was on the cover of Nature's 10 March 2022 issue.
