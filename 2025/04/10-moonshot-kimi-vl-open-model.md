# Moonshot AI Kimi-VL open vision-language model

- **Date:** 2025-04-10
- **Who:** Moonshot AI
- **Type:** model

Moonshot AI open-sourced Kimi-VL and Kimi-VL-Thinking, a mixture-of-experts vision-language model with 2.8 billion active parameters and a 128K context window. Coverage highlights long-document understanding, multi-step reasoning and user-interface understanding across text, images and video. Benchmark claims are Moonshot's own.
