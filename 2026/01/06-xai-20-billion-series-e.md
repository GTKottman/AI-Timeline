# xAI Closes $20 Billion Series E

- **Date:** 2026-01-06
- **Who:** xAI; investors Valor Equity Partners, StepStone Group, Fidelity, Qatar Investment Authority, MGX, Baron Capital Group; strategic investors Nvidia and Cisco Investments
- **Type:** funding

xAI announced an upsized $20 billion Series E, with Nvidia and Cisco Investments as strategic backers alongside financial investors. Trade coverage put the valuation at about $230 billion, but xAI did not confirm that figure, and individual investor amounts were not broken out. Bloomberg reporting cited in one article put Nvidia's expected share at up to $2 billion; that is also unconfirmed by xAI.
