# Microsoft unveils Maia 200 inference accelerator

- **Date:** 2026-01-26
- **Who:** Microsoft
- **Type:** hardware

Microsoft announced Maia 200, its second-generation in-house AI accelerator, built on TSMC 3nm and aimed at inference. It has 216GB of HBM3e at 7 TB/s and over 10 petaFLOPS at FP4, and Microsoft says it gives 30% better performance per dollar than its current fleet. Microsoft said it will serve OpenAI's GPT-5.2 models and power Microsoft Foundry and Microsoft 365 Copilot, with a Maia SDK preview including PyTorch and Triton support. It is another step in hyperscalers building custom chips to cut reliance on Nvidia.
