# Perplexity AI raises $73.6M at a $520M valuation

- **Date:** 2024-01-04
- **Who:** Perplexity AI
- **Type:** funding

AI search startup Perplexity raised $73.6 million at a valuation of about $520 million. It was one of the first large rounds for an AI-native search product that answers with cited web sources, and the round set up a fast climb in valuation later in 2024.
