# Waymo gets California's first driverless testing permit

- **Date:** 2018-10-30
- **Who:** Waymo (Alphabet); California DMV
- **Type:** policy

The California DMV issued Waymo the state's first permit to test fully driverless cars on public roads, with no safety driver. The permit covered day and night driving on city streets, rural roads and highways, starting with a fleet of about 40 cars around Mountain View, Sunnyvale, Los Altos and Palo Alto, with a remote operator able to intervene. Carrying members of the public needed a separate approval from the California Public Utilities Commission. It mattered as the first regulator sign-off for an AI driving system operating without a human backup in California.
