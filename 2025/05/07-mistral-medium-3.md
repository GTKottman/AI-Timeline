# Mistral AI announces Mistral Medium 3

- **Date:** 2025-05-07
- **Who:** Mistral AI
- **Type:** model

Mistral AI announced Mistral Medium 3, priced at $0.4 per million input tokens and $2 per million output tokens, and available through Mistral's platform and Amazon SageMaker. Mistral said it reached over 90% of Claude Sonnet 3.7's benchmark performance at a fraction of the cost. The launch aimed at enterprises that wanted cheaper frontier-class coding and STEM performance with on-premises options.
