# Trump signs Executive Order 14409 on voluntary early government access to frontier AI models

- **Date:** 2026-06-02
- **Who:** President Donald Trump, US federal government (Treasury, NSA, CISA, Commerce)
- **Type:** policy

On June 2, 2026 Trump signed Executive Order 14409, "Promoting Advanced Artificial Intelligence Innovation and Security". It creates a voluntary framework in which developers can give the government pre-release access to covered frontier models for up to 30 days before release. The government identifies covered models through classified benchmarking of advanced cyber capabilities, and agencies have 60 days to act. The order states that it creates no mandatory government licensing, preclearance or permitting requirement.
