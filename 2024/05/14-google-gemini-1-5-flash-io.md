# Google announces Gemini 1.5 Flash at I/O 2024

- **Date:** 2024-05-14
- **Who:** Google
- **Type:** model

At Google I/O 2024, Google introduced Gemini 1.5 Flash, a lighter and faster model for high-volume use. The same keynote also said a desktop Gemini Nano would come to Chrome. It widened the Gemini range from large to low-latency tiers.
