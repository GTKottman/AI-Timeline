# OpenAI launches ChatGPT Projects for organising chats and files

- **Date:** 2024-12-05
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** product

ChatGPT Projects, announced on the first day of OpenAI's "12 Days" event, lets users group chats, files, custom GPTs and coding work in one folder. Each project can have its own name, colour, files and custom instructions, and projects can be shared. Plus, Pro and Team users got it first, with Enterprise and Edu promised for early 2025.
