# Google RT-1: Robotics Transformer for real-world control

- **Date:** 2022-12-13
- **Who:** Google Robotics
- **Type:** model

Google posted RT-1, a Transformer policy trained on large sets of real robot demonstrations. The paper showed that a single model could scale across many real-world robot tasks, which pointed toward robots trained on large data like language models.
