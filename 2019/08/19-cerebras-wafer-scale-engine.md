# Cerebras unveils the Wafer Scale Engine at Hot Chips

- **Date:** 2019-08-19
- **Who:** Cerebras Systems
- **Type:** hardware

Cerebras presented the Wafer Scale Engine, a single silicon wafer with 1.2 trillion transistors and about 400,000 AI-optimized cores, at Hot Chips. It was pitched as the largest chip ever built, aimed at AI training. The performance claims (100 to 1,000 times faster than an Nvidia V100) were vendor claims, but the design showed a different path from GPU clusters.
