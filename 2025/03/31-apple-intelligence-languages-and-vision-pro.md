# Apple Intelligence expands to new languages and Vision Pro

- **Date:** 2025-03-31
- **Who:** Apple
- **Type:** product

On March 31, 2025 Apple released iOS 18.4, iPadOS 18.4 and macOS Sequoia 15.4, which add Apple Intelligence in French, German, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish, Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese, plus localized English for Singapore and India. The same day, visionOS 2.4 brought Apple Intelligence to Apple Vision Pro, initially for U.S. English only. This was a rollout expansion, not a new AI product.
