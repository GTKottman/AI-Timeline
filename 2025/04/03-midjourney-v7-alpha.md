# Midjourney V7 alpha

- **Date:** 2025-04-03
- **Who:** Midjourney
- **Type:** model

Midjourney released V7 in alpha, its first new image model in nearly a year. Personalization was on by default, and a Draft Mode ran at half the cost and about ten times the speed at lower resolution. Some features, including upscaling and retexturing, were missing at launch.
