# Meta NLLB-200: No Language Left Behind translation

- **Date:** 2022-07-11
- **Who:** Meta AI
- **Type:** model

Meta posted the No Language Left Behind paper, a machine translation model covering 200 languages, many of them low-resource. It came with the Flores-200 benchmark and aimed to bring translation to languages that commercial systems had ignored.
