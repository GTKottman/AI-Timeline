# Alibaba Qwen releases Qwen3.5 small models

- **Date:** 2026-03-02
- **Who:** Alibaba Qwen team
- **Type:** model

The Qwen3.5 small family adds four dense models at 0.8B, 2B, 4B and 9B parameters, all under Apache 2.0 and natively multimodal with a 262K context window. Artificial Analysis scored the 9B reasoning variant at 32 on its Intelligence Index. Small open models with vision that run on consumer hardware widen local and edge use of capable AI.
