# Microsoft DeBERTa 1.5B tops SuperGLUE human baseline

- **Date:** 2021-01-06
- **Who:** Microsoft Research
- **Type:** research

Microsoft's 1.5-billion-parameter DeBERTa scored 89.9 on the SuperGLUE macro-average, against a human baseline of 89.8, making the single model the first to pass that line. An ensemble reached 90.3 and took the top leaderboard spot. Google's T5 + Meena scored 90.2 within days.
