# Ai2 releases Tulu 3, a fully open post-training recipe and model family

- **Date:** 2024-11-21
- **Who:** Allen Institute for AI (Ai2)
- **Type:** model

Ai2 released Tulu 3, a family of post-trained language models in 8B and 70B sizes, together with its training data, data mixes, recipes, code and evaluation framework. Ai2 claimed the models matched or beat proprietary models from OpenAI, Mistral and Google on math, instruction following and chat benchmarks; those are its own numbers. The arXiv technical report followed on November 22. Full release of the post-training stack made the process reproducible for outside researchers.
