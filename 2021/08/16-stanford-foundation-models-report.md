# Stanford CRFM publishes "On the Opportunities and Risks of Foundation Models"

- **Date:** 2021-08-16
- **Who:** Stanford Center for Research on Foundation Models
- **Type:** paper

A large Stanford-led report defined "foundation models" as broadly trained models adapted to many downstream tasks, such as BERT, GPT-3 and CLIP. It covered capabilities, technical principles, applications and societal risks, and it named homogenization as a concern. The term became the standard way to talk about large pretrained models.
