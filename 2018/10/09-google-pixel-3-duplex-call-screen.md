# Google Pixel 3 launches Call Screen and Duplex on the phone

- **Date:** 2018-10-09
- **Who:** Google
- **Type:** product

At the Made by Google event, Google launched Pixel 3 with Call Screen, which uses the Assistant to answer unknown callers, ask who is calling, and show a live transcript. Duplex booking was set to roll out in US cities from November. Under a new policy, the assistant identified itself as an automated service, answering criticism of the May Duplex demo.
