# Mistral AI raises 600 million euros in Series B

- **Date:** 2024-06-11
- **Who:** Mistral AI; investors include General Catalyst (lead), Lightspeed, a16z, Nvidia, Samsung Venture Investment, Salesforce Ventures
- **Type:** funding

Paris-based Mistral AI closed a Series B of 600 million euros (about 640 million USD), split roughly between equity and debt, at a valuation of about 6 billion USD. It came about a year after its seed round and six months after its 385 million euro round in December 2023. It made Mistral one of the best-funded European AI labs and set up its push to compete with OpenAI and Anthropic on frontier models.
