# Anthropic raises $580M Series B

- **Date:** 2022-04-29
- **Who:** Anthropic
- **Type:** funding

Anthropic announced a $580 million Series B to build large-scale experimental infrastructure for studying and improving the safety of compute-heavy AI models. Its post also summarized early work on reverse-engineering small language models and on making large language models more helpful and harmless. Press coverage reported the round was led by FTX CEO Sam Bankman-Fried, with Caroline Ellison, Jim McClave, Nishad Singh, Jaan Tallinn and the Center for Emerging Risk Research taking part; Anthropic's own post does not name a lead.
