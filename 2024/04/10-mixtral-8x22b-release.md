# Mistral releases Mixtral 8x22B open-weight model

- **Date:** 2024-04-10
- **Who:** Mistral AI
- **Type:** model

Mixtral 8x22B is a larger sparse mixture-of-experts model released with open weights, posted first as a torrent-style link and then on Hugging Face. It extended Mistral's open line to a bigger scale and kept open-weight models competitive with closed frontier systems.
