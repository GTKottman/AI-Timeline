# Baidu opens ERNIE Bot to the general public in China

- **Date:** 2023-08-31
- **Who:** Baidu
- **Type:** product

Baidu opened its ERNIE Bot chatbot to the general public in China after regulatory approval under the new generative-AI measures that took effect August 15. It had been in invited testing since March 16, 2023. Reports said Baidu was among the first companies approved, with 11 firms reported as approved in total. Mass-market generative-AI services in China must pass a security assessment before public release.
