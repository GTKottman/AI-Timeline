# Anthropic launches the Anthropic Institute think tank

- **Date:** 2026-03-11
- **Who:** Anthropic; Jack Clark
- **Type:** other

Anthropic founded the Anthropic Institute, a think tank led by co-founder Jack Clark, to address the challenges powerful AI will pose to societies. It combines three existing internal teams: the Frontier Red Team, Societal Impacts and Economic Research.
