# ICML 2026 Outstanding Paper Awards (Seoul)

- **Date:** 2026-07-05
- **Who:** ICML 2026 (held July 6 to 11 at COEX, Seoul); Zanlin Ni, Shenzhi Wang, Yang Yue and co-authors; Fan Chen, Sinho Chewi, Constantinos Daskalakis, Alexander Rakhlin; Sarah Ball and Phil Hackemann; Mnih et al. (Test of Time)
- **Type:** competition

ICML announced its 2026 awards on July 5, 2026 (per its blog post dated 2026/07/05), ahead of the July 6 to 11 conference in Seoul. Two Outstanding Papers went to diffusion models: "The Flexibility Trap: Rethinking the Value of Arbitrary Order in Diffusion Language Models" and "High-Accuracy Sampling for Diffusion Models and Log-Concave Distributions." An Outstanding Position Paper, by Sarah Ball and Phil Hackemann, argues alignment work is building a censor's toolkit, and the Test of Time Award went to Mnih et al.'s 2016 "Asynchronous Methods for Deep Reinforcement Learning." Secondary coverage disagrees on the number of winners, so check the official ICML post.
