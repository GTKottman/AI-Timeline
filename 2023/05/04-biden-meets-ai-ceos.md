# Biden and Harris meet AI company CEOs on AI risks

- **Date:** 2023-05-04
- **Who:** White House; Sam Altman (OpenAI), Dario Amodei (Anthropic), Satya Nadella (Microsoft), Sundar Pichai (Alphabet)
- **Type:** policy

Vice President Harris led a White House meeting with the chief executives of four AI companies, and President Biden dropped in, and the White House said companies have a moral duty to make sure products are safe. The readout covered transparency, safety evaluation and security against malicious use. The same day the administration announced $140 million for seven new AI research institutes, and the companies agreed to public evaluation of their systems at DEF CON in August. It mattered as the first direct executive-branch meeting with frontier lab leaders after ChatGPT.
