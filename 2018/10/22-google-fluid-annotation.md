# Google publishes Fluid Annotation, a machine-assisted image labeling interface

- **Date:** 2018-10-22
- **Who:** Google AI (Vittorio Ferrari and co-authors; Mykhaylo Andriluka)
- **Type:** software

Fluid Annotation is a human-machine interface for labeling every object and background region in an image. It starts from the output of a strong segmentation network (Mask R-CNN in the press coverage) and lets annotators correct, add or remove regions in the order they choose. The authors reported about three times less annotation time than LabelMe on COCO+Stuff. It mattered as a practical way to speed up building the labeled datasets that vision models depend on.
