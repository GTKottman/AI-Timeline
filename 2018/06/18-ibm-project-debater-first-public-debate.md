# IBM Project Debater holds its first public debate with humans

- **Date:** 2018-06-18
- **Who:** IBM Research (Project Debater); debaters Noa Ovadia and Dan Zafrir
- **Type:** research

On June 18, 2018 in San Francisco, IBM's Project Debater debated two Israeli human debaters. In the first debate, against Noa Ovadia, it argued for subsidizing space exploration; the second debate, against Dan Zafrir, covered telemedicine. A snap poll found most of the audience felt the system enriched their knowledge more than its human opponent did. It mattered as a public demonstration of AI building open-domain spoken argument, though results on who won were mixed.
