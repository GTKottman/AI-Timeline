# Reflection AI unveils Beam, its first open-weight model

- **Date:** 2026-10-05
- **Who:** Reflection AI (Nvidia-backed)
- **Type:** model

Reflection AI announced Beam on October 5, 2026, its first open-weight model: a 501-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with 23 billion parameters active, built for coding, reasoning and agentic work. Reflection says Beam matches GLM-5.2 on advanced reasoning at 3 to 4 times lower inference compute, and concedes that Kimi K3 is still stronger in absolute terms. Weights and a technical report were promised later in October; the sources checked do not confirm release, and all performance figures are the company's own.
