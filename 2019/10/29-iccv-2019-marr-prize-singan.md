# ICCV 2019 best paper (Marr Prize) goes to SinGAN

- **Date:** 2019-10-29
- **Who:** Tamar Rott Shaham, Tali Dekel, Tomer Michaeli (Technion and Google Research); ICCV 2019 organizers
- **Type:** competition

The Marr Prize at ICCV 2019 in Seoul went to "SinGAN: Learning a Generative Model from a Single Natural Image," a generative model trained on one photo that can produce varied, realistic images at several scales. The best student paper award went to "PLMP: Point-Line Minimal Problems in Complete Multi-View Visibility." Synced Review reported the winners on October 29, 2019; the conference ran October 27 to November 2.
