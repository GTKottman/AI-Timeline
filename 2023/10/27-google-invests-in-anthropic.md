# Google to invest up to $2 billion in Anthropic

- **Date:** 2023-10-27
- **Who:** Google (Alphabet); Anthropic
- **Type:** funding

Google agreed to invest up to $2 billion in Anthropic, the maker of the Claude assistant: $500 million upfront and $1.5 billion more over time. The Wall Street Journal broke the story on October 27, citing people familiar with the matter, and Anthropic's spokesperson confirmed the commitment. It came after Google's earlier $300 million investment for a 10% stake in April, and Amazon's plan to invest up to $4 billion. It showed the big cloud providers backing rival frontier labs as well as building their own models.
