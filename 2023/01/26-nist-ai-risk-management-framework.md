# NIST releases AI Risk Management Framework 1.0

- **Date:** 2023-01-26
- **Who:** US National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST)
- **Type:** policy

NIST released version 1.0 of its AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0), a voluntary framework for managing risks to individuals, organizations and society from AI systems. It came with a companion playbook, explainer video, roadmap and crosswalk. NIST said about 400 sets of formal comments from more than 240 organizations shaped the framework, which was developed over about 18 months. It became a common reference point for companies and regulators writing AI governance rules.
