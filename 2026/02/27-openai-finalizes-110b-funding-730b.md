# OpenAI finalizes $110B funding round at $730B pre-money valuation

- **Date:** 2026-02-27
- **Who:** OpenAI; Amazon ($50B, $15B upfront), Nvidia ($30B), SoftBank ($30B)
- **Type:** funding

OpenAI finalized a $110 billion funding round at a $730 billion pre-money valuation, anchored by Amazon, Nvidia and SoftBank. Bloomberg called it the largest funding round in OpenAI's history. OpenAI said the money goes to infrastructure, global distribution and new products including its enterprise platform Frontier. Amazon's $50 billion was to be paid in stages, with $35 billion conditional. One outlet's headline said $100 billion; the body and most reports say $110 billion.
