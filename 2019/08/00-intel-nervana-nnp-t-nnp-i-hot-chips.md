# Intel Nervana NNP-T and NNP-I Unveiled at Hot Chips

- **Date:** 2019-08-00
- **Who:** Intel
- **Type:** hardware

Intel presented its Nervana neural network processors at Hot Chips 2019: the NNP-T (code name Spring Crest), built to train deep learning models at scale within a power budget, and the NNP-I (code name Spring Hill), an inference chip on Intel's 10nm process with Ice Lake cores. The chips were described as upcoming AI accelerators, showing Intel's push into dedicated deep learning ASICs. The exact conference day was not confirmed in the sources, so only the month is recorded.
