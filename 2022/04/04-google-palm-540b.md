# Google PaLM: 540-billion-parameter Pathways Language Model

- **Date:** 2022-04-04
- **Who:** Google Research
- **Type:** model

Google Research introduced PaLM, a dense 540B-parameter language model trained on 6,144 TPU chips with its Pathways system. PaLM beat GLaM, GPT-3 and Gopher on 28 of 29 benchmark tasks, and it showed strong multilingual and reasoning results at a new scale.
