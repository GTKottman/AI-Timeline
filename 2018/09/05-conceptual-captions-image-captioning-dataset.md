# Google releases Conceptual Captions, a 3.3 million image-caption dataset

- **Date:** 2018-09-05
- **Who:** Google AI
- **Type:** dataset

Google AI introduced Conceptual Captions, about 3.3 million image and caption pairs automatically extracted and filtered from billions of web pages. Google reported that models trained on it generalized better than competing approaches. The release also launched the Conceptual Captions Challenge for the machine learning community, giving researchers a large, cleaner alternative to hand-written caption data.
