# Google releases Gemma 2 2B

- **Date:** 2024-07-31
- **Who:** Google / Google DeepMind
- **Type:** model

Google released the 2-billion-parameter version of Gemma 2, completing the Gemma 2 family after the 9B and 27B models of June 27. It shipped the same day as ShieldGemma and Gemma Scope, and Google's official Gemma release log lists it there. Coverage gave no benchmark figures for the 2B model.
