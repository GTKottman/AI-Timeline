# SpiNNaker brain-inspired machine reaches one million cores and is switched on

- **Date:** 2018-11-02
- **Who:** University of Manchester (SpiNNaker project, initially funded by EPSRC and now supported by the EU Human Brain Project)
- **Type:** hardware

The University of Manchester switched on a SpiNNaker machine with its landmark one-millionth processor core. SpiNNaker is a neuromorphic computer built from many small chips that exchange spikes, meant to simulate spiking neural networks in real time. The university described the project as 20 years in conception and over 10 years in construction. Its creators eventually aim to model up to a billion biological neurons in real time.
