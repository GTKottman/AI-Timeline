# Deep reinforcement learning from human preferences paper

- **Date:** 2017-06-12
- **Who:** Paul Christiano, Jan Leike, Tom Brown, Miljan Martic, Shane Legg, Dario Amodei (OpenAI and DeepMind)
- **Type:** paper

The paper showed that agents can learn complex behaviours, including Atari games and simulated robot locomotion, from human comparisons of short trajectory segments, without access to the reward function. Human feedback was under 1% of the agent's environment interactions. It is an early basis for the reward-modelling ideas later used in RLHF.
