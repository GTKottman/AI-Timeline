# Google Gemma 3 QAT quantization-aware models

- **Date:** 2025-04-18
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** model

Google released quantization-aware trained (QAT) versions of all four Gemma 3 sizes (1B, 4B, 12B and 27B), so they keep high quality at 4-bit weights and run on consumer GPUs. Google said the 27B model fits on an RTX 3090, and its reported int4 size dropped from 54GB to 14.1GB. Launch partners included Ollama, LM Studio, MLX and llama.cpp; the size figures are Google's own.
