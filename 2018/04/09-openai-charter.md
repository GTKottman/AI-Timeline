# OpenAI publishes its Charter

- **Date:** 2018-04-09
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** policy

OpenAI published a short Charter stating the principles it says should govern development of artificial general intelligence. Its mission is that AGI, meaning highly autonomous systems that outperform humans at most economically valuable work, benefits all of humanity. The Charter also said OpenAI would publish most of its research but expected safety and security concerns to reduce that publishing over time. It was an early public statement of how a leading lab intended to balance openness and safety.
