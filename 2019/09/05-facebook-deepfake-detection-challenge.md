# Facebook Deepfake Detection Challenge Launched

- **Date:** 2019-09-05
- **Who:** Facebook AI, Partnership on AI, Microsoft, and academics from Cornell Tech, MIT, University of Oxford, UC Berkeley, University of Maryland and University at Albany-SUNY
- **Type:** competition

Facebook, Microsoft, the Partnership on AI and several universities launched the Deepfake Detection Challenge (DFDC) to spur research on detecting manipulated video. Facebook committed $10 million, per Reuters-syndicated coverage, and commissioned researchers to build a dataset of realistic deepfakes using paid actors and no user data. The Partnership on AI's Steering Committee on AI and Media Integrity was to oversee the contest. The dataset of commissioned deepfakes was to be released in December 2019.
