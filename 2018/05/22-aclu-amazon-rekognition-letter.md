# ACLU and coalition urge Amazon to stop selling Rekognition to governments

- **Date:** 2018-05-22
- **Who:** ACLU and ACLU of Northern California; more than two dozen civil-rights groups; Amazon (Jeff Bezos)
- **Type:** policy

The ACLU released public-records emails showing Amazon marketing its Rekognition facial-analysis service to law enforcement. Records showed the City of Orlando and the Washington County Sheriff's Office in Oregon had been customers since 2017, with Orlando operating it as a real-time facial surveillance system. A coalition of more than two dozen civil-rights organizations sent Jeff Bezos a letter asking Amazon to stop selling the technology to governments. It was an early public fight over facial recognition sold to police.
