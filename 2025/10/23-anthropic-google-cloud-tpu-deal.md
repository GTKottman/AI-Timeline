# Anthropic and Google agree to multibillion-dollar deal for up to one million TPUs

- **Date:** 2025-10-23
- **Who:** Anthropic; Google Cloud
- **Type:** hardware

Anthropic and Google Cloud confirmed a deal that gives Anthropic access to up to one million Google TPUs to train and run Claude. Anthropic said it chose TPUs for their price-performance and efficiency. Reports put the value at tens of billions of dollars and more than one gigawatt of capacity coming online in 2026. Financial terms were not disclosed. Anthropic also keeps using AWS Trainium and NVIDIA GPUs, and Amazon remains its primary training partner.
