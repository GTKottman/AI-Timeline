# Facebook publishes details of Fairness Flow bias toolkit

- **Date:** 2021-03-31
- **Who:** Facebook (Meta) AI
- **Type:** software

On 31 March 2021 Facebook published a blog post describing Fairness Flow, an internal toolkit for checking how AI models and labels perform across user groups such as gender or age. It splits a dataset by group and estimates performance per subset, but Facebook said it is only a diagnostic and cannot resolve fairness on its own. Outside coverage called the tool a pilot used voluntarily, and a civil rights audit urged mandatory bias detection. The post matters because it shows a large platform publicly describing its own bias-measurement tooling.
