# AI21 Labs raises $64M

- **Date:** 2022-07-12
- **Who:** AI21 Labs
- **Type:** funding

Israeli language-model company AI21 Labs announced a $64 million funding round to expand its AI-powered language services and its Jurassic model line. The company positioned itself as a competitor to OpenAI for enterprise text generation, and it had recently described its Jurassic-X system, which pairs language models with external tools. The round funded product and API growth.
