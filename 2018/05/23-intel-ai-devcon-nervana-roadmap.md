# Intel AI DevCon lays out Nervana NNP roadmap

- **Date:** 2018-05-23
- **Who:** Intel (Naveen Rao, AI Products Group)
- **Type:** hardware

At Intel's first AI DevCon in San Francisco, Naveen Rao updated the roadmap for Intel's AI chips. The first commercial Nervana NNP, the NNP-L1000 (Spring Crest), was slated for 2019, with 3-4 times the training performance of the first-generation Lake Crest and support for bfloat16. Intel also said over half of its US enterprise customers were using cloud tools on Xeon processors for early AI work.
