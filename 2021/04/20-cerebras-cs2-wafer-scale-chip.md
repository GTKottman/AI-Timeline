# Cerebras launches CS-2 wafer-scale AI system

- **Date:** 2021-04-20
- **Who:** Cerebras Systems
- **Type:** hardware

Cerebras released the CS-2, built on its second-generation wafer-scale engine: 850,000 cores, 2.6 trillion transistors on TSMC 7 nm, and 40 GB of on-chip SRAM. Its single-chip design was an alternative to clusters of GPUs for training very large models.
