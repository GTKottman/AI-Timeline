# Meta launches Muse personal AI agent

- **Date:** 2026-09-08
- **Who:** Meta
- **Type:** product

Meta introduced Muse, a personal AI agent that takes actions for users, such as writing and sending emails, arranging trips and breaking long-term goals into steps. It launched in the United States on iOS, Android and the web, and asks for approval before sensitive actions such as sending email or making a purchase.
