# Ai2 releases Olmo 3 fully open models

- **Date:** 2025-11-20
- **Who:** Allen Institute for AI (Ai2)
- **Type:** model

Ai2 released the Olmo 3 family of fully open 7B and 32B models, including Olmo 3-Base, Olmo 3-Think and Olmo 3-Instruct, under Apache 2.0. Ai2 released the weights and the checkpoints from each training milestone, and pretrained the models on its Dolma 3 open dataset. Ai2 claimed it outperformed fully open models such as Stanford's Marin and open-weight models such as Llama 3.1, a comparison Ai2 made itself. Its release set a high bar for open science, with the training process open to inspection and not only the weights.
