# California Senate passes SB 1047 by 32 to 1

- **Date:** 2024-05-21
- **Who:** California State Senate; Sen. Scott Wiener
- **Type:** policy

The California Senate passed SB 1047 by 32 votes to 1, sending the frontier AI safety bill to the Assembly. The vote showed strong early legislative support for model-safety duties before the industry lobbying that followed.
