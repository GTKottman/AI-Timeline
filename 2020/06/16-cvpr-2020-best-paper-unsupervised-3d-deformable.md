# CVPR 2020 best paper: unsupervised learning of 3D deformable objects

- **Date:** 2020-06-16
- **Who:** Shangzhe Wu, Christian Rupprecht and Andrea Vedaldi (University of Oxford); CVPR 2020 organisers
- **Type:** competition

The CVPR 2020 best paper award went to "Unsupervised Learning of Probably Symmetric Deformable 3D Objects from Images in the Wild." The method learns 3D shape and pose from ordinary single-view image collections, with no 3D annotations, and models objects that are only probably symmetric. The conference was held virtually. The award is confirmed by the project repository and third-party lists; the official CVPR awards page was not reached.
