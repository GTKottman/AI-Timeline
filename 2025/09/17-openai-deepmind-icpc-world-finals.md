# OpenAI and Google DeepMind AI systems perform at ICPC World Finals

- **Date:** 2025-09-17
- **Who:** OpenAI; Google DeepMind
- **Type:** competition

At the ICPC World Finals, an OpenAI system solved all twelve problems, and Google's Gemini 2.5 placed second. It was a strong result in the world's top university programming contest.
