# Intel agrees to acquire Mobileye for $15.3 billion

- **Date:** 2017-03-13
- **Who:** Intel, Mobileye
- **Type:** acquisition

Intel announced it would buy Mobileye, an Israeli maker of machine-learning-based driver assistance and autonomous driving vision systems, for about $15.3 billion in cash ($63.54 per share). Intel put the market for vehicle systems, data and services at up to $70 billion by 2030. It was one of the largest AI-related acquisitions of the period and signaled that large chipmakers would buy computer-vision and AI companies for self-driving cars.
