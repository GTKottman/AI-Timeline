# Black Forest Labs launches with $31M and the FLUX.1 image models

- **Date:** 2024-08-01
- **Who:** Black Forest Labs; Robin Rombach; Andreas Blattmann; Patrick Esser
- **Type:** model

Black Forest Labs, founded by former Stability AI researchers, announced itself with a $31 million seed and released the FLUX.1 family of image models, including open dev and schnell variants. The models were widely praised for text and hand rendering and were later integrated into Grok and Mistral's Le Chat.
