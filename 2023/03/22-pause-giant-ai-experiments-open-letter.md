# Pause Giant AI Experiments open letter

- **Date:** 2023-03-22
- **Who:** Future of Life Institute (FLI) and its signatories
- **Type:** policy

The Future of Life Institute published an open letter asking all AI labs to pause, for at least six months, the training of systems more powerful than GPT-4, and to use the time to build shared safety protocols. It drew tens of thousands of signatures and became the main public call for a training pause. FLI dates the letter March 22, 2023.
