# Google publishes supervised speaker diarization with 7.6% error on CALLHOME

- **Date:** 2018-11-12
- **Who:** Google AI (Chong Wang and colleagues)
- **Type:** research

Google AI described a supervised approach to speaker diarization, the task of working out who is speaking at each moment in multi-speaker audio. Each speaker is tracked by separate, interleaved states of one shared recurrent network. The method reached a 7.6% diarization error rate on the NIST SRE 2000 CALLHOME benchmark with online decoding, and the core algorithms were released publicly.
