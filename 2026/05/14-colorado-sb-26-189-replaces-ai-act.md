# Colorado governor signs SB 26-189, replacing the 2024 AI Act

- **Date:** 2026-05-14
- **Who:** Governor Jared Polis, Colorado legislature
- **Type:** policy

Governor Jared Polis signed SB 26-189 on May 14, 2026 (one law-firm summary gives May 20), repealing and replacing the 2024 Colorado Artificial Intelligence Act before it took effect. A federal magistrate judge had blocked enforcement of the 2024 law in April 2026 after xAI sued. The replacement is much narrower: it drops the original risk-management programs, annual impact assessments and broad algorithmic-discrimination duties in favour of notice and transparency rules for high-risk AI, including employment decisions. It takes effect January 1, 2027, and enforcement waits for Attorney General rulemaking.
