# US National Security Commission on AI releases final report

- **Date:** 2021-03-01
- **Who:** National Security Commission on Artificial Intelligence (NSCAI)
- **Type:** policy

The NSCAI, chaired by Eric Schmidt, released its final report warning that the US was not prepared to defend or compete in the AI era. Its headline recommendation was a sharp rise in federal non-defense AI R&D spending, to about $32 billion by fiscal 2026. The report shaped later US debate on AI funding and national strategy.
