# OpenAI releases ChatGPT app for iOS

- **Date:** 2023-05-18
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** product

OpenAI launched an official ChatGPT app for iPhone, free in the US, with cross-device chat sync, Whisper-powered voice input and no ads. Plus subscribers got GPT-4 and faster responses on mobile. An Android app was promised for later. It mattered because it made ChatGPT a first-party mobile product and brought voice use of Whisper to everyday users.
