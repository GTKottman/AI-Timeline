# NVIDIA AI Workbench and Hugging Face training service at SIGGRAPH

- **Date:** 2023-08-08
- **Who:** NVIDIA; Hugging Face
- **Type:** software

At SIGGRAPH 2023, NVIDIA announced AI Workbench, a unified workspace for building, moving and collaborating on AI projects from a PC or workstation to data centers, public cloud or DGX Cloud. It also announced a partnership that gives Hugging Face users access to NVIDIA DGX Cloud for training and tuning models, with a Hugging Face "Training Cluster as a Service" slated to launch in the coming months. The moves aimed to make custom generative-AI model training easier for enterprises.
