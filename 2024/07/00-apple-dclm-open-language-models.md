# Apple releases DCLM open language models with open data

- **Date:** 2024-07-00
- **Who:** Apple (with University of Washington, Toyota Research Institute, Stanford and others)
- **Type:** model

Apple released DCLM (DataComp for Language Models), a set of fully open language models with weights, training code and datasets, including DCLM-7B and DCLM-1B on Hugging Face. The 7B model was trained on 2.5 trillion tokens of Common Crawl data curated by the DataComp team, and Apple said it beat Mistral-7B on benchmarks with far less compute. The sources checked do not agree on the month (the model card lists June 2024, press coverage July 2024), so the day is set to 00.
