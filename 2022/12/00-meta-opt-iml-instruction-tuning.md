# Meta releases OPT-IML instruction-tuned models

- **Date:** 2022-12-00
- **Who:** Meta AI
- **Type:** model

Meta released OPT-IML, instruction-tuned versions of its OPT language models at 30B and 175B parameters, alongside OPT-IML Bench, a suite of about 2,000 NLP tasks grouped into roughly 100 categories. The paper reported a zero-shot improvement of about 7% over the base OPT models and competitive results with task-specific instruction models. It extended Meta's open-model line into instruction tuning. The exact day was not confirmed; coverage is dated late December 2022.
