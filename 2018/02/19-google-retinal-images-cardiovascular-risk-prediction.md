# Google predicts cardiovascular risk factors from retinal photos

- **Date:** 2018-02-19
- **Who:** Google Research, Verily Life Sciences, Stanford School of Medicine
- **Type:** research

Google Research described a deep learning model trained on retinal images from 284,335 patients. It predicted cardiovascular risk factors such as smoking status and blood pressure, and estimated major adverse cardiac events with moderate accuracy (AUC about 0.7). It showed that a routine eye photo could carry medical signal the eye doctor does not read.
