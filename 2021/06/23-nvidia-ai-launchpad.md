# NVIDIA launches AI LaunchPad

- **Date:** 2021-06-23
- **Who:** NVIDIA (launch partner Equinix)
- **Type:** product

NVIDIA unveiled AI LaunchPad on June 23, 2021, a program that gives enterprises access to NVIDIA AI infrastructure through hybrid-cloud providers. Equinix was the first partner, with the first LaunchPad-enabled Equinix locations slated to come online later that summer. The program aimed to move enterprises from prototypes to production without buying their own AI hardware.
