# Trump administration rescinds the Biden AI diffusion rule

- **Date:** 2025-05-13
- **Who:** US Department of Commerce (Bureau of Industry and Security); Under Secretary Jeffrey Kessler
- **Type:** policy

The Commerce Department formally rescinded the Biden-era AI Diffusion Rule, days before its May 15 compliance date. That rule would have capped AI chip sales to most countries outside 18 close allies and tiered the world by access. Commerce said it would replace it with direct negotiations with countries, and issued guidance that using Huawei Ascend chips violates US export law.
