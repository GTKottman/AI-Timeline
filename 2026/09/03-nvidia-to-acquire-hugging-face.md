# Nvidia agrees to acquire Hugging Face for about $13 billion

- **Date:** 2026-09-03
- **Who:** Nvidia; Hugging Face
- **Type:** acquisition

On September 3, 2026 Nvidia announced, in a blog post by Jensen Huang, that it will buy the open AI model platform Hugging Face for about $12.9 billion. Huang said Hugging Face "will remain an open platform for the entire AI ecosystem". The Information first reported the $12.9 billion price on August 27, 2026, citing an unnamed source, and the deal was not confirmed by either side at that time. Nvidia's blog confirmation did not include the payment split or closing timeline. It is Nvidia's second-largest deal after its $20 billion Groq agreement in December.
