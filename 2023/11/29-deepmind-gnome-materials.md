# DeepMind's GNoME reports 2.2 million new stable crystals

- **Date:** 2023-11-29
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** research

DeepMind's GNoME deep learning system predicted the stability of new inorganic crystals, reporting 2.2 million newly stable materials, about 380,000 of them the strongest candidates for lab synthesis. It showed AI doing large-scale scientific discovery in materials for chips, batteries and solar cells.
