# OpenAI releases GPT-4.1, GPT-4.1 mini and GPT-4.1 nano

- **Date:** 2025-04-14
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** model

OpenAI released three models at once: GPT-4.1, GPT-4.1 mini and GPT-4.1 nano. They were API-only at launch and aimed at coding and instruction following, with a context window of up to 1 million tokens. Third-party pricing pages list the full GPT-4.1 at $2 input / $8 output per million tokens.
