# Scale AI raises $18M Series B led by Index Ventures

- **Date:** 2018-08-07
- **Who:** Scale AI; Index Ventures (lead), Accel, Y Combinator, Drew Houston, Justin Kan
- **Type:** funding

Scale AI, which uses a network of about 10,000 human contractors to label raw data for AI systems, announced an $18 million Series B led by Index Ventures, with Accel and Y Combinator returning. Its autonomous-vehicle customers included self-driving companies. PitchBook dated the round's close to May 2018, which differs from the announcement date. It mattered as an early sign that human-labeled training data had become a funded business line for self-driving AI.
