# EU AI Act obligations for general-purpose AI models become applicable

- **Date:** 2025-08-02
- **Who:** European Commission
- **Type:** policy

Obligations for providers of general-purpose AI models under the EU AI Act became applicable. Providers had to meet transparency and documentation requirements, with extra duties for models that pose systemic risk. It was the first binding AI rule of this kind in a major market.
