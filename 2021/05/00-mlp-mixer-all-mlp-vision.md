# Google introduces MLP-Mixer, an all-MLP architecture for vision

- **Date:** 2021-05-00
- **Who:** Tolstikhin, Houlsby, Kolesnikov, Beyer et al. (Google Research, Brain Team)
- **Type:** paper

Google Research published MLP-Mixer (arXiv 2105.01601), an image architecture built only from multi-layer perceptrons, with no convolutions or attention. It alternates token-mixing MLPs across image patches with channel-mixing MLPs within patches. With large training data or modern regularization it reached competitive ImageNet results at pretraining and inference cost comparable to leading models. The work challenged the view that convolution or attention is required for strong vision models. It was published at NeurIPS 2021. The exact posting day was not confirmed.
