# NVIDIA launches Omniverse Cloud at GTC 2022

- **Date:** 2022-03-00
- **Who:** NVIDIA
- **Type:** software

At the spring GTC 2022 keynote (March 2022; exact day not confirmed), Jensen Huang announced Omniverse Cloud, a service for real-time 3D design collaboration among creators and engineers. Coverage describes it as NVIDIA's first software- and infrastructure-as-a-service offering, with components including Nucleus Cloud, DRIVE Sim and Replicator. It was NVIDIA's bet that 3D worlds for AI would need shared cloud infrastructure.
