# Anthropic launches a Claude iPhone app and a business tier

- **Date:** 2024-05-01
- **Who:** Anthropic
- **Type:** product

Anthropic released a Claude app for iPhone and a Team business plan, competing with ChatGPT's mobile app and enterprise offering. It was Anthropic's first consumer mobile product.
