# Andrej Karpathy leaves Tesla as head of AI

- **Date:** 2022-07-13
- **Who:** Tesla / Andrej Karpathy
- **Type:** people

Andrej Karpathy, Tesla's director of AI and Autopilot Vision, announced in July 2022 that he was leaving the company after more than five years. He had led the computer-vision and neural-network work behind Tesla's self-driving system.
