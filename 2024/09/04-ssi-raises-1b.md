# Safe Superintelligence raises $1 billion at a $5 billion valuation

- **Date:** 2024-09-04
- **Who:** Safe Superintelligence Inc.; Sequoia; a16z; DST Global; SV Angel
- **Type:** funding

Sutskever's Safe Superintelligence raised $1 billion from SV Angel, DST Global, Sequoia and Andreessen Horowitz at a $5 billion valuation. The money was for computing power and hiring, and it showed how much investors would back a research-only lab.
