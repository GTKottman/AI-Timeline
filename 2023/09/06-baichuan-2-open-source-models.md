# Baichuan releases open-source Baichuan 2 model family

- **Date:** 2023-09-06
- **Who:** Baichuan AI
- **Type:** model

Baichuan AI released the Baichuan 2 family of open models in Beijing, including 7B and 13B base models, a 13B chat model and a 4-bit quantized version. The company said the models beat Llama 2 on MMLU and CMMLU and allowed free commercial use. The release added a strong bilingual Chinese-English open-weight option to the field alongside Llama 2.
