# Broadcom to lend Anthropic up to $42 billion to lease TPU chips

- **Date:** 2026-10-01
- **Who:** Broadcom; Anthropic
- **Type:** funding

Reuters reported, citing Broadcom's IPO filing, that Broadcom would provide up to $42 billion of financing to Anthropic through convertible notes that could convert into Anthropic shares. The proceeds are restricted to Anthropic's lease obligations for TPU capacity that Broadcom co-develops with Google, which Reuters says covers about a third of a five-year, $125.2 billion commitment. No notes had been issued, and Anthropic does not expect any sale before its IPO. The filing flags potential conflicts of interest, since Broadcom is both chip supplier and financier.
