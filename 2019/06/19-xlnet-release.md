# XLNet released by CMU and Google Brain

- **Date:** 2019-06-19
- **Who:** Carnegie Mellon University and Google Brain (Zhilin Yang, Zihang Dai, et al.)
- **Type:** model

XLNet is a generalized autoregressive pretraining method for language. Its abstract says it outperforms BERT on 20 tasks, often by a large margin. It learns from factorization orders over the word sequence rather than fixed left-to-right or masked prediction. It was one of the strongest pretrained language models of mid-2019.
