# Anthropic introduces Labs division

- **Date:** 2026-01-13
- **Who:** Anthropic; Mike Krieger
- **Type:** people

Anthropic said Instagram co-founder Mike Krieger is joining its Labs team, which works on experimental products, to work with Ben Mann. Ami Vora was named to lead Anthropic's Product organization instead.
