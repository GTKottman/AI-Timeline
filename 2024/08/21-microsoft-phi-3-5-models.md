# Microsoft releases Phi-3.5 Mini, MoE and Vision models

- **Date:** 2024-08-21
- **Who:** Microsoft
- **Type:** model

Microsoft released three Phi-3.5 models: Phi-3.5-Mini-Instruct (3.8B, 128K context), Phi-3.5-MoE-Instruct (a mixture-of-experts model with 41.9B total and 6.6B active parameters) and Phi-3.5-Vision-Instruct (4.15B). The Mini model was trained on 3.4 trillion tokens, and Microsoft released them under the MIT license. Microsoft's own benchmarks claimed the models beat Gemini 1.5 Flash and Llama 3.1 on some tasks, and OpenRouter lists the Mini model's release as August 21, 2024.
