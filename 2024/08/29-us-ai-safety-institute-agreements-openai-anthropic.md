# US AI Safety Institute signs agreements with OpenAI and Anthropic

- **Date:** 2024-08-29
- **Who:** US AI Safety Institute (NIST); OpenAI; Anthropic
- **Type:** policy

NIST's US AI Safety Institute signed memoranda of understanding with OpenAI and Anthropic, giving the Institute access to major new models before and after public release. The agreements set up joint work on evaluating capability and safety risks and on mitigations, and the Institute would give safety feedback working with the UK AI Safety Institute. Google was reported to be in talks but was not a signatory at the time.
