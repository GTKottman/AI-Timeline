# Google I/O 2019: on-device Assistant, ML Kit and edge AI

- **Date:** 2019-05-07
- **Who:** Google
- **Type:** product

At Google I/O 2019 Google said Google Assistant would run its speech models on-device, making it about 10 times faster, and it showed conversation that works without the "Hey, Google" trigger. It also added translation, object detection and tracking, and AutoML Vision Edge to ML Kit for developers. Pichai said Google had shrunk the speech models, which typically need about 100GB, to under 0.5GB so they fit on a phone.
