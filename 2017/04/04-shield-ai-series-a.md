# Shield AI raises $10.5M Series A led by Andreessen Horowitz

- **Date:** 2017-04-04
- **Who:** Shield AI (San Diego); Andreessen Horowitz (lead), with Homebrew, Bloomberg Beta and Founder Collective; Peter Levine joined the board
- **Type:** funding

San Diego AI drone startup Shield AI closed a $10.5 million Series A led by Andreessen Horowitz, reported by The Register and DroneLife on April 4, 2017. Peter Levine of Andreessen Horowitz joined Shield's board with the round. Some aggregators list $11 million; the press reports used $10.5 million.
