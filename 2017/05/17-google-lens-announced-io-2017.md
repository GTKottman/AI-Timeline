# Google announces Google Lens at I/O 2017

- **Date:** 2017-05-17
- **Who:** Google
- **Type:** product

Google first announced Google Lens at I/O 2017 as a camera-based tool that recognizes objects, text and places. It let users act on what they photographed, with the Assistant helping. Lens was shown as a preview on Pixel phones in October 2017.
