# Mira Murati launches Thinking Machines Lab

- **Date:** 2025-02-18
- **Who:** Thinking Machines Lab; Mira Murati, Barret Zoph, John Schulman
- **Type:** people

Mira Murati, the former OpenAI CTO, launched Thinking Machines Lab on February 18, 2025, with about 30 researchers and engineers, roughly two-thirds of them from OpenAI. Barret Zoph was named chief technology officer and John Schulman chief scientist.
