# Amazon Lex becomes generally available to all AWS customers

- **Date:** 2017-04-19
- **Who:** Amazon Web Services
- **Type:** product

AWS made Amazon Lex, the conversational AI service behind Alexa's deep learning, available to all customers for building voice and text chatbots. The release added publishing channels including Slack, Facebook Messenger and Twilio SMS. It mattered because it turned Alexa-grade speech and language models into a managed service other companies could use.
