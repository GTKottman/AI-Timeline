# DeepMind begins work with US Department of Veterans Affairs on patient deterioration prediction

- **Date:** 2018-02-00
- **Who:** DeepMind (Google), US Department of Veterans Affairs
- **Type:** research

DeepMind announced work with the US Department of Veterans Affairs to use machine learning to predict acute kidney injury and general patient deterioration. It extended DeepMind's healthcare work from the UK NHS to a US federal health system.
