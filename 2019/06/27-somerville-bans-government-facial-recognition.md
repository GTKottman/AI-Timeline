# Somerville, Massachusetts bans government use of facial recognition

- **Date:** 2019-06-27
- **Who:** Somerville City Council (sponsor: Councilor Ben Ewen-Campen; Mayor Joseph Curtatone supportive)
- **Type:** policy

Somerville's city council voted 11-0 to ban government use of facial recognition, covering police investigations and municipal surveillance programs. It was the first East Coast city to pass such a ban and the second US city after San Francisco. The ban applies to government use, and the council sent it to Mayor Joseph Curtatone, who had said he supported it. Vote date is 27 June per the Boston Globe and ACLU of Massachusetts; WBUR reported it on 28 June.
