# Salesforce agrees to acquire Doti

- **Date:** 2025-11-13
- **Who:** Salesforce, Doti
- **Type:** acquisition

Salesforce signed a definitive agreement to acquire Doti, an Israel-based enterprise AI search startup that lets workers retrieve information from internal apps including Salesforce CRM and Slack. The aim was a single agentic search layer across Salesforce products, with Slack as the conversational interface, and an expansion of Salesforce's AI R&D hub in Israel. Terms were not disclosed in the announcement. Salesforce's own news page says the deal completed on December 1, 2025.
