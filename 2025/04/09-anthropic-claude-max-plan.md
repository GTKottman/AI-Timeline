# Anthropic launches Claude Max plans at $100 and $200 a month

- **Date:** 2025-04-09
- **Who:** Anthropic
- **Type:** product

Anthropic launched Claude Max, a subscription tier above Pro. It costs $100 a month for five times Pro's usage and $200 a month for twenty times. Coverage says the $200 tier was priced against OpenAI's $200 ChatGPT Pro plan. Gigazine dated the announcement April 10, likely a time-zone difference.
