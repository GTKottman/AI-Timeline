# Emergent Abilities of Large Language Models

- **Date:** 2022-06-15
- **Who:** Google Research and Stanford University researchers
- **Type:** paper

The paper defined emergent abilities as capabilities that appear in larger language models but are absent in smaller ones, so they cannot be predicted by extrapolating from small models. The idea shaped how labs talked about scaling and what capabilities to expect from bigger models.
