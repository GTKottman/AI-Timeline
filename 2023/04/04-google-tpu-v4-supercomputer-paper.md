# Google publishes TPU v4 supercomputer paper

- **Date:** 2023-04-04
- **Who:** Google (Norman P. Jouppi, David Patterson and co-authors)
- **Type:** hardware

Google posted a paper describing TPU v4, a 4,096-chip supercomputer with optically reconfigurable interconnect and SparseCores for embedding-heavy models. Google reported the system was about 1.2x to 1.7x faster than comparable NVIDIA A100 systems and used less power. It mattered as public evidence that a hyperscaler's custom accelerator and networking could match or beat NVIDIA's GPUs for large training runs.
