# EleutherAI releases Common Pile v0.1, an 8TB openly licensed text dataset

- **Date:** 2025-06-00
- **Who:** EleutherAI, University of Toronto, Hugging Face, Allen Institute for AI
- **Type:** dataset

EleutherAI and partners released The Common Pile v0.1, an 8TB pretraining corpus built only from public domain and openly licensed text across about 30 sources. They also released the collection code, the training mixture and two 7B models, Comma v0.1-1T and Comma v0.1-2T, trained on it. It mattered because it showed a large LLM corpus can be built without unlicensed text while staying roughly competitive.

Exact day not confirmed from the sources (arXiv 2506.05209, Gigazine report dated 2025-06-09).
