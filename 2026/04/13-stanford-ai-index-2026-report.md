# Stanford releases 2026 AI Index Report

- **Date:** 2026-04-13
- **Who:** Stanford Institute for Human-Centered AI (HAI)
- **Type:** research

The 2026 AI Index is a 423-page, nine-chapter annual report covering research, technical performance, responsible AI, economy, science, medicine, education, policy and public opinion. It reports that models now match or exceed human baselines on PhD-level science questions and competition math, and that Terminal-Bench success rose from 20% in 2025 to 77.3% in 2026. It also documents falling transparency among the largest labs and first concrete evidence of AI displacing entry-level work.
