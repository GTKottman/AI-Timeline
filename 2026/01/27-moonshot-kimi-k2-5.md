# Moonshot AI releases open-weight Kimi K2.5

- **Date:** 2026-01-27
- **Who:** Moonshot AI
- **Type:** model

Moonshot released Kimi K2.5, an open-weight natively multimodal model that handles text, images and video, with about 1T total and 32B active parameters and a 256K context. It was Moonshot's first flagship to accept image and video input. Its Agent Swarm feature raised BrowseComp from 60.6% to 78.4%, per company figures. Benchmark claims are self-reported. It joined the Chinese open-weight wave competing with US closed frontier models.
