# Tacotron 2: natural text-to-speech with WaveNet vocoder

- **Date:** 2017-12-16
- **Who:** Google (Jonathan Shen, Ruoming Pang et al.)
- **Type:** paper

Tacotron 2 is a two-stage text-to-speech system: a sequence-to-sequence model converts text to mel spectrograms, and a WaveNet-based vocoder turns them into audio. Its speech scored a mean opinion score of 4.53, against 4.58 for professional recordings. It set a new quality bar for synthetic speech.
