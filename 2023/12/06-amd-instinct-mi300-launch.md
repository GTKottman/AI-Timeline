# AMD launches Instinct MI300 accelerators and ROCm 6 at Advancing AI

- **Date:** 2023-12-06
- **Who:** AMD, Microsoft, Meta, Oracle, Dell, HPE, Lenovo, Supermicro
- **Type:** hardware

At its Advancing AI event AMD launched the Instinct MI300 series data center accelerators, including the MI300X GPU and MI300A APU, built on the CDNA 3 architecture. It also released ROCm 6 as its open software stack. Microsoft, Meta, Oracle, Dell, HPE and others said they would adopt the chips, AMD positioned it as a rival to Nvidia data center GPUs.
