# OpenAI releases GPT-4.1, GPT-4.1 mini and GPT-4.1 nano

- **Date:** 2025-04-14
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** model

OpenAI released three models at once: GPT-4.1, GPT-4.1 mini and GPT-4.1 nano. They were API-only at launch and aimed at coding and instruction following, with a context window of up to 1 million tokens. One source gives the flagship price as $2 input / $8 output per million tokens. The models later reached ChatGPT Plus and Pro users, which one source dates to May 14.
