# Musk-led group makes $97.4 billion bid for OpenAI's nonprofit

- **Date:** 2025-02-10
- **Who:** Elon Musk and a consortium (Valor Equity Partners, Baron Capital, Atreides, Vy Capital, 8VC, xAI per reports); OpenAI board
- **Type:** other

A consortium led by Elon Musk offered $97.4 billion to buy the nonprofit that controls OpenAI, reported on February 10, 2025. Sam Altman publicly declined, and OpenAI's board unanimously rejected the offer on February 14, saying any reorganization would strengthen the nonprofit. Reporting said the bid targeted OpenAI's plan to convert its for-profit arm into a conventional company.
