# Meta AI releases FLAVA, a foundational vision-and-language model

- **Date:** 2021-12-08
- **Who:** Facebook AI Research (Singh, Hu, Goswami, Couairon, Galuba, Rohrbach, Kiela)
- **Type:** model

Facebook AI Research submitted FLAVA to arXiv on 8 December 2021 (arXiv 2112.04482). It is a single model meant to serve as a foundation across vision, language and combined vision-language tasks. It combines contrastive cross-modal alignment with multimodal fusion, which earlier systems usually handled separately. The authors evaluated it on 35 tasks, and the paper was later presented at CVPR 2022.
