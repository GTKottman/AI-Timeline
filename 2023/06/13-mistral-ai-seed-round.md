# Mistral AI raises 105 million euro seed round

- **Date:** 2023-06-13
- **Who:** Mistral AI (Arthur Mensch); Lightspeed Venture Partners (lead)
- **Type:** funding

Paris-based Mistral AI, founded about a month earlier, raised a seed round of about 105 million euros (around $113 million), led by Lightspeed Venture Partners. Dealroom data put it as the largest seed round ever recorded in Europe. The company had no product yet at the time; the FT reported it planned to launch a large language model in early 2024.
