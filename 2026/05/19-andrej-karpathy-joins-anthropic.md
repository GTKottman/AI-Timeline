# Andrej Karpathy joins Anthropic's pre-training team

- **Date:** 2026-05-19
- **Who:** Andrej Karpathy, Anthropic
- **Type:** people

Karpathy, an OpenAI co-founder and former Tesla AI lead, joined Anthropic on its pre-training team under Nick Joseph. He will help start a team that uses Claude to accelerate pretraining research, and says his education work continues. Axios called it a major talent win for Anthropic in the race for senior researchers.
