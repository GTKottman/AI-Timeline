# ACLU and coalition urge Amazon to stop selling Rekognition to governments

- **Date:** 2018-06-18
- **Who:** ACLU and ACLU of Northern California; a coalition of about 70 civil-rights and human-rights organizations; Amazon (Jeff Bezos)
- **Type:** policy

After the ACLU released public-records emails showing Amazon marketing its Rekognition facial-analysis service to law enforcement, a coalition sent a letter to Amazon CEO Jeff Bezos asking the company to stop supporting government surveillance with the technology. The coalition warned it could be misused against communities of color and immigrants. The coalition letter is dated June 18, 2018. It was an early public fight over facial recognition sold to police.
