# Mistral AI launches Mistral Large and partners with Microsoft

- **Date:** 2024-02-26
- **Who:** Mistral AI; Microsoft
- **Type:** model

Mistral released Mistral Large, its flagship proprietary model, alongside the smaller Mistral Small. Microsoft announced a minority investment in Mistral and made its models available through Azure. The deal tied a European open-weight lab to a major cloud and signalled that Mistral would also sell closed models.
