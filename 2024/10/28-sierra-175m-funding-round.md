# Sierra raises $175 million at $4.5 billion valuation

- **Date:** 2024-10-28
- **Who:** Sierra (co-founded by Bret Taylor and Clay Bavor)
- **Type:** funding

Sierra, the enterprise AI customer-service agent startup co-founded by Bret Taylor and Clay Bavor, raised $175 million led by Greenoaks Capital, with Thrive Capital and ICONIQ also participating. The round valued the company at $4.5 billion, up from about $1 billion earlier in 2024. The deal showed investors valuing AI agents that do customer service for enterprises.
