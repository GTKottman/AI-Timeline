# NVIDIA unveils N1 and N1X Arm laptop chips at Computex

- **Date:** 2026-06-01
- **Who:** NVIDIA (partners reported: Dell, Lenovo, ASUS, MSI, HP, Microsoft)
- **Type:** hardware

NVIDIA showed its first Arm-based PC system-on-chips, N1 and N1X, in Jensen Huang's Computex 2026 keynote in Taipei. The flagship N1X is reported to pair a 20-core Arm CPU with a Blackwell GPU and large unified LPDDR5X memory, aimed at Windows-on-Arm laptops against Qualcomm's Snapdragon X line. Sources disagree on partner names and launch timing, and no independent benchmarks were available at reveal. The exact keynote date was not confirmed in the sources checked.
