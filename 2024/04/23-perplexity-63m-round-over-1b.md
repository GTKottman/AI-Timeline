# Perplexity raises $63M and is valued above $1 billion

- **Date:** 2024-04-23
- **Who:** Perplexity AI
- **Type:** funding

Perplexity raised about $63 million at a valuation above $1 billion, led by Daniel Gross, with earlier backers such as Jeff Bezos and Nvidia also taking part. Coverage said the company was generating about $20 million in annual recurring revenue at the time. It was one of the fastest-growing AI search startups of 2024.
