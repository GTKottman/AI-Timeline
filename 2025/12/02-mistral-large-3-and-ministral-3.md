# Mistral releases Mistral Large 3 and Ministral 3

- **Date:** 2025-12-02
- **Who:** Mistral AI
- **Type:** model

Mistral released Mistral Large 3, a sparse mixture-of-experts model with 675B total and 41B active parameters, alongside three small Ministral 3 models with image understanding. The family was open-weight and aimed at both cloud and local deployment.
