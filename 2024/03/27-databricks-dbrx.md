# Databricks releases DBRX open mixture-of-experts model

- **Date:** 2024-03-27
- **Who:** Databricks
- **Type:** model

Databricks released DBRX, an open general-purpose LLM with 132 billion total parameters, of which about 36 billion are active per input, using a fine-grained mixture-of-experts design built on its MegaBlocks project. Databricks claimed it beat LLaMA 2 70B, Mixtral and Grok-1 on several benchmarks; those figures are the company's own. Its open license allowed commercial use for most companies, and it was positioned as a template for enterprises to train their own models.
