# DeepSeek app becomes top free iPhone app in the US, and AI stocks drop

- **Date:** 2025-01-27
- **Who:** DeepSeek; Nvidia and other AI-chip stocks
- **Type:** other

The DeepSeek chatbot app became the most-downloaded free iOS app in the US, passing ChatGPT. On the same day Nvidia shares fell about 17 percent, in what was reported as the largest one-day market value loss in US history. Investors reacted to the prospect that cheaper models could reduce demand for AI chips.
