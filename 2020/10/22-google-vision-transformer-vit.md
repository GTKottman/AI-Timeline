# Google Brain posts Vision Transformer (ViT) paper

- **Date:** 2020-10-22
- **Who:** Google Research, Brain Team
- **Type:** paper

The arXiv paper "An Image is Worth 16x16 Words: Transformers for Image Recognition at Scale" (arXiv:2010.11929) showed that a plain transformer applied to image patches gets excellent results against state-of-the-art CNNs when pretrained on large datasets, with substantially fewer compute resources to train. It challenged the assumption that convolutions are required for strong image classification. The paper was later published at ICLR 2021.
