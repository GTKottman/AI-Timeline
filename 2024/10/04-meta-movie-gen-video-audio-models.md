# Meta announces Movie Gen video and audio generation models

- **Date:** 2024-10-04
- **Who:** Meta AI
- **Type:** model

Meta announced Movie Gen, a family of generative models: a 30-billion-parameter video model that generates up to 16-second clips from text or images and also handles editing of generated content, and a 13-billion-parameter audio model that adds synced sound effects, ambient sound and music to video (up to 45 seconds). Meta's blind-test comparisons against other video models are its own claims. The models were a research announcement and were not publicly usable at launch.
