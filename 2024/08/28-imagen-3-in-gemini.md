# Google adds Imagen 3 image generation to Gemini and launches Custom Gems

- **Date:** 2024-08-28
- **Who:** Google / Google DeepMind
- **Type:** product

Google added Imagen 3 to the Gemini apps and launched Custom Gems, user-built chatbot personas for Gemini Advanced, Business and Enterprise users, rolling out on desktop and mobile in more than 150 countries. Imagen 3 rolled out across Gemini in the coming days, with image generation of people starting as an early-access version in English. Google had paused image generation in Gemini in February after historically inaccurate images. Google said it does not support photorealistic images of identifiable individuals, depictions of minors, or excessively gory, violent or sexual scenes.
