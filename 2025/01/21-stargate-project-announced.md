# Stargate AI infrastructure project announced at the White House

- **Date:** 2025-01-21
- **Who:** OpenAI, SoftBank, Oracle, MGX; announced by President Trump
- **Type:** funding

OpenAI, SoftBank, Oracle and MGX announced the Stargate Project, a plan to invest up to $500 billion in US AI infrastructure over four years. The announcement was made publicly at the White House, with $100 billion to be deployed first.
