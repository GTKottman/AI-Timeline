# Google unveils fourth-generation TPU v4 pods

- **Date:** 2021-05-18
- **Who:** Google
- **Type:** hardware

At Google I/O on May 18, 2021 Google detailed TPU v4, its fourth-generation AI accelerator. Google said it roughly doubled v3 performance and that each pod links 4,096 chips, with a full pod above one exaflop in bfloat16 terms. The chips were offered through Google Cloud rather than sold as hardware. The release set a new scale for publicly offered AI training compute.
