# Google introduces Performers: linear-complexity transformers (FAVOR+)

- **Date:** 2020-09-30
- **Who:** Google Research, DeepMind, University of Cambridge and Alan Turing Institute (Krzysztof Choromanski, Lucy Colwell et al.)
- **Type:** paper

The arXiv paper "Rethinking Attention with Performers" introduced FAVOR+, which approximates softmax attention with random features so that transformer cost grows linearly with sequence length instead of quadratically. Google's research blog covered it on October 23, 2020. The paper was accepted as an oral at ICLR 2021.
