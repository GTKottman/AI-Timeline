# Google fires Margaret Mitchell, co-lead of Ethical AI team

- **Date:** 2021-02-19
- **Who:** Google, Margaret Mitchell
- **Type:** people

Google fired Margaret Mitchell, co-lead of its Ethical AI research team, on February 19, 2021. Google said she broke its code of conduct and security policies by moving confidential files out of the company. Mitchell had been locked out of corporate email after an investigation began in January, and the firing followed the December 2020 exit of co-lead Timnit Gebru. The case became a central example of tension between AI ethics research and corporate control of research.
