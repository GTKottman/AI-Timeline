# SoftBank plans 10 GW AI data center in Ohio

- **Date:** 2026-03-20
- **Who:** SoftBank
- **Type:** other

The US Department of Energy announced on March 20, 2026 a public-private project at the former Portsmouth uranium enrichment site in Ohio, with SoftBank's SB Energy planning a 10-gigawatt AI data center complex. Its power plan is about 9.2 GW of gas generation; a local report cites a $33.3 billion, 9.2 GW gas plant. It is a plan under pursuit, not a finalized build, but it signals the scale of AI infrastructure now being sited around new power supply.
