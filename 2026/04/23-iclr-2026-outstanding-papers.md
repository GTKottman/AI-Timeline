# ICLR 2026 Outstanding Paper Awards (Rio de Janeiro)

- **Date:** 2026-04-23
- **Who:** ICLR 2026 (International Conference on Learning Representations); winning authors include "Transformers are Inherently Succinct" and "LLMs Get Lost in Multi-Turn Conversation"
- **Type:** competition

ICLR 2026 ran in Rio de Janeiro from April 23 to 27, and its main-conference Outstanding Paper awards went to "Transformers are Inherently Succinct" and "LLMs Get Lost in Multi-Turn Conversation," according to a curated list and an MPI-SWS press release that says only two of more than 5,000 accepted papers received the award. One summary adds the Muon optimizer paper "The Polar Express" as a third winner while another lists it as an honorable mention, so the official ICLR list should be checked. Several workshop-level outstanding paper awards were also given separately. The date given is the conference start, not a verified award-ceremony day.
