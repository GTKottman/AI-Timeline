# DeepSeek releases DeepSeek-MoE open models

- **Date:** 2024-01-11
- **Who:** DeepSeek
- **Type:** model

DeepSeek released DeepSeek-MoE 16B base and chat models, 16.4 billion parameters trained from scratch on 2 trillion English and Chinese tokens. It is a mixture-of-experts design that activates only part of its parameters per token. The checkpoints were released for research use.
