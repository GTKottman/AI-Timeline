# Amazon agrees to invest up to $4 billion in Anthropic

- **Date:** 2023-09-25
- **Who:** Amazon / Anthropic
- **Type:** funding

Amazon agreed to invest up to $4 billion for a minority stake in Anthropic, and AWS became Anthropic's main cloud provider for critical workloads. The deal included Trainium and Inferentia chips for training and serving, and Claude on Amazon Bedrock.
