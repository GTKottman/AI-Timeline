# Anthropic agrees to pay at least $1.5 billion to settle authors' class action

- **Date:** 2025-09-05
- **Who:** Anthropic; authors in Bartz v. Anthropic
- **Type:** policy

Anthropic agreed to pay at least $1.5 billion to settle the class action brought by authors over the use of pirated books to train Claude. It followed the June fair-use ruling that found piracy was not covered.
