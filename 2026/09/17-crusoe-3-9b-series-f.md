# Crusoe raises $3.9 billion Series F at $30.9 billion valuation

- **Date:** 2026-09-17
- **Who:** Crusoe; lead investors Atreides Management, Mubadala Capital and Valor Equity Partners; NVIDIA and Qatar Investment Authority also participated
- **Type:** funding

Crusoe, an AI infrastructure provider, announced a $3.9 billion Series F at a $30.9 billion post-money valuation on September 17, 2026, up from a $10 billion valuation in its Series E a year earlier. The company reports more than $140 billion in contracted value and over 6 GW of contracted capacity, and is moving toward modular "Spark" data centers. Some outlets describe the $3.9 billion as only a first close, and an SEC Form D summary shows less sold so far.
