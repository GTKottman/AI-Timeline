# Google unifies its AI work under the "Google AI" name

- **Date:** 2018-05-07
- **Who:** Google
- **Type:** other

Google announced that its research efforts would be branded Google AI, covering the company's state-of-the-art research across teams. The Google Research blog and its linked social channels were renamed, and older links were redirected. It signaled that AI had become the company's central research focus.
