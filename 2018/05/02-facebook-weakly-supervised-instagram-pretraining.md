# Facebook pretrains vision models on billions of Instagram hashtags

- **Date:** 2018-05-02
- **Who:** Facebook AI Research (Dhruv Mahajan and colleagues)
- **Type:** paper

The paper "Exploring the Limits of Weakly Supervised Pretraining" (arXiv:1805.00932) trained convolutional networks to predict hashtags on up to 3.5 billion public Instagram images. The model trained on 3.5 billion images reached 85.4% top-1 accuracy on ImageNet after fine-tuning, the highest single-crop ImageNet-1k top-1 result reported at the time. It showed that noisy social-media labels at very large scale can be a practical pretraining source for image recognition.
