# Cursor raises $2.3 billion at $29.3 billion valuation

- **Date:** 2025-11-13
- **Who:** Anysphere (Cursor); investors Accel, a16z, NVIDIA, Google
- **Type:** funding

Anysphere, the maker of the AI coding editor Cursor, raised a $2.3 billion Series D at a $29.3 billion valuation. NVIDIA and Google were new backers in the round, and Accel and Andreessen Horowitz also invested.
