# Facebook shuts down M, its AI and human personal assistant

- **Date:** 2018-01-08
- **Who:** Facebook
- **Type:** product

Facebook announced it was discontinuing M, a Messenger personal assistant that combined AI with human trainers and was first launched in 2015. The last day of service was January 19, 2018. Facebook said it would keep M's suggestions feature and use what it learned in other AI projects.
