# Facebook Bans Manipulated Media and Deepfakes

- **Date:** 2020-01-06
- **Who:** Facebook
- **Type:** policy

Facebook announced a policy to remove manipulated photos and videos, often called deepfakes. Media is removed if it is edited or synthesized in a way an average viewer would not notice and that would mislead them about what a subject said, or if AI or machine learning was used to merge, replace or superimpose content so it looks authentic. Parody, satire and word-order-only edits are excluded, and fact-checked false content gets reduced reach and warning labels rather than removal. Axios reported that critics had targeted Facebook's decision to leave up a doctored Pelosi video the previous year.
