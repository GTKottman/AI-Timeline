# Vermont court lets state lawsuit against Clearview AI proceed

- **Date:** 2020-09-11
- **Who:** Vermont Attorney General (T.J. Donovan), Clearview AI, Judge Helen Toor (Chittenden Superior Court)
- **Type:** policy

The Chittenden Superior Court denied Clearview AI's motion to dismiss the state's suit over collecting and selling images of Vermonters. The judge rejected Clearview's First Amendment, Section 230, vagueness and standing arguments, holding that the claims concern how Clearview uses data rather than third-party speech. The ruling kept one of the first state-level challenges to a facial recognition company's data practices alive.
