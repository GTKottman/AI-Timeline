# Google open-sources BERT

- **Date:** 2018-11-02
- **Who:** Google AI Language (Jacob Devlin, Ming-Wei Chang)
- **Type:** model

Google released the TensorFlow code and pre-trained BERT-Base and BERT-Large checkpoints on GitHub, with a Colab notebook for fine-tuning on Cloud TPUs. BERT pre-trains a bidirectional Transformer, and Google reported state-of-the-art results on 11 NLP tasks. Open weights let any team fine-tune state-of-the-art language models on its own hardware, and BERT became a foundation for much later NLP work.
