# Amazon AlexaTM 20B few-shot multilingual model

- **Date:** 2022-08-02
- **Who:** Amazon Alexa AI (Saleh Soltan, Prem Natarajan and co-authors)
- **Type:** model

Amazon described AlexaTM 20B, a 20-billion-parameter multilingual encoder-decoder model pre-trained on 12 languages for about 1 trillion tokens. The authors reported state-of-the-art one-shot summarization, beating the 540B PaLM decoder on that task, and state-of-the-art one-shot translation on Flores-101. The arXiv paper was submitted on 2 August 2022.
