# ChatGPT search rolls out to all users, free tier included

- **Date:** 2024-12-16
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** product

On Day 8 of the "12 Days" event, OpenAI made ChatGPT search available to everyone, with a mobile version and search inside Advanced Voice Mode. The search tool had first appeared in October 2024, so sources differ on whether this was a new launch or an upgrade. The free-access part is the change most sources agree on.
