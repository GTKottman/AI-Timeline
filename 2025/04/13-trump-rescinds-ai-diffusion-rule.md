# Trump administration rescinds the Biden AI diffusion rule

- **Date:** 2025-05-13
- **Who:** US Department of Commerce (Bureau of Industry and Security); Under Secretary Jeffrey Kessler
- **Type:** policy

The Commerce Department's BIS formally rescinded the Biden-era AI Diffusion Rule, which was set to take effect May 15. BIS said it would not enforce the rule and would issue a replacement rule in the future. The same day it issued guidance that using or servicing Huawei Ascend 910B, 910C and 910D chips is prohibited.
