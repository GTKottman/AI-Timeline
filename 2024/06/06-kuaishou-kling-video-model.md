# Kuaishou unveils Kling text-to-video model

- **Date:** 2024-06-06
- **Who:** Kuaishou Technology
- **Type:** model

Kuaishou unveiled Kling, its proprietary text-to-video model, and opened it to public testing through its KuaiYing video app. Kuaishou claimed clips up to two minutes long at 30 fps and up to 1080p, and it was the first Chinese video model that ordinary users could try, positioned as a rival to OpenAI's Sora. Coverage dates the announcement to June 6, while Wikipedia gives June 10 for the beta, so the day is taken from the earlier source.
