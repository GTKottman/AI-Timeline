# Mistral releases OCR 3 document model

- **Date:** 2025-12-17
- **Who:** Mistral AI
- **Type:** model

Mistral announced OCR 3, a document-understanding model that extracts text and embedded images from forms, scans, complex tables and handwriting, and outputs markdown with HTML-reconstructed tables. Mistral reported a 74% win rate over its OCR 2 on its internal customer-based test set; those figures are vendor-reported. It is priced at $2 per 1,000 pages, halved on the batch API, and is part of the wave of specialist document models competing with general LLM vision.
