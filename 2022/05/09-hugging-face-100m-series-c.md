# Hugging Face raises $100M Series C at $2B valuation

- **Date:** 2022-05-09
- **Who:** Hugging Face; led by Lux Capital with Sequoia and Coatue
- **Type:** funding

Hugging Face announced a $100 million Series C on May 9, 2022, led by Lux Capital with Sequoia and Coatue joining; TechCrunch put the valuation at $2 billion. It followed a $40 million Series B from Addition about a year earlier. The round funded hiring and product work on the Hub, the open platform where machine learning models and datasets are shared.
