# AWS launches Trainium, its custom chip for ML training

- **Date:** 2020-12-01
- **Who:** Amazon Web Services (Andy Jassy keynote)
- **Type:** hardware

AWS unveiled Trainium at re:Invent as its second custom AI chip after the Inferentia inference chip. AWS claimed better price-performance than comparable GPU instances, with figures that were vendor claims, and said it would support TensorFlow, PyTorch and MXNet through the same SDK as Inferentia. Trainium was a sign that cloud providers would design their own training silicon to cut dependence on Nvidia.
