# California AB 489 bars AI from implying a healthcare license

- **Date:** 2025-10-13
- **Who:** Governor Gavin Newsom, California legislature
- **Type:** policy

California Governor Gavin Newsom signed AB 489, which bars AI systems from using terms, letters or phrases that falsely suggest a healthcare license, or that advice comes from a licensed person. It takes effect January 1, 2026, and licensing boards enforce it, with each violation a separate offense. The bill passed with 39 Senate and 79 Assembly votes. Sources give the signing date as October 12 or 13, 2025.
