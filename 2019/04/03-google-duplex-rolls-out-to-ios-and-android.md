# Google Duplex booking assistant rolls out to iOS and Android

- **Date:** 2019-04-03
- **Who:** Google
- **Type:** product

Google expanded Duplex, its AI system that makes restaurant reservations by phone, beyond Pixel phones to iOS and Android devices in the US. Google added a disclosure at the start of each call and let businesses opt out of automated calls after privacy concerns. The rollout showed conversational voice AI working in live phone calls with real businesses.
