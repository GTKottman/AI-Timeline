# Stability AI releases Stable Diffusion 3 Medium weights

- **Date:** 2024-06-12
- **Who:** Stability AI
- **Type:** model

Stability AI published the open weights of Stable Diffusion 3 Medium, a 2-billion-parameter image model, on Hugging Face. It was much smaller than the 8-billion-parameter SD3 announced in April, so it could run on consumer GPUs, and Stability said it improved photorealism, hands and faces, and text rendering. Sources disagree on the licence: one describes a non-commercial licence, another a research licence plus a paid creator licence for businesses.
