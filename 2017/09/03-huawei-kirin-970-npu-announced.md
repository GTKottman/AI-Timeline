# Huawei unveils Kirin 970 with dedicated neural processing unit

- **Date:** 2017-09-03
- **Who:** Huawei (HiSilicon)
- **Type:** hardware

Huawei unveiled the Kirin 970 at IFA 2017 in Berlin, its first smartphone chipset with a built-in neural processing unit for on-device deep learning. Huawei claimed the NPU delivered about 25 times the performance and 50 times the efficiency of a quad-core Cortex-A73 CPU on AI tasks, figures from the company's own testing. It mattered as one of the first phone chips to put a dedicated AI accelerator in mass-market hardware.
