# DeepMind Chinchilla: compute-optimal training of a 70B model

- **Date:** 2022-03-29
- **Who:** DeepMind
- **Type:** model

DeepMind posted the paper 'Training Compute-Optimal Large Language Models' and its 70B-parameter Chinchilla model, trained on about four times more data than the 280B Gopher with the same compute budget. Chinchilla beat Gopher, GPT-3 and Megatron-Turing NLG on many tasks and reached 67.5% on MMLU, which changed how labs balance model size and training data.
