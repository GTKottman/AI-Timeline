# OpenAI publishes "AI and Compute"

- **Date:** 2018-05-16
- **Who:** OpenAI
- **Type:** research

OpenAI estimated that the compute used in the largest AI training runs had grown exponentially since 2012, doubling about every 3.4 months, a pace far faster than Moore's Law. The post gave the field a concrete way to think about scaling and hardware demand. Later commentators questioned the exact doubling time, but the post became a widely cited reference on scaling.
