# Getty Images sues Stability AI over training on its photos

- **Date:** 2023-01-17
- **Who:** Getty Images
- **Type:** policy

Getty Images began legal action against Stability AI in London over alleged copying of millions of copyrighted photos to train Stable Diffusion. A later US suit followed in February 2023. The case became a bellwether for training-data copyright disputes.
