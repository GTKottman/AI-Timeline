# ICLR 2022 outstanding paper awards announced

- **Date:** 2022-04-20
- **Who:** ICLR 2022 program chairs (Yan Liu, Chelsea Finn, Yejin Choi, Marc Deisenroth)
- **Type:** competition

The ICLR 2022 program chairs named seven Outstanding Paper Award winners and three Honorable Mentions from about 1,095 submissions, ahead of the virtual conference held April 25 to 29. The winners include "Bootstrapped Meta-Learning", "Hyperparameter Tuning with Renyi Differential Privacy", "Neural Collapse Under MSE Loss", "Learning Strides in Convolutional Neural Networks" and "Analytic-DPM".
