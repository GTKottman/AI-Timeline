# Timnit Gebru forced out of Google's Ethical AI team

- **Date:** 2020-12-02
- **Who:** Google; Timnit Gebru (former technical co-lead, Ethical AI team)
- **Type:** people

Google ended the employment of Timnit Gebru, co-lead of its Ethical AI team, after a dispute over a research paper on the risks of large language models. Gebru said she was fired; Google said she had resigned. The episode became a major public controversy over research independence and diversity in AI labs.
