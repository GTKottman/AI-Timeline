# Microsoft signs 20-year nuclear power deal to restart Three Mile Island unit

- **Date:** 2024-09-20
- **Who:** Microsoft, Constellation Energy
- **Type:** funding

Microsoft agreed to buy the output of Constellation's Three Mile Island Unit 1 for 20 years to restart the plant, renamed the Crane Clean Energy Center, with a target online date of 2028. The deal is a 20-year power purchase agreement to supply Microsoft's data centers with carbon-free power. Regulatory approvals, including Nuclear Regulatory Commission review, were still pending at announcement.
