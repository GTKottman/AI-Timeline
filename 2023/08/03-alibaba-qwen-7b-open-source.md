# Alibaba open-sources Qwen-7B and Qwen-7B-Chat

- **Date:** 2023-08-03
- **Who:** Alibaba Cloud (Tongyi Qianwen)
- **Type:** model

Alibaba open-sourced Qwen-7B, a 7-billion-parameter model pretrained on about 2.2 trillion tokens of Chinese and English text, books and code, plus a chat-tuned Qwen-7B-Chat. Both were posted on ModelScope and Hugging Face on August 3. Companies with more than 100 million monthly active users need a separate license from Alibaba, and smaller ones may use the models free. Alibaba's own C-Eval score (59.6) was the highest among the Chinese models it compared.
