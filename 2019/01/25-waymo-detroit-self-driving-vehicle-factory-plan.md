# Waymo plans a self-driving vehicle factory in the Detroit area

- **Date:** 2019-01-25
- **Who:** Waymo (Alphabet)
- **Type:** product

Waymo said it would set up a factory in the Detroit area to build its self-driving hardware into vehicles, starting with Chrysler Pacifica minivans and later Jaguar I-Pace crossovers. The plan came with a state incentive grant of up to $8 million from the Michigan Strategic Fund. Building its own production line was a step toward running a self-driving fleet at scale rather than retrofitting cars one at a time. Reports differ on the exact day, but the month is January 2019. The plant opened later in 2019.
