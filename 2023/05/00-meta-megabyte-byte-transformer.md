# Meta publishes MEGABYTE multiscale byte-level model paper

- **Date:** 2023-05-12
- **Who:** Meta AI
- **Type:** paper

Meta AI published MEGABYTE, a multiscale transformer that predicts sequences of around a million bytes by splitting them into patches with a local model and a global model. The design lowers attention cost and allows more parallel computation, and the authors reported competitive results against subword models, suggesting byte-level models could replace tokenization.
