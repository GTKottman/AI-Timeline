# Google research: real-time sign language detection for video calls

- **Date:** 2020-10-01
- **Who:** Google Research (Amit Moryossef, then a Google Research intern)
- **Type:** research

Google published a lightweight model that detects when a person is signing, so video conferencing tools can treat the signer as the active speaker. It uses pose-estimation features, reaching about 80% accuracy with a linear model and up to 91% with a recurrent model, and it runs in under 4 ms per frame. A demo was shown at ECCV 2020.
