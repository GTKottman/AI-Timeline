# Stability AI StableCode: open-source coding LLM

- **Date:** 2023-08-08
- **Who:** Stability AI
- **Type:** model

Stability AI announced StableCode, its first coding LLM, in variants covering a base completion model with 4K context, an instruction-tuned model, and a long-context model with up to 16,000 tokens. The base model drew on BigCode's programming-language data, and the models were posted to Hugging Face. It was aimed at everyday programmers and at learners.
