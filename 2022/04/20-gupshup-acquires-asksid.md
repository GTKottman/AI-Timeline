# Gupshup acquires AskSid

- **Date:** 2022-04-20
- **Who:** Gupshup; AskSid (Bengaluru)
- **Type:** acquisition

Gupshup, a conversational messaging platform, announced on April 20, 2022 that it had acquired AskSid, a Bengaluru conversational-AI provider for e-commerce and retail, for an undisclosed sum. Gupshup said AskSid's full-stack solution automates 92% of support conversations for its customers; this is a company-reported figure. The deal came about two weeks after Gupshup's purchase of Active.ai.
