# Get To The Point: Summarization with Pointer-Generator Networks

- **Date:** 2017-04-14
- **Who:** Abigail See, Peter J. Liu, Christopher D. Manning (Stanford)
- **Type:** paper

arXiv paper that combines copying words from the source text with generating words from a fixed vocabulary, using a pointer-generator network, plus a coverage mechanism to stop repetition. The authors said it beat the abstractive summarization state of the art on CNN/Daily Mail by at least 2 ROUGE points. It was published at ACL 2017, and the code was released on GitHub.
