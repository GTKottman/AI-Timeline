# DeepMind AlphaGeometry solves olympiad geometry problems

- **Date:** 2024-01-17
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** research

DeepMind published AlphaGeometry in Nature, an AI system for olympiad-level geometry that pairs a neural language model with a symbolic deduction engine. It solved 25 of 30 benchmark problems (IMO-AG-30) within the standard time limit, against 10 for the previous best method, Wu's method, and close to the average human gold medalist score. It was trained on synthetic data instead of human proofs, which mattered as an early example of neuro-symbolic reasoning reaching olympiad level.
