# Prototypical Networks for Few-shot Learning

- **Date:** 2017-03-15
- **Who:** Jake Snell, Kevin Swersky, Richard S. Zemel
- **Type:** paper

arXiv paper that represents each class by the mean embedding of its few labelled examples (its prototype) and classifies new inputs by Euclidean distance to those prototypes. The authors argued this simpler inductive bias beat more complex meta-learning architectures in few-shot classification. They also extended the method to zero-shot learning on CU-Birds.
