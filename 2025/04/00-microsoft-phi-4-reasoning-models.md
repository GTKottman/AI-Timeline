# Microsoft releases Phi-4-reasoning, Phi-4-reasoning-plus and Phi-4-mini-reasoning

- **Date:** 2025-04-00
- **Who:** Microsoft
- **Type:** model

Microsoft released a family of small open-weight reasoning models under the MIT license. Phi-4-reasoning and Phi-4-reasoning-plus are about 14 billion parameters, and Phi-4-mini-reasoning is about 3.8 billion. The release day is unconfirmed: one source gives April 30, 2025 and no second source was found.
