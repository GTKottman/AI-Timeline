# Anthropic hires Instagram co-founder Mike Krieger as chief product officer

- **Date:** 2024-05-15
- **Who:** Anthropic; Mike Krieger
- **Type:** people

Anthropic hired Instagram co-founder Mike Krieger as chief product officer to lead its consumer and developer products. The hire was a sign of Anthropic building out product leadership to compete with OpenAI.
