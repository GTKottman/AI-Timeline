# Facebook AI Research releases Blender open-domain chatbot

- **Date:** 2020-04-29
- **Who:** Facebook AI Research
- **Type:** model

Facebook AI Research introduced Blender, an open-domain chatbot that the company said beat Google's Meena in conversational quality. It combined large-scale pretraining with blended skills such as empathy and personality in its training. The release pushed the open-domain chatbot race forward and set the stage for BlenderBot 2 later in the year.
