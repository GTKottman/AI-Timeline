# Zhipu releases GLM-4.6

- **Date:** 2025-09-30
- **Who:** Zhipu AI / Z.ai
- **Type:** model

Zhipu AI (Z.ai) released GLM-4.6, an update to GLM-4.5 with a mixture-of-experts design of about 357 billion parameters and a context window expanded from 128K to 200K tokens. It targets agentic reasoning, coding and long-context work, and it is reported as MIT-licensed and available on OpenRouter. The release date and specs rest on aggregator listings, not an official Z.ai page, so they should be checked against Z.ai's release notes.
