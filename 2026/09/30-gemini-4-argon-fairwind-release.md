# Google releases Gemini 4 Argon to selected cyber defenders

- **Date:** 2026-09-30
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** model

Google announced Gemini 4 Argon on September 30, 2026, and made it available first to selected cyber defenders through the Fairwind Program. It has a one-million-token output limit, up from 64,000 tokens in the previous model.
