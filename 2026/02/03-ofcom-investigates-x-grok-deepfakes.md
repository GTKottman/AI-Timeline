# UK regulator Ofcom opens investigation into X over Grok deepfakes

- **Date:** 2026-02-03
- **Who:** Ofcom; X; xAI
- **Type:** policy

Britain's communications regulator Ofcom opened an investigation into X over sexualised deepfakes made with Grok. It was one of the first formal regulatory probes of an AI image tool's safety failures under UK online-safety law.
