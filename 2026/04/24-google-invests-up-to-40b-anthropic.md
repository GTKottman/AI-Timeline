# Google commits up to $40 billion to Anthropic

- **Date:** 2026-04-24
- **Who:** Google (Alphabet), Anthropic
- **Type:** funding

Google committed $10 billion in cash at a $350 billion valuation now, with another $30 billion conditional on Anthropic meeting performance targets. The money supports a major expansion of Anthropic's compute capacity. It came days after Amazon's up-to-$25 billion commitment, so the two largest clouds are now both funding one frontier lab.
