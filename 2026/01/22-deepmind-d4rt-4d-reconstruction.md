# Google DeepMind unveils D4RT for dynamic 4D scene reconstruction and tracking

- **Date:** 2026-01-22
- **Who:** Google DeepMind
- **Type:** research

Google DeepMind announced D4RT (Dynamic 4D Reconstruction and Tracking), a feedforward model that answers queries about any point in space and time to produce depth, point tracks and camera parameters in one interface. DeepMind reports 18x to 300x speedups over earlier methods, including a one-minute video processed in about five seconds on one TPU, and state-of-the-art results on TAPVid-3D. The figures are DeepMind's own claims. The underlying arXiv paper dates from December 2025.
