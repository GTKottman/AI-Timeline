# NIST announces US AI Safety Institute leadership and consortium

- **Date:** 2024-02-08
- **Who:** US Department of Commerce, NIST
- **Type:** policy

NIST announced the executive leadership of the new US AI Safety Institute (USAISI), with Elizabeth Kelly as its director and Elham Tabassi as chief technology officer, and it announced a consortium of over 200 commercial and academic participants. The institute was created in response to the October 2023 executive order on safe and trustworthy AI. Membership in most cases required a consortium agreement with NIST. It became the main US government body for testing and evaluating frontier AI models.
