# Researchers use generative AI to design working bacteriophage genomes

- **Date:** 2026-08-06
- **Who:** Academic researchers (published in Science)
- **Type:** research

Researchers reported using generative AI to design novel bacteriophage genomes that worked against phage-resistant bacteria, published in Science. It was an early demonstration of generative models designing functional whole genomes.
