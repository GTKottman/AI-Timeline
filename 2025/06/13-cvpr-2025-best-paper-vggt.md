# VGGT wins CVPR 2025 Best Paper

- **Date:** 2025-06-13
- **Who:** Jianyuan Wang, Minghao Chen, Nikita Karaev, Andrea Vedaldi, Christian Rupprecht, David Novotny (Oxford VGG and Meta AI)
- **Type:** competition

CVPR and the IEEE Computer Society announced VGGT as the sole Best Paper of CVPR 2025. VGGT is a feed-forward network that estimates 3D scene properties such as camera poses and depth in one pass over many input views. It is a learned, fast alternative to classic structure-from-motion methods.
