# Google launches the Inclusive Images Competition on Kaggle

- **Date:** 2018-09-06
- **Who:** Google Research, with the NeurIPS Competition Track
- **Type:** competition

Google announced the Inclusive Images Competition on Kaggle. Entrants trained models on Open Images, which is mostly North American and European imagery, and were judged on images from other parts of the world. The goal was models that stay accurate when training data is geographically skewed, a fairness problem that affects many deployed vision systems.
