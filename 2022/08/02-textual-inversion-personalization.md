# Textual Inversion: personalize text-to-image models with a few images

- **Date:** 2022-08-02
- **Who:** Tel Aviv University and NVIDIA researchers
- **Type:** paper

Textual Inversion teaches a frozen text-to-image model a new word that stands for a concept shown in three to five example images. It let users personalize generated images through ordinary prompts without retraining the full model.
