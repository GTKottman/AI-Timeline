# Graphcore raises $50 million from Sequoia Capital

- **Date:** 2017-11-12
- **Who:** Graphcore, Sequoia Capital
- **Type:** funding

Graphcore raised $50 million led by Sequoia Capital, a few months after its $30 million Series B. The round came amid a wave of investment in AI chip startups that were trying to challenge NVIDIA in training hardware. It showed that investors were funding alternative AI accelerator designs.
