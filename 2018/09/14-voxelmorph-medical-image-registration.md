# VoxelMorph: learned medical image registration

- **Date:** 2018-09-14
- **Who:** Guha Balakrishnan, Amy Zhao, Mert R. Sabuncu, John Guttag, Adrian V. Dalca (MIT and Harvard/MGH)
- **Type:** research

VoxelMorph trains a neural network to predict how to warp one 3D medical scan to match another. Its accuracy is close to leading methods, but it runs far faster than per-pair optimisation. It showed deep learning making medical image analysis practical at scale.
