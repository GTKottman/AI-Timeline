# MiniMax releases M2.5 agent model

- **Date:** 2026-02-12
- **Who:** MiniMax
- **Type:** model

MiniMax released M2.5, a successor to its M2 line aimed at agentic and office work, in a standard and a lower-latency variant. Reported figures include 80.2% on SWE-Bench Verified and 76.3% on BrowseComp, and about 37% faster task completion than M2.1. Listings put the weights under a modified MIT license, and the specs (context length, active parameters) differ across providers, so they are provisional.
