# Instinct raises $1 billion Series C for personal AI agent

- **Date:** 2026-09-28
- **Who:** Instinct (founder Noah Shinn); investors Sequoia Capital, Benchmark and Coatue
- **Type:** funding

San Francisco startup Instinct raised $1 billion in a Series C at a reported $10 billion valuation, roughly four times its valuation at a $250 million Series B a month earlier. Its personal agent is reached by text or phone and handles tasks such as trip planning, grocery orders, ticket booking and subscription cancellation. Sources differ on when the invite-only service launched (August or February 2026). The round does not disclose revenue or user counts.
