# Facebook expands its Montreal AI research lab

- **Date:** 2018-09-20
- **Who:** Facebook AI Research (FAIR), led in Montreal by Joelle Pineau
- **Type:** other

Facebook announced it would grow its Montreal AI research lab, with reports saying the team would expand to about 60 people. Coverage gave conflicting multipliers (triple versus quadruple the researchers), so the exact target is unclear. The lab's early work covered medical imaging and generating content from photographs. The expansion added to FAIR's network of research hubs outside Silicon Valley.
