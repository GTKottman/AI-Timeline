# Google Imagen Video: text-to-video diffusion cascade

- **Date:** 2022-10-05
- **Who:** Google Research
- **Type:** model

Google posted Imagen Video, a text-conditional video generator built from a cascade of video diffusion models. It produced high-definition clips from text, extending the Imagen image approach to motion.
