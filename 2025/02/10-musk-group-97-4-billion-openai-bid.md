# Musk-led group makes $97.4 billion bid for OpenAI's nonprofit

- **Date:** 2025-02-10
- **Who:** Elon Musk and a consortium (reported members include Valor Equity Partners, Baron Capital and xAI); OpenAI board
- **Type:** other

A consortium led by Elon Musk offered $97.4 billion to buy the nonprofit that controls OpenAI, reported on February 10, 2025. OpenAI's board unanimously rejected the bid on Friday, February 14, 2025. Chairman Bret Taylor said any reorganization would strengthen the nonprofit. The bid targeted OpenAI's plan to convert its for-profit arm into a public benefit corporation.
