# CheXNet: radiologist-level pneumonia detection on chest X-rays

- **Date:** 2017-11-14
- **Who:** Stanford ML Group (Pranav Rajpurkar, Andrew Ng et al.)
- **Type:** paper

CheXNet is a 121-layer convolutional network trained on the public ChestX-ray14 dataset of about 112,000 frontal X-rays. It flags pneumonia with a heatmap of the regions behind the prediction and was reported to match or beat the average performance of four academic radiologists. It became a widely cited example of deep learning in medical imaging.
