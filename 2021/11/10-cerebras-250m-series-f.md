# Cerebras raises $250 million Series F at over $4 billion valuation

- **Date:** 2021-11-10
- **Who:** Cerebras Systems; led by Alpha Wave Ventures and Abu Dhabi Growth Fund
- **Type:** funding

Cerebras Systems, maker of the wafer-scale CS-2 AI system, announced a $250 million Series F at a valuation above $4 billion on November 10, 2021. The company's press release names Alpha Wave Ventures and Abu Dhabi Growth Fund as leads. The release says the company had raised about $720 million to date, while some outlets report about $750 million.
