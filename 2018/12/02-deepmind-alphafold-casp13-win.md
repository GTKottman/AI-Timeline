# DeepMind's AlphaFold wins CASP13 protein structure prediction

- **Date:** 2018-12-02
- **Who:** DeepMind
- **Type:** competition

AlphaFold placed first in the 13th Critical Assessment of protein Structure Prediction (CASP13), held in Cancun. It produced accurate structures for 24 of 43 free-modelling domains, while the next best method managed 14. DeepMind's win was an early signal that deep learning could make large gains on protein structure, a problem central to drug discovery.
