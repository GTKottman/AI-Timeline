# OpenAI agrees to acquire Neptune

- **Date:** 2025-12-03
- **Who:** OpenAI; Neptune (Neptune Labs, experiment tracking and training-monitoring tools)
- **Type:** acquisition

OpenAI announced a definitive agreement to acquire Neptune, a startup whose tools let researchers track experiments and monitor model training. Reports said the deal is all-stock and below $400 million, and that Neptune would wind down its external service for other customers, including Samsung and HP. The acquisition brings model-training observability in-house for OpenAI's research teams.
