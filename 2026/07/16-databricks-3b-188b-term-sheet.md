# Databricks signs term sheet for $3 billion round at $188 billion valuation

- **Date:** 2026-07-16
- **Who:** Databricks, Coatue Management (lead)
- **Type:** funding

Databricks signed a term sheet on July 16, 2026 for a round at a $188 billion valuation, led by existing investor Coatue. The valuation is about 40% above the earlier mark near $134 billion. The Wall Street Journal reported the round at about $3 billion; Databricks has not confirmed that amount, and the round had not closed when reported. The company says the money is aimed at products including Genie, Unity AI Gateway and Lakebase, and one source adds acquisitions. An earlier report of a $10 billion raise is not supported by the sources found.
