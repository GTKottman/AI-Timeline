# Stability AI releases Stable LM 2 1.6B

- **Date:** 2024-01-19
- **Who:** Stability AI
- **Type:** model

Stability AI released Stable LM 2 1.6B, a small language model with base and instruction-tuned (Stable LM 2 Zephyr) variants, trained on 2 trillion tokens across seven languages: English, Spanish, German, French, Italian, Dutch and Portuguese. Stability claimed it beat comparable sub-2B models and small models such as Phi-2 on most benchmarks. It mattered because it made a multilingual model small enough to run on modest hardware, under a non-commercial research license with a membership option for commercial use.
