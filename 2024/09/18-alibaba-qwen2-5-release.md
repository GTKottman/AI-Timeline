# Alibaba releases Qwen2.5 model family

- **Date:** 2024-09-18
- **Who:** Alibaba Cloud (Qwen team)
- **Type:** model

Qwen2.5 expanded the Qwen family with many model sizes from 0.5B to 72B parameters and specialised Coder and Math variants. Its pre-training data grew from 7 trillion to 18 trillion tokens, more than Qwen2 was trained on. It became one of the most-downloaded open model families of late 2024.
