# US drafts worldwide AI chip export rules

- **Date:** 2026-03-05
- **Who:** US Commerce Department (reported draft), Nvidia and AMD (most affected)
- **Type:** policy

News outlets reported on March 5, 2026 that the US was drafting new AI chip export rules covering most high-end processors sold by US companies. Under the reported draft, licensing would depend on the amount of computing power in an export, with large shipments (above 200,000 GB300 units owned by one company in one country) likely requiring host-government involvement. The draft text was not public when reported, so the details are press accounts only.
