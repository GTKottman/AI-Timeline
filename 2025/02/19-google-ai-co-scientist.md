# Google launches AI co-scientist, a multi-agent research assistant

- **Date:** 2025-02-19
- **Who:** Google Research (built on Gemini 2.0)
- **Type:** research

Google introduced the AI co-scientist, a system built on Gemini 2.0 that takes a research goal in plain language and returns testable hypotheses, a summary of related literature and a possible experimental approach. Several specialised agents work together on the output. Google framed it as a collaborator for expert scientists rather than an automated scientist, and early access went to its Trusted Tester Program.
