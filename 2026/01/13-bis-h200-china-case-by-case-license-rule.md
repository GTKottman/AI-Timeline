# Commerce Moves H200-Class AI Chip Exports to China to Case-by-Case Review

- **Date:** 2026-01-13
- **Who:** US Commerce Department, Bureau of Industry and Security (BIS); Nvidia (H200); AMD (MI325X)
- **Type:** policy

BIS announced a final rule that replaces the presumption of denial for AI chips such as Nvidia's H200 and AMD's MI325X with case-by-case license review for end users in China and Macau, if security conditions are met. The eligibility test is technical (total processing performance below 21,000 and DRAM bandwidth below 6,500 GB/s), and applicants must certify US supply sufficiency and third-party testing and screening of Chinese buyers. The rule took effect January 15, 2026 after Federal Register publication, following Trump's December 2025 decision to allow H200 sales to approved Chinese customers.
