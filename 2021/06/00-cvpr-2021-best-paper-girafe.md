# CVPR 2021 best paper award goes to GIRAFFE

- **Date:** 2021-06-00
- **Who:** M. Niemeyer, A. Geiger (University of Tubingen / MPI-IS); CVPR 2021 organizers
- **Type:** competition

CVPR 2021 gave its best paper award to "GIRAFFE: Representing Scenes as Compositional Generative Neural Feature Fields," which models scenes as compositions of separately controllable objects in a generative neural radiance-style model, enabling control over object placement and identity. Honorable mentions included "Exploring Simple Siamese Representation Learning" by Chen and He. The exact announcement day during the June conference was not confirmed.
